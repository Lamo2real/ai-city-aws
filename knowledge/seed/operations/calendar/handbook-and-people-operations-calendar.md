**When to use this:** When writing the handbook or planning the year: mandatory written items, fixed annual duties and dates, handbook contents and the people-operations calendar.

**Source section:** 23. Handbook and the yearly people-operations calendar

### Mandatory written items

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary sources; orientation only. Written items by headcount are: a work-environment policy from 10 employees ([Arbetsmiljöverket, SAM](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/arbeta-med-arbetsmiljon/systematiskt-arbetsmiljoarbete-sam/)); a documented pay survey from 10 and documented active measures from 25 ([Diskrimineringslagen 3 kap. 13 to 14 §§](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/)); and written whistleblowing procedures from 50 ([Visselblåsarlagen 5 kap.](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2021890-om-skydd-for-personer-som-rapporterar_sfs-2021-890/)). Guidelines and routines against harassment, sexual harassment and reprisals are required of every employer ([Diskrimineringslagen 3 kap. 6 §](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/)).

### Fixed annual duties and dates

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary sources; orientation only. Annual duties are the pay survey every year ([Diskrimineringslagen 3 kap. 8 §](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/)) and the yearly follow-up of work-environment management ([Arbetsmiljöverket, SAM](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/arbeta-med-arbetsmiljon/systematiskt-arbetsmiljoarbete-sam/)). Fixed dates are the statutory holiday-year turnover on 1 April and the main holiday in June to August with two months' notice of placement ([Semesterlagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/semesterlag-1977480_sfs-1977-480/)); the wellness allowance runs per tax year with receipts ([Skatteverket](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/arbeteochinkomst/formaner/personalvardsformanmotionochfriskvard.4.7459477810df5bccdd4800014540.html)); and a new price base amount applies each 1 January ([Regeringen, Sep 2026](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/09/prisbasbeloppfor-2027-faststallt/)).

### Handbook contents

**[INFERENCE]** A small Swedish employer's handbook covers: employment terms and working hours; overtime approval and records; holiday rules and booking; sick reporting and the certificate from day eight; the rehabilitation routine; parental leave and care-of-sick-child notice; work-environment policy, task allocation and incident reporting; the routine against victimisation and harassment; equal-treatment and pay-setting criteria; remote-work and home-office rules; IT, monitoring and privacy notice; AI-use policy; whistleblowing; expenses and benefits including the wellness allowance; conflicts of interest and side jobs; alcohol and drugs; and offboarding. No authoritative Swedish model handbook was retrieved; this list is the researchers' synthesis.

### People-operations calendar

**[INFERENCE]** The table is the researchers' synthesis of a yearly calendar anchored to the statutory duties above; the months for pay review and surveys are suggestions, not sourced practice. The earlier notes' entry for a possible pay-transparency start on 1 January 2027 has been removed because the 26 March 2026 government statement superseded it.
