**When to use this:** When a question touches work-environment duties: employer responsibility, safety representative and committee, systematic work environment management (AFS 2023:1), organisational and social work environment, remote work. Orientation for a human to confirm.

**Source section:** 19. Swedish work-environment law

### The employer's core duties under the Work Environment Act

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source (Arbetsmiljölagen 1977:1160, text consolidated through SFS 2026:1001; what that 2026 amendment changed was not identified); orientation only. The employer takes every measure needed to prevent ill health and accidents and removes a risk at its source where possible (3 kap. 2 §). The employer systematically plans, directs and checks the work, investigates work injuries, assesses risks continuously, schedules measures that cannot be taken at once, documents the work environment and action plans to the extent the business requires, and runs an organised adaptation and rehabilitation activity (3 kap. 2 a §) ([Arbetsmiljölagen, riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetsmiljolag-19771160_sfs-1977-1160/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The employer provides the occupational health service the working conditions call for, as an independent expert resource (3 kap. 2 c §). Employees get knowledge of conditions and risks, the training they need and work adapted to their individual circumstances (3 kap. 3 §). Deaths, serious injuries and serious incidents are reported to the authority without delay (3 kap. 3 a §). Employees take part in the work, follow rules, use protective equipment and report immediate serious danger (3 kap. 4 §) ([Arbetsmiljölagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetsmiljolag-19771160_sfs-1977-1160/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Breach of an order or prohibition under the Work Environment Act can bring a fine or up to one year in prison (8 kap. 1 §), and sanction fees for breaches of regulations range from SEK 1,000 to SEK 1,000,000 (8 kap. 5 §) ([Arbetsmiljölagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetsmiljolag-19771160_sfs-1977-1160/)).

### Safety representative from five employees, safety committee from fifty

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. One or more safety representatives (skyddsombud) are appointed at a workplace where at least five employees are regularly employed (Arbetsmiljölagen 6 kap. 2 §). A safety committee (skyddskommitté) is required where at least fifty employees are regularly employed, and at smaller workplaces if the employees ask for it (6 kap. 8 §) ([Arbetsmiljölagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetsmiljolag-19771160_sfs-1977-1160/)). Who appoints the representative and the length of the term were not extracted.

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The safety representative takes part in planning changes, is informed of changes affecting the work environment, gets the paid time off the role needs and has access to the documents and information needed (6 kap. 4 to 6 §§). The representative may demand measures or an investigation; the employer confirms receipt in writing and answers without delay, and without action in reasonable time the Work Environment Authority decides on an order or prohibition (6 a §). The representative may stop work that involves immediate and serious danger pending the authority's decision and is not liable for damage caused by doing so (7 §) ([Arbetsmiljölagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetsmiljolag-19771160_sfs-1977-1160/)).

### Systematic work environment management: AFS 2023:1

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Since 1 January 2025 systematic work environment management (SAM) sits in the regulation AFS 2023:1, decided 15 September 2023, which replaced AFS 2001:1. It has 16 sections and one annex. SAM covers all physical, organisational and social conditions that matter for the work environment (5 §), is continuous, and is followed up every year and after reorganisations or serious incidents ([AFS 2023:1, av.se](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/publikationer/foreskrifter/afs-20231/); [Arbetsmiljöverket, SAM](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/arbeta-med-arbetsmiljon/systematiskt-arbetsmiljoarbete-sam/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Under AFS 2023:1 four things must be in writing from ten employees: the work-environment policy (7 §), the routines saying when, by whom and with whose participation the work is done (8 §), the allocation of work-environment tasks (9 §) and the result of the annual follow-up (14 §). Three things must be in writing regardless of size: risk assessments, which state which risks exist and whether they are serious (11 §); an action plan for measures not taken immediately (13 §); and written instructions where the work involves serious risks (10 §) ([AFS 2023:1](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/publikationer/foreskrifter/afs-20231/)).

### Organisational and social work environment: AFS 2023:2, chapter 2

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The regulation AFS 2023:2 on planning and organising work-environment work was decided on 15 September 2023 and has been in force since 1 January 2025. It has nine chapters: general provisions; organisational and social work environment; work adaptation; first aid and crisis support; violence and threats; lone work; pregnant, recently delivered and breastfeeding employees; minors; and records of on-call time, overtime and additional hours ([AFS 2023:2, av.se](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/publikationer/foreskrifter/afs-20232/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Chapter 2 of AFS 2023:2 defines unhealthy workload as demands that exceed resources more than temporarily, which harms health when prolonged and recovery is insufficient, and defines victimisation (kränkande särbehandling) as hostile acts directed at one or more employees that can cause ill health or exclusion from the workplace community (2 kap. 3 §). A business that hires in agency staff is treated as their employer for this chapter (2 §) ([AFS 2023:2](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/publikationer/foreskrifter/afs-20232/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Under chapter 2 of AFS 2023:2 managers and supervisors must know how to prevent and handle unhealthy workload and victimisation and must be given the conditions to apply that knowledge (4 §). The employer sets goals for the organisational and social work environment with employee participation and makes them known; the goals are written where there are ten or more employees (5 §) ([AFS 2023:2](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/publikationer/foreskrifter/afs-20232/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Under chapter 2 of AFS 2023:2 tasks and authority must not create unhealthy workload, meaning resources are matched to demands. Employees must know their tasks, the expected result, which tasks to prioritise when time is short, whom to ask for help and their authority. The employer acts against severely psychologically straining tasks (6 to 8 §§) and prevents the scheduling of working time from causing ill health (9 §) ([AFS 2023:2](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/publikationer/foreskrifter/afs-20232/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Under chapter 2 of AFS 2023:2 the employer states that victimisation is not accepted and counters conditions that can cause it. Routines must say who receives a report, what the recipient does, how the report is handled and how those affected get help quickly, and the routines are made known to all employees. No headcount threshold applies (10 to 11 §§) ([AFS 2023:2](https://www.av.se/arbetsmiljoarbete-och-inspektioner/publikationer/foreskrifter/afs-20232/)).

**[INFERENCE]** Corrected claim: the regulation page for AFS 2023:1 names only AFS 2001:1 as replaced. That the older regulation on organisational and social work environment (AFS 2015:4) was absorbed into chapter 2 of AFS 2023:2 is an inference from the matching content (knowledge, goals, workload, working time, victimisation); the repeal list confirming it was not read.

**[INFERENCE]** The chapter 2 duty that employees know their tasks, the expected result, priorities when time is short and their authority is the legal twin of the house-style rule that expectations are written down. One written role document can serve both.

### Remote and hybrid work

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary sources (the statute and a ministerial answer); orientation only. The Work Environment Act has no section specific to work in the employee's home; its scope is every activity in which an employee works for an employer (1 kap. 2 §) ([Arbetsmiljölagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetsmiljolag-19771160_sfs-1977-1160/)). In an answer to the Riksdag dated 7 April 2021, the employment minister stated that the employer's work-environment responsibility also holds when employees work remotely, stressing risk assessments and cooperation with safety representatives; the answer does not address employer access to the home ([Riksdagen, answer to question 2020/21:2350, 7 Apr 2021](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/H8122350.html)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 30 Jul 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source. On 30 July 2026 the government instructed the Work Environment Authority to analyse remote and hybrid work in relation to employment of people with disabilities and to write practical guidance that also sets out employers' responsibility for work environment, rehabilitation and reasonable adjustments in remote and hybrid work, with a report due 31 July 2028. The decision does not itself state how responsibility applies in the home ([Government decision A2026/00752, 30 Jul 2026](https://regeringen.se/contentassets/e571be55fa834076a0d4d41d2b804f7a/uppdrag-till-arbetsmiljoverket-att-ta-fram-vagledning-om-distans--och-hybridarbete.pdf)).

**[INFERENCE] [GAP]** The statute's scope and the 2021 ministerial answer point the same way: the systematic duties (risk assessment, measures, follow-up) cover remote work. How far the duty reaches inside a private home is not settled by either source. The Work Environment Authority's own remote-work guidance was not opened, so whether the employer may enter the home and how to risk-assess a home office remain unverified.

### Minimum compliance pack by size

**[INFERENCE]** Minimum work-environment pack for a company of 10 to 49 people: written policy; written routines; written task allocation; documented annual follow-up; written goals for the organisational and social work environment; written risk assessments and action plans; a victimisation routine known to all; managers trained in workload and victimisation; an occupational health service contract as needed; and a safety representative (from five employees). At 50, add the safety committee. This list is the researchers' synthesis of the duties above.

**[INFERENCE]** Three different counting rules meet at ten employees: the SAM documents (AFS 2023:1), the written goals for the organisational and social work environment (AFS 2023:2) and the pay-survey documentation (Discrimination Act). One annual review cycle can cover all three.
