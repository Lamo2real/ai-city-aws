**When to use this:** When a day-to-day people question touches Swedish employment law: contract forms, unions and MBL, working time, holiday, sick pay, parental leave, discrimination, whistleblowing, non-EU hires. Orientation for a human to confirm.

**Source section:** 18. Swedish employment framework for day-to-day operations

### Contract forms

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The permitted fixed-term forms are special fixed-term employment, substitute employment and seasonal employment (LAS 5 §). Special fixed-term employment converts to permanent after more than 12 months in a five-year period, and a substitute post after more than two years in five (5 a §). At age 69 the objective-reasons requirement and several other protections stop applying (33 §) ([LAS](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/)).

### Unions and co-determination without a collective agreement

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Unions have a right to negotiate about their members (MBL 10 §). The duty to negotiate before major changes (11 §) binds employers with a collective agreement; an employer without one must negotiate when a matter specifically concerns a union's member, and before redundancies and certain business transfers (13 §) ([MBL](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1976580-om-medbestammande-i-arbetslivet_sfs-1976-580/)).

**[INFERENCE]** A company without a collective agreement still has MBL duties in three situations: redundancies, business transfers, and matters specific to a union member. Operations therefore asks whether the person is a union member before any individual adverse decision, and handles the answer as sensitive data.

### Working time

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source (text consolidated through SFS 2022:450); orientation only. Ordinary working time is 40 hours a week (Arbetstidslagen 5 §). General overtime is at most 48 hours per four weeks or 50 per month and 200 per year (8 §), with up to 150 more hours for special reasons (8 a §). Total working time is at most 48 hours per seven days on average over up to four months (10 b §) ([Arbetstidslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetstidslag-1982673_sfs-1982-673/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Daily rest is 11 consecutive hours including midnight to 05:00 (13 §); weekly rest is 36 consecutive hours (14 §); no one works more than five hours without a break (15 §); schedule changes are announced two weeks ahead (12 §). The employer keeps records of on-call time, overtime and additional part-time hours, open to employees and unions (11 §) ([Arbetstidslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetstidslag-1982673_sfs-1982-673/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The Working Hours Act exempts work the employer cannot supervise, managerial staff and those who largely control their own time (2 §), and collective agreements may deviate from most rules (3 §). The Work Environment Authority supervises working time and can issue orders backed by fines (20 to 22 §§) ([Arbetstidslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetstidslag-1982673_sfs-1982-673/)).

**[FAST-MOVING, Sep 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Unlawful overtime carries a strict-liability sanction fee of 1% of the price base amount per hour and employee (Arbetstidslagen 26 §), about SEK 596 per hour at the 2027 amount of SEK 59,600 ([Arbetstidslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arbetstidslag-1982673_sfs-1982-673/); [Regeringen, Sep 2026](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/09/prisbasbeloppfor-2027-faststallt/)).

**[INFERENCE]** "Trust-based hours" clauses rely on the 2 § exemption and do not remove the employer's work-environment responsibility for workload.

### Holiday

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source (text consolidated through SFS 2014:424); orientation only. Statutory holiday is 25 days a year (Semesterlagen 4 §). The statutory holiday year runs 1 April to 31 March with the preceding year as the earning year (3 §). Four weeks fall in June to August unless otherwise agreed (12 §), and the employer gives two months' notice of placement when there is no agreement (11 §) ([Semesterlagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/semesterlag-1977480_sfs-1977-480/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Days above 20 can be saved for up to five years (18 and 20 §§). Holiday pay for monthly salaried staff is normal pay plus 0.43% of monthly pay per day, with 12% on variable pay (16 a §); otherwise it is 12% of earnings (16 b §). Absence that still earns holiday pay includes sickness up to 180 days per earning year and parental leave up to 120 days, or 180 for single parents (17 to 17 a §§) ([Semesterlagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/semesterlag-1977480_sfs-1977-480/)).

### Sick pay and rehabilitation

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source (text consolidated through SFS 2025:938); orientation only. The employer pays sick pay for 14 calendar days at 80% of lost pay, less a waiting deduction of 20% of one average week's sick pay, with at most ten deductions in twelve months; a relapse within five days continues the same period (Sjuklönelagen 6 to 7 §§). A medical certificate is needed from the seventh calendar day after the sick report, in practice day eight (8 §). Contracts shorter than a month give sick pay only after 14 days worked (3 §) ([Sjuklönelagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-19911047-om-sjuklon_sfs-1991-1047/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The employer reports the case to the Social Insurance Agency from day 15 and by day 21 (the statute says within seven calendar days after the sick-pay period), and failure can be fined. By day 30 the employer must have a written return-to-work plan if the absence is expected to pass 60 days. The rehabilitation duty includes adapting tasks, hours or the workplace and keeping in contact ([Försäkringskassan, sick employee days 1 to 90](https://www.forsakringskassan.se/arbetsgivare/sjukdom-och-skada/sjuk-medarbetare-dag-1-90); [Sjuklönelagen 12 and 19 §§](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-19911047-om-sjuklon_sfs-1991-1047/)). The later rehabilitation checkpoints were not on the page read.

### Parental leave, parental benefit and care of a sick child

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source (text consolidated through SFS 2025:733); orientation only. A parent has a right to full leave until the child is 18 months old and beyond that while on full parental benefit (Föräldraledighetslagen 5 §). Working time may be cut by up to a quarter until the child turns eight (7 §), extended to the end of the second school year from 1 July 2028. The employee gives two months' notice of planned leave, and one week or as soon as possible for care of a sick child (13 §). Leave may be split into three periods a year (10 §), and the employer can postpone an early return by up to a month (15 §) ([Föräldraledighetslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/foraldraledighetslag-1995584_sfs-1995-584/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. After six months' employment a parent of a child under eight may request flexible arrangements such as remote work; the employer answers in reasonable time and gives reasons for a refusal (Föräldraledighetslagen 15 a to 15 c §§) ([Föräldraledighetslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/foraldraledighetslag-1995584_sfs-1995-584/)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 24 Aug 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source (page last updated 24 August 2026; the page does not name the year its amounts apply to); orientation only. Parental benefit is 480 days per child, shared by the parents: 390 days at the income-based level and 90 days at the minimum level of SEK 180 a day. Ninety of the income-based days are reserved for each parent and cannot be transferred (60 for children born 2015 or earlier); other days can be transferred, including to someone other than a parent. An employee with regular income usually gets about 80% of pay, and the income-based day rate is shown as SEK 250 to 1,259 for a full day ([Försäkringskassan, parental benefit](https://www.forsakringskassan.se/privatperson/foralder/foraldrapenning)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 24 Aug 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. To get the income-based level for the first 180 days a parent needs an income corresponding to at least SEK 7,083 a month for at least 240 days before the expected birth. Days can be used until the child turns 12 or finishes school year five, at most 96 days can be kept after the child's fourth birthday, and twins give 660 days ([Försäkringskassan, parental benefit, updated 24 Aug 2026](https://www.forsakringskassan.se/privatperson/foralder/foraldrapenning)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 10 Nov 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source (page last updated 10 November 2026, covering children under 12); orientation only. Temporary parental benefit for care of a sick child (VAB) is 120 days per child per year, shared among those who care for the child. Once 60 days are used, it is no longer paid for days when the child's regular carer is ill, only when the child is ill or has a care visit. Compensation is a little under 80% of qualifying income, and income above 7.5 price base amounts is not covered. A certificate from a doctor or nurse is needed when the child is ill for more than seven days, weekends included. The application must reach the Social Insurance Agency within 30 days of the first day at home ([Försäkringskassan, VAB](https://www.forsakringskassan.se/privatperson/foralder/vard-av-barn-vab)).

### Discrimination: active measures and harassment

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Active measures follow a continuous four-step method (investigate risks, analyse causes, act, follow up) across five areas (Diskrimineringslagen 3 kap. 2 to 3 §§). Guidelines and routines against harassment, sexual harassment and reprisals are mandatory for every employer (6 §), and the work is done in cooperation with employees (11 §). On learning that an employee feels harassed, the employer must investigate and take reasonable measures (2 kap. 3 §) ([Diskrimineringslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/)).

### Whistleblowing

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Internal whistleblowing channels are mandatory from 50 employees at the start of the year (lag 2021:890, 5 kap. 2 §), and private employers with 50 to 249 employees may share intake and investigation (3 §). The requirements are independent designated persons, written procedures, written and oral reporting and a meeting on request, receipt confirmed within seven days, feedback within three months, and records kept at most two years after the case closes ([Visselblåsarlagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2021890-om-skydd-for-personer-som-rapporterar_sfs-2021-890/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The bans on hindering reports and on reprisals protect employees, applicants, interns, volunteers, self-employed contractors, board members and active shareholders, with a reversed burden of proof (1 kap. 8 §, 3 kap. 1 and 5 §§). These protections are not tied to the 50-employee threshold for channels ([Visselblåsarlagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2021890-om-skydd-for-personer-som-rapporterar_sfs-2021-890/)).

### Hiring non-EU staff: rules from 1 June 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 Jun 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. For a work permit, pay must be at least 90% of the median, SEK 34,470 a month on a median of SEK 38,300, and must also match collective-agreement or customary levels. The rule applies to first-time applicants decided on or after 1 June 2026, wherever the team sits ([Migrationsverket, pay requirement](https://www.migrationsverket.se/ordforklaringar/lonekrav-for-arbetstillstand.html); [Migrationsverket news, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.migrationsverket.se/arbetsgivare/nyhetsarkiv-for-arbetsgivare/nyheter/2026-06-01-nya-regler-for-arbetstillstand-trader-i-kraft.html)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 Jun 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. A reduced floor of 75% (SEK 28,725) applies to listed occupations, including IT technicians and systems administrators. Former students and researchers on a first work permit, and some young technology or life-science companies (under five years old, under 100 staff), are also exempted from the 90% level ([Migrationsverket, pay requirement](https://www.migrationsverket.se/ordforklaringar/lonekrav-for-arbetstillstand.html)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 Jun 2026] [CONTESTED]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Extensions of pre-June permits applied for between 1 June and 1 December 2026 are assessed under the older requirement; from 2 December 2026 extensions must meet the new one. The two Migration Agency pages describe the older rule differently: one calls it 80% of the median, the other the "good livelihood" requirement ([Migrationsverket news, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.migrationsverket.se/arbetsgivare/nyhetsarkiv-for-arbetsgivare/nyheter/2026-06-01-nya-regler-for-arbetstillstand-trader-i-kraft.html); [Migrationsverket, pay requirement](https://www.migrationsverket.se/ordforklaringar/lonekrav-for-arbetstillstand.html)). Any extension that can be filed by 1 December 2026 for a pre-June permit holder should be checked now.

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 Jun 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Permits of a year or less need comprehensive health insurance, the EU Blue Card can now run four years, and an application can be refused because of the employer's own offences or sanctions ([Migrationsverket news, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.migrationsverket.se/arbetsgivare/nyhetsarkiv-for-arbetsgivare/nyheter/2026-06-01-nya-regler-for-arbetstillstand-trader-i-kraft.html)).
