**When to use this:** Before processing employee or candidate data, monitoring, background checks or offence data, or setting retention. Orientation for a human to confirm.

**Source section:** 20. Employee data and monitoring

### Consent is weak in employment

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source (IMY); orientation only. Consent carries little weight as a legal basis in employment because of the dependency relationship, and the employer must be able to show lawful handling ([IMY, working life](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/)).

### Monitoring

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. The steps IMY expects before monitoring are a specific purpose, a documented balancing of interests, clear information in advance, proportionality (group level before individual level), consideration of a data protection impact assessment for systematic email or web monitoring, and prevention such as site blocking before after-the-fact checks ([IMY, monitoring of employees](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/kontroll-och-overvakning-av-anstallda/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Not allowed as a rule, per IMY: reading private files or email (with an exception for serious suspicion of disloyalty or crime), reusing data for an incompatible purpose, and monitoring that leaves staff feeling constantly watched. Security motives weigh more than efficiency motives ([IMY, monitoring of employees](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/kontroll-och-overvakning-av-anstallda/)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 Apr 2025]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Since 1 April 2025 workplace camera use needs no IMY permit but does need a documented balancing of interests ([IMY, monitoring of employees](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/kontroll-och-overvakning-av-anstallda/)).

### Offence data and background checks

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Private employers may process offence data only with support in law, to handle legal claims, to meet a legal duty, under IMY's regulation IMYFS 2024:1, or with an IMY permit. Consent never works as a basis ([IMY, offence data](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/tillaten-behandling--vilka-krav-galler/brottsuppgifter/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. On background checks, asking a question or glancing at a paper criminal-record extract without recording falls outside GDPR. Scanning, noting or structuring the content falls inside it and is not permitted without specific legal support; even logging that an extract was shown is processing and is generally not a relevant data point. Sector laws for schools and work with children give explicit authority ([IMY, offence data](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/tillaten-behandling--vilka-krav-galler/brottsuppgifter/)).

### Retention points found

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Whistleblowing case data may not be kept longer than two years after the case closes ([Visselblåsarlagen 7 kap. 8 §](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-2021890-om-skydd-for-personer-som-rapporterar_sfs-2021-890/)). Candidate data is deleted or anonymised when the recruitment ends, subject to the claim window ([IMY, recruitment systems](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/rekryteringssystem-och-kompetensdatabaser/)).

**[GAP]** IMY guidance on GPS tracking, drug testing and social-media screening of candidates was cut off or not on the pages read. Whether Sweden has enacted a general law on employer background checks was not searched further.
