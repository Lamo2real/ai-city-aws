**When to use this:** As the default approach to training, delegation and approval limits, hiring funnels, culture and performance follow-up. Swedish law overrides the firing advice (section 3); use the brand's own voice. Read part 1 for how reliable the sourcing is.

**Source section:** 2. House style: Alex and Leila Hormozi's operating models (part 2 of 2)

### Training: show, observe, release (and the "3Ds" label)

**[HOUSE STYLE]** The training sequence Alex Hormozi describes in his video "If I Wanted to Create a Business That Runs Itself" (4 November 2025, about 28 minutes) has three steps: the trainee watches the task being done, then does it while observed, then works alone with the trainer available. The transcript names no framework for this ([Rosetta transcript, primary transcript, 4 Nov 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/if-i-wanted-to-create-a-business-that-runs-itself-here-s-what-i-d-do)).

**[HOUSE STYLE] [CONTESTED]** The label "document, demonstrate, duplicate" (the 3Ds) was not found on any primary Hormozi source. A third-party site attributes it to the book $100M Leads without a chapter reference. As that site describes it: document means writing every step as a literal checklist a newcomer could follow and testing it on someone unfamiliar; demonstrate means performing the process with the checklist visible while narrating the reasoning; duplicate means the trainee performs with the checklist while the trainer observes and corrects without taking over ([Margin Notes, 3Ds training model, second-hand](https://margin-notes-600.pages.dev/frameworks/3ds-training-model)). The knowledge base may keep the 3Ds as house vocabulary but attributes the label to the third-party summary and the behaviour to the 4 November 2025 video.

**[HOUSE STYLE]** The same third-party page gives counts that may be the summariser's own additions: demonstrate two to three times, and have the trainee complete the full process independently at least three times at acceptable quality before working unsupervised ([Margin Notes, second-hand](https://margin-notes-600.pages.dev/frameworks/3ds-training-model)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** The "graduation" test in the 4 November 2025 video is that the new person reaches roughly 80% of the owner's result, with 60 to 80% accepted early, and people are judged on speed of improvement and not on starting level. Recurring decisions are converted into if-then rules, and scorecards and KPIs track results. Unaudited ([Rosetta transcript, 4 Nov 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/if-i-wanted-to-create-a-business-that-runs-itself-here-s-what-i-d-do)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Leila Hormozi's training principle, from the summary of her 21 May 2026 episode, is that people learn by trying and not by hearing an explanation ([Podcast Rex summary](https://podcastrex.com/shows/build-with-leila-hormozi/the-hiring-system-that-lets-your-business-run-solo-ep-362)). Workshop notes add a related principle from Alex: everything is trainable, and the leader models the behaviour, then role-plays and drills each person until it matches ([Dickie Bush, 12 takeaways](https://dickiebush.substack.com/p/12-takeaways-from-alex-hormozis-scaling)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Feedback and coaching name observable behaviours (arrive earlier, speak faster, take more calls) and not traits, according to Shortform summaries of two episodes ("How To Raise Your Standards TODAY" with Leila Hormozi, 25 Nov 2024, and "Diagnose Before You Scale Q&A") ([Shortform summary, 25 Nov 2024](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2024-11-25-episode-summary-how-to-raise-your-standards-today-w-leila-hormozi-ep-795); [Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-02-05-episode-summary-diagnose-before-you-scale-q-a-ep-1001)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** An older training kit appears in show notes for a 29 September 2017 episode, "How to Train Employees": a "Four Rs" document per role (roles, responsibilities, requirements, results), a daily "perfect day" checklist, digital training material per position and defined communication cycles. This is show notes only and predates 2024 ([Audioscrape, The Game Ep. 25](https://audioscrape.com/podcast/the-game-with-alex-hormozi/episode/how-to-train-employees--ep-25)).

### Delegation, approval limits and a business that runs without the owner

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Delegation works, in Alex Hormozi's 5 July 2021 video, when people know what is wanted (in writing), how to do it (trained) and why it matters (purpose tied to the bottom line and the mission). He defines scaling yourself as getting results through others ([Rosetta transcript, 5 Jul 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-scale-yourself-the-what-why-how-framework)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** His 4 November 2025 video gives a five-step method for a business that runs without the owner. Step one is a time study: log activity every 15 minutes for a week, then list every task in detail. Step two assigns each task to a project, a process or a person and colours it: green means delegate now, yellow means it needs a one-off project or a process first, red means it needs a skill or a hire the company lacks. Step three moves the owner's work from doing to recruiting, strategy and incentive alignment. Step four takes the founder out of marketing with systems that collect customer proof such as reviews and recorded calls reused as content. Step five is a stress test: step away (he suggests about 90 days) and fix whatever breaks ([Rosetta transcript, 4 Nov 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/if-i-wanted-to-create-a-business-that-runs-itself-here-s-what-i-d-do)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Decision rights in the same video: give staff spending or approval limits so they can act unsupervised. His examples range from about $500 to $100,000 depending on company size; these are illustrations and unaudited. The video gives no team meeting cadence ([Rosetta transcript, 4 Nov 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/if-i-wanted-to-create-a-business-that-runs-itself-here-s-what-i-d-do)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Illustrative claims in the 4 November 2025 video, all unaudited: a business with $10M revenue and $2M profit valued at about six times profit once it runs without the owner, and a delegation example in which 50 hours of work cost 5 hours of owner time ([Rosetta transcript, 4 Nov 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/if-i-wanted-to-create-a-business-that-runs-itself-here-s-what-i-d-do)). Workshop notes add that as the owner leaves customer-facing work the job becomes "selling" to employees to drive performance and retention, and that above about $3M to $5M of revenue the owner should hire a proven expert to build a function; the note-taker regretted about 200 hours spent learning paid advertising ([Dickie Bush, 12 takeaways](https://dickiebush.substack.com/p/12-takeaways-from-alex-hormozis-scaling)).

### Hiring and the talent funnel

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Leila Hormozi's hiring model, from the summary of her 21 May 2026 episode, is to run talent acquisition with the rigour of customer acquisition. Her mapping: the job description works like a sales page, the interview like a sales call, onboarding like fulfilment and retention like lifetime value ([Podcast Rex summary, second-hand](https://podcastrex.com/shows/build-with-leila-hormozi/the-hiring-system-that-lets-your-business-run-solo-ep-362)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Alex Hormozi's hiring methods in the 4 November 2025 video: interview far more candidates than feels necessary (his claim is 600 developers interviewed for one chief technology officer hire, at 30 to 60 minutes each, unaudited); use group interviews, including for director and executive roles; prefer candidates who describe past work in detailed metrics and who can teach the owner something; and hire the first good person in a role, then use that person as the pattern for later hires ([Rosetta transcript, 4 Nov 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/if-i-wanted-to-create-a-business-that-runs-itself-here-s-what-i-d-do)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** A Shortform summary of "How To Raise Your Standards TODAY" (25 November 2024) reports that Leila Hormozi watches every interview for highly important candidates, reviews interview recordings, flags details such as response speed and rejects finalists who miss the bar ([Shortform summary, 25 Nov 2024](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2024-11-25-episode-summary-how-to-raise-your-standards-today-w-leila-hormozi-ep-795)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** A Shortform summary of "Diagnose Before You Scale Q&A" reports Alex Hormozi's approach to sales hiring: metrics-based interviews assessing performance, learning ability, process adherence and teamwork; scripted video submissions and group interviews to see how candidates respond to feedback; base pay plus incentives; a profit-sharing pool of 10 to 20% with about two-thirds reserved for future hires; and starting salespeople on outbound before promoting them to inbound. Unaudited ([Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-02-05-episode-summary-diagnose-before-you-scale-q-a-ep-1001)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Shortform summaries of two 2026 episodes ("Improving Team Performance Through Elimination" and "Give Me 15 Minutes and I'll Fix Your Company Culture") report his claim that top performers reveal themselves within 7 to 14 days, so long probation periods add little ([Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-01-episode-summary-improving-team-performance-through-elimination-ep-995); [Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-10-27-episode-summary-give-me-15-minutes-and-i-ll-fix-your-company-culture-ep-1010)). Shortform's own counterpoint is that a 7 to 14 day window may not suit complex roles. Section 3 explains why a Swedish employer still uses the statutory probation period deliberately.

**[HOUSE STYLE]** In the 5 July 2022 video he says moving an underperformer to another role is wrong nine times out of ten, and that the role should be filled against the track record one would normally require ([Rosetta transcript, 5 Jul 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-simple-managerial-framework-that-changed-my-business)). This collides with the Swedish redeployment duty described in section 3.

### Culture and standards

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Alex Hormozi defines culture as the enacted, mostly unspoken rules about which behaviours get rewarded or punished, learned first-hand and by watching others. Inconsistent enforcement by status teaches people that status decides. Leaders act as reinforcers, and the standard must cascade through each layer ([Shortform summary of "Improving Team Performance Through Elimination"](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-01-episode-summary-improving-team-performance-through-elimination-ep-995); [Shortform summary of "Give Me 15 Minutes and I'll Fix Your Company Culture"](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-10-27-episode-summary-give-me-15-minutes-and-i-ll-fix-your-company-culture-ep-1010)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** The same summaries report these rules about standards: the real standard is set by the weakest performer a team keeps; who is let go signals culture more than who is promoted; standards rise as the company wins, tested by asking whether the leader five years from now would accept today's team; and standards are set per department or role ([Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-01-episode-summary-improving-team-performance-through-elimination-ep-995); [Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-10-27-episode-summary-give-me-15-minutes-and-i-ll-fix-your-company-culture-ep-1010)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** From the Shortform summary of the 25 November 2024 episode: Leila Hormozi says standards dilute with growth unless the team stays concentrated around people who share values and work ethic; she distinguishes being nice (avoiding conflict) from being kind (the harder act that helps the person) and tells people where they stand and what to change. Alex Hormozi's lines in the same episode are that a leader gets the business the leader tolerates, that fewer, better people is the aim, and that the person with the highest standards should make the decision ([Shortform summary, 25 Nov 2024](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2024-11-25-episode-summary-how-to-raise-your-standards-today-w-leila-hormozi-ep-795)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** His lateness story, from a video dated 26 July 2021: a gym owner locked the door at the meeting start time and dismissed the latecomers to reset basics as non-negotiable, with a companion story of a manager who quietly lowered the owner's standards. The point he draws is that advanced people still do the basics ([Rosetta transcript, "We fired everyone who was late", 26 Jul 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/we-fired-everyone-who-was-late-this-is-why)). The story is US practice and cannot be copied in Sweden; see section 3.

### Performance and firing frameworks

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Leila Hormozi's F.I.R.E. framework, from detailed notes on her episode "How to Know If It's Time to Let Someone Go" (20 June 2025), names four triggers: Fails to perform despite the same coaching others received; Ignores values (gossip, disrespect, drama, confidentiality breaches); Rejects feedback (agrees to change and does not); Exhibits apathy. Her rule is that consistently showing two or more means it is time to part ways ([Metacast episode notes, second-hand, 20 Jun 2025](https://metacast.app/podcast/build-with-leila-hormozi/WBcLe3ng/how-to-know-if-it-s-time-to-let-someone-go-ep-287/bslfgifb)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** In the same episode she says about 90% of terminations at her company in the prior six months were for values and not performance (unaudited). Her "cold versus cancer" test separates a temporary dip that is treatable with feedback and support, where outside life factors are considered, from a long-standing pattern that spreads and is fixed only by removal. High performers who create drama must still go ([Metacast episode notes, 20 Jun 2025](https://metacast.app/podcast/build-with-leila-hormozi/WBcLe3ng/how-to-know-if-it-s-time-to-let-someone-go-ep-287/bslfgifb)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Her process in that episode: give clear, direct feedback and confirm the person had the same coaching as peers; track whether feedback is acted on; plan the exit to limit team impact and prepare messaging; do not treat someone who is being kept passive-aggressively; be direct, kind and fast. She frames firing as not being a punishment. The episode gives no fixed number of warnings, no improvement-plan timeline, no severance and no legal steps ([Metacast episode notes, 20 Jun 2025](https://metacast.app/podcast/build-with-leila-hormozi/WBcLe3ng/how-to-know-if-it-s-time-to-let-someone-go-ep-287/bslfgifb)).

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Alex Hormozi on speed, from the two Shortform summaries: removing the bottom of a team lifts it more than adding one star; he contrasts "local empathy" for the individual with "global empathy" for the whole team; the leader's job is to absorb the discomfort and shorten the delay between knowing and acting while treating the person with respect; give limited chances; judge on measurable results. He allows that early-stage companies may have to accept weaker hires temporarily ([Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-01-episode-summary-improving-team-performance-through-elimination-ep-995); [Shortform summary](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-10-27-episode-summary-give-me-15-minutes-and-i-ll-fix-your-company-culture-ep-1010)).

**[CONTESTED]** Shortform's own counterpoints, which are not the Hormozis', are that coaching may be cheaper than removal and that a strict removal culture can create fear ([Shortform summary of "Improving Team Performance Through Elimination"](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-01-episode-summary-improving-team-performance-through-elimination-ep-995)). No independent evidence on the Hormozi firing approach was found.

### The house-style operating loop for this department

**[INFERENCE]** The house-style operating loop for the Operations department has five steps. First, each quarter name the one constraint and the one outcome that would change everything. Second, classify every proposed project as more, better or new and require a higher hurdle (about 20% expected gain, per the unaudited workshop note) for anything new. Third, time-box work to about 12 weeks with few people. Fourth, review weekly the few numbers that define the constraint. Fifth, re-diagnose when the constraint moves. This loop is the researchers' synthesis of the sources in this section.

**[INFERENCE]** The what, how, why diagnosis applies before blaming a person or an agent: check the written expectation, then training and tools, then incentives and purpose. Paired with the training sequence, every standard operating procedure is the "document" step, a recorded walkthrough is the "demonstrate" step, and supervised runs with a sign-off count are the "duplicate" step. Applied to agents, the three causes become an unclear instruction (prompt or brief), a missing capability (tools, context, examples) or a misaligned incentive (evaluation criteria that reward the wrong thing). The agent adaptation is the researchers' own; the Hormozis have not published anything about managing AI agents.
