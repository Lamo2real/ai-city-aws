**When to use this:** When onboarding a new hire: statutory deadlines in the first weeks (written terms within seven days), what the evidence supports, the onboarding checklist and house-style training.

**Source section:** 14. Onboarding

### Statutory deadlines in the first weeks

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Written information on core employment terms is due no later than the seventh calendar day after work starts for one group of items and within one month for the rest, and any change must be documented no later than when it takes effect (LAS 6 c to 6 e §§). The itemised list of what the information must contain was not read item by item ([LAS, riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Probation may run for at most six months. Ending it early needs at least two weeks' written advance notice, and the employee's union is notified if the employee is a member. Probation that is not ended becomes permanent employment (LAS 6 § and 31 §) ([LAS](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Employment is presumed full time unless otherwise agreed, and on request the employer must explain a part-time arrangement in writing within three weeks (LAS 4 a §) ([LAS](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/)). A new hire who starts after 31 August in a holiday year is entitled to only five holiday days that year under the statute (Semesterlagen 4 §) ([Semesterlagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/semesterlag-1977480_sfs-1977-480/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Employees must be told clearly about any monitoring of IT, email or performance no later than when data collection begins ([IMY, monitoring of employees](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/kontroll-och-overvakning-av-anstallda/)).

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 Jun 2026]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. For a non-EU hire, the employer must notify the Swedish Migration Agency if the person has not started within four months of the permit decision ([Migrationsverket, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.migrationsverket.se/arbetsgivare/nyhetsarkiv-for-arbetsgivare/nyheter/2026-06-01-nya-regler-for-arbetstillstand-trader-i-kraft.html)).

### What the evidence supports

**[VENDOR]** Role clarity and having the materials to do the job are among the twelve engagement conditions Gallup measures in its Q12 survey, which supports front-loading expectations and equipment in onboarding ([Gallup, Q12 meta-analysis page](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/649487/world-largest-ongoing-study-employee-experience.aspx)).

**[GAP]** Evidence on onboarding outcomes (Gallup onboarding figures, the Bauer and SHRM Foundation "four Cs", time-to-productivity) and on 30/60/90-day plans was not retrieved. Google's data-driven onboarding guide (August 2025) is listed on re:Work but was not read ([Google re:Work, hiring](https://rework.withgoogle.com/intl/en/subjects/hiring)).

### Onboarding checklist and house-style training

**[INFERENCE]** Onboarding checklist for Operations. Before day one: signed contract, written-terms document ready, payroll and pension enrolment, insurance enrolment, an IT ticket for laptop, accounts and least-privilege access, privacy notice and monitoring notice, and a work-environment introduction booked. Days one to seven: hand over the written terms (legal deadline day seven), have policies acknowledged, and run a home-office risk self-assessment if the person works remotely. Days 30, 60 and 90: documented goals and a check-in at each point. Before month five ends: an explicit probation decision by a human, so the six-month limit is not passed by default.

**[INFERENCE]** Diarise the probation end date on the day the contract is signed; missing it converts the hire to permanent employment by operation of LAS 6 §.

**[HOUSE STYLE] [INFERENCE]** The house style treats onboarding as fulfilment in the hiring funnel and trains by showing, observing and releasing (section 2). For each task in a new hire's role the department provides a written checklist, a recorded walkthrough and supervised runs, and uses the unaudited graduation heuristic of roughly 80% of the benchmark result, with 60 to 80% accepted early ([Rosetta transcript, 4 Nov 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/if-i-wanted-to-create-a-business-that-runs-itself-here-s-what-i-d-do)). Formal onboarding programmes are a level 6 move in the roadmap (20 to 49 people) ([Podcast Rex transcript, 9 Apr 2026](https://podcastrex.com/shows/build-with-leila-hormozi/scale-your-business-like-a-pro-in-2026-ep-350/transcript)).
