**When to use this:** Whenever the law, the evidence and the house style point different ways: which wins, why the firing advice does not transfer to Sweden, the collision table, and where evidence and house style agree.

**Source section:** 3. Precedence: law first, evidence second, house style third

### The precedence rule

**[INFERENCE]** When sources collide, law wins over evidence and evidence wins over house style. The house style is the default only where neither of the other two layers speaks. An agent that finds a house-style instruction pointing one way and a legal rule pointing another follows the legal rule, records the collision in the decision record and escalates to a named human if the matter concerns a person's employment, pay, data or health. This precedence rule is the owner's instruction, expressed here as the researchers' synthesis.

### Why the firing advice does not transfer to Sweden

**[INFERENCE] [DURABLE]** The Hormozis' fast-removal advice reflects US at-will employment, where an employer can usually end employment without stating a reason. Swedish law works differently. Under LAS an employer needs objective reasons (sakliga skäl) for dismissal, which are either shortage of work or reasons tied to the individual, and objective reasons do not exist if the employer can reasonably offer the employee other work ([LAS 7 §, checked on primary source](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/)). Under MBL an employer with no collective agreement at all must still negotiate with every affected union before redundancy dismissals ([MBL 13 §, checked on primary source](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1976580-om-medbestammande-i-arbetslivet_sfs-1976-580/)). The transferable parts of the house style are early, direct, behaviour-specific feedback, written expectations, and not delaying the decision to start a proper process. This is orientation, not legal advice.

### Where evidence and house style agree

**[INFERENCE]** Several house-style rules are supported by independent sources and need no override. Fixing one constraint at a time matches the five focusing steps ([TOC Institute](https://www.tocinstitute.org/five-focusing-steps.html)). Writing every process as a checklist and training against it matches standardized work and the job-instruction documents the Lean Enterprise Institute lists ([LEI, Standardized Work](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/standardized-work/)). Reviewing a few numbers weekly matches the scorecard practice in EOS and 4DX ([EOS Worldwide, Level 10 Meeting](https://www.eosworldwide.com/blog/level-10-meeting); [FranklinCovey, The 4 Disciplines](https://www.franklincovey.com/the-4-disciplines/)). Written spending and approval limits match the explicit decision roles of RAPID and DACI ([Bain, RAPID](https://www.bain.com/insights/rapid-decision-making); [Atlassian, DACI](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/daci)). Writing expectations down matches GitLab's documentation-first practice ([GitLab Handbook, Asynchronous work](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/company/culture/all-remote/asynchronous/)).
