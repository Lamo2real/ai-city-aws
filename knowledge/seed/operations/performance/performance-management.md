**When to use this:** When setting up or running performance follow-up: the evidence, Swedish limits (LAS, data) and a light performance cycle. Warnings and outcomes are a human's decision.

**Source section:** 15. Performance management

### What the evidence says

**[VENDOR]** Gallup's Q12 meta-analysis covers 183,806 business units across 736 studies; top-quartile units on engagement show 23% higher profit than bottom-quartile units, with lower absenteeism, turnover and safety incidents. Gallup attributes roughly 70% of the variance in team engagement to the manager. These are the vendor's own data and analysis; the 70% figure is best used as a prompt to invest in manager training and not as a precise estimate ([Gallup, Q12 meta-analysis page](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/649487/world-largest-ongoing-study-employee-experience.aspx)).

**[DURABLE]** CIPD's evidence review on performance feedback (introduction only was read) finds that research broadly supports feedback as a performance tool but that poorly delivered feedback can reduce performance ([CIPD, performance feedback review](https://www.cipd.org/en/knowledge/evidence-reviews/performance-feedback/)).

**[GAP]** Not verified: CIPD's review on goal setting and appraisal, Google's Project Oxygen and Project Aristotle, promotion frameworks, manager-training curricula and engagement-survey design.

### Swedish limits on performance follow-up

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. IMY's position is that following up performance is allowed when needed for the contract, for example results-based pay, or for planning work, but it must not be unreasonable or degrading, and routine monitoring of how staff carry out tasks or take breaks is generally not allowed ([IMY, monitoring of employees](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/kontroll-och-overvakning-av-anstallda/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Free-text assessments in competence databases raise the risk of unlawful processing; IMY prefers fixed fields and requires entries to be factual, accurate and not offensive ([IMY, recruitment systems and competence databases](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/dataskydd-pa-olika-omraden/arbetsliv/rekryteringssystem-och-kompetensdatabaser/)).

**[DURABLE]** Legal status: checked on primary source; orientation only. Training and skills development, and pay and terms, are active-measures areas under the Discrimination Act, and promotion decisions carry the same merit-information duty as hiring ([Diskrimineringslagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/diskrimineringslag-2008567_sfs-2008-567/)).

**[FAST-MOVING, Jul 2026]** Legal status: read on a non-official reproduction of the AI Act; orientation only. AI systems used to monitor and evaluate workers' performance and behaviour, or to decide promotion, are listed as high-risk in Annex III point 4(b) ([AI Act Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/)).

### A light performance cycle

**[INFERENCE]** A light cycle that fits a small company: quarterly goals, weekly or fortnightly one-to-ones, two written reviews a year, and a calibration meeting before the pay review so that the annual pay survey has documented criteria to analyse.

**[HOUSE STYLE] [INFERENCE]** The house-style contribution to performance management is the what, how, why diagnosis before any conclusion about the person, behaviour-specific feedback, telling people where they stand, and written expectations (section 2). Leila Hormozi's F.I.R.E. triggers and her "cold versus cancer" test serve only as internal signals for opening a documented process ([Metacast notes, 20 Jun 2025](https://metacast.app/podcast/build-with-leila-hormozi/WBcLe3ng/how-to-know-if-it-s-time-to-let-someone-go-ep-287/bslfgifb)).

**[INFERENCE]** A performance-improvement process in Sweden doubles as the evidence trail for dismissal on personal grounds: documented expectations, support, a written warning and a redeployment check all map onto the LAS 7 § test ([LAS](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-198280-om-anstallningsskydd_sfs-1982-80/)). Because a dismissal for personal reasons may not rest only on facts known for more than two months, the documentation starts as soon as the problem is known.
