**When to use this:** When planning LinkedIn ads for B2B, especially with executives or experts who post.

## Thought Leader Ads are the clearest efficiency lever

**[VENDOR, 21 Feb 2026, 2,828 ads]** Thought Leader Ads, which boost posts from personal profiles, show median landing-page CPC of $3.06 versus $13.23 for single image, yet receive only 7 to 10% of budgets; top performers are written in first person with links low in the text, and a 15 to 20% test allocation is suggested ([ZenABM](https://zenabm.com/blog/tla-benchmarks/)). Low-friction content offers convert at 10 to 15% against 2 to 5% for demo offers, so sequence offers accordingly ([ZenABM](https://zenabm.com/blog/linkedin-ads-benchmarks-2026)). **[FAST-MOVING; excerpts]** Eligibility requires that the member's profile displays their full name and the post is public; a call-to-action button is not supported; the scope is "your company's thought leaders' posts and public content from LinkedIn members", so not employees only; the member must approve each sponsorship request; and the advertiser needs a Page admin or Sponsored Content poster role plus creative manager access on the ad account. A Creator Marketplace exists in Campaign Manager ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1399568); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a5965137); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a420316); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a9306043)).
