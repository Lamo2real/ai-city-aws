**When to use this:** When choosing LinkedIn ad formats.

## Formats: lead forms, documents, messages, CTV and the end of Accelerate

**[FAST-MOVING; excerpts]** Lead Gen Forms allow a maximum of 12 fields including up to three custom questions, and LinkedIn recommends three to four fields because fewer fields likely improve conversion ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a423364); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a421181)). Document ads accept files up to 100 MB and 300 pages, PDF recommended, served in feed and on LinkedIn Audience Network, with a lead form attached under the lead generation objective ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a493903); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a737898)). **Since mid-October 2024 LinkedIn supports EU targeting for Sponsored Messaging conversation and message ad sets**, which supersedes the earlier EU block; a dedicated page covers Sponsored Messaging in the EEA and another lists extra EU ad-policy prohibitions tied to Ireland's Online Safety Code ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a425071); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a544586); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a7171708)). **Accelerate campaigns ended as a standalone campaign type on 4 August 2026**; AI generation of images, text and variants is available for single image, message and conversation ads inside the standard flow ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1640350?lang=en-US); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1640349)). Connected TV ads require the "Connected TV Only" ad set toggle and are **limited to the United States and Canada with English as audience language** ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a5966603)). **[INFERENCE]** Any rule saying message ads cannot run in the EU is out of date, though EEA-specific conditions on the unread page probably still limit reach; any instruction to "create an Accelerate campaign" should be rewritten to use the standard flow with AI creative assistance; and LinkedIn connected TV is not available for Swedish campaigns.
