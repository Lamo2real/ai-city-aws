## Structure, audience sizes, bidding and budgets from LinkedIn's own pages

**[FAST-MOVING; LinkedIn help pages read as search-index excerpts only, Nov 2026]** LinkedIn's help pages now use a campaign > **ad set** > ad structure where older material said "campaign". The minimum audience size for an ad set is **300 member accounts, "but we suggest a minimum of 50,000 to drive results"** ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a423690); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a422123)). LinkedIn's business site recommends over 50,000 for Sponsored Content and Text Ads and over 15,000 for Message Ads, and its success guide repeats 50,000 for every objective ([LinkedIn](https://business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/targeting/ad-targeting-best-practices); [LinkedIn Ads Success Guide](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/marketing-solutions/images/winbackdocads/pdfs/lms-linkedIn-ads-success-guide-a.pdf)). **[VENDOR, Mar 2026]** One agency guide recommends 50,000 to 300,000 and turning off Audience Network initially ([Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/complete-guide-linkedin-advertising)). Company and contact lists must match at least 300 member accounts; contact lists can take up to 48 hours to process; and location is a required targeting facet ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a423102); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1489764); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a421822)). Lookalike audiences are discontinued and Predictive Audiences, built from first- or third-party source data plus LinkedIn's AI, are the replacement ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1631056); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a423698)).

**[FAST-MOVING; excerpts]** The three bidding strategies are Maximum delivery (automated, aims to spend the full budget), Cost cap (a cost per result used as a benchmark) and Manual bidding. Run cost cap for at least 15 days; with Maximum delivery, a large budget and a small audience can mean the same members see ads more often ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a421112); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a706289); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a423490)). **Daily spend can reach up to double the daily budget** ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a422101)). Minimums seen in excerpts: an ad set budget of at least $10, a $30 minimum campaign daily budget with Dynamic Group Budget, and $50,000 per campaign for Premiere video ads ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a441432); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1502134); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a9958014)). The Conversions API sends online and offline conversion events server-to-server, set up through a partner, a direct API or Google Tag Manager ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1655394); [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a1718034)). **[INFERENCE]** Treat 300 as a hard delivery floor and 50,000 (15,000 for message ads) as the planning number, and set pacing alerts knowing that a day can spend twice its budget.
