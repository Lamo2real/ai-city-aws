**When to use this:** When planning organic LinkedIn posting for people or company pages.

## Organic: topical consistency from named people

**[FAST-MOVING, Mar 2026]** LinkedIn rebuilt its feed on LLM-based retrieval and a sequence-based ranker that uses dwell and skip signals ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/linkedin-rebuilds-its-feed-from-scratch-with-llms-and-gpu-powered-ranking/)). **[Practitioner, 2 Jun 2026]** Richard van der Blom advises keeping about 80% of posts on one main topic, posting 2 to 3 times a week, favouring text-plus-image posts and carousels, and using newsletters, which he says drive about 80% of his LinkedIn-attributed conversions; off-topic commenting confuses topical classification ([Creator Science](https://creatorscience.com/podcast/richard-van-der-blom-2/)). **[VENDOR study, 2025 data, 2.45M posts]** On company pages, native documents (7.00%) and multi-image posts (6.45%) lead engagement rate and link posts trail (3.25%) ([Socialinsider, 16 Mar 2026](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/linkedin-benchmarks/)). Fully AI-generated posts are reported to get about 2.8 times less reach ([SocialPilot, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/linkedin-algorithm)). Widely repeated "weights" (saves worth 5 likes, a 60% link penalty) trace to vendor blogs without primary evidence and are directional only. The thought-leadership evidence for B2B buying groups is in section 16.
