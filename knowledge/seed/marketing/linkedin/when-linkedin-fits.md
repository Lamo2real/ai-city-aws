**When to use this:** When deciding whether to use LinkedIn ads or how to judge them.

## When LinkedIn ads fit, and how to measure them

**[VENDOR, data Aug 2024 to Jul 2025]** Dreamdata reports LinkedIn ROAS of 121% versus 67% for Google Search and 51% for Meta in B2B, with LinkedIn taking 41% of B2B paid budgets, but the average B2B journey runs **272 days** with 10 stakeholders and 88 touchpoints ([PPC Land, Mar 2026](https://ppc.land/linkedin-ads-hit-121-roas-as-b2b-buyer-journeys-stretch-to-272-days/)). **[VENDOR, Feb 2026, 211 companies]** Median single-image CPC is $13.23 with 0.42% CTR; CTR is weakly negatively correlated with pipeline (rho −0.170) while spend correlates with pipeline (rho 0.566); median monthly spend is $2,693 ([ZenABM](https://zenabm.com/blog/linkedin-ads-benchmarks-2026)). **[VENDOR, second-hand, not re-verified]** A separate dataset of 87 B2B campaigns and over $5M of spend reports $10.11 cost per click, $60 CPM, 0.65% CTR and $276 cost per lead ([42 Agency via Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/tax-accounting-linkedin-ads-statistics)). **[INFERENCE]** Optimising on CTR or cost per lead misleads; measure at account level with CRM sync and the Conversions API and judge over 6 to 12 months.
