**When to use this:** Before publishing any Meta ad, and when assessing legal exposure from Meta's AI features.

## Review Meta's generative features at ad level before every publish

**[FAST-MOVING, Oct 2026; high priority]** Advantage+ creative enhancements can change backgrounds, brightness and format and generate up to 150 variations, and have been on by default since October 2025. Known failures include an REI bike ad altered to show extra handlebars and a missing crank arm, pulled in June 2026. Many options sit in collapsed menus and apply unless unchecked, with no account-level blanket opt-out described ([PPC Land, 22 Oct 2026](https://ppc.land/meta-makes-advertisers-solely-responsible-for-its-ai-ad-edits-from-oct-30/)). Meta's notice also said "product browsing" would be added to existing ads from 28 October 2026 unless advertisers opted out ([beri.net, 25 Oct 2026](https://www.beri.net/article/meta-commercial-terms-november-20-2026-advantage-plus-ai-features-advertiser-liability-indemnity-crawling-arbitration-opt-out)). A consultancy cautionary case records that in 2025 Meta's automated ad tools produced off-brand creative even after advertisers disabled some AI features ([Alvarez & Marsal, Mar 2026](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/sites/default/files/2026-03/The%20Agent-First%20Marketing%20Function_0.pdf)).

## The proposed "solely responsible" AI terms were paused and never took effect

**[FAST-MOVING, status 11 Nov 2026]** In October 2026 Meta previewed updates to its Commercial Terms, Product Catalog Terms, Self-Serve Ad Terms and Ad Creative Generative AI Terms. Press quotes of the preview said its "AI Features" section stated that the features "may make mistakes and may not always produce accurate, complete, or suitable results" and that "you are solely responsible for and assume any and all risks associated with such AI features"; reporting originating with Adweek on 22 October said this was not limited to features the advertiser had switched on, and Meta's spokesperson did not directly answer that point, saying individual Advantage+ features can be turned off ([PPC Land, 22 Oct 2026](https://ppc.land/meta-makes-advertisers-solely-responsible-for-its-ai-ad-edits-from-oct-30/); [Relevant Audience, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.relevantaudience.com/meta/meta-commercial-terms-ai-features-advertiser-liability-october-30/); [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/advertisers-pull-meta-ads-that-had-unwanted-ai-glow-ups/)). The timeline: first reported 15 October; on 16 October Meta removed "train" from a website-crawling clause; on 23 October the effective date moved from 30 October to 20 November; on 28 October Meta pledged consultation; injunctions in Norway and Brazil, a UK CMA investigation and a US senators' letter followed; and on **5 November 2026 Meta removed the preview and announced a pause** ([Nieman Lab, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/11/meta-pulls-down-controversial-terms-of-service/); [PPC Land, 23 Oct 2026](https://ppc.land/meta-delays-website-crawling-terms-to-nov-20-after-publisher-complaints/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/meta-pauses-updated-business-ad-terms/596493/)). Meta's help centre now says the updates "are now paused and will not take effect on November 20, 2026", that it is continuing to consult stakeholders, and that "our existing terms remain in effect" ([Meta Business Help Center, read 10 Nov 2026](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2481113349080785)). The live Commercial Terms still show an effective date of 6 March 2024 and the preview URL returns "Content Not Found" ([Meta Commercial Terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/commercial_terms)).

**[DURABLE risk; secondary]** The practical exposure is unchanged by the pause. The current March 2024 terms already contain an advertiser indemnity for third-party claims related to "your services, actions, content or information" and cap Meta's liability at the greater of $100 or the amount paid in the prior twelve months ([beri.net, 25 Oct 2026](https://www.beri.net/article/meta-commercial-terms-november-20-2026-advantage-plus-ai-features-advertiser-liability-indemnity-crawling-arbitration-opt-out)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents should treat the liability change as proposed, delayed, then paused indefinitely with no new effective date, and should still audit default-on Advantage+ creative enhancements on every ad before publishing.
