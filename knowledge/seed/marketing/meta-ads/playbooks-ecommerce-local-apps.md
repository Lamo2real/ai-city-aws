**When to use this:** When planning a Meta campaign for an online store, a local business or an app.

## Playbooks by objective

**Ecommerce.** **[VENDOR, calendar 2025, nearly 35,000 brands]** Triple Whale medians are CPM $13.48, CTR 2.19%, CPA $38.17, ROAS 1.93 and conversion rate 1.57%, with CPMs up 8 to 38% year on year by industry ([Triple Whale, 7 Apr 2026](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks)). **[INFERENCE]** Use the full Advantage+ sales configuration, keep catalog quality high, brief roughly 30% of assets as partnership ads, separate existing customers with the two-ad-set structure, and measure on 7-day click cross-checked against blended efficiency.

**Local.** **[VENDOR, Mar 2026, US-centric]** Single locations often do better with Reach, Traffic or Leads with calls than with Store Traffic; budgets of about $10 to $20 a day minimum, with bursts under about $300 a month; creative should show the real storefront, team and local landmarks ([Benly, 24 Mar 2026](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/meta-ads-local-business)). For Swedish accounts convert radius guidance to kilometres.

**Apps.** **[VENDOR, Nov 2026]** Advantage+ app campaigns limit targeting to country, operating system and language and allow up to 50 images or videos; start with highest-volume bidding and move to a cost goal after 2 to 3 weeks ([Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-advantage-plus-app-campaigns-guide)).
