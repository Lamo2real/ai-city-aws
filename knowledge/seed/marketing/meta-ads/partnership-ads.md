**When to use this:** When planning creator content in Meta campaigns or setting creator permissions.

## Partnership ads carry the strongest multi-source evidence

**[PLATFORM CLAIM, Oct 2026]** A Meta meta-analysis of 20 EMEA conversion lift studies (January to June 2025) found campaigns with at least 30% of assets as partnership ads had 45% lower median cost per incremental purchase and 35% higher median incremental ROAS, at 92% confidence, below the usual 95%. Independent support exists: Charlie Oscar found 30% more incremental conversions across 94 marketing mix models, and the IPA (October 2025) scored creators at a long-term ROI index of 151. Setup: obtain account-level permissions from creators, run partnership ads in the same ad set as regular ads (or the same campaign with Advantage+ campaign budget), and use Advantage+ placements and Reels; creators without a Facebook Page will not deliver to Facebook placements ([PPC Land, 11 Oct 2026](https://ppc.land/meta-says-partnership-ads-cut-incremental-purchase-costs-45-in-emea-tests/)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM; two differing sets]** Meta is also relayed as saying partnership ads achieve 19% lower CPA and 53% higher CTR when added to business-as-usual campaigns, while a Meta for Business post cites 19% higher CTR, 10% higher conversion rate and 5% lower CPA; quote the specific claim with its source ([Aspire, relaying Meta](https://www.aspire.io/blog/insider-tips-meta-partnership-ads); [Meta for Business post](https://www.facebook.com/metaforbusiness/posts/your-audience-already-trusts-creators-partnership-ads-put-that-trust-to-work-wit/1610033204497712/)).

**[FAST-MOVING; official pages read as search excerpts]** "Branded content ads are now called partnership ads." Creators must tag business partners whenever there is an exchange of value, and the post then shows a "Paid partnership with" label; affiliate-link content needs the label but not a tagged partner; brands pay creators off-platform only. Account-level permission lets an advertiser run ads from partner content that tags it through the paid partnership label, an @mention, a people tag, a product tag or Collab, and both sides must meet eligibility requirements ([Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/788160621327601); [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/711659943695726); [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/906775900606811)). Platform labels do not replace legal disclosure wording (sections 17 and 19).
