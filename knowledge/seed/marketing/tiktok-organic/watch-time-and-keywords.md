**When to use this:** When planning or writing organic TikTok videos.

## Organic: watch time, completion, and saying the keyword

**[FAST-MOVING, updated 14 Oct 2026]** The strongest signals are watch time, completion rate, replays and shares; TikTok has never confirmed a points system. The system reads on-screen text and spoken words, so keyword-rich captions, voiceover and overlays matter as much as or more than hashtags for search. Longer videos of 1 to 3 minutes gain distribution when completion stays high ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/)). **[VENDOR study, Oct 2025, 11.4M posts]** Posting 2 to 5 times a week raises mean views per post 17% over once weekly, but median views stay flat at every frequency; more posting mostly buys more chances at a breakout ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)).
