**When to use this:** When measuring YouTube campaigns or quoting YouTube costs.

## Measurement is partly access-gated

**[DURABLE]** Brand Lift and Search Lift both require a Google account representative and the market-specific budget minimums in section 6; Format Segment Reports give like-for-like comparison inside multi-format campaigns ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15542751)). **[INFERENCE]** Small advertisers should rely on self-service Conversion Lift where they have 1,000 conversions, platform-comparable conversions and geo holdouts.

## Cost benchmarks are weak; quote ranges with their source

**[VENDOR compilations; treat as rough]** Store Growers (19 February 2026, compiling third-party studies) gives typical CPM of US$5 to $10; skippable in-stream CPM US$5 to $10 and cost per view US$0.05 to $0.10; non-skippable CPM US$6 to $10; bumper CPM US$3.24 to $4.37; in-feed CPM US$3 to $8; Shorts CPM about US$4; and connected TV CPM US$8.72 to $10.01 (2024 data, possibly outdated). CTR by format from a November 2025 Pixability dataset is 0.5 to 2.0% for skippable in-stream, under 0.3% for non-skippable, under 0.1% for bumper, 1.0 to 3.0%+ for in-feed and 0.1 to 0.5% for Shorts. Seasonal CPM runs from about US$1.98 to $2.50 in January and February to US$5 to $7+ in October and November. The same source's view-rate tables rely on a 2018 report and its connected-TV cost-per-view figures are internally inconsistent; do not use them ([Store Growers](https://www.storegrowers.com/youtube-ads-benchmarks/)). **[VENDOR]** Optmyzr's Video campaign CPA swung from US$25.97 to US$227.95 between Q1 2025 and Q1 2026, which Optmyzr itself says reflects "attribution loss from platform migration, not real performance" ([Optmyzr](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/google-ads-benchmark-report-q1-2026/)). A widely surfaced "YouTube Ads Benchmarks 2026" page withdrew all its tables on 4 October 2026, stating they could not be substantiated ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/youtube-ads-benchmarks-2026-cpv-cpm-ctr-industry)). **[INFERENCE]** Present any YouTube cost number as a wide range with a date and a vendor-compilation label.
