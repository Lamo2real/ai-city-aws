**When to use this:** When planning reach and frequency on YouTube or buying connected TV through Google.

## Sequencing suits fixed-order narratives; target frequency suits reach

**[DURABLE; Google Ads Help, fetched 11 Nov 2026]** A video ad sequence campaign is made of steps, each with one ad group and one video, with bidding and targeting at campaign level; progression to the next step is triggered by an impression, a view or a skip. Four templates exist: Introduce and reinforce (long then short), Prompt and inspire (short then long), Attract and direct (short, long, short) and Engage and differentiate (four short ads). Target CPM is recommended; formats are skippable in-stream, non-skippable in-stream, bumper or a mix; inventory is YouTube only; targeting is by audience and demographics only, with keywords, placements and topics available as exclusions. By default a person sees one sequence every 7 days, optionally every 30 days, and no traffic estimates are provided ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9161595)). **[DURABLE; Google Ads Help]** Target frequency is a Video reach campaign subtype chosen at creation. Goals are **2 to 7 per week or 4 to 12 per month for multi-format**, and 2 to 4 per week or 4 to 8 per month for non-skippable or skippable-plus-bumper. It is a target, not a cap, so some users can exceed it. It does not support Google video partners, contextual targeting, shared budgets or political campaigns, and allows one ad group per campaign. Google's practice notes are to supply at least as many unique creatives as the frequency number, target broadly, run at least 7 days with 2 to 4 weeks working best, and avoid reusing creatives in other campaigns because reported frequency will undercount; reach and frequency reports can lag up to 72 hours ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12400225)).

## Connected TV: raise TV bids, keep all surfaces on

**[DURABLE; Google Ads Help]** Google does not recommend building frequency on connected TV alone, because target frequency is not optimised for single-surface delivery; the suggested approach is to raise bids for the TV surface while keeping all surfaces on ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12400225)). **[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026; PLATFORM CLAIM]** Shoppable connected TV became available in Demand Gen in the January 2026 drop, and Google says Demand Gen campaigns that include TV screens average 7% more conversions at the same ROI in an internal 2025 global experiment ([Google Ads & Commerce blog, 22 Jan 2026](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/demand-gen-drop-january-2026/)).
