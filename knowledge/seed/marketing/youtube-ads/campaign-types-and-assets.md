**When to use this:** When planning a YouTube or Demand Gen campaign or briefing its video assets.

## Choose the campaign type from the objective

**[FAST-MOVING naming, 2026; DURABLE logic]** For awareness use Video Reach Campaigns (non-skippable, target frequency or efficient reach); for consideration use Video View Campaigns; for action use Demand Gen. **[PLATFORM CLAIM]** Mixing in-stream, in-feed and Shorts drove about 35% more unique reach than in-stream alone in reach campaigns and about 94% higher ROAS in view campaigns. **[DURABLE]** Plan for about 3 exposures per week, keep targeting broad, and avoid content or placement targeting that limits delivery; start bids from historic skippable in-stream levels because bids set too low cause under-pacing ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15542751)).

## Supply three orientations and several lengths, built on ABCD

**[DURABLE]** Upload horizontal, square and vertical video. For reach campaigns mix short (under 10 seconds or a 6-second bumper), medium (10 to 20 seconds) and long (over 30 seconds) cuts with the brand shown early and often; for view campaigns build a narrative over 30 seconds with authentic content; aim for at least 80% of the ABCD awareness principles ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15542751)). ABCD stands for Attention (hook early, tight framing and pacing, audio and text reinforcement), Branding (show up in the first 5 seconds and throughout, early, often and richly, seen and said), Connection (make people think or feel, humanise, focus on one message) and Direction (a specific call to action, reinforced with audio) ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM]** Google says ABCD-aligned ads deliver on average a 30% lift in short-term sales likelihood and a 17% lift in long-term brand contribution; the underlying study (Google and Kantar, April 2021, 11,000 ads for the 30% figure) is footnoted and not shown ([Think with Google](https://business.google.com/us/think/future-of-marketing/youtube-video-ad-creative/); [Google Business](https://business.google.com/us/resources/articles/abcds-of-effective-video-ads/)).

## Demand Gen creative, creators and lag

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Google's creative benchmark per Demand Gen campaign is at least one ad with 3 vertical, 3 square and 3 horizontal images plus one video in each orientation. Add new assets before removing old ones, pause only low performers that have had at least 14 days, and merge any ad group with fewer than 30 conversions in 30 days ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14693848)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM, 2026]** Creator assets deliver an average 20% lift in conversions, 45% of Shorts viewers are not on TikTok, and only 40% of Demand Gen conversions happen in the first 30 days ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-marketing-live-2026-every-announcement-that-actually-matters)). Google has a commercial interest in the long-lag framing, so validate with holdouts. When comparing Demand Gen to paid social use the "Conversions (Platform Comparable)" metric ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14693848)).
