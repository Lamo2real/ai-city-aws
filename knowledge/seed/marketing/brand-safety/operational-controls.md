**When to use this:** When setting up or auditing brand-safety controls for a campaign.

## What an agent can operationalise

**[INFERENCE]** Current practice reduces to five controls: a written, brand-specific suitability policy with risk tiers by content category; inclusion lists and curated private marketplaces for open-web buys plus exclusion lists for made-for-advertising and AI-slop domains; platform inventory filters at the most conservative tier appropriate, with placement, content, topic and keyword exclusions (for X, the adjacency controls in section 12; for Google, rebuilding exclusions when Display migrates to Demand Gen, section 6); third-party verification with confirmation that classification is content-level for video and social; and a quarterly log-level audit of where spend ran. Decisions about acceptable content should be made unilaterally per brand and documented; an agent must not benchmark or coordinate exclusion lists with competitors. Over-blocking news and using long keyword lists reduces reach on quality publishers; the lever the ANA data rewards is fewer, better domains.
