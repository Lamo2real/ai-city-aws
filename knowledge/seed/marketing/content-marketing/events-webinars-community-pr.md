## Events, webinars, community and digital PR

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** 82% of B2B marketers allocated budget to content-led experiences in 2026 (78% in 2025), and 31% gave them a large or majority share (17% in 2025); in-person events (82%) and virtual events (70%) are the most used formats, and events are the second 2027 investment priority at 35%, behind owned media at 42% and ahead of paid media and AI tools at 30% each ([Content Marketing Institute](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/articles/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research)). **[VENDOR, 2025 platform data]** ON24 reports a **60% average registrant-to-attendee conversion**, 239 average attendees per webinar, average viewing time of about 50 minutes, and on-demand viewing at 43 to 50% of attendance; the page gives inconsistent figures for the last two ([ON24](https://www.on24.com/blog/key-takeaways-from-the-webinar-benchmarks-report/)). **[INFERENCE]** Webinars and events suit considered B2B purchases where several stakeholders need education and sales can follow up on engagement signals; community suits products with frequent use and peer-to-peer value; digital PR suits brands with real data or expertise to earn coverage and, now, AI-answer citations. Measure webinars by registrant-to-attendee rate, engaged minutes, call-to-action and meeting conversions and influenced pipeline; community by active-member rate, member-to-member reply share and retention of members against matched non-members; and digital PR by quality of referring publications, branded search lift, share of voice in AI answers and assisted conversions, not raw link counts.
