**When to use this:** When running or reviewing a content marketing programme.

## Process and measurement an agent can run

**[INFERENCE; the researchers' synthesis, not a single source]** A process consistent with the evidence: pick a small number of pillars tied to buyer problems and commercial intent instead of informational keywords; anchor each pillar with one original-research or expert-collaboration asset per quarter; derive cluster pieces, newsletter issues, social cuts, a webinar and sales enablement from that asset; require a named human editor for everything; distribute through owned channels (email list, community, events) because search referral is shrinking; and refresh existing pieces instead of adding thin posts. The measurement stack implied by the data is pipeline or revenue influenced, conversion rate of content-sourced visits, email subscriber growth and engagement, branded search and direct traffic, share of AI answers and citations, and retention and referral rate, with sessions and rankings treated as diagnostics only. For an AI agent producing content, the three top-reported failure modes (accuracy, quality control and brand voice) are exactly what review gates should check, and Google's requirement to fact-check all AI-generated content (section 8) applies.
