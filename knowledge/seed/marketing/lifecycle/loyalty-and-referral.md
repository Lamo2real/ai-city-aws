## Loyalty and referral: demand a holdout

**[VENDOR-SPONSORED, Feb 2026]** Antavo's Global Customer Loyalty Report 2026 says nearly 9 in 10 programme owners who measure performance report positive ROI, 7 in 10 consumers join mainly to save money, and 91% of programme owners struggle with data analysis ([Bloomreach summary](https://www.bloomreach.com/en/blog/antavo-loyalty-report-2026)). **[INFERENCE]** "Positive ROI among those who measure" is a survivorship statistic, and member-versus-non-member comparisons are confounded because better customers join. The honest test is a holdout that withholds enrolment offers or rewards from a random group, tracking incremental margin and not member revenue. This fits the Double Jeopardy evidence in section 1 that loyalty has a ceiling set by brand size.
