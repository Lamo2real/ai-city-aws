**When to use this:** When judging email campaign performance.

## Campaign benchmarks vary by vendor; pick one and track clicks

**[VENDOR, Dec 2024 to Nov 2025, 3.6M campaigns, medians]** MailerLite's all-industry medians are open rate 43.46%, click rate 2.09%, click-to-open 6.81% and unsubscribe 0.22% (up from 0.08% in 2024, which it links to Gmail making unsubscribing easier). By industry: ecommerce 32.67% open and 1.07% click; software and web apps 39.31% and 1.15%; non-profit 52.38% and 2.90%; media 42.97% and 4.10%; consulting 45.96% and 2.41% ([MailerLite](https://www.mailerlite.com/blog/compare-your-email-performance-metrics-industry-benchmarks)). Omnisend reports open rates rising from 26.6% in 2024 to 30.7% in 2025 and click-to-conversion rising from 5.9% to 9% ([Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/2026-ecommerce-marketing-report)). The DMA UK 2026 benchmark, via Validity, gives click rates of 1.0% for retail and 1.2% for travel and a B2B delivered rate up from 90.7% to 95.6% ([Validity](https://www.validity.com/blog/the-2026-dma-email-benchmark-report-what-the-numbers-really-mean-for-revenue/)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026; secondary report of the GDMA benchmark]** The one credible Swedish benchmark: **Sweden's unique open rate is 42.27% against a global 34.15%, its click rate 3.0% against 2.99%, and its click-to-open 8.03% against 7.73%** ([Spotler](https://spotler.com/blog/what-sweden-can-teach-the-rest-of-europe-about-email-marketing)). **[INFERENCE]** Working "healthy" ranges for ecommerce campaigns in 2026 are click rate of about 1.1 to 1.7% (3.4% is top decile), unsubscribe of about 0.2% and flow click rate of about 5.6%. Year-on-year unsubscribe comparisons need care because one-click headers and Gmail's subscription manager raised rates mechanically.
