**When to use this:** When planning what campaigns should say over a year, or when measuring brand health beyond awareness.

## Use category entry points as the message plan

**[DURABLE; Jenni Romaniuk, July 2022]** Category entry points (CEPs) are the cues buyers use to access memory in a buying situation, both internal (motives, emotions) and external (location, time). Identify them with the seven W's: when, where, while, with what, why, how feeling, and with or for whom; in B2B, do this from both the business's and the professional's perspective. Prioritise with the three C's: credibility (the brand is not at a mental disadvantage on the CEP and the portfolio can deliver), competitiveness (fewer rivals hold a mental advantage) and commonality or currency (how often the situation occurs or its money value). The output is a shortlist of five to eight CEPs, and each execution should carry one CEP ([Ehrenberg-Bass](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/category-entry-points-in-a-business-to-business-b2b-world); [LinkedIn B2B Institute](https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/b2b-institute/cep-in-b2b)). **[DURABLE]** To measure mental availability, show each CEP with a brand list and a "none" option and let respondents pick any brands. The expected score for a brand on a CEP is (column total × row total) ÷ grand total; a deviation above +5 percentage points is a mental advantage and below −5 a disadvantage. Three metrics follow: Mental Market Share is the brand's CEP links divided by all brands' links (the institute's Barclays example is 3,732 ÷ 37,948 = 9.8%); Mental Penetration is buyers with at least one link divided by the sample (472 ÷ 609 = 78%); Network Size is the brand's links divided by buyers with any link (about 7.9) ([Ehrenberg-Bass](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/category-entry-points-in-a-business-to-business-b2b-world)). The institute's worked example contains a small internal inconsistency (3,732 versus 3,742 links) that does not affect the formulas. A fourth metric, Share of Mind, appears in vendor implementations and not in the institute's page ([quantilope](https://www.quantilope.com/methods/mental-availability)). **[INFERENCE]** A CEP shortlist is a ready-made message plan: five to eight situations, one per execution, rotated over time, each carrying the same distinctive assets.
