**When to use this:** Before a redesign, rebrand or new creative system, and when briefing creative that must carry brand assets.

## Protect distinctive brand assets

**[DURABLE; Romaniuk, c. 2013]** Distinctive brand assets are non-name elements (colour, logo, character, jingle, font) that trigger the brand. They are judged on uniqueness (the asset evokes only this brand) and fame (everyone links it to the brand), and uniqueness matters more. The Distinctive Asset Grid has four quadrants: use or lose (famous and unique), invest (unique but not famous), avoid (famous but not unique) and test or ignore (neither). Consistency builds assets; Tropicana's 2009 pack change is cited at an estimated $26.3 million in lost sales ([Ehrenberg-Bass](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/brands-of-distinction)). In practice, show the asset unbranded to category buyers and ask which brand it belongs to; fame is the share linking it to your brand and uniqueness is its exclusivity against competitors ([Umbrex, secondary](https://umbrex.com/resources/frameworks/marketing-frameworks/distinctive-brand-assets-framework-ehrenberg-bass/)).
