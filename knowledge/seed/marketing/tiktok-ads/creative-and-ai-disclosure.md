**When to use this:** When producing TikTok ads or publishing AI-made content on TikTok.

## Creative burns fast on TikTok

**[DURABLE principles, official page Jun 2025]** Shoot vertical 9:16 at 720p or better with sound, keep content inside safe zones, introduce the proposition in the first 3 seconds, and end with a clear call to action; use 3 to 5 creatives per ad group; refresh when delivery declines consistently ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices)). **[VENDOR, 31 Jul 2026]** Fatigue arrives in days to two weeks and in 48 to 72 hours at high spend; suggested refresh is every 2 to 3 weeks at $50 to $200 a day, weekly at $200 to $1,000, and every 3 to 5 days above $1,000; a modular approach of 1 body, 5 hooks and 3 calls to action gives 15 variants ([Influee](https://influee.co/blog/tiktok-ad-fatigue)).

## Symphony and AI disclosure: an approval gate, not an automatic label

**[FAST-MOVING; TikTok pages updated Apr to Nov 2026]** Symphony Creative Studio is a free AI creative platform for organic and paid content covering video generation, image generation, avatar videos, translation and dubbing, a video editor and a Symphony Agent; Symphony also comprises Symphony Automation inside Ads Manager and a Symphony API ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-symphony-creative-studio); [TikTok blog](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/tiktok-symphony-ai-creative-suite)). TikTok's ad policy requires disclosure of significantly AI-modified content: fully AI-generated image, video or audio; real footage substantially altered so the main subject is shown doing something they did not do; and voice cloning of the main subject. Disclose with TikTok's label or a clear disclaimer, caption, watermark or sticker. For undisclosed content, "your ad will be rejected or restricted". Minor edits such as lighting, colour, background removal and denoising are exempt, and TikTok's advice is to label if unsure ([TikTok Advertising Policies](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content)). Symphony output that meets disclosure requirements is auto-labelled "AI-generated"; Ads Manager has an AI-generated content disclaimer toggle for third-party-tool content; the label cannot be changed after submission; and TikTok may auto-label content carrying C2PA Content Credentials ([TikTok blog](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/symphony-creative-studio); [TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-ad-disclaimers-in-tiktok-ads-manager); [TikTok AI Safety Essentials PDF](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/AI_Safety_Essentials_for_Advertisers_on_TikTok.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** Unlike Meta's mostly automatic labelling, AI disclosure on TikTok is an approval gate: the agent should set the disclaimer toggle for any third-party AI video with synthetic people or voices.
