**When to use this:** When setting up TikTok tracking, judging results, or checking what TikTok allows in Europe.

## Tracking and benchmarks

**[DURABLE]** TikTok's recommended setup is Pixel plus Events API with event deduplication and all match keys ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/events-api)). **[VENDOR, Aug 2025 to Jul 2026, 5,900+ ecommerce brands]** Triple Whale medians are CPM $4.08, CTR 0.61%, CPA $17.07 and ROAS 1.51; low ROAS in beauty and health likely reflects sales landing in TikTok Shop outside pixel attribution ([Triple Whale](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/tiktok-benchmarks)).

## Ownership, Europe, minors and regulation

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** TikTok has operated in the US since 22 January 2026 under the TikTok USDS Joint Venture, with the algorithm retrained on US data ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/tiktoks-confirmed-u-s-deal-still-leaves-unanswered-questions)). **[CONTESTED]** Sources conflict on whether ByteDance or the joint venture runs advertising and ecommerce ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/tiktok-s-us-overhaul-gives-advertisers-greater-certainty--though-questions-remain)). Benchmark US performance separately from global case studies. **[FAST-MOVING, 28 May 2026]** TikTok Shop in Europe covers France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Spain and the UK, with Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland added from 15 June 2026; **Sweden is not among the markets named** ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-shop-expands-across-europe?lang=en-150)). **[FAST-MOVING; TikTok page updated Oct 2026]** TikTok delivers **no personalised ads to users aged 13 to 17 in the EEA, UK, Switzerland and Brazil**, and advertisers' ability to target under-18s is limited in the US, Canada, EEA, UK, Switzerland, Latin America, Turkey and Indonesia. Globally, political, religious and sensitive-content ads are not permitted (approved government or charity public-service campaigns excepted); financial services and healthcare or pharmaceutical ads are adults-only; and weight loss, weight gain and muscle gain ads are not permitted ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/protecting-minors-on-tiktok-advertising-initiatives)). TikTok prohibits advertising by political figures and entities ([TikTok Community Guidelines](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity)). **[FAST-MOVING, 6 Feb 2026]** The European Commission preliminarily found TikTok in breach of the Digital Services Act over addictive design, which could change infinite scroll and the recommender in the EU; the outcome is open ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-tiktoks-addictive-design-breach-digital-services-act)). **[FAST-MOVING, May 2026]** TikTok launched a TikTok for Business MCP server so AI tools can manage campaigns ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/major-ad-tool-announcements-from-tiktok-world-2026/820197/)); its scope and the guardrails it implies are in section 21.
