## Lead generation on TikTok

**[FAST-MOVING; TikTok help pages, lead-quality page updated June 2026]** For Instant Forms, choose the Lead generation objective, set Instant Form as the optimisation location at ad group level, and attach or create the form at ad level; TikTok suggests instant forms when the aim is to lower friction "and qualify them later" ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/set-up-lead-generation-with-instant-form?lang=en)). Documented CRM integrations include HubSpot, Salesforce, Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Constant Contact, Google Sheets, Zapier, LeadsBridge and Make, plus a Leads Center for download and management; the suite also includes Messaging Ads, Call Ads and website-form lead campaigns ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/available-crm-integrations-tiktok-lead-generation?lang=en); [TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/lead-generation-objective?lang=en)). Lead quality optimisation matches ads to people more likely to convert further after form submission; the practice is to post back upper- and deeper-funnel events through website, offline or CRM connection and map them to standard events, and it suits longer funnels ([TikTok Ads Help Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-lead-quality-optimization?lang=en)).
