**When to use this:** When choosing where to earn presence for AI visibility or reporting AI-answer results.

## Which sources get cited differs by engine and is unstable

**[VENDOR, 31 Mar 2026, US, 30M sources]** Peec AI's top cited domains overall are Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Wikipedia, Forbes, G2, Yelp, Facebook, Medium and TechRadar. YouTube leads in Google AI Mode and AI Overviews; Reddit leads in Gemini and Perplexity; Wikipedia leads in ChatGPT ([Peec AI](https://peec.ai/blog/top-domains-cited-by-ai-search-analysis-based-on-30m-sources)). "Best X" lists are 43.8% of cited page types ([Ahrefs, 15 May 2026](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-chatbot-traffic/)). **[CONTESTED, volatile]** Tinuiti found Reddit at 24% of Perplexity citations in January 2026 and TikTok near zero at 0.02% of all citations ([Tinuiti](https://tinuiti.com/research-insights/research/ai-citation-trends-report-q1-2026/)), but Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations fell from about 3.8% to about 0.5% within weeks in August 2026 after a fan-out change (provisional data), so "Reddit SEO" is not a reliable route to ChatGPT visibility ([Semrush, Aug 2026](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/)).

## Citations and mentions are different outcomes

**[VENDOR, 2026]** About 40% of citations do not name the cited brand, ranging from 52% on Perplexity to 19% on Copilot; track four states per prompt and engine: cited and mentioned, cited only, mentioned only, neither. Putting the brand name in the same sentence as a key statistic is a hypothesis for reducing unnamed citations, not a proven tactic ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/ghost-citation-problem-ai-483794)).
