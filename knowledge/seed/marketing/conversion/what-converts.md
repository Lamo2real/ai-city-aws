**When to use this:** When building or reviewing a campaign landing page.

## Message match, single-purpose pages and social proof are recommended but unquantified

**[VENDOR]** Unbounce defines message match as how closely the landing page headline, copy and design match the ad or link that brought the visitor, and a landing page as a campaign-specific page with one call to action and no site navigation; it gives no effect size ([Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/landing-page-best-practices/); [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/what-is-a-landing-page/)). Social proof, guarantees and risk removal are asserted as anxiety reducers in the four-forces model ([jobstobedone.org](https://jobstobedone.org/the-four-forces/)). **[INFERENCE]** Message match also matters mechanically on Google, where landing page experience is a Quality Score component and page content drives AI Max headlines (section 6), and on Meta, where landing pages are reviewed for policy (section 4).

## Accessibility is a legal requirement for EU consumer purchase flows

**[DURABLE since 28 Jun 2025]** The European Accessibility Act covers B2C ecommerce purchase journeys including payment and requires conformance with EN 301 549, which incorporates WCAG 2.1 AA; the Swedish implementation and sanctions are in section 19.
