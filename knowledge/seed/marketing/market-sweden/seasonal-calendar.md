**When to use this:** When planning seasonal campaigns, delivery cut-offs or sale periods for Sweden.

## The Swedish seasonal calendar

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026 dates; vendor calendar consistent with standard date rules]** Alla hjärtans dag is 14 February; the winter school holiday (sportlov) falls in weeks 7 to 10 depending on region; Easter 2026 ran from Skärtorsdag on 2 April through Annandag påsk on 6 April; **Mors dag is the last Sunday in May** (31 May 2026); Nationaldagen is 6 June; **Midsommarafton was 19 June 2026**; school starts in August, varying by municipality; **Fars dag is the second Sunday in November** (8 November 2026); Singles Day is 11 November; **Black Week 2026 runs 23 to 29 November with Black Friday on 27 November** and Cyber Monday on 30 November; Julafton is 24 December; and post-Christmas sales start from 25 December ([Abicart](https://www.abicart.com/blog/key-dates-to-keep-track-of-in-2026-a-complete-guide-for-e-commer)). **[FAST-MOVING, 6 Nov 2026]** HUI Research forecasts about **SEK 104 billion of retail sales in December 2026**, 6% above December 2025 in fixed prices and a record if met; Black Week is when Christmas shopping starts for many, and toys make over a fifth of annual sales in December ([Realtid/TT](https://www.realtid.se/tt-nyhet/sankta-skatten-satter-fart-pa-julhandeln-spas-na-104-miljarder/)). For reference, Svensk Handel forecast Black Week 2024 sales at SEK 9.7 billion with about six in ten Swedes planning to shop ([Aktiespararna, 21 Nov 2024](https://www.aktiespararna.se/nyheter/black-week-spas-ga-starkare-i-ar-enligt-svensk-handel)). **[INFERENCE]** Sweden celebrates on the eve: Julafton and Midsommarafton are the main days, so delivery cut-offs and campaign ends should be set a day earlier than in UK or US calendars, and Mors dag and Fars dag differ from UK and US dates. Black Week price claims are exactly where the 30-day lowest-prior-price rule applies (section 19), and for a multi-week peak Google's seasonality adjustments are the wrong tool (section 6).
