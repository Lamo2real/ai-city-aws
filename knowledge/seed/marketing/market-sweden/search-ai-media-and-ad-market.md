## Search and AI: Google at about 86%, and six in ten Swedes use AI services

**[FAST-MOVING, Oct 2026]** StatCounter puts Swedish search share at Google 85.82%, Bing 8.68%, DuckDuckGo 1.87% and Yahoo 1.79% ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/sweden)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** **Six in ten Swedes use AI services**, a sharp rise on 2025, and just over eight in ten if Google's AI overviews are counted ([Internetstiftelsen](https://svenskarnaochinternet.se/)). The 2025 baseline (fieldwork January 2025, n = 3,362) was 41% of internet users having used an AI tool in the past year, ChatGPT at about 34%, and 21% asking AI things they would otherwise have searched for, rising to over four in ten among those born in the 2000s; 95% of adults used Mobile BankID ([Svenskarna och internet 2025](https://svenskarnaochinternet.se/app/uploads/2025/09/internetstiftelsen-svenskarna-och-internet-2025.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** AI-answer visibility is a real channel in Sweden alongside Google.

## Video, audio and news reach

**[FAST-MOVING; Mediebarometern 2025, daily reach, ages 9 to 85]** Streamed TV reaches 68% daily; **YouTube 52%, which has overtaken scheduled linear TV at 46%**; radio and podcasts combined 75%; podcasts and radio on demand 29%; and daily newspapers 60% (digital 54%, print 14%). 48% watch streamed TV for over an hour a day and 19% watch YouTube for over an hour ([Nordicom](https://nordicom.gu.se/en/latest/news/youtube-car-radio-and-fiction-books-how-swedes-consumed-media-2025)).

## The Swedish ad market: SEK 52 billion of media advertising, 78% of it online

**[FAST-MOVING; IRM annual statistics 2025]** Total Swedish advertising and marketing investment was **SEK 92.3 billion in 2025** (+2.8%). Media advertising was SEK 52.2 billion (+3.3%): internet SEK 40.5 billion (+6.4%), TV SEK 3.7 billion (−9.9%), out-of-home SEK 2.2 billion, printed newspapers SEK 1.9 billion (−7.7%), direct mail SEK 1.9 billion (−5.5%), radio SEK 1.05 billion and cinema SEK 0.13 billion. Sponsorship was SEK 10.0 billion (+7.2%) and events SEK 3.3 billion (+10.1%) ([IRM](https://www.irm-media.se/om-oss/irms-aarsstatistik/)). Internet's share of media advertising is therefore about 78% by the researchers' arithmetic. IRM reports Q3 2026 ad investment **up 9.3%** on Q3 2025 ([IRM](https://www.irm-media.se/aktuellt/nyheter/q3-2026/)). On 11 March 2026 IAB Sverige formalised the expulsion of Meta over deceptive advertising ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/swedens-influencer-ad-crackdown-fines-court-rulings-and-eu-law-coming/)).
