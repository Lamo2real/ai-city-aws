**When to use this:** When someone asks for Swedish or Nordic performance benchmarks.

## Performance benchmarks: email only

**[FAST-MOVING; secondary report of the GDMA benchmark]** Sweden's email unique open rate is 42.27% (global 34.15%, Netherlands 48.9%), its click rate 3.0% (global 2.99%) and its click-to-open 8.03% (global 7.73%); Swedish senders averaged 41,506 recipients per campaign ([Spotler, updated 19 Aug 2026](https://spotler.com/blog/what-sweden-can-teach-the-rest-of-europe-about-email-marketing)). **[INFERENCE]** Opens are inflated everywhere by Apple's privacy feature; read this as "Sweden is above average on opens and average on clicks", not as a target. **[VENDOR, low credibility]** One vendor lists Meta CPM and CPC of $9.10 and $1.30 for Sweden, $10.50 and $1.55 for Norway and $9.80 and $1.45 for Denmark, described as projections with no sample or method ([Adamigo](https://www.adamigo.ai/blog/meta-ads-cpm-cpc-benchmarks-by-country-2026)); do not store these as benchmarks. The nearest usable paid-search reference is the European ecommerce dataset in section 6 (CPCs of about EUR 0.31 to 0.41).
