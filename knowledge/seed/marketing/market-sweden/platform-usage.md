**When to use this:** When choosing platforms or estimating reach for Swedish audiences.

## Platform usage: YouTube, Facebook and Instagram are the mass-reach platforms

**[FAST-MOVING; Internetstiftelsen, Svenskarna och internet 2026, published 9 Jun 2026, page updated 28 Sep 2026]** Share of Swedes using each platform: **YouTube 77%, Facebook 69%, Instagram 66%, Snapchat 38%, LinkedIn 27%, TikTok 22%, Pinterest 18%, Reddit 16%, X 13%, Threads 7% and Bluesky 2%**. Messaging: Messenger 66%, WhatsApp 51%, Discord 16%, Signal 8% and Telegram 4%. Among internet users aged 8 and over, 94% used some social medium in the past year, 90% weekly and 83% daily. YouTube use has declined since 2021 and fewer use Facebook daily. People aged 20 and over dominate on Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Threads, while those aged 8 to 19 mainly use TikTok, Roblox and Snapchat; more men use X and Discord and more women Instagram and Pinterest ([Svenskarna och internet 2026](https://svenskarnaochinternet.se/utvalt/sociala-medier/)). **[FAST-MOVING; Nordicom Mediebarometern 2025, published 5 May 2026]** On an average day in 2025, social media reached 84% of people aged 9 to 85 (97% of those aged 15 to 24); Facebook is most common among those over 44; Snapchat, Instagram and TikTok are most popular among 15 to 24 year olds; and Instagram and Facebook lead among 25 to 44 year olds ([Nordicom](https://nordicom.gu.se/en/latest/news/youtube-car-radio-and-fiction-books-how-swedes-consumed-media-2025)).

**[FAST-MOVING; DataReportal Digital 2026: Sweden, data mostly October 2025]** Sweden has 10.7 million people, 10.5 million internet users (98.3%) and 8.58 million social media user identities. Platform-reported ad reach: YouTube 8.58 million; Facebook 5.65 million; Instagram 5.55 million; TikTok 3.51 million adults (41.2% of adults); LinkedIn 6.20 million registered members; Snapchat 4.81 million; Pinterest 2.54 million; X 2.03 million. DataReportal warns that declines in ad reach often reflect platform data corrections ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-sweden)). **[INFERENCE]** Survey usage and platform-reported reach diverge sharply (TikTok 22% of people against 41% of adults by ad reach). Use Internetstiftelsen for "who uses it" and DataReportal for "how many accounts can be targeted".
