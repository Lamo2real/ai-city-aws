**When to use this:** When writing or reviewing copy for Swedish or Nordic audiences.

## Copy norms for Swedish and Nordic advertising

**[Practitioner guidance from a Nordic translation agency, undated; not evidence]** All Nordic languages address the reader with the informal second person singular; modesty is valued and self-praise can read as bragging, so "You will love this" becomes roughly "We think you are going to like this"; English politeness fillers (please, thank you, sorry, unfortunately) sound unnatural and should be removed; titles are rarely used; and for Sweden the advice is to write from the company's perspective with "vi". The same source characterises Norwegians as getting straight to the point and Finns as frank ([Sandberg Translation Partners](https://stptrans.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/How-to-adjust-tone-of-voice-for-the-Nordic-market.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** A default Swedish copy style is "du" address, plain factual benefit statements, no superlatives that would need proof under Marketing Act section 10 anyway ("bäst", "billigast", "miljövänlig"), few exclamation marks, and concrete delivery, returns and payment information near the call to action. Do not reuse Swedish copy unchanged in Norway, Denmark or Finland; each needs its own language version and local payment and delivery signals.
