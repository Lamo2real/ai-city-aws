## Ecommerce, payment and delivery: Swish, BankID and three delivery options

**[FAST-MOVING; PostNord E-barometern Q2 2026 via nShift, 24 Sep 2026]** Swedish ecommerce grew 8% in Q2 2026 against Q2 2025, with all eight categories growing: pharmacy +13%, groceries +10%, consumer electronics +10%, building supplies +9%, sports and leisure +8%, clothing and shoes +5%, furniture and home +4%, and books and media +2%. For the most recent delivery, service points fell from 53% in Q2 2020 to 47%, home delivery from 35% to 30%, and **parcel lockers rose from 3% to 19%**. One in three shoppers abandoned a purchase because the preferred delivery option was missing, and nearly four in ten abandoned one in the past year over payment problems or uncertainty (37% unsure the payment was secure, 36% lacking their preferred payment method, 31% doubting the shop was genuine). **61% say BankID or Freja verification makes checkout feel safe**, and 57% say the same of recognisable payment options. One in three ecommerce parcels came from abroad, and secondhand is at least 12% of parcels ([nShift](https://nshift.com/blog/swedish-ecommerce-trends-postnord-e-barometern-q2-2026)). **[FAST-MOVING; PostNord, E-commerce in the Nordics, Spring 2026]** 88% of Swedes shopped online in the last 30 days (Norway 86%, Denmark 85%, Finland 84%). **Swish is the most used and most preferred payment method in Sweden**, ahead of card and invoice; in Norway it is Vipps; in Denmark card is most used and MobilePay most preferred; in Finland online bank payment leads. Swedes most prefer home delivery, with lockers overtaking service points as second preference; Finland has the highest locker preference. Marketplaces Swedes shop from are Amazon (33%), Temu (27%) and Zalando (25%). Only 14% of Swedes will pay extra for greener delivery but 52% will wait longer, 38% bought second-hand in the last month, and 68% say a clear return policy matters. Sweden is the first or second foreign origin for Danish, Finnish and Norwegian shoppers ([PostNord](https://www.postnord.com/siteassets/PDF/PN_E-commerce_Spring_2026.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** Checkout basics for Sweden are Swish plus card plus an invoice or part-payment option, BankID verification, visible recognisable payment logos, a clear returns page, and all three delivery options (service point, locker, home), since stated preference (home) and actual use (service point) differ.
