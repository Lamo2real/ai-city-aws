**When to use this:** When choosing which platforms to add on a limited budget.

## Choosing platforms on a limited budget

**[INFERENCE; the researchers' synthesis, not a sourced framework]** Figures come from different datasets, currencies and attribution models and are not comparable like for like.

| Platform | Best fit | Deprioritise when | Minimum viable commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | DTC, apps, consumer brands; demand creation for under-45s | No capacity for several new videos a week; high-ticket B2B | Daily budget 10 to 30x target CPA, 6+ creatives, Pixel plus Events API |
| LinkedIn | B2B with contract values that absorb $10+ clicks; ABM | Low-priced B2C; payback needed within weeks | About $2.7K to $6.5K a month, audience of 50,000+, CRM connected, people posting 2 to 3x a week |
| Pinterest | Home, fashion, beauty, food, DIY; catalog retail, mainly North America | Non-visual services, urgent lead generation | Catalog plus tag, one Performance+ campaign, fresh Pins for 1 to 2 quarters |
| Reddit | SaaS, tech, gaming, finance, considered purchases | Brands unwilling to engage transparently | $50 to $150 a day test; listening-first organic plan |
| Snapchat | Young-audience direct response, apps, local | Older or B2B audiences; under $500 a month | About 50 conversions per window, 2 to 4 week learning |
| X | Real-time events, news, tech and finance conversation | Evergreen performance goals; low tolerance for adjacency risk | Small moment-based tests with exclusion lists set first |

US usage context: YouTube 84%, Facebook 71%, Instagram 50%, TikTok 37% and Reddit 26% of adults ([Pew Research Center, 20 Nov 2025](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2025/11/20/americans-social-media-use-2025/)). Swedish usage differs materially (YouTube 77%, Facebook 69%, Instagram 66%, Snapchat 38%, LinkedIn 27%, TikTok 22%, Pinterest 18%, Reddit 16%, X 13%) and is detailed in section 20.
