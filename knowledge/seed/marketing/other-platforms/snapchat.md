**When to use this:** When considering Snapchat for young audiences, apps or local direct response.

## Snapchat is young-audience direct response

**[FAST-MOVING, Q3 2026]** About 77% of Snap revenue is direct response; North American daily users fell 6% to 92M while Europe held 98M ([PPC Land, 4 Nov 2026](https://ppc.land/snap-ad-revenue-gains-4-as-subscriptions-supply-74-of-q3-growth/)). Snap reported 956 million monthly and 483 million daily users in Q1 2026, with Dynamic Product Ads revenue up 30% year on year ([Enrich Labs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/snapchat-ads-complete-guide-2026)). **[VENDOR, Feb 2026]** Automated bidding needs at least 50 conversions in the optimisation window and a 2 to 4 week learning period; spend under $500 a month is hard for automation ([Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/snapchat-smart-campaign-solutions-2026-smart-ads-smart-bidding-smart-budget)). **[VENDOR; one dataset relayed by a second vendor, not read at source]** Snapchat 2026 medians across 2,431 campaigns: CPC $0.84, CTR 1.04%, awareness CPM $5.84, conversions CPM $27.10, and apparel first-purchase cost about $19.80; 7 to 15 second vertical video ran at CPM $10.20 and CTR 1.04% against CPM $12.80 and CTR 0.78% for 15 to 30 seconds ([Enrich Labs citing AdLiftr](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/snapchat-ads-complete-guide-2026)). **[INFERENCE]** The roughly 4.6-times gap between awareness and conversion CPMs means Snapchat benchmarks must always be objective-matched. In Sweden Snapchat is the fourth most used social platform at 38% (section 20), so it matters more there than global averages suggest.
