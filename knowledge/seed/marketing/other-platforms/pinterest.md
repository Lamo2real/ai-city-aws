## Pinterest is visual search with a long payoff

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Pinterest had 631M monthly users in Q1 2026 and **640 million at 30 June 2026**, with Q2 2026 revenue of $1,180 million (+18%), over 72% of impressions from search surfaces and about 74 to 75% of revenue from the US and Canada ([PPC Land, May 2026](https://ppc.land/pinterest-q1-2026-what-the-ad-platform-changes-mean-for-advertisers/); [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/pinterest-statistics-2026-marketing-data-points)). It recommends Performance+ as the default for consideration, conversion and catalog campaigns; launch with defaults, A/B test against the current setup, then scale. **[PLATFORM CLAIM]** Internal tests show 10%+ better CPC and CPA ([Pinterest Business, 19 Mar 2026](https://business.pinterest.com/blog/pinterest-performance-plus-campaigns/)). **[VENDOR, 2026]** Organic is keyword SEO: one primary keyword in the Pin title, supporting terms in the description, fresh images for existing URLs; 96% of top searches are unbranded; expect meaningful traffic over one to two quarters. Pinterest's developer terms require a human to choose each Pin that publishes, which constrains automated agents ([Tailwind](https://www.tailwindapp.com/blog/pinterest-seo)). Standard images are 2:3 at 1000 x 1500 ([Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/)). **[Benchmarks are thin]** Pinterest publishes no average cost per click, CPM or CTR, only changes (Q2 2026: ads served +16% and average ad price +1% year on year; Q1 2026: impressions +24% and pricing −5%). The often-quoted "$0.83 CPC, 0.39% CTR, $9.20 CPM" has no source with a stated method; WebFX's range is $0.50 to $1.50 per click from 2024 to 2025 campaigns ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/pinterest-statistics-2026-marketing-data-points)). An aggregator with no stated method quotes CPM $5.10, CPC $1.50 and CTR 0.50%, which is low confidence ([Benchmarketing](https://www.benchmarketing.org/benchmarks/pinterest-ads)).
