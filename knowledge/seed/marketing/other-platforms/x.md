**When to use this:** When considering X for live events, news, tech or finance conversations.

## X is a moment-based niche with adjacency controls to set before launch

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** X began rolling out a rebuilt AI-powered Ads Manager on 30 April 2026; eMarketer estimates 2026 ad revenue at $2.46B, about half of Twitter's 2021 level ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/x-announces-a-rebuilt-ad-platform-powered-by-ai)). Agencies use it for live conversation around events and real-time moments, not evergreen campaigns ([Digiday, 1 Jun 2026](https://digiday.com/marketing/xs-advertiser-base-is-beginning-to-resemble-its-pre-musk-era/)). Trade reporting says fewer users and ongoing brand-safety questions have kept revenue below pre-2022 levels ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/elon-musk-pressures-potential-spacex-investors-to-advertise-on-x/816660/)), and in January 2026 Grok's generation of sexualised images of real people triggered regulatory responses, with one trade publication calling brand risk on X "spiking" ([MarketingProfs, 9 Jan 2026](https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/54159/ai-update-january-9-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-three-weeks)).

**[FAST-MOVING; X business pages read 11 Nov 2026]** Formats are Image, Video, Carousel (up to six swipeable items) and Text Ads; Vertical Video Ads (full-screen, sound-on, with the call to action appearing after one second); Amplify Pre-roll and Amplify Sponsorships (the latter not self-serve); Timeline Takeover and Spotlight Takeover; X Live; Dynamic Product Ads for retargeting and prospecting; and Collection Ads with a hero plus thumbnails for up to six products ([X Business](https://business.x.com/en/advertising/formats)). Adjacency Controls let an advertiser exclude up to **4,000 keywords and 2,000 author handles** from appearing directly before or after ads in the Home Timeline at account level, and X says it cannot guarantee 100% effectiveness. Other controls are automated Sensitivity Settings, an industry-standard blocklist, placement exclusions for profiles, search and replies, Amplify exclusions and app exclusions on the X Audience Platform. Third-party measurement by DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science covers English-language Home Timeline content only; X cites over 99% Brand Safety Floor compliance, and it is not in the Media Rating Council accreditation process. The page's latest dated item is 2023 ([X Business](https://business.x.com/en/help/ads-policies/brand-safety.html)). **[VENDOR]** X's standard CTR counts all engagements, so use link clicks for cross-platform comparison ([AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/what-is-a-good-ctr)). **[INFERENCE]** Realistic 2026 use is real-time and event-led awareness (takeovers, live, pre-roll against sports, news and tech), conversation-driven niches and catalogue retargeting, with keyword and handle exclusion lists set before launch; X's "99%" claim does not extend to replies, search, profiles or non-English content, which includes Swedish.
