**When to use this:** When deciding how much to invest in creative or briefing brand versus activation assets.

## Creative quality outweighs reach and targeting in measured sales lift

**[DURABLE in direction; CPG data]** NCSolutions' "Five Keys to Advertising Effectiveness" (2023 update of the 2017 Nielsen Catalina meta-study, nearly 450 CPG campaigns across digital and TV) attributes incremental sales **49% to creative** (49% in 2017), 21% to brand (15%), 14% to reach (22%), 11% to targeting (9%) and 5% to recency (5%) ([NCSolutions](https://info.ncsolutions.com/hubfs/2023%20Five%20Keys%20to%20Advertising%20Effectiveness/NCS_Five_Keys_to_Advertising_Effectiveness_E-Book_08-23.pdf)). **[VENDOR research on award entrants]** Kantar and WARC matched about 450 ads from Kantar's Link database with ROMI data from WARC and found the most creative and effective ads generated more than four times the profit ([Kantar](https://www.kantar.com/north-america/inspiration/advertising-media/the-art-of-proof-how-creative-quality-drives-profit)). **[INFERENCE]** The 49% figure covers short-term CPG sales lift and should not be generalised to B2B or long-term brand effects; the often-quoted Nielsen "47%" was not verified. The practical consequence matches Binet's advice in section 1: when cutting, protect the quality and longevity of fewer ads.

## Emotion and "right-brain" features predict long-term effect

**[VENDOR]** System1's Star Rating (1 to 5) is built from emotional response and is presented as predicting long-term growth; its Spike Rating predicts short-term sales over 8 to 10 weeks from branding strength and emotional intensity, with 1.33 and above "exceptional" ([System1](https://system1group.com/uncategorized/our-metrics-explained)). **[DURABLE; Orlando Wood, *Lemon*, IPA 2019]** UK ads averaged 3.9 "left-brain" features against 2.3 "right-brain" features, and in 100 UK and 100 US TV ads over 60% of ads with the fewest right-brain features scored 1 star. Right-brain features include characters, dialogue, a sense of place, humour and melodic music; left-brain features include voiceover, product focus, on-screen text and rhythmic soundtracks ([LBB](https://lbbonline.com/news/are-ads-becoming-too-far-left-brain)). Across 126 radio ads, right-brain feature count correlated with Star Rating at R = +0.47 and left-brain count at R = −0.44 ([System1 and Radiocentre](https://2235762.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/2235762/Listen%20Up!%20System1%20and%20Radiocentre%20eBook.pdf)). This agrees with Binet and Field's B2B finding that emotional campaigns produced 1.4 very large business effects against 0.2 for rational ones (section 1). **[INFERENCE]** There is a tension to manage: direct-response platform guidance favours on-screen text and product focus for sound-off feeds, while brand-building evidence favours character, story and music. Use the first for activation assets and the second for brand assets, and do not ask one execution to do both jobs.
