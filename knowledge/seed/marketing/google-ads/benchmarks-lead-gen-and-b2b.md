**When to use this:** When judging lead-generation or B2B Google Search performance.

## Benchmarks: US lead generation, US ecommerce, European ecommerce, B2B SaaS

**[VENDOR, Apr 2025 to Mar 2026, US Search only, 13,474 campaigns]** WordStream medians are CTR 6.64%, CPC $5.42, conversion rate 8.18% and cost per lead $66.69, with Business Services at $93.69 per lead and Attorneys at $131.63. The data skews to US small business and lead-style conversions and should not be applied to ecommerce or non-US markets ([WordStream, 2026](https://wordstream.com/blog/2026-google-ads-benchmarks)).

**[VENDOR, agency compilation with visible inconsistencies; rough orientation only]** For B2B SaaS, TripleDart reports non-branded Google Search CPC of $5.34 and CTR of 4.04% in July 2025 against $4.13 and 5.47% in August 2024, a tech and SaaS CPA of $133.52, and non-brand search share of budget falling from 38.1% to 32.8%. Paid leads convert from marketing-qualified to sales-qualified at 30% against 51% for non-paid, while later stages are similar (sales-qualified to opportunity 66% versus 62%; opportunity to win 48% versus 45%). Vertical examples (CPC, conversion rate, cost per lead): cybersecurity $7.80, 3.2%, $150 to $200; fintech $8.80, 1.23%, $180 to $240; marketing software $5.10, 4.9%, $80 to $105; developer tools $5.50, 4.2%, $95 to $130. Median paid cost per sales-qualified lead ranges from $620 to $10,240 by vertical. The same report contains conversion rates above 100% in its high ranges and a figure labelled both cost per lead and CPA ([TripleDart](https://www.tripledart.com/guides/b2b-saas-inbound-marketing-report)). **[INFERENCE]** Because pipeline metrics vary by more than 10 times between verticals, platform-level cost-per-lead benchmarks carry little decision value for B2B SaaS; use the account's own cost per opportunity.
