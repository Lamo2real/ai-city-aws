**When to use this:** When running Display or remarketing on Google, or planning Demand Gen.

## Display is moving into Demand Gen

**[FAST-MOVING, announced 26 May 2026; secondary reporting of Google guidance]** Google confirmed that standalone Display campaigns are being retired. Display inventory is not going away, and Display-only buying remains possible inside Demand Gen through channel controls. A migration tool rolls out in phases from June 2026; creation of new standalone Display campaigns ends at a later unspecified date; remaining eligible campaigns are auto-migrated at an unspecified date; and full migration is expected in 2027. Existing campaigns stay editable until migrated ([Search Engine Journal, 26 May 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-is-retiring-standalone-display-campaigns-in-favor-of-demand-gen/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/googles-display-ads-are-dead-here-is-what-replaces-them-in-2026/)). Mechanics reported from Google's guidance: use "Upgrade to Demand Gen" from the Edit menu, with up to 100 campaigns per batch recommended; the new campaign is named "[Original name] #2" and the original is set to Removed; up to 42 days of history carries over, cutting learning to about 1 to 2 days; migrated ads go through approval again; Pay for Conversions switches to pay-per-click with target CPA; and active Conversion Lift studies should not be migrated. Demand Gen does not offer Manual CPC, viewable-impressions bidding or Pay for Conversions; it offers Maximize conversions, Maximize conversion value, target CPA, target ROAS, Maximize clicks and a new Target CPC, plus campaign total budgets for flighted campaigns. HTML5 for the Display Network in Demand Gen is "coming soon", GIF is not listed, and Lookalike segments replace Similar Audiences. Demand Gen surfaces are YouTube, Gmail, Discover, the Google Display Network and Maps (beta). Migration advice attributed to Google is to keep Display-Network-only selected during migration and add other channels later, start with similar bids, limit bid changes to plus or minus 15% with at least a week between changes, and consolidate ad groups with fewer than about 30 conversions in 30 days ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/googles-display-ads-are-dead-here-is-what-replaces-them-in-2026/)). A practitioner adds: audit placement exclusions, app exclusions, managed placements and brand-safety controls and rebuild them in Demand Gen before migration is forced ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-is-retiring-standalone-display-campaigns-in-favor-of-demand-gen/)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM]** Google says adding the Display Network to Demand Gen yields an average 9.5% ROI increase ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/googles-display-ads-are-dead-here-is-what-replaces-them-in-2026/)). **[VENDOR, 21,425 accounts]** Optmyzr reports Display volume down 17.3% year on year from Q1 2025 to Q1 2026 while Demand Gen volume rose 53.2% and Demand Gen CPC rose 33.33% ([Optmyzr](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/google-ads-benchmark-report-q1-2026/)). **[INFERENCE]** Plan on Display as a channel inside Demand Gen; remarketing setups that rely on Manual CPC need a new bidding plan, and migrating first with Display-only and widening channels later is the lower-variance path.
