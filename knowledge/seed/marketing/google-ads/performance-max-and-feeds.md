**When to use this:** When building or auditing Performance Max or Shopping campaigns.

## Performance Max: inputs matter more than controls

**[FAST-MOVING, Sept 2026]** Performance Max now offers campaign-level negative keywords (limit raised from 100 to 10,000), brand exclusions, up to 50 search themes, URL exclusions, and search-term, asset and channel reporting ([Linear Design](https://lineardesign.com/blog/google-performance-max-a-practical-guide/); [Optmyzr](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/performance-max-2025-updates-study-analysis/)). **[DURABLE]** Build each asset group around one theme so every combination stays accurate, upload deliberate video instead of relying on auto-generated video, and treat Ad Strength as a completeness flag that does not predict profit. Audience signals guide learning without restricting delivery, so prioritise first-party lists. Split campaigns by budget, target, country, promotion, inventory or new-customer objective, not by catalog category alone. Exact-match Search keywords identical to a query generally take priority over Performance Max. The channel report is diagnostic only. Reconcile results against analytics, CRM, revenue, margin, returns and cancellations ([Linear Design](https://lineardesign.com/blog/google-performance-max-a-practical-guide/)). **[INFERENCE]** Brand handling is the highest-impact decision for honest measurement: without brand exclusions or a brand split, reported ROAS includes demand that would have converted anyway. **[VENDOR, 2024 data, older]** Optmyzr's study of 24,702 campaigns cites 60+ conversions a month for Performance Max to work effectively ([Optmyzr](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/performance-max-2025-updates-study-analysis/)).

## Feed quality is the ecommerce foundation

**[DURABLE]** Audit titles, product type, Google product category, brand, GTIN, MPN, price, availability, images and promotions, and use custom labels for margin, season, bestseller status and inventory priority ([Linear Design](https://lineardesign.com/blog/google-performance-max-a-practical-guide/)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Google's retail guide adds: `short_title` under 65 characters, largest available image (minimum 300 x 300), availability synced with stock, and at least 50 approved products when applying manual product filters ([Google retail PDF, 2026](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/youtube_demand_gen_for_retail_best_practices_2026.pdf)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Agentic commerce is arriving: the Universal Commerce Protocol launched 11 January 2026, with checkout buttons in main search from 5 May 2026 and Universal Cart rolling out in the US from 19 May 2026; expansion was announced for Canada, Australia and the UK, with no EU date in the notes ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-marketing-live-2026-every-announcement-that-actually-matters)). Merchant Center free-listing requirements and Product structured data are in section 8.
