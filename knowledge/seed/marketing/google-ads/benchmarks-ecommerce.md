**When to use this:** When judging an ecommerce Google Ads account against the market.

## Benchmarks: US lead generation, US ecommerce, European ecommerce, B2B SaaS

**[VENDOR, calendar 2025, 18,000+ ecommerce brands, mainly US]** Triple Whale reports overall Google Ads ROAS of **3.68** (down 10.03% year on year), median CPA $23.74 (up 12.35%), median CPM $12.79 (up 10.01%), conversion rate down 9.28% and CTR up 7.49%, with no split by campaign type. Industry examples: Pets and Animals CPA $25.15, conversion rate 4.43%, ROAS 2.84; Travel Accessories and Luggage CPA $28.23, conversion rate 2.63%, ROAS 4.30; Health and Wellness ROAS 2.12; Consumer Electronics ROAS 3.02 ([Triple Whale, updated 6 Feb 2026](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/google-ads-benchmarks)).

**[VENDOR, Q1 2026, over EUR 650M of European ecommerce spend; values read from charts, approximate]** Smarter Ecommerce's Market Observer gives European CPCs of **EUR 0.37 to 0.40 for Performance Max, EUR 0.31 to 0.35 for Shopping and EUR 0.39 to 0.41 for Search**; CTR of 13.5 to 15% for Search, 1.6 to 1.8% for Shopping and 1.5 to 1.6% for Performance Max; and conversion rates of about 4.5% for Search (above 6% in Q1 2025) and about 3.0 to 3.5% for Shopping and Performance Max, with mobile near 2.5% and desktop about 5%. Median ROAS was about 7.0 in early January 2026 and 5.0 to 6.2 through late January and February, and average order value about EUR 80 to 85. Year on year, Shopping impressions fell about 17% and Performance Max 11%, CPC rose about 11% in Shopping and 6.5% in Performance Max, Search conversions fell about 22% and Shopping conversions rose about 32%. Median Shopping impression share of large marketplaces was Amazon 34 to 37%, Temu 20 to 21%, eBay 17 to 18% and AliExpress 15 to 19%, and mobile is nearly 80% of Performance Max and Shopping volume ([smec Q1 2026](https://smarter-ecommerce.com/en/smec-market-observer/reports/Q1_2026/)).

**[VENDOR, 21,425 accounts, Q1 2025 to Q1 2026]** Optmyzr's all-campaign baseline for Q1 2026 is CTR 2.22%, conversion rate 6.20%, CPA $13.27 and ROAS 430.9%, with Search CTR at 12.15%. Year on year, Shopping CPC rose 10.64% with ROAS down 1.03%; Performance Max CPC rose 5.66% with ROAS down 4.53%; Search CPC rose 1.36% with ROAS up 2.47% ([Optmyzr](https://www.optmyzr.com/blog/google-ads-benchmark-report-q1-2026/)). **[INFERENCE]** European ecommerce CPCs are far below typical US figures, so US benchmarks should not be applied to EU accounts; and across all three datasets Shopping and Performance Max CPCs rose roughly 6 to 11% with flat or lower ROAS, so a 2026 account holding ROAS flat is outperforming the trend.
