**When to use this:** When deciding whether to switch on AI Max or how to reach AI Overviews and AI Mode with ads.

## AI Max: run it as a controlled experiment

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** AI Max for Search is a feature suite on Search campaigns combining search term matching, text customisation and final URL expansion ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15910366?hl=en)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM, 2025 data]** Google says activating it yields about 14% more conversions or value at similar CPA or ROAS for non-retail advertisers, and 27% for campaigns relying mainly on exact and phrase. **[CONTESTED; independent data from late 2025]** Smarter Ecommerce's analysis of 250+ retail campaigns found AI Max conversions at roughly 35% lower ROAS than other match types in the same campaigns, and a four-month single-account test showed $100.37 cost per conversion for AI Max against $43.97 for phrase. Google's reported test requirements are a $50 minimum daily budget and an eight-week test with a one to two week learning phase ([PPC Land, 6 Nov 2025](https://ppc.land/independent-tests-show-ai-max-underperforms-traditional-match-types/)). Controls include brand inclusions and exclusions, locations of interest and URL exclusions. **[DURABLE]** Landing-page hygiene matters more under AI Max because page content drives generated headlines and matching ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167110?hl=en)). **[INFERENCE]** Roll out via campaign experiment, non-brand first, with brand and URL exclusions set, judged over about eight weeks. No independent post-May-2026 study was found.

## Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode need broad or keywordless matching

**[FAST-MOVING, June 2026]** Per Google's Ads Liaison, within AI Overviews only broad match or keywordless targeting is eligible and exact match cannot serve there, though both can serve above or below. Broad match stays eligible without AI Max. Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode are reported with other top-of-page ads, with no segmented reporting yet ([TechWyse, 11 Jun 2026](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/ginny-marvin-ai-max-ai-search-eligibility-gml-2026)). **[CONTESTED]** PPC Land reported that the new AI Search formats require AI Max or Performance Max with text customisation ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-marketing-live-2026-every-announcement-that-actually-matters)), while the Liaison named only Business Agent for Leads as requiring them; treat format-level requirements as unsettled. **[PLATFORM CLAIM, May 2026]** AI Overviews has more than 2.5 billion monthly users and AI Mode over 1 billion.
