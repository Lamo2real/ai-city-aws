**When to use this:** Before B2B email to Danish, Swedish, Norwegian or Finnish recipients.

## B2B email rules differ across the Nordics

**[DURABLE in outline; law-firm table dated January 2024]** Sweden: B2C opt-in with soft opt-in available; B2B opt-out if the email relates to the recipient's professional role, otherwise opt-in. **Denmark: opt-in for both B2C and B2B**, with the soft opt-in as the only exception. Norway: B2C opt-in; B2B individualised addresses opt-in, non-individualised addresses such as post@ opt-out. Finland: B2C opt-in; B2B individualised addresses opt-in unless the person's role is essentially related to the product, in which case opt-out; generic addresses opt-out. All four are described as requiring a completed transaction for the soft opt-in, so an enquiry or quote request alone does not qualify ([Fieldfisher, Jan 2024](https://res.cloudinary.com/fieldfisher/image/upload/v1706627634/PDFs/EU_e-marketing_requirements_-_January_2024_lqpl6s.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** That "transaction required" reading predates case C-654/23; treat free-sign-up soft opt-in as unsettled at national level. A single Nordic B2B cold-email programme is not possible on one rule set: Denmark needs consent; in Sweden, Norway and Finland use role-relevant content and prefer generic company addresses; always include an unsubscribe address.
