**When to use this:** Before publishing influencer, creator, affiliate or employee posts aimed at Swedish audiences.

## Sweden: advertising must be recognisable, "samarbete" is not enough, and several parties are liable

**[DURABLE, Sweden; statute read on primary]** Marketing Act section 9 requires all marketing to be designed and presented so that it is clear that it is marketing ([Riksdagen](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)). State it at the very start, mark affiliate links, and do not rely on an end-of-post disclosure ([Företagarna, 2018](https://www.foretagarna.se/driva-eget-foretag/marknadsforing/vad-galler-vid-annonsmarkning-av-samarbeten-pa-youtube-instagram-och-bloggar)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026; secondary]** Konsumentverket's report 2026:3 on influencer marketing records about 100 targeted supervision cases in recent years and 52 cases from a spring 2025 sweep on platforms used by young people, finds industry knowledge of marketing law "varying and in many cases deficient", and notes that previously supervised actors re-offend. The case-law principles as reported are that the average consumer must recognise advertising on a fleeting encounter; **"i samarbete med" and "#samarbete" are not enough**; remuneration includes products, trips and other benefits; and an influencer promoting their own company's products can be the primary responsible party. The Consumer Ombudsman can attach conditional fines of up to SEK 1,500,000 per violation ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/swedens-influencer-ad-crackdown-fines-court-rulings-and-eu-law-coming/); [Legal 500 / Rouse, 27 Feb 2026](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/sweden/media-telecoms-it-entertainment/influencer-marketing-in-sweden-challenges-and-regulatory-practice)). **[DURABLE, Sweden; industry self-regulation dated April 2025]** The joint recommendation of Sveriges Kommunikationsbyråer, Sveriges Annonsörer and IAB Sverige, hosted by the advertising ombudsman, recommends the labels **"Reklam från X", "Marknadsföring från X" or "Betalt samarbete med X"**; advises against "I samarbete med"; calls very short labels like "AD" risky; says to put the label first in the text and not hidden behind "more", and in video to show it at the advertising section or throughout; says platform paid-partnership labels may not be sufficient on their own, so an own label should be added; and notes that affiliate links, own-business posts and employees' posts for an employer can all trigger the duty ([Rekommendation för reklammarkering](https://reklamombudsmannen.org/media/1amjljh3/rekommendation-f%C3%B6r-reklammarkering-i-some-komm-sva-iab.pdf)).
