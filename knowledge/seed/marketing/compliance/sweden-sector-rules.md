## Sweden: sector rules for alcohol, gambling, credit and tobacco

**[DURABLE, Sweden; statutes read on primary]** Alcohol marketing requires particular moderation and **may not target or depict children or young people under 25**; commercial alcohol ads are barred from TV, on-demand TV and radio, and video-sharing platform providers may not place them before, during or after user-generated videos; images are limited to the product, its ingredients, packaging or trademark ([lagen.nu, Alkohollag](https://lagen.nu/2010:1622)). Gambling marketing to consumers requires moderation, may not target under-18s, may not be sent as direct marketing to self-excluded players, and must state the minimum age and contact details for a gambling-problem support organisation ([lagen.nu, Spellag](https://lagen.nu/2018:1138)). **[FAST-MOVING, Sweden]** A new Consumer Credit Act (2026:1011) **enters into force on 20 November 2026**. Credit marketing must observe moderation; may not create false expectations about credit availability or cost, state that debts or payment defaults matter little, or imply that credit replaces savings, improves finances or raises living standards; must show the effective interest rate with a representative example at least as prominently as other rates; must include a notice that credit carries costs, the risks of indebtedness and where to get budget and debt advice; and must tell consumers when a personalised credit offer is based wholly on automated processing. Certain cost-free invoice credits repaid within 50 days are exempt from parts of the chapter ([lagen.nu, Konsumentkreditlag 2026:1011](https://lagen.nu/2026:1011)). **[INFERENCE]** A merchant promoting "betala senare" in ads published on or after 20 November 2026 should check whether its provider's product is an exempt invoice credit and otherwise include the risk notice. **[DURABLE; secondary]** Tobacco and e-cigarette advertising is prohibited on digital platforms ([Legal 500 / Rouse](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/sweden/media-telecoms-it-entertainment/influencer-marketing-in-sweden-challenges-and-regulatory-practice)).
