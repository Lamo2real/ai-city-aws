**When to use this:** Before publishing claims, reviews, sale prices or Black Week offers in Sweden.

## Sweden: sanctions, misleading claims and reviews

**[DURABLE, Sweden; statute read on primary]** Marketing Act section 10 bans incorrect claims and misleading presentations, expressly including a product's effect on health and the environment, and treats omitting or obscuring material information as misleading. Section 12 c requires a trader giving access to consumer reviews to state whether and how it ensures reviews come from consumers who used or bought the product. Under sections 29 to 31 the market disruption fee for intentional or negligent breaches runs from SEK 10,000 up to **4% of the trader's turnover** (up to the equivalent of EUR 2 million where turnover cannot be established), and it also applies to anyone who materially contributed to the breach, which reaches agencies and influencers ([lagen.nu](https://lagen.nu/2008:486)).

## Sweden: sale claims must show the lowest price of the prior 30 days

**[DURABLE, Sweden; statute read on primary]** The Price Information Act (2004:347) requires price information when specific products are offered, including unit price for goods and prices including VAT. Under section 7 a, when a product is offered with a statement that the price has been reduced, the earlier price must also be given, and **the earlier price must be the lowest price the trader applied in the 30 days before the reduction**; for gradual reductions it is the price before the first reduction ([lagen.nu](https://lagen.nu/2004:347)). **[FAST-MOVING, 15 Oct 2026]** A government proposal would limit section 7 a to goods instead of all products from 1 March 2027, keeping the 30-day rule ([Regeringen](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/c1ca1ae2860342019326591ba33dc84f/lrr-andring-i-reglerna-om-information-vid-prissankningar-webb.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** For any "rea" or Black Week claim, show the lowest price of the previous 30 days as the reference price, for goods and services alike until the amendment takes effect. Singles Day discounts on 11 November lower the reference price for Black Week two weeks later.
