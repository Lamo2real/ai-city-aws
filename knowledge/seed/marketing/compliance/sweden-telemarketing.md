## Sweden: telemarketing needs written confirmation after the call

**[DURABLE, Sweden; Konsumentverket page read on primary]** When a company calls a consumer or prompted the call, the consumer must confirm the agreement in writing after the call (signed paper, email or SMS approval, or BankID); the company may not collect it during the call and must be able to prove it, and without it the agreement is invalid. Callers must state the sales purpose, a 14-day withdrawal right applies, and consumers can block sales calls through NIX-Telefon ([Konsumentverket](https://konsumentverket.se/en/articles/rules-for-telemarketing)). **[FAST-MOVING, 9 Jul 2026]** A government inquiry is considering a prior-consent requirement, a ban or a strengthened blocking register, with its report due 15 January 2027; no law change yet ([Regeringen](https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/4d7e166bffd143f781a4edeab6aa38ae/ett-starkt-konsumentskydd-vid-telefonforsaljning-hr202601525/)).
