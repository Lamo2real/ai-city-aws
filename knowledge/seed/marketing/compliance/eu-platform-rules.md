**When to use this:** When planning or judging Meta targeting in the EU, or targeting by sensitive traits or age.

## Meta's less-personalised option weakens EU targeting by an unknown amount

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** After a EUR 200M Digital Markets Act fine in April 2025, Meta committed on 8 December 2025 to give EU users a choice between fully personalised ads and "less personalised ads" using less data, presented from January 2026, with the Commission gathering evidence on "impact and uptake" ([European Commission](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/meta-commits-give-eu-users-choice-personalised-ads-under-digital-markets-act-2025-12-08_en); [The Current](https://www.thecurrent.com/marketing/data-privacy-meta-users-option-less-personalized-ads-eu)). Meta's compliance report says the option "uses 90% less data than personalized ads", that revised choice flows rolled out to all users in January 2026, and that **a non-dismissible reminder is scheduled for January 2027** for users who clicked "OK" without choosing; the ad-free subscription costs EUR 5.99 a month on web and EUR 7.99 on mobile ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/metas-2026-dma-report-reveals-whatsapp-ads-a-eu200m-fine-and-a-defiant-stance-on-personalized-advertising/)). Total reach is unchanged but targeting is coarser for those users ([adlibrary.com, 20 Sep 2026](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ads-eu-2026)). A weak secondary report says Meta's ad sales slowed after the model was introduced, without quantifying it ([Renascence, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.renascence.io/news/129219/meta-ad-sales-slow-as-eu-users-opt-for-less-personalised-ads)).

## Digital Services Act advertising rules

**[DURABLE, since 17 Feb 2024]** Platforms must show that an ad is an ad, on whose behalf it runs and its main targeting parameters; no ads based on profiling with special-category data, and no profiling-based ads to users the platform is reasonably certain are minors ([Arthur Cox](https://www.arthurcox.com/knowledge/eu-digital-services-act-new-digital-advertising-landscape/)).
