**When to use this:** Before running cause-led, social-issue or political messaging in the EU.

## Political and issue advertising is closed on Meta and Google in the EU

**[DURABLE since Oct 2025]** The EU political advertising regulation has applied since 10 October 2025 and covers messages designed to influence an election, referendum or "a legislative or regulatory process", which reaches issue-based content; fines run up to 6% of annual turnover. Google stopped serving political ads in the EU and Meta ended political, electoral and social-issue ads there in early October 2025 ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/extensive-regulation-kills-digital-political-advertising-in-eu/); [Open Terms Archive](https://opentermsarchive.org/en/publications/political-advertising-after-the-ttpa-exit-adaptation-and-the-future-of-platform-governance/)); Meta's help centre confirms such ads cannot be created or edited in the EU ([Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/298000447747885)), and TikTok does not permit political ads at all (section 10). **[INFERENCE]** Cause-led or sustainability messaging risks being classified as a social issue and rejected with no authorisation route; route such campaigns to legal review.
