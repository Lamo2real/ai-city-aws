**When to use this:** Before publishing AI-generated images, video, audio or chatbots in the EU.

## AI Act Article 50 has applied since 2 August 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; law-firm summaries]** Article 50 obligations have applied since 2 August 2026: users must be told when they are dealing with a chatbot unless it is obvious; synthetic audio, image, video and text must be machine-readably marked by providers; and deployers must disclose deepfakes, meaning AI-generated or manipulated image, audio or video resembling real persons, places or events. AI-generated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest must be labelled unless it has undergone human review and someone holds editorial responsibility ([Goodwin, Aug 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force); [Sidley, 24 Jun 2026](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2026/06/24/eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-preparing-for-compliance-by-2-august-2026/)). **The Commission published the final Code of Practice on marking and labelling AI-generated content on 10 June 2026**; it is voluntary and open for signature, and it asks providers for machine-readable marking in at least two layers where necessary plus detection tools, and deployers for clear deepfake labels, a common EU icon or equivalent label, and audio disclaimers where visual labels are impossible ([Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/06/european-commission-publishes-final-code-of-practice-on-marking-and-labelling-aigenerated-content)). The Commission adopted its transparency guidelines on 20 July 2026. The AI Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 in force 27 July 2026, gave providers of generative systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 until 2 December 2026 to meet the marking obligation; deployer duties were not delayed ([Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force); [Usercentrics](https://usercentrics.com/knowledge-hub/eu-ai-act-high-risk-delay-article-50-transparency-consent/)). **Penalties for transparency breaches run up to EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher** ([Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force)). The WFA published an Article 50 labelling guide for marketers on 17 September 2026 ([WFA](https://wfanet.org/ai-community)). **[INFERENCE]** For a marketing agent: AI-generated realistic images, video or audio of real people, places or events in ads need a visible AI label now; ordinary AI-assisted copy that a human reviews and takes responsibility for does not need a label under the text rule; and chatbots must identify themselves.
