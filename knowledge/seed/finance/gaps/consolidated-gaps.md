### Corrections applied in this document

**[INFERENCE]** Where a later research pass corrected an earlier one, only the corrected claim is used above. The 2026 income base amount is SEK 83,400 and not SEK 80,600, with SEK 80,600 evidently the 2025 figure behind the 2026 3:12 amounts ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). Income tax return deadlines follow Skatteverket's single date per year-end group, replacing an unverified paper-and-digital pair ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)). The late-filing fee scale of SEK 7,500, 7,500 and 15,000 is confirmed from the statute ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)). Växa-stöd exists in 2026 as a refund, replacing an earlier doubt based on its absence from the rate table ([Skatteverket, regler för växa-stöd](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/vaxastod.4.361dc8c15312eff6fd37447.html)). The blanket statement that an option programme forces a move to K3 carries the relief-rule caveat ([BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)). The corporate tax rate of 20.6% is confirmed on Skatteverket ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). The COSO generative-AI paper, earlier known by title only, was read ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).
