**When to use this:** For corporate income tax, tax returns and payments, periodiseringsfond, losses, 3:12 owner taxation, the R&D deduction and the 2027 budget changes.

**Source section:** 9. Corporate tax, owner taxation and R&D incentives

### Corporate income tax is 20.6%

**[FAST-MOVING, income year 2026; checked on primary source]** The corporate income tax rate for legal entities is 20.6% for income year 2026 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). An aktiebolag is always obliged to file a return, even if dormant ([Skatteverket brochure SKV 294, edition 24](https://www.skatteverket.se/download/18.6491ed82199c4722daef0c/1761209691103/skatteregler-for-aktie-och-handelsbolag-skv294-utgava24.pdf)). No source read mentions a change to the rate for 2027 ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)).

### Income tax return deadlines by financial year-end

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source]** The company income tax return (Inkomstdeklaration 2) is due by financial year-end as follows: year-ends January to April 2026, 1 December 2026; May to June 2026, 15 January 2027; July to August 2026, 1 April 2027; September to December 2026, 2 August 2027. Skatteverket's page shows one date per year-end group and does not distinguish paper from electronic filing ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)). For December 2025 year-ends the deadline was 3 August 2026 whether filed digitally or on paper ([Driva Eget, updated 16 Feb 2026](https://driva-eget.se/artiklar/alla-datum-for-deklarationen-2026)).

### The Inkomstdeklaration 2 e-service closes on 29 December 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 29 Dec 2026; checked on primary source]** Skatteverket is discontinuing the "Inkomstdeklaration 2" e-service on 29 December 2026 and points to other digital routes such as declaration software ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)). **[INFERENCE]** A company that files its own return needs software that can submit digitally, or an agent, for the 2026 tax year.

### Late fee for the company income tax return

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** The late fee for the company income tax return is SEK 6,250, and up to three fees can be charged depending on the delay; non-filing can lead to an estimated assessment, a tax surcharge and loss of F-tax approval ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)).

### Extra payments to avoid interest

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source]** The last dates for an extra payment to the tax account to avoid cost interest are, for amounts over SEK 30,000 and up to SEK 30,000 respectively: year-ends January to April 2026, 12 June 2026 and 3 September 2026; May to June 2026, 12 August 2026 and 3 November 2026; July to August 2026, 12 October 2026 and 4 January 2027; September to December 2026, 12 February 2027 and 3 May 2027 ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)). A cash agent puts the relevant date in the 13-week forecast as soon as the year-end tax estimate shows a shortfall.

### Final tax notices and residual tax

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source]** For year-ends September to December 2025 the final tax notice comes on 7 to 9 December 2026 digitally and residual tax must be on the tax account by 12 March 2027; for year-ends January to April 2026 the residual tax date is 12 July 2027; for May to June 2026 it is 13 September 2027. Roughly 90 days are given after a decision ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)).

### Periodiseringsfond: defer up to 25% of profit for six years

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026 rate; checked on primary source]** A legal entity may allocate up to 25% to a periodiseringsfond (30% for individuals). Notional income equals the government borrowing rate (2.55% at 30 November 2025) times the fund balance at the start of the tax year, with a floor of 0.50% ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). Each allocation must be reversed to taxation no later than the sixth year after the allocation year, earlier reversal is allowed, and for tax years ending in 2025 the notional rate was 1.96% ([Skatteverket brochure SKV 294, edition 24](https://www.skatteverket.se/download/18.6491ed82199c4722daef0c/1761209691103/skatteregler-for-aktie-och-handelsbolag-skv294-utgava24.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** For a tax year ending in 2026 the notional rate should be 2.55%, following the rule as described; the pages read do not state this in so many words.

### Tax losses carry forward without time limit

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Tax losses are carried forward to the next year's return with no time limit as long as the company exists; after an ownership change an amount cap (beloppsspärr) or a group-contribution block (koncernbidragsspärr) can restrict their use ([Skatteverket brochure SKV 294, edition 24](https://www.skatteverket.se/download/18.6491ed82199c4722daef0c/1761209691103/skatteregler-for-aktie-och-handelsbolag-skv294-utgava24.pdf)). **[GAP; unverified background]** The cap formula (commonly cited as 200% of acquisition cost) and the five-year block were not verified, which matters before any funding round or sale that changes control.

### Group contributions and dividends between companies

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Group contributions are deductible for the giver and taxable for the recipient and must be reported openly in both returns for tax years with the same filing date. Dividends received on business-related shares (näringsbetingade andelar) are tax-free for an aktiebolag, listed shares held under one year excepted. A company paying dividends to individuals must file a control statement, and only CSD-registered companies withhold 30% tax, which closely held companies normally are not ([Skatteverket brochure SKV 294, edition 24](https://www.skatteverket.se/download/18.6491ed82199c4722daef0c/1761209691103/skatteregler-for-aktie-och-handelsbolag-skv294-utgava24.pdf)). **[GAP; unverified background]** The more-than-90% ownership test for group contributions and withholding tax on dividends to foreign shareholders were not verified.

### Base amounts for 2026 and 2027

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source for 2026, secondary source only for 2027]** For 2026 the price base amount (prisbasbelopp) is SEK 59,200, the raised price base amount SEK 60,500 and the income base amount (inkomstbasbelopp) SEK 83,400; the state income tax threshold is SEK 643,000 at 20% and the breakpoint SEK 660,400 for people under 66 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). For 2027 the price base amount is SEK 59,600 ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)). The 2027 income base amount and tax tables were not found, and Skatteverket publishes the 2027 state-tax threshold once the budget is settled ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)).

### Low-value asset limit

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; secondary source and inference]** The limit for immediate expensing of low-value assets is half a price base amount: SEK 29,800 excluding VAT for 2027 ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)). **[INFERENCE]** For 2026 the same rule gives SEK 29,600, calculated from the verified price base amount of SEK 59,200 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)).

### Deductible representation amounts

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Deductible representation excluding VAT in 2026 is SEK 0 for meals, SEK 60 for simple refreshments, SEK 180 for events such as theatre or a staff party, and SEK 300 for representation and advertising gifts ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)).

### 3:12 rules for owner-managers: reformed from income year 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, in force 1 Jan 2026; secondary source only]** The Riksdag adopted new 3:12 rules for closely held companies on 26 November 2025; they entered into force on 1 January 2026 and first apply to tax years starting after 31 December 2025 ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna)). Dividends and gains within the limit amount are taxed at 20% for the owner, amounts above it mainly as employment income up to a ceiling, and above the ceiling as capital income at 30% ([Ekonomifakta, 3:12-reglerna, updated 8 Jan 2026](https://www.ekonomifakta.se/sakomraden/skatt/skatt-pa-foretagande-och-kapital/312-reglerna_1210959.html)). All 3:12 figures in this document rest on audit-firm and reference-site summaries; Skatteverket's own page was not reached.

### 3:12 grundbelopp: SEK 322,400 for 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, income year 2026; secondary source only]** The grundbelopp is four income base amounts, stated as SEK 322,400 for 2026, and replaces the old simplification rule. An owner of shares in several closely held companies gets one grundbelopp in total, split in proportion to holdings, and within a company it is split across the shares ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna)). **[INFERENCE]** SEK 322,400 equals four times SEK 80,600, which is evidently the prior-year (2025) income base amount, since Skatteverket lists the 2026 income base amount as SEK 83,400 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)); an earlier research inference that the 2026 income base amount was SEK 80,600 was wrong and is not used.

### 3:12 wage-based allowance and its SEK 644,800 deduction

**[FAST-MOVING, income year 2026; secondary source only]** The wage-based allowance is 50% of the wage base allocated to the owner's shares, after a standard deduction of eight income base amounts (SEK 644,800 for 2026) per owner; it is capped at 50 times the owner's or a close relative's own salary, and spouses are calculated jointly. The owner's own minimum-salary requirement and the 4% capital-share requirement are abolished, so every owner of qualified shares can use the wage-based allowance ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna); [Ekonomifakta, 3:12-reglerna, updated 8 Jan 2026](https://www.ekonomifakta.se/sakomraden/skatt/skatt-pa-foretagande-och-kapital/312-reglerna_1210959.html)). **[INFERENCE]** An owner-managed SaaS company needs total group cash wages above SEK 644,800 per owner before any wage-based allowance arises; below that only the grundbelopp gives 20%-taxed dividend room.

### Other 3:12 changes

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2029; secondary source only]** Saved dividend allowance is no longer uplifted with interest, and an interest-based addition is given only on the part of the shares' cost base above SEK 100,000. The waiting period for shares to stop being qualified is cut from five to four years, first for owners whose period began in 2023 or later, and one source lists the four-year periods as applying from income year 2027. The 2026 limit amount, built on 2025 wages, is computed under the new rules and reported on form K10 in spring 2027. Wages in a company owned through an alternative investment fund are excluded from the wage base, the special subsidiary definition is replaced by the Companies Act definition, and old index and capital-base rules for pre-1990 and pre-1992 shares end on 1 January 2029 ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna); [Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)).

### 3:12 amounts for income year 2027 are not confirmed

**[INFERENCE] [GAP]** If the grundbelopp continues to be four income base amounts of the preceding year, it would be SEK 333,600 for income year 2027 and the wage-deduction threshold SEK 667,200 (four and eight times SEK 83,400). This is arithmetic on an assumed lag convention that was not read. The ceiling amounts for employment-income taxation and the interest rate on the cost base above SEK 100,000 were not found.

### R&D deduction on employer contributions: the current rule

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Under the current rule (lag 2023:747 as described in a government bill draft of 13 August 2026) employer contributions and the general payroll fee are reduced by 20% of the contribution base for staff working with R&D; the contributions may not fall below the old-age pension fee of 10.21%; the cap is SEK 3 million per calendar month per group; and the employee must have worked with R&D at least half of working time and at least 15 hours in the month ([regeringen.se, lagrådsremiss on a new R&D tax incentive, 13 Aug 2026](https://regeringen.se/contentassets/a57a22d3f04c4a10b86509f88c90b529/ett-nytt-skatteincitament-for-forskning-och-utveckling.pdf)). Skatteverket's rate table lists the deduction as minus 20% for 2026 ([Skatteverket, arbetsgivaravgifter](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/arbetsgivaravgifter.4.233f91f71260075abe8800020817.html)).

### R&D deduction: a monthly payroll control

**[INFERENCE]** The R&D deduction is claimed monthly in the employer return and depends on each employee's share of working time, so time-allocation evidence per employee per month is a payroll control and not a year-end exercise. For a SaaS company with qualifying developers the saving is material, which justifies a monthly sign-off by the engineering lead.

### R&D proposals for 2027 are not law

**[FAST-MOVING, proposed for 1 Jan 2027, not enacted; checked on primary source for the proposal]** Proposed and dependent on the 2027 budget: simplified R&D definitions and removal of the 15-hour requirement (lagrådsremiss decided 11 June 2026); a cap indexed to 36 income base amounts per calendar month per employer from 1 January 2027; and a new optional enhanced cost deduction of an extra 200% of qualifying R&D wage costs (300% in total) for tax years starting after 31 December 2026, for work performed in the EEA, with the same half-time requirement and no cap in the proposal ([regeringen.se, lagrådsremiss on a new R&D tax incentive, 13 Aug 2026](https://regeringen.se/contentassets/a57a22d3f04c4a10b86509f88c90b529/ett-nytt-skatteincitament-for-forskning-och-utveckling.pdf); [regeringen.se, tax proposals remitted ahead of the 2027 budget](https://regeringen.se/regeringens-politik/skatt-och-tull/skatteforslag-som-remitterats-infor-hostbudgeten-2027/)). The bill draft also proposes changes to the Swedish top-up tax act from 1 January 2027 so that the new deduction is handled in that regime ([regeringen.se, lagrådsremiss on a new R&D tax incentive, 13 Aug 2026](https://regeringen.se/contentassets/a57a22d3f04c4a10b86509f88c90b529/ett-nytt-skatteincitament-for-forskning-och-utveckling.pdf)).

### The 2027 budget bill: transitional, presented 12 November 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 9 Nov 2026; checked on primary source]** The budget bill for 2027 is handed to the Riksdag on 12 November 2026 at 08:00 by the caretaker government ([regeringen.se, press release 9 Nov 2026](https://www.regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/11/presentation-av-budgetpropositionen-for-2027/)). It follows transitional-budget principles: 2026 appropriation levels carry over, with exceptions for indexation, existing law and contracts, EU obligations, essential public functions and non-controversial bills with political consensus that cannot wait; temporary measures from earlier years are not extended; the same principles apply to revenue proposals where relevant. Motions run until 27 November and the framework vote is on 9 December 2026 ([regeringen.se, Finance Minister's slides, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.regeringen.se/globalassets/regeringen/dokument/finansdepartementet/ppt-till-presstraffar/presentationsbilder-fran-presstraff-med-finansministern-den-27-oktober-2026.pdf)). The press describes it as containing no election pledges or new policy such as tax cuts ([TV4/TT, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.tv4.se/artikel/tt-261027-budget1-ff495b05/valloften-far-vanta-i-ny-budget)).

### What is already decided for 2027 and what is only proposed

**[FAST-MOVING, 2 Nov 2026; secondary source only]** Already decided for 2027: the price base amount of SEK 59,600 and low-value asset limit of SEK 29,800; the end of youth employer-contribution relief with pay for September 2027; four-year 3:12 time limits from income year 2027; food VAT at 6% through 2027; and a cash-register XML requirement from 1 January 2027. Still only proposed for 1 January 2027: the R&D deduction changes, a deduction for sponsorship costs that maintain or improve the company's reputation, new rules on an employee's place of work and a statutory definition of business travel, tax-free competence support from registered transition organisations, a higher earned-income credit for seniors, and the turnover-based split of input VAT in mixed activities ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter); [regeringen.se, tax proposals remitted ahead of the 2027 budget](https://regeringen.se/regeringens-politik/skatt-och-tull/skatteforslag-som-remitterats-infor-hostbudgeten-2027/)). **[INFERENCE]** A knowledge base should mark the proposed items "check after 12 November and 9 December 2026" and not load them as 2027 rules; whether they count as non-controversial with consensus is unknown, as is whether a new government tables an amending budget in early 2027.

### Other 2026 tax changes

**[FAST-MOVING, in force 1 Jan 2026; secondary source only]** Special income tax for non-residents (SINK) was cut from 25% to 22.5% from 1 January 2026, the earned-income tax credit was strengthened (which affects withholding tables), and the travel-deduction floor was raised from SEK 11,000 to SEK 15,000 ([Forvis Mazars, law changes in force 1 January 2026](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/sv/insikter/nyheter-artiklar/artiklar-fraan-forvis-mazars-nyhetsbrev/lagaendringar-som-traedde-i-kraft-1-januari-2026)). From 1 January 2026 crypto-asset service providers must report transactions to Skatteverket, relevant only if the company holds or accepts crypto ([Forvis Mazars, law changes in force 1 January 2026](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/sv/insikter/nyheter-artiklar/artiklar-fraan-forvis-mazars-nyhetsbrev/lagaendringar-som-traedde-i-kraft-1-januari-2026)).

### Gaps on corporate tax: transfer pricing and permanent establishment are the largest

**[GAP; unverified background]** Transfer pricing and permanent establishment were not researched in any pass and are the largest remaining tax gap. The unverified recollection is an arm's-length rule in Inkomstskattelagen 14 kap. 19 §, a documentation duty for larger groups, and permanent-establishment rules in Inkomstskattelagen 2 kap. 29 § and treaty Article 5. **[INFERENCE]** A Swedish SaaS company that hires a single remote employee abroad or signs contracts through a local salesperson carries permanent-establishment and local payroll registration risk in that country; no verified rule text supports a threshold, so each such hire goes to a tax adviser first. Also unverified: preliminary tax instalment dates and the rule for revising a preliminary return (commonly cited as the 12th, 17th in January and August), and tax-account interest rates.
