### Suggested internal calendar

**[INFERENCE]** A recurring internal calendar that fits these dates is: working days 1 to 5 for the close; the 12th (or 17th) for the tax-account payment covering the employer return, withheld tax, preliminary tax and, for smaller companies, VAT; the 26th for VAT in large payers; the month after each quarter for the OSS return; and a monthly equity check. **[GAP]** Skatteverket's "Viktiga datum" pages and Bolagsverket's calendar were not reached, and the periodic-summary and preliminary-tax dates are unverified.
