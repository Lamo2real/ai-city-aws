**When to use this:** When deciding how the department is organised, how commitments are set with OKRs, or who owns quality for AI features.

**Source section:** 11. Operating models and the changing product role

### Delivery teams, feature teams and empowered product teams

**[DURABLE; practitioner opinion]** SVPG describes three team types. A delivery team has a product owner acting as backlog administrator. A feature team works from a roadmap of features, is measured by output, and has a product manager acting as project manager. An empowered product team is given problems, is measured by outcomes, and has a product manager who owns value and viability ([SVPG, Product vs Feature Teams, Aug 2019, modified Oct 2023](https://svpg.com/product-vs-feature-teams)).

### OKRs carry the commitment

**[VENDOR; practitioner opinion]** ProdPad's position is that OKRs hold the commitment so that the roadmap can stay flexible ([ProdPad, Now-Next-Later roadmap, Oct 2022](https://www.prodpad.com/blog/invented-now-next-later-roadmap/)). SVPG's warning sign is OKRs that have decayed into feature lists ([SVPG, Product vs Feature Teams, Aug 2019, modified Oct 2023](https://svpg.com/product-vs-feature-teams)). **[GAP]** OKR-writing guidance for product teams was not retrieved.

### Product ops and experimentation ops

**[FAST-MOVING, Oct 2026; practitioner opinion]** Cagan says product ops "went in a different direction" from what he intended and proposes experimentation ops as an enablement function that supports teams who own their own experiments ([SVPG, Experimentation, AI and the Product Model, Oct 2026](https://www.svpg.com/experimentation-ai-and-the-product-model/)). **[GAP]** Definitions and the scope of product ops were not retrieved.

### The product role after agentic AI

**[FAST-MOVING, 6 Nov 2026; practitioner opinion]** SVPG's November 2026 position is that companies are building "software factories", that fewer feature-team product managers should be needed, that the product owner role is being phased out, and that the question becomes what to build. The durable skills it names are product sense, discovery, and designing evaluations for probabilistic products. It also expects many firms to keep the project model because they prize predictability ([SVPG, AI and the Future of the Product Role, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.svpg.com/ai-and-the-future-of-the-product-role/)). **[GAP]** SVPG's 2026 pieces on its changed coaching stance and on "build to learn versus build to earn" were confirmed by title and date only ([SVPG articles index](https://www.svpg.com/articles/)).

### AI adoption among product teams

**[FAST-MOVING, Q4 2025; survey]** In the PLA/ProductPlan survey, AI maturity among product teams was: limited workflows 36.9%, experimenting 32%, embedded in many workflows 18.9%, a core strategic capability 6.1%, and none 6.1%. The top concern was data privacy or security, at 53.3% ([Product-Led Alliance statistics page citing the PLA/ProductPlan State of Product Management 2026, published May 2026](https://www.productledalliance.com/product-management-statistics/)). ProductPlan's 2026 landing page adds that nearly three-quarters of product managers expect their role to blend across disciplines and that more than half of organisations hesitate to expand AI adoption for reasons other than usefulness ([ProductPlan, State of Product Management Report 2026, landing page](https://www.productplan.com/ebooks/the-state-of-product-management-report-2026)).

**[FAST-MOVING, reported Sep 2025; survey]** In Atlassian's State of Product 2026, AI saves product teams 10 to 60 minutes a day, and the top adoption barriers are poor integration with other tools and then lack of trust ([Mind the Product summary of Atlassian's State of Product 2026, Sep 2025](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/inside-atlassians-state-of-product-2026-key-takeaways/)). **[GAP]** Data on adoption of prototyping tools by product managers was not retrieved.

### The product manager as quality authority for AI features

**[FAST-MOVING, Sep 2025; practitioner opinion]** In AI products the product manager is often the principal domain expert who labels outputs and arbitrates quality, and ongoing eval upkeep is put at about 30 minutes a week after setup ([Lenny's Newsletter, Building eval systems that improve your AI product, 9 Sep 2025](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/building-eval-systems-that-improve); [Lenny's Newsletter, Why AI evals are the hottest new skill, 25 Sep 2025](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/why-ai-evals-are-the-hottest-new-skill)). **[INFERENCE]** In an agent department the quality authority for each AI feature should be a named human or a named agent with a human reviewer, because the method depends on one consistent judge.

### Sweden and EU: privacy as an adoption constraint

**[INFERENCE]** The privacy concern reported by 53.3% of product teams ([Product-Led Alliance statistics page citing the PLA/ProductPlan State of Product Management 2026, published May 2026](https://www.productledalliance.com/product-management-statistics/)) is likely stronger in Sweden and the EU, although no EU-specific source was fetched. Agent workflows that touch customer data need a documented lawful basis before they are used for discovery synthesis; the Data and AI and Legal departments own that decision.
