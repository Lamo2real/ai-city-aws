**When to use this:** When applying the house style to a software product, a SaaS offer ladder, packaging, pricing or retention: use the adapted form, and send the listed promotional tactics to Legal first.

**Source section:** 3. Adapting the house style to software

### Why the service-business models need adapting

**[INFERENCE]** Hormozi's models were built for businesses that sell transformation through people: gyms, coaching, done-for-you services and paid communities. Software differs in four ways that matter for product decisions: the subscription is the product and not the last step of a ladder, marginal delivery cost is low except where AI usage is metered, retention norms are far higher, and EU consumer and marketing law constrains several of his promotional tactics. The paragraphs below give the department's adapted defaults. Each adaptation is the researchers' synthesis unless a source is cited.

### What transfers to software unchanged

**[INFERENCE; adapts HOUSE STYLE]** Five house-style ideas transfer to software without change: the four-lever value test ([Dean Yeong, book notes on $100M Offers](https://www.deanyeong.com/book-notes/100m-offers)), the four market indicators as a segment screen ([Hormozi, The Key To Finding a Profitable Market, third-party transcript, video dated 21 Jan 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-key-to-finding-a-profitable-market)), the obstacle list before, during and after the outcome as a discovery prompt ([SuperSummary, $100M Offers, section 3 summary](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-offers/section-3-summary/)), the fast first win as an activation goal ([Hormozi, How to Get Your Customers to Stay Forever, third-party transcript, transcribed Aug 2026](https://sozai.app/transcript/get-customers-to-stay-forever/)), and the tagged-cancellation routine as a feedback loop ([Hormozi, How to Get Your Customers to Stay Forever, third-party transcript, transcribed Aug 2026](https://sozai.app/transcript/get-customers-to-stay-forever/)). His focus rule also agrees with SVPG's first strategy principle, focus ([SVPG, The Product Operating Model, Feb 2023, modified Jul 2024](https://svpg.com/the-product-operating-model-an-introduction)); both imply a short list of problems and an explicit not-doing list.

### The obstacle list becomes an opportunity list

**[INFERENCE; adapts HOUSE STYLE]** In software discovery, Hormozi's list of every obstacle the customer meets before, during and after the outcome ([SuperSummary, $100M Offers, section 3 summary](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-offers/section-3-summary/)) is the same artefact as the opportunity space in Teresa Torres's opportunity solution tree ([Product Talk, Opportunity Solution Trees, Dec 2023, modified 29 Sep 2026](https://www.producttalk.org/opportunity-solution-trees/)). The difference is the evidence standard: Torres requires opportunities to come from story-based customer interviews, so the department drafts the obstacle list as a hypothesis and confirms each item against real customer stories before it enters the tree.

### Trim and stack for software

**[INFERENCE; adapts HOUSE STYLE]** For software, trimming means removing or declining features whose cost-to-serve and added complexity exceed their value, and stacking means packaging the high-value, low-marginal-cost remainder. Hormozi's subtraction case (churn cut by deleting content) supports removing low-use features, but it is one unaudited community example ([Hormozi, How to Get Your Customers to Stay Forever, third-party transcript, transcribed Aug 2026](https://sozai.app/transcript/get-customers-to-stay-forever/)). The only usage dataset in this research, Pendo's 2019 report, found about 80% of tagged features rarely or never used, with a definition that inflates the unused share (section 9; [Pendo, The 2019 Feature Adoption Report](https://www.pendo.io/resources/the-2019-feature-adoption-report/)). Features required by law, accessibility or security are never candidates for trimming regardless of usage.

### The offer ladder mapped to SaaS

**[INFERENCE; adapts HOUSE STYLE]** The table maps the four stages of Hormozi's offer ladder ([SuperSummary, $100M Money Models summary, book published 2025](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)) onto SaaS mechanics. The key change is order: in SaaS the subscription is the core product, so continuity comes first and not last.

| Ladder stage | His version | SaaS equivalent | What changes | Source of the original |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attraction | Win-your-money-back, giveaway, decoy, buy-X-get-Y, pay less now or more later | Free trial, freemium, low-priced entry tool | Money-back, giveaway and penalty mechanics need legal review for EU consumers | [SuperSummary, $100M Money Models summary, book published 2025](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/) |
| Upsell | Classic, menu, anchor, rollover | Higher tier, add-ons, seats, usage or credits | Trigger on usage signals, not on a sales conversation alone | [SuperSummary, $100M Money Models summary, book published 2025](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/) |
| Downsell | Payment plan, trial with penalty, feature downsell; never the same thing for less | Feature-reduced tier, monthly instead of annual | His rule matches good-better-best logic; penalty trials need legal review | [SuperSummary, $100M Money Models summary, book published 2025](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/) |
| Continuity | Bonus, commitment discount, waived fee; built last | The subscription itself; annual commitment discount | Continuity is the starting point in SaaS | [SuperSummary, $100M Money Models summary, book published 2025](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/) |

### The 30-day payback rule is an aspiration in SaaS

**[CONTESTED; adapts HOUSE STYLE]** Hormozi's aim of recovering acquisition cost within about 30 days, and his rule that 30-day gross profit should exceed twice the sum of acquisition cost and cost of goods ([SuperSummary, $100M Money Models summary, book published 2025](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/); [jxnl.co report on the Money Models launch, 20 Aug 2025](https://jxnl.co/writing/2025/08/20/the-100m-money-models-the-complete-report)), are cash rules for service businesses. **[INFERENCE]** The researchers' reading is that the rule is stricter than typical SaaS payback norms and should be treated as an aspiration and not a benchmark. **[GAP]** No SaaS payback benchmark was fetched in this research, so the comparison is unquantified here.

### His churn figures are not SaaS benchmarks

**[CONTESTED]** Hormozi's community figures (80% average monthly retention, churn over 20% in the first three months) describe paid community memberships ([Hormozi, How to Get Your Customers to Stay Forever, third-party transcript, transcribed Aug 2026](https://sozai.app/transcript/get-customers-to-stay-forever/)). The last large public panel on software retention, from June 2020 and therefore possibly outdated, put good six-month user retention at about 60% for SMB and mid-market SaaS and about 70 to 75% for enterprise SaaS ([Lenny's Newsletter, What is good retention, Jun 2020, modified Jan 2024](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/what-is-good-retention-issue-29)). Agents must not use his churn figures as targets or as acceptable levels for a B2B SaaS product; they use his diagnostic routine (front-loaded churn, activation, subtraction, tagged cancellation reasons) and the company's own cohorts.

### Premium pricing needs a predictability check in software

**[CONTESTED; adapts HOUSE STYLE]** Hormozi's premium-price advice ([Dean Yeong, book notes on $100M Offers](https://www.deanyeong.com/book-notes/100m-offers)) assumes high-touch delivery where a higher price funds better service. Software pricing in 2026 must also track usage cost and buyer predictability: internal cost and margin was the top factor in pricing AI features in a 2025 survey ([Growth Unhinged, 2025 State of B2B Monetization, survey fielded Apr-May 2025](https://kylepoyar.substack.com/p/2025-state-of-b2b-monetization)), and 2026 guidance treats the customer's ability to predict the bill as a pre-change check ([Growth Unhinged, The AI monetization debate shifts again, Oct 2026](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/usage-based-pricing-for-ai)). The department keeps his principle of pricing on value and his rule against discounting the identical product, and applies the pricing evidence in section 10 for model and structure.

### Lock-in is a weak retention strategy in the EU

**[INFERENCE; adapts HOUSE STYLE]** The second-hand summary of the 12 May 2026 episode has him favouring recurring revenue with switching costs ([Shortform summary of The Game Ep 969, 12 May 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-05-12-episode-summary-solving-6-scaling-problems-ep-969)). For a company selling in the EU, the Data Act has applied since 12 September 2025 and gives customers a framework for switching between data-processing providers while restricting unfair contract terms (checked on the Commission's own policy page, not on the regulation text; [European Commission, Data Act policy page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act)). Retention therefore has to come from delivered value and not from making exit hard. Whether a given SaaS product falls inside the Data Act's switching rules is a question for Legal.

### Promotional tactics that need legal review before software use

**[INFERENCE] [GAP]** His course lists scarcity and urgency as a module ([Acquisition.com, Offers training, checked Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/training/offers)), and his attraction and downsell offers include win-your-money-back, giveaways, trials with penalties and cards on file ([SuperSummary, $100M Money Models summary, book published 2025](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). The EU legal fit of these tactics was not researched. Fake countdown timers, pre-selected paid add-ons and cancellation that is harder than sign-up are among the expected themes of the planned Digital Fairness Act according to a law-firm summary (secondary source only; [Heuking, 8 May 2026](https://www.heuking.de/en/news-events/newsletter-articles/detail/digital-fairness-act-implementation-obligations-companies-need-to-prepare-for.html)). Until Legal has cleared a specific mechanic for a specific market, agents treat these tactics as unavailable for consumer-facing flows.
