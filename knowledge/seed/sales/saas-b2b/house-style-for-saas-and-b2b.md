**When to use this:** When applying the house style to a SaaS product or to B2B deals of different sizes.

**Source section:** 12. Mapping the house style onto a SaaS business and onto B2B deals of different sizes

### Why a mapping is needed

**[GAP]** No verified primary source gives SaaS-specific or enterprise-specific sales guidance from Hormozi; his examples are consumer and small-business services ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** Everything in this section is the researchers' mapping of his structures onto SaaS and onto B2B deals of different sizes, using the sourced evidence in sections 13 to 22. The deal-size bands used are practitioner conventions that were not fetched from a source; tune them against the department's own data.

### What transfers to SaaS unchanged

**[INFERENCE]** Five parts of the house style transfer to SaaS without change: speed to lead on demo requests, trial sign-ups that meet the product-qualified threshold and pricing enquiries; the Clarify, Label and Overview sequence as the discovery structure; outcome-first presentation in demos; measuring every stage through to cash collected and beyond; and the Reinforce step as structured onboarding. **[VENDOR]** The supporting data: top inbound teams convert a median 62% of qualified form fills into booked meetings when a calendar is shown immediately ([RevenueHero, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.revenuehero.io/blog/speed-to-lead-benchmarks-for-saas)); winning demos open with the topic the buyer spent most time on in discovery ([Gong Labs demo study, Sep 2017](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/sales-demos/)); and feature-dumping correlates with win rates falling from 26% to 5% ([Gong Labs, Jul 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/driving-next-steps-isnt-enough-this-is-what-really-moves-deals-forward/)).

### Attraction offers become free trials, freemium and reverse trials

**[INFERENCE]** In SaaS the attraction offer is the free entry point: a time-boxed trial, a freemium tier or a reverse trial. The decoy offer maps to a basic tier displayed beside a recommended tier. The conditional trial maps most closely to a card-required trial, with the consumer-law limits in section 11. **[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Conversion data relayed from a ChartMogul, Growth Unhinged and ProductLed report on 200 B2B products: free-to-paid medians of 4 to 6% for trials without a card, 25 to 35% for trials with a card and 3 to 5% for freemium, and per 1,000 visitors about 10.5 payers for a card-required trial against 3.6 for a standard trial and about 5 for freemium ([Userpilot summary](https://userpilot.com/blog/free-trial-conversion-rate/)). The figures come through an aggregator page.

### Speed to lead becomes product-qualified lead routing

**[INFERENCE]** For trial and freemium users, the setter function is triggered by usage and not by a form: define a product-qualified threshold (fit, activation and intent) and have a seller or agent contact only those users. **[VENDOR]** ProductLed defines a product-qualified lead by fit, value reached and intent, and reports that such leads convert to paid at a 25% median against a 9% baseline in its survey of more than 600 SaaS companies ([ProductLed on PQLs, 2021, updated Jun 2024](https://productled.com/blog/how-to-define-pqls); [ProductLed benchmarks](https://productled.com/blog/product-led-growth-benchmarks)); the survey date is uncertain.

### The payment ladder becomes annual prepay versus monthly

**[INFERENCE]** His full-price, prepay-discount, half-down, plan ladder maps onto SaaS billing terms: list monthly price as the reference, annual prepay at a discount, then quarterly or monthly billing on an annual commitment, then month-to-month. His roughly 20% prepay incentive ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)) sits at the top of the commonly used annual-discount range, which is a practitioner convention of about 15 to 20% that was not sourced. **[VENDOR]** The retention case for pushing annual terms is sourced: annual plans show 10 to 20 points higher median net revenue retention than monthly plans ([ChartMogul, The AI churn wave, 10 Dec 2025](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/)).

### "Never the same thing for less" becomes tier and give-get discipline

**[INFERENCE]** The feature downsell maps to a lower tier or fewer seats; the terms downsell maps to a shorter commitment at a higher unit price. A discount on the identical package is given only in exchange for something (longer term, annual prepay, volume, a case study), under a written approval matrix. **[VENDOR]** One vendor's guardrail guidance says an AI sales agent should never negotiate custom pricing tiers, generate spontaneous discounts or alter standard payment terms ([YTT AI](https://www.ytt-ai.com/post/sales-ai-guardrails-what-your-agent-should-never-say)).

### Upsell offers become expansion motions

**[INFERENCE]** The classic upsell maps to cross-sell of an adjacent module; the menu upsell to a prescribed plan with unneeded add-ons removed; the anchor upsell to presenting the top tier first; the rollover upsell to crediting current spend toward an upgrade. His advice to pitch at the halfway to two-thirds point of delivery ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)) maps to expansion triggers such as usage approaching plan limits, a new team or use case, or a promoted champion. **[VENDOR]** Expansion supplies about 40% of new annual recurring revenue at the median in Benchmarkit's survey of 2024 data and wins at 45% against 19% for new business in Ebsta's ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf); [Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Continuity offers are the subscription itself

**[INFERENCE]** In SaaS, continuity is the core product. His continuity discount maps to multi-year or annual commitments, and the waived fee to waiving onboarding or setup fees for commitment. For consumers and sole traders the Swedish renewal-reminder rule applies ([Lag (2014:1449)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2014-1449.html)).

### Price raises become renewal uplifts

**[INFERENCE]** His six-part letter maps to a renewal notice: why now, value delivered (usage and outcomes from the customer's own account), what is coming, what the price protects, the increase in small units, and a route for hardship cases. **[FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** Nordic software companies averaged a 5.9% annual price increase in 2025 ([Monterro Nordic Software Benchmark 2026](https://www.monterro.com/benchmark-report-2026/)).

### Referrals and affiliates become partner programmes

**[INFERENCE]** His affiliate steps map to referral, reseller and integration partner programmes, with deal registration and separate tracking of partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue. **[VENDOR]** Partner referrals had the highest channel efficiency in Ebsta's 2024 data at 1.3 times the average ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Setter and closer become SDR and account executive, or one full-cycle role

**[INFERENCE]** His setter and closer split maps to the SDR and account-executive split; at low contract values or inbound-heavy volume a single full-cycle agent does both. **[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Full-cycle selling was at 45 to 46% adoption in Ebsta's report, and 38% of account executives had sole or shared ownership of renewals and expansion in the Bridge Group's 2026 survey ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en); [Bridge Group 2026 AE report, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2026-ae-models-motions-metrics)).

### Where the house style stops fitting: multi-stakeholder deals

**[CONTESTED]** The one-call close and the single-decision-maker objection model do not fit committee purchases. Forrester's 2026 survey of nearly 18,000 buyers reports an average of 13 internal and 9 external influencers, with procurement a decision-maker in 53% of buying cycles ([Forrester blog, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/state-of-business-buying-2026); [Forrester press release, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/)). **[INFERENCE]** In those deals the authority bucket stops being a partner objection and becomes stakeholder mapping; the avoidance bucket is handled with the indecision playbook; and closing techniques are replaced by mutual next steps (sections 13 to 15).

### Mapping by segment

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Owner-operator and small-business B2B (one or two decision-makers, days to weeks) | Speed to lead, full framework, setter and closer flow, stage metrics | Authority handling for a business partner; anchors on real list prices | Quick four-field qualifier; interactive demo; clear next step ([GTMnow, Mar 2022](https://gtmnow.com/evolution-sales-welcome-neat-selling/); [Gong Labs, Jul 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/driving-next-steps-isnt-enough-this-is-what-really-moves-deals-forward/)) | Sole traders treated as natural persons for email consent ([Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)) |
| SaaS self-serve (roughly under $5k a year) | Attraction offer, Reinforce as activation, continuity, upsell timing | No live close; agent acts as sales-assist chat | Product-qualified thresholds ([ProductLed on PQLs, 2021, updated Jun 2024](https://productled.com/blog/how-to-define-pqls)) | AI disclosure in chat; online withdrawal function for consumers ([artificialintelligenceact.eu, Article 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/); [CMS Wistrand, 15 Jun 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/new-withdrawal-button-required-on-swedish-e-commerce-platforms-from-19-june-2026)) |

| Mid-market (roughly $25k to $100k, three to six stakeholders) | Discovery sequence, obstacle surfacing before price, stage metrics | Authority bucket becomes champion and economic-buyer access; no closing techniques | Lightweight MEDDICC record, multi-threading, trial or proof ([MEDDICC.com history page](https://meddicc.com/resources/who-created-meddic); [Gong, Jan 2026](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-sales-insights-of-2025)) | Quotes approved by a human before sending ([Avtalslag (1915:218)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1915218-om-avtal-och-andra-rattshandlingar_sfs-1915-218/)) |
| Enterprise (over roughly $100k, ten or more stakeholders) | Problem-first discovery, proof-driven conviction, Reinforce as structured onboarding | Most of the close; pricing presentation becomes options with objective criteria | Full MEDDPICC, influence map, team selling, indecision playbook at decision ([Rework on Miller Heiman](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/pipeline-management/miller-heiman-strategic-selling); [Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)) | Procurement, data processing agreement, sanctions screening (section 30) |
