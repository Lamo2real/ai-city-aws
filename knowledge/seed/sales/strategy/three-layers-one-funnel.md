**When to use this:** When planning how the sales department works end to end or choosing a sales motion.

**Source section:** 1. Strategy overview: three layers, one funnel, speed and proof over pressure

### The three-layer rule that governs every agent decision

**[INFERENCE]** The department runs on three layers in strict order. Layer one is compliance: outreach consent rules, Swedish consumer contract rules, EU unfair-practice law, AI disclosure and data protection (sections 27 to 31). Layer two is independent evidence on what works in sales conversations and with 2025 to 2026 buyers (sections 13 to 16). Layer three is the house style, Hormozi's structures, which fills every space the first two layers leave open (sections 2 to 10). A worker agent picks the house-style move by default, checks whether an evidence rule changes it for this deal size or buyer state, and checks whether a compliance gate blocks it for this country, recipient type and channel. Section 11 lists the known collisions and says which rule wins in each.

### Design for how buyers actually buy in 2025 to 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Buyers do most of their work before and without sellers, then want a human for validation. Gartner's survey of 646 B2B buyers (fielded August to September 2025, published 9 March 2026) found 67% prefer a rep-free experience ([Gartner press release, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience)), and Gartner's related release on 645 buyers found 69% prefer to validate AI-generated insights with a sales rep ([Gartner press release, 20 May 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-survey-finds-sixty-nine-percent-of-b-two-b-buyers-turn-to-sales-reps-to-validate-ai-generated-insights)). 6sense's 2025 report on more than 4,000 buyers found 94% of buying groups rank preferred vendors before first seller contact and that the pre-contact favourite wins about 77 to 80% of the time ([6sense via Business Wire, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112018032/en/)). **[INFERENCE]** The strategic consequence is that the department earns revenue in three places: being fast and precise when a buyer raises a hand, being present and credible before the buying window opens (warm routes, proof that third parties can validate), and helping a hesitant buyer decide safely in the middle of the journey. Late persuasion of an account that already favours a rival has low odds.

### Choose the sales motion by deal complexity, not by fashion

**[DURABLE]** Sources agree that deal complexity (number of stakeholders, cycle length, customisation) determines method. Rackham's research on more than 35,000 sales calls found that small sales need little deliberation while large sales involve many people over months, and that closing pressure which works in small sales hurts in large ones ([Sales Society, Aug 2022](https://salessociety.substack.com/p/the-fascinating-science-of-spin-selling)). Practitioner guidance says heavy frameworks such as MEDDPICC are overkill for deals with one or two decision-makers and that hybrids are acceptable if the rules are written down ([Demodesk, Jul 2026](https://demodesk.ai/blog/sales-qualification-frameworks-how-to-choose); [MEDDICC.com framework comparison](https://meddicc.com/resources/meddicc-versus-other-qualification-frameworks-like-bant)). **[INFERENCE]** The house style is at its strongest in single-decision-maker, short-cycle sales (consumers, owner-operators, small businesses) and needs the adaptations in section 12 once a deal involves a buying committee.

### Run one funnel from first touch to expansion

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Winning by Design's Bowtie Standard defines one data model across product-led, AI-led and human-led motions: Awareness, Education, Prioritization, Selection, Mutual Commit, Onboarding, Retention and Adoption, and Expansion, each with a volume, a conversion and a time metric ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)). **[VENDOR]** Expansion deals win at 45% in 52 days against 19% in 91 days for new business in Ebsta and Pavilion's 2024 pipeline data on 655,000 opportunities ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). **[INFERENCE]** Post-sale stages therefore belong in the same pipeline, with owners and service levels, and the house style's Reinforce step and upsell timing slot into the right-hand side of that funnel.

### Map the manager agent and worker agents onto proven roles

**[INFERENCE]** The manager agent maps to Hormozi's sales director plus a revenue-operations function: it owns the scripts and playbooks, reviews transcripts against a rubric, tracks stage metrics, enforces gates and retires weak script variants. Worker agents map to setters or SDRs (speed to lead, qualification, booking, reminders), closers or account executives (discovery, offer, objections, close), a sales-engineer role (technical answers and demos) and a customer-success role (onboarding, health, renewal and expansion). The funnel to instrument end to end is leads, contacted, booked, showed, offered, closed and cash collected, extended on the post-sale side by onboarded, retained and expanded. This mapping is the researchers' synthesis of the Hormozi notes and the pipeline notes, not a sourced design.

### Compete on speed, relevance and truth, because volume is the losing game

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** The measurable openings for an AI department are on the inbound and warm side. In Clay's September 2026 test of 6,346 B2B demo and contact forms only 32% received any email reply and about 5% of replies arrived within five minutes ([Clay, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/claygent-experiment-speed-to-lead)). On the cold side the yields are low and falling: Smartlead's H1 2026 data on more than 850 million emails shows a median reply of 0.74% per contact ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)), and Belkins' managed campaigns fell from 0.50% reply per email in H1 2025 to 0.40% in H2 2025 ([Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents should spend their advantage on research depth per prospect and on response speed, keep cold volumes low, and treat every claim as something the buyer's own AI will check.

### Measure against your own trailing data first

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** The Bowtie paper states that self-benchmarking over the trailing 12 months is more accurate for operating decisions than industry benchmarks ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** Every benchmark in this document is a starting reference for a new team; once the department has its own stage data, the manager agent should compare against its own history by segment and motion and use the external figures only as a sanity check.
