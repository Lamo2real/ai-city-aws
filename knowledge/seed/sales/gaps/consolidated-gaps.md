**Source section:** 32. Consolidated gaps the knowledge base must not paper over

### Gaps in the house-style material

**[GAP]** Not verified on primary sources: the official list of his closes; any numeric follow-up cadence or no-show sequence; one-call versus two-call guidance by price; the money-model offer scripts (read only through a third-party summary); the offer-stack presentation and guarantee types from his first book; his policy on refund requests; the contents of the paid workshop handbook; and any SaaS or enterprise guidance from him. No independent study of his system's effectiveness exists in the sources read, and all his numbers are self-reported.

### Gaps in methodology and conversation evidence

**[GAP]** No independent replication of the SPIN, Challenger or JOLT research; no empirical comparison of qualification frameworks; no sourced material on Solution Selling, Value Selling or Voss-style negotiation; no evidence on mutual action plans, proposal format or discount ceilings; no conversation-analytics research from anyone but Gong; no test of whether conversation metrics transfer to AI-agent chat or email; and no research-grade source on B2C high-ticket selling.

### Gaps in pipeline, metrics and SaaS benchmarks

**[GAP]** No primary stage-by-stage conversion benchmarks; no win rate or cycle by deal size; no pipeline-coverage or forecast-accuracy norm; no discount-rate benchmark; no time-to-value, health-score or referral-conversion benchmark; no partner commission norms; and thin European data, with one Nordic benchmark that lacks win rates and attainment. The ACV bands, stage exit criteria, formulas' rule-of-thumb thresholds and compensation conventions are unsourced practitioner defaults.

### Gaps in prospecting, inbound and AI evidence

**[GAP]** No independent study of intent-data accuracy; no robust Nordic outbound benchmarks; no controlled study of multichannel lift; no voicemail, SMS or direct-mail evidence; no sourced safe limits for mailboxes per domain or calls per number; no B2C lead-handling evidence; no modern open-method study linking response speed to revenue; no data on whether AI-agent responses convert as well as human ones; and no independent comparison of AI and human SDR pipeline.

### Gaps in the legal research

**[GAP]** Not confirmed on primary sources: whether the Swedish email opt-in covers named employees' work addresses; amendments to the Marketing Act after 2022; Danish and Dutch B2B email rules and Danish call rules; the new Consumer Credit Act's detail; the content of the Swedish B2B telemarketing code and the anti-corruption code's benefit thresholds; Swedish regulator guidance on call recording; the AI Act amending regulation and Swedish enforcement of Article 50; the status of the Digital Fairness Act, the EU late-payment proposal and the EU-US data framework; and all points marked "unverified, not fetched". The outcome of the Swedish telephone-sales inquiry is due on 15 January 2027.
