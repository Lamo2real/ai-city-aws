**When to use this:** Before selling to anyone in Sweden: phone sales to consumers, written confirmation, email, credit and payment plans, prices.

**Source section:** 28. Compliance: Sweden in detail

### Consumer telephone sales: identity, purpose and written acceptance

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Under the Swedish Distance Contracts Act (2 kap. 3 a §, consolidated through SFS 2026:1018), when the trader calls on its own initiative the caller must state at the start the trader's identity, the purpose, the caller's name and relation to the trader; the offer must be confirmed in writing or another durable form; and the contract is concluded only when the consumer accepts in writing after the call, failing which it is invalid ([Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)). The Consumer Agency (page reviewed 6 September 2026) adds that acceptance during the call, for example by BankID, makes the contract invalid; the rule covers new and existing customers; hidden-number calls breach good marketing practice; the principal is primarily liable and a hired call centre can be liable as accessory; phone sales of pension-related services are banned; and a consumer may access an existing audio file of the call ([Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)). The written-acceptance rule also applies when the trader prompted the consumer to call, and not when the consumer calls unprompted ([Konsumentverket, consumer page](https://www.konsumentverket.se/konsumentratt/telefonforsaljning-regler/)).

### The NIX register

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Companies calling consumers for sales, marketing or fundraising should scrub lists against the NIX register first; a cleared number may be called for two months from the register version date; existing customers need not be checked, though the register suggests removing them anyway; web scrubbing costs 17 öre per number ([NIX-Telefon för företag](https://www.nixtelefon.org/for-foretag)). **[INFERENCE]** Consumer cold calling in Sweden is opt-out today; B2B calls sit outside the register, which defines consumers as natural persons acting outside business, but the B2B industry code and the good-practice standard still apply.

### The Swedish industry code for consumer telephone sales

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** The code issued by Kontakta and SWEDMA (1 July 2021) sets calling hours of weekdays 08 to 21 and weekends 10 to 18 with none on Christmas, Easter or Midsummer Eve; a maximum of two calls a day per number per campaign and 20 in 30 days; no silent calls; honest identification with a callable number; offer and acceptance recorded with consent and kept six months; the contract sent within five working days; and an existing-customer exception covering customers of the past 12 months ([Kontakta/SWEDMA ethical rules, 1 Jul 2021, PDF copy](https://telekomradgivarna.se/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/etiska-regler-for-forsaljning-och-marknadsforing-over-telefon-till-konsument-2021-07-01.pdf)). Kontakta also publishes rules for remote customer communication and, with a trade federation, a B2B telemarketing code (checked on primary source for existence only, [Kontakta](https://www.kontakta.se/etiska-regler-for-forsaljning-och-marknadforing-over-telefon-till-konsument); [SWEDMA rules index](https://www.swedma.se/radgivning-2/regler-riktlinjer/)). **[GAP]** The B2B telemarketing code's content was not opened.

### Conduct the Consumer Agency expects on calls

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Consumer Agency expects sellers to state the sales purpose first with no survey pretext, respect a wish to end the call, give reasonable time to reflect and make no exaggerated claims; its 2023 review of credit brokers found all of these breached. It ties false "free" claims to blacklist point 20 and payment demands without a valid order to point 29 ([Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)).

### A ban or opt-in for consumer telephone sales is under inquiry

**[FAST-MOVING, Jul 2026; checked on primary source]** On 9 July 2026 the government appointed an expert to assess strengthening the blocking-register model, a prior-consent requirement, and a full or sector-limited ban on consumer telephone sales initiated by the trader; the report is due 15 January 2027 ([Regeringen press release, 9 Jul 2026](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/07/forbud-mot-telefonforsaljning-ska-utredas/); [Regeringen committee directive, Jul 2026](https://regeringen.se/rattsliga-dokument/kommittedirektiv/2026/07/ett-starkt-konsumentskydd-vid-telefonforsaljning/)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2024-2026; secondary source only]** The Consumer Agency proposed in summer 2025 either a total ban or a French-style opt-in, the register covers about 3 million numbers, and the agency earlier called for a ban on phone sales of credit ([Realtid, 12 Aug 2026](https://www.realtid.se/juridik/telefonforsaljning-kan-forbjudas-i-sverige-frankrike-har-redan-gjort-det/); [SVT, 12 Oct 2024](https://www.svt.se/nyheter/inrikes/konsumentverket-vill-se-hardare-tag-mot-telefonforsaljning)). **[GAP]** The inquiry's outcome is unknown until January 2027.

### Marketing Act: aggressive marketing, identification and price offers

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Section 7 bans aggressive marketing, and the practices in Annex I of Directive 2005/29/EC are always unfair. Section 9 requires all marketing to be identifiable as marketing and to show who is responsible. Section 12 requires an offer with a price to include main characteristics, price, trader identity and address, and withdrawal information. Section 18 allows comparative advertising only if it is not misleading, is objective and does not discredit. Section 20 a bars emails that urge visits to sites whose marketing breaches section 9. The market disturbance fee runs from SEK 10,000 up to 4% of turnover ([Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)).

### Distance contracts: pre-contract information and the 14-day withdrawal right

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Distance Contracts Act requires pre-contract information (identity, main characteristics, total price and fees, payment and delivery, withdrawal right and who pays returns, duration and termination terms). The withdrawal period is 14 days; for services it runs from the contract date; it never starts before withdrawal information is given and ends at the latest one year after it would otherwise have expired. Exceptions include custom goods, fully performed services with express consent, digital content started with consent, and dated leisure services ([Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)).

### The online withdrawal function

**[FAST-MOVING, Jun 2026; statute checked on primary source, date from secondary source]** A new provision (2 kap. 10 a §) requires that for contracts concluded through an online interface the trader provide a withdrawal function that is easy to reach for the whole period and clearly labelled, with a confirmation step and a receipt without delay ([Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)). It applies from 19 June 2026 under Directive (EU) 2023/2673, may sit behind a login, creates no new withdrawal right, and its absence can be treated as unfair marketing ([CMS Wistrand, 15 Jun 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/new-withdrawal-button-required-on-swedish-e-commerce-platforms-from-19-june-2026)).

### Automatic renewal of consumer contracts

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** For fixed-term consumer contracts that renew unless terminated, the trader must send a written reminder at least one month before the last termination date; without it the consumer may terminate immediately after renewal. The rule does not apply if the consumer can exit within three months after renewal, and it protects consumers only ([Lag (2014:1449)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2014-1449.html)). **[INFERENCE]** B2B auto-renewal clauses are enforceable subject to the general reasonableness clause of contract law; long silent renewals to small businesses carry adjustment risk.

### The new Consumer Credit Act from 20 November 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, Nov 2026; checked on primary source]** The Riksdag adopted the new act on 27 May 2026; it and its consequential amendments enter into force on 20 November 2026 and implement Directive (EU) 2023/2225 ([Riksdagen, bet. 2025/26:CU26](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/_HD01CU26/)). The government's summary of 30 March 2026: more credit types are covered, including interest-free credit offered in stores by a third party; creditworthiness assessment is stricter; the withdrawal period starts only when withdrawal information is received; lenders must offer forbearance options before collection; and more credit providers and brokers need authorisation ([Regeringen, 30 Mar 2026](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/03/regeringen-foreslar-ny-konsumentkreditlag-for-starkt-skydd-for-konsumenter/)). **[GAP]** The act's statute number, marketing rules, cost caps and exact scope for deferred invoices were not confirmed.

### Price information and the 30-day prior-price rule

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Price Information Act applies to traders offering products to consumers. Price means the final price including VAT and other taxes; extra fees are stated separately; goods also need a unit price. Under section 7 a, a price-reduction claim must show the lowest price applied in the 30 days before the cut. Breaches are handled under the Marketing Act ([Prisinformationslag (2004:347)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/prisinformationslag-2004347_sfs-2004-347/)).

### Consumer sales, consumer services and contract law basics

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** These points come from background knowledge and were not checked on the statutes. Consumer Sales Act (2022:260): mandatory in the consumer's favour; the trader is liable for defects existing at delivery for three years; defects appearing within two years are presumed original; digital content is covered ([Konsumentköplag (2022:260)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konsumentkoplag-2022260_sfs-2022-260/)). Consumer Services Act (1985:716): professional performance, a duty to advise against work that does not benefit the consumer, a reasonable price if none is agreed, and approximate quotes exceeded by at most 15% ([Konsumenttjänstlag (1985:716)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konsumenttjanstlag-1985716_sfs-1985-716/)). Contracts Act (1915:218): an offer binds the offeror during the acceptance period; an acceptance with changes is a rejection plus a new offer; section 36 allows unreasonable terms to be adjusted; sections 30 and 33 cover fraud and bad faith ([Avtalslag (1915:218)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1915218-om-avtal-och-andra-rattshandlingar_sfs-1915-218/)).
