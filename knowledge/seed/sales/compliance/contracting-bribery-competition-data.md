**When to use this:** Before a contract step, a gift or referral incentive, a competitor comparison, or handling personal data.

**Source section:** 30. Compliance: contracting, anti-bribery, competition and data handling

### A quote can bind the company

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** Under the Swedish Contracts Act offers bind, a late or modified acceptance counts as a new offer, and the reasonableness clause also applies between businesses, though more restrictively ([Avtalslag (1915:218)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1915218-om-avtal-och-andra-rattshandlingar_sfs-1915-218/)). **[INFERENCE]** An agent that sends a quote can commit the company. Agents label drafts as non-binding indications unless a human has approved the quote, and every quote carries a validity date and a reference to the standard terms.

### Data processing agreements, e-signatures and late payment

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** A written contract with defined content is required whenever a processor handles personal data for a controller (GDPR Article 28, [GDPR on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)). An electronic signature cannot be denied legal effect solely for being electronic, and only a qualified electronic signature equals a handwritten one ([Regulation (EU) No 910/2014](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj)). On late payment, Swedish law implementing Directive 2011/7/EU requires express agreement for payment terms between businesses over 30 days, sets statutory interest at the reference rate plus eight points and provides fixed compensation of SEK 450 ([Directive 2011/7/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/7/oj); [Räntelag (1975:635)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rantelag-1975635_sfs-1975-635/)). **[FAST-MOVING, Jul 2024; secondary source only]** The Commission's 2023 proposal for a strict 30-day cap stalled and was reported as likely to be withdrawn ([GTR, 24 Jul 2024](https://www.gtreview.com/news/europe/exclusive-eu-to-scrap-late-payment-reforms-after-industry-backlash/)). **[GAP]** Its current status and any Swedish bill capping B2B payment terms were not confirmed.

### Sanctions and export control

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** EU restrictive measures bind all EU persons and bar making funds or economic resources available to listed parties; dual-use items fall under Regulation (EU) 2021/821; US rules can reach non-US sellers of US-origin items or dollar transactions ([EU Sanctions Map](https://www.sanctionsmap.eu/); [Regulation (EU) 2021/821](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/821/oj); [OFAC search](https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/)). **[INFERENCE]** A sanctions and ownership screen belongs in the lead-qualification gate, before a quote, not at invoicing.

### Gifts, hospitality and anti-bribery

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Swedish business anti-corruption code is issued by the Swedish Anti-Corruption Institute, complements the legislation, requires preventive work and gives guidance on benefits and intermediaries; the current version was adopted on 10 June 2020 and applies from 14 August 2020 ([Institutet Mot Mutor, Näringslivskoden](https://www.institutetmotmutor.se/naringslivskoden/)). **[DURABLE; secondary source only]** The 2020 revision added expectations of management-led prevention and third-party management and calls for special care toward the public sector ([Tidningen Balans](https://tidningenbalans.se/nyheter/imm-uppdaterar-naringslivskoden-korruptionsrisker-finns-i-alla-organisationer/)). **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The Swedish Criminal Code covers giving and taking bribes and trading in influence ([Brottsbalk (1962:700)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/brottsbalk-1962700_sfs-1962-700/)); the UK Bribery Act creates strict corporate liability for failing to prevent bribery by associated persons and applies to any organisation carrying on business in the UK ([Bribery Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/23/contents)); the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act bars corrupt payments to foreign officials, with third-party agents a main risk ([DOJ FCPA unit](https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-fraud/foreign-corrupt-practices-act)). **[GAP]** The code's section on permissible benefits was not opened and no value thresholds were confirmed.

### Competition law limits on sales talk

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** Swedish and EU competition law ban price-fixing, market sharing, bid rigging and exchange of competitively sensitive information, with fines up to 10% of turnover; resale price maintenance imposed on distributors is a hardcore restriction ([Konkurrenslag (2008:579)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konkurrenslag-2008579_sfs-2008-579/); [Article 101 TFEU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:12008E101)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents never discuss prices, customers or bids with competitors, must not ingest or relay competitors' non-public prices, and need a dated, verifiable source for any claim about a competitor.

### Bought, enriched and scraped lead data

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Publicly available contact data still requires data-protection compliance, and objectors go on a suppression list ([ICO, B2B marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)); the balancing test must be documented ([IMY, Intresseavvägning, updated 15 Jun 2026](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/rattslig-grund/intresseavvagning/)). **[DURABLE; secondary source only]** Dutch regulators have fined organisations for buying marketing lists without verifying the consents in them ([iGDPR](https://www.igdpr.eu/en/gdpr-email-marketing-consent/)). **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The French authority fined a contact-data vendor EUR 240,000 in December 2024 for harvesting LinkedIn contact details that users had restricted ([CNIL on Kaspr](https://www.cnil.fr/en/data-scraping-kaspr-fined-eu240000)); LinkedIn's user agreement prohibits scraping and bots regardless of what data-protection law permits ([LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement)).

### Lead-record fields, retention and transfers

**[INFERENCE]** Each lead record should carry source, collection date, lawful basis, Article 14 notice date, country and recipient type so that agents can gate on them. A workable retention default is to delete or anonymise prospects with no engagement after a fixed, documented period while keeping a minimal suppression record indefinitely. **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** GDPR sets no statutory CRM period; the controller must set and justify one; international transfers need an adequacy decision or standard clauses with an impact assessment ([GDPR on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj); [European Commission, EU-US data transfers](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/eu-us-data-transfers_en)). **[GAP]** No regulator-set retention period for B2B prospects was found for Sweden, and the current status of the EU-US framework was not checked.
