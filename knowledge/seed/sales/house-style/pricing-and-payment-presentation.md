**When to use this:** When presenting price, payment options, guarantees or the offer stack at the close.

**Source section:** 7. House style: pricing and payment presentation at the close

### Anchor high, then step down through terms

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He sets the first number high enough to surprise and then steps down; his example is naming $100,000 before $10,000 (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[DURABLE]** Academic negotiation research supports opening first with a credible anchor and warns that extreme single numbers can read as greedy and damage trust ([Harvard Program on Negotiation, Mar 2026](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/anchoring-bias-negotiation-get-ahead-range-offer/)). Section 11 states the rule agents follow: the anchor must be a real, purchasable price or option.

### The payment ladder: full price, prepay, half down, plan

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He quotes the full price, then offers about 20% off for paying in full, about 10% off for half down, then a payment plan, and rolls the plan into automatic recurring billing at the end. His worked example is $99 a week for 45 weeks (about $4,500), about $3,600 prepaid and about $4,050 with half down ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The point-of-sale order for agents is: anchor on the premium or full price, prepay incentive, half down, payment plan, feature downsell or conditional trial, then continuity. Consumer payment plans in Sweden fall under the credit rules in section 28, and automatic rollover falls under the renewal rules there.

### Never sell the identical thing for less

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** The rule summarised from his 2025 book is that a downsell changes terms or features and never sells the identical thing for less ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). **[VENDOR]** This is consistent with Ebsta and Pavilion's 2024 data, in which top performers cut negotiation time by 24% (49% on expansion deals) by not discounting ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)), and with the JOLT finding that discounting a wavering buyer tends to deepen indecision ([Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)).

### The diagnostic and prescription sale

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** For local businesses he describes a pre-sale questionnaire, collecting payment details up front, mapping current state, desired state and obstacle, then tying price to the outcome; he claims a lift in average customer value from about $200 to about $800 ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). The lift is unaudited. Collecting payment details before an agreement is restricted in section 11.

### Guarantees as a closing tool

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript and second-hand summary]** He uses a short no-penalty refund window of three days to defuse partner and "think about it" objections, and immediate onboarding to reduce remorse and refunds ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). **[VENDOR]** Gong's 2017 data associated risk-reversal language (opt-outs, guarantees, service levels) with a 32% higher win rate ([Gong, Apr 2017](https://www.gong.io/blog/winning-sales-conversations)). **[GAP]** No verified Hormozi policy on handling refund requests was found, and the guarantee types from his first book were not re-verified. Section 11 covers the interaction with the statutory 14-day withdrawal right.

### Presenting the offer stack

**[GAP]** Listing an offer's components and bonuses with their values before the price comes from his first book and was not re-verified on a primary source in this research, so it is not detailed here. Any stated value for a component must be truthful under the marketing rules in section 28.
