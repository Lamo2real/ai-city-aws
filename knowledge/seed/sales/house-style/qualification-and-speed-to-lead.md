**When to use this:** When a new lead arrives, when booking calls and when following up or chasing no-shows.

**Source section:** 5. House style: qualification, speed to lead, show rates and follow-up

### Qualification as he teaches it

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Setters qualify for the problem and for budget and do not quote price in a way that disqualifies people; closers do not pitch until they know why the prospect is there, the goal and the main pain ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[GAP]** No primary source was found in which he teaches BANT or a variant by that name. **[INFERENCE]** His implied qualification set is close to BANT without the label: need and pain (Clarify, Label), budget (the setter check and access to funds), authority (raising the partner question before price) and timing (the time obstacle). If the department uses an acronym checklist, present it as house terminology and not as his.

### Speed to lead: contact within about a minute

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** In a video of 8 March 2026 he is summarised as setting a target of contact inside 60 seconds, citing a 391% lift from fast contact, recommending one full-time person whose only job is calling leads as they arrive, and describing an owner closing about 55% of leads against a typical 10% ([Summify summary of 'How to Close More Leads', 8 Mar 2026](https://summify.io/discover/how-to-close-more-leads/)). These are his claims. **[VENDOR]** The 391% figure matches a 2012 vendor study by Velocify on about 3.5 million leads, which reported that calling within one minute raised conversion 391% ([Ainora study roundup, 2026](https://ainora.lt/blog/lead-response-time-statistics-every-study-2026)); treat it as directional. A second-hand index says he treated leads left for 30 minutes to several hours, and a failure to double-dial, as faults ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). Sections 11, 27 and 28 limit immediate calling by consent, register and calling-hour rules.

### Call new bookings immediately and be available when prospects are

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He teaches calling a new booking immediately to qualify and, if the prospect is free, running the sales conversation then. He says being available when prospects are, including evenings and weekends, was the strongest predictor of total sales across his companies (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). That statement is his own unaudited claim.

### Book close in time and pull appointments forward

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Same-day or next-day appointments show better than those booked five or more days out, and future appointments should be pulled forward into open same-day slots ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[VENDOR]** A single-company data point agrees: at Cognism, meetings booked more than four days out showed at about 60% in 2025 ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)).

### Personal reminders, not robotic ones

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Reminders should be a personalised voice memo or video from the setter or closer, including on the morning of the appointment, in preference to automated texts, with optional small personal pre-call touches ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** A second-hand index describes a three-way introduction in which a nurture specialist joined setter, closer and prospect to transfer rapport ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[INFERENCE]** For AI agents, "personal" means a reminder that references the prospect's stated goal and names who they will meet; AI-generated voice messages fall under the automated-calling and AI-disclosure rules in sections 27 and 31.

### Always book a meeting from a meeting

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** No conversation ends without a scheduled next step ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[VENDOR]** Gong's 2021 analysis of 8,382 deals found deals where next steps were never discussed closed at 5% against 20% where they were ([Gong Labs, Jul 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/driving-next-steps-isnt-enough-this-is-what-really-moves-deals-forward/)).

### Between-call work and the priority list

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He prescribes a separate off-call checklist for working leads, replacing non-responders with responsive leads, and keeping a visible priority list of high-value prospects to pursue ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Show-rate and setter benchmarks he uses

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** He is summarised as using about 70% as the show-rate benchmark for appointment businesses and about three booked appointments a day per setter, having raised one team's target from two to four ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** His case claims, unaudited: one team moved show rate from 49% to about 70%, close rate from 27% to 41%, upfront cash from 47% to 82% and monthly sales from 56 to 93 in about 60 days; a second moved first-call show from 74% to 91%, the share booking the close call from 53% to 94% and close-call show from 63% to 87% ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Lead quality is a marketing metric

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** He is summarised as telling teams to optimise advertising for cost per sale and not the cheapest lead, citing a team that was attracting the wrong age group ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[INFERENCE]** The manager agent reports cost per sale and disqualification reasons by lead source back to the marketing department.

### What was not found on follow-up and no-shows

**[GAP]** No verified Hormozi source gives a numeric follow-up cadence (attempts, days, channel mix) or a scripted no-show or no-sale follow-up sequence; a third-party video on show-rate tactics exists but its summary would not load ([Recapio listing](https://recapio.com/digest/24-ways-alex-hormozi-increased-show-up-rate-by-stefan-van-de-vlasakker)). A commonly repeated "four pillars of lead nurture" label (availability, speed, personalisation, volume) is consistent with the verified items but was not found on a primary page. The free training page lists no standalone lead-nurture playbook as of 11 November 2026 ([acquisition.com/training, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/training)). Use the evidence-based cadences in sections 24 and 25 for the numbers.
