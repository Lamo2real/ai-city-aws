His books page lists three books and no upcoming title ([acquisition.com/books, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/books)). The main free sales trainings are the three videos cited throughout sections 2 to 7 ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework); [Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process); [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). 2026 sales content found consists of a March video on closing more leads, a social video of eight closes and a September podcast episode, all read second-hand ([Summify summary of 'How to Close More Leads', 8 Mar 2026](https://summify.io/discover/how-to-close-more-leads/); [BAM, 11 Jun 2026](https://nowbam.com/8-closes-from-alex-hormozi-that-work-for-real-estate-agents/); [Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)).

### Which business types his examples cover

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Most of his worked examples are consumer and small-business services: gyms, clinics, high-ticket coaching and services sold to small-business owners; his objection examples are owner-operator objections ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM]** His workshop page names home services, med spas, law, real estate, B2B software and construction as industries served ([acquisition.com Sales Workshop page, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/workshop-sales)). **[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** For a consumer education brand in September 2026 he is summarised as advising free content for reach, a paid intensive tier, a weekly webinar in place of one-to-one messages and a two-tier checkout ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). **[GAP]** No verified primary source gives SaaS-specific sales guidance from him (demo structure, trials, seat expansion); a claim that his software company reached $1.7 million a month through tiered affiliates is second-hand and unverified ([Deciphr summary of Ep 595](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-10--affiliates-and-partners-ep-595)), and facts about his involvement with the Skool platform come from a weak third-party page that cites no primary sources ([SkoolPrep, Apr-Aug 2026](https://skoolprep.com/alex-hormozi-skool)).

### Criticisms and limits

**[VENDOR]** An AI sales software vendor's guide of 22 July 2026 argues that CLOSER moves too fast for six-month consultative enterprise cycles, fits poorly with multi-stakeholder and relationship-led deals and commodity buyers, and does not cover cold outreach ([11x guide, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.11x.ai/guides/alex-hormozi-sales-training-method)); read it as opinion from an interested party. **[DURABLE]** Independently of Hormozi, James Muir, citing Rackham's research, argues that classic closing techniques help more in small sales and are counter-productive in large complex ones ([Top Sales World, 25 Mar 2020](https://topsalesworld.com/why-closing-gambits-dont-work-on-large-sales)). **[GAP]** No reliable documented complaints or regulatory actions about his own sales practices were found, and no rigorous independent study of CLOSER's effectiveness exists in the sources read.

### Tactics that need guardrails before agents use them

**[INFERENCE]** The researchers flag these house-style tactics as needing guardrails, as risk flags and not findings of wrongdoing: acting first and seeking forgiveness on partner objections; resourcefulness pushes with financially stretched consumers; unlimited looping; the trial with penalty and card-on-file upsells, which need clear disclosure and consent; disabling comments on price-raise notices; and contacting leads within 60 seconds at volume, which meets telemarketing and consent rules. Section 11 resolves each one.
