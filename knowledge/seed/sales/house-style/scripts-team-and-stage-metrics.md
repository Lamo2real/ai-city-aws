**When to use this:** When writing scripts, coaching, structuring setter and closer roles or diagnosing a broken sales process.

**Source section:** 6. House style: scripts, team system and stage metrics

### Scripts are question-based, memorised and recorded

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Question-based scripts make performance transferable and auditable, and recording every call keeps reps on script and compliant (3 December 2020, [Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). Stories and obstacle responses are memorised, with flashcard drilling of objection responses ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework); [Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). **[INFERENCE]** For agents, the script is the playbook prompt and the retrieval set of approved stories and responses; "memorised, not improvised" becomes "retrieved from the approved set, not generated freely".

### Daily training and the daily huddle

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He describes daily training of about 60 minutes, five days a week: roughly 25 minutes reading the script aloud, a few minutes drawing out obstacles, then role-play, plus recording reviews (2 June 2021, [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). The daily huddle reads customer testimonials aloud to sustain belief through rejection ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)).

### Weekly one-to-one on three tagged calls

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Each week the rep submits three tagged calls (best, worst, average) and the manager checks four things: whether the problem was clarified, whether past attempts and pain were overviewed, whether the sale was asked for, and whether objections were handled ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). He also reviews hot streaks, not only failures, and uses recordings to train new reps ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The manager agent's transcript rubric starts from those four checks, extended with the compliance checks in section 31.

### Competition, leaderboards and removing the bottom performers

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He runs a daily leaderboard, a close alert, six-week contests, three-person teams and a prize budget of around 25% of a month's pay per head, and removes the bottom 10% on a fixed rhythm, claiming roughly a 30% productivity jump each time ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). The productivity figure is unaudited. **[INFERENCE]** For agents, the analogue is retiring or retraining the lowest-converting prompt or script variants on a fixed cycle; the human-resources practice itself is outside this knowledge base.

### Setter then closer, with a video between calls

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** In his 2024 case the flow is a first call with a setter, then a close call booked the same or next day, with a video sales letter between calls covering benefits and pre-handling objections. Setters are paid on shows, not only bookings ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[GAP]** No verified statement was found of when he prefers a one-call close over a two-call close by price point.

### In-house over outsourced, and total sales cost under 10%

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He describes replacing an outsourced team paid 20% of revenue with an in-house team at about 9% all-in including the director; close rate fell from about 80% to 42% but total sales rose because far more prospects reached the close call. He targets total sales cost under 10% of revenue, pays setters and closers separately with closers paid more, and ratchets commission up with close rate, with illustrative tiers of 10%, 15% and 25% ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). All figures are his claims.

### Hire the director first and remove reps who will not follow the process

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He advises hiring one strong sales director first (experience in the same product or industry, metric-driven, coaching temperament), found by direct outreach or recruiter at a volume of hundreds to a thousand messages, who then builds the team; reps who will not follow the process are removed ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Stage metrics: show rate, offer rate, close rate, cash collected

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He tracks show rate, offer rate, close rate and cash collected, not close rate alone, and measures close rate against both shows and offers so that reps cannot inflate it by offering only to the easiest prospects. The best leads are routed to the best closers ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[INFERENCE]** The department's house funnel is leads, contacted, booked, showed, offered, closed and cash collected, with two close-rate views (per show and per offer) reported side by side.

### Diagnostic sequence for a broken sales process

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** His turnaround order is: secret-shop the process, find the constraint, anticipate objections, install the new process, then add recurring revenue ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The manager agent can run the same order on its own funnel: sample real conversations, find the stage with the worst conversion against history, fix that stage first.
