**Source section:** 1. Overview: what the Media department is for and how its knowledge is layered

### The three-layer rule that governs every media decision

**[INFERENCE]** The Media department runs on three layers in strict order. Layer one is rights and disclosure: consent for any real person's likeness or voice, copyright and licence scope for every component, AI disclosure under EU AI Act Article 50 and platform rules, advertising identification under Swedish marketing law, and data protection (sections 17 to 22). Layer two is evidence and official platform guidance: platform help pages, standards bodies and audience research (sections 5 to 12 and 16). Layer three is the house style, Hormozi's published content-production structures, which fill every space the first two layers leave open (section 2). A worker agent picks the house-style move by default, checks whether official guidance or evidence changes it for this platform and format, and checks whether a rights or disclosure gate blocks it. Section 3 lists the known collisions and says which rule wins.

### What the department produces and the one mechanism that drives it

**[INFERENCE]** The department produces video, audio, images and design for the company and delivers them packaged for each platform. The mechanism the notes support best is a single pipeline: one long-form recording is planned heavily in advance, recorded, cut into many platform-native short pieces, published on a steady cadence, measured, and the winning hooks are handed to Marketing for paid tests. This is a synthesis of Hormozi's primary statements and YouTube's analytics guidance, not a design any single source states ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed); [YouTube Help, audience retention, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en)).

### Map the manager agent and worker agents onto roles

**[INFERENCE]** The manager agent maps to a head of media plus a rights and quality gatekeeper: it classifies each brief by risk (people, claims, paid use, minors, regulated sector), picks tools by rights position first, enforces the review gates, maintains the rights log and the winners register, and reviews the approved tool list monthly. Worker agents map to scriptwriter and researcher, editor and clipper, designer and thumbnail maker, audio engineer, captioner and accessibility checker, generative-media operator, and packaging and delivery operator. A second-hand, vendor-sourced description of Hormozi's own team lists comparable roles (a social media producer per brand, short-form editors, a writer, a videographer, a chief media officer and an AI and automation specialist), with a headcount estimated from social posts at 15 to 25 or more as of April 2026 ([Clipflow, undated, covers to Apr 2026](https://clipflow-ig-analysis.pages.dev/hormozi/)). The mapping onto agents is the researchers' synthesis.

### Compete on clarity, proof and rights hygiene, not on effects

**[INFERENCE]** Three independent lines of material point the same way. Hormozi reports, as his own unaudited observation from April 2024, that his best-performing pieces stressed clear language and his worst stressed production ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). YouTube's own test tool rewards the title and thumbnail that earn the most watch time, which favours accurate packaging ([YouTube Help, A/B test titles and thumbnails, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)). Kantar's November 2025 analysis found that ads where generative AI was obvious did worse on branded cut-through ([Kantar, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.kantar.com/Inspiration/Advertising-Media/Rethinking-AI-generated-advertising)). The department should therefore spend its effort on the idea, the proof and the script, keep visuals functional, and treat rights and disclosure as part of quality.

### Measure against the company's own recent output first

**[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** YouTube compares a video's typical retention against the channel's own 10 latest videos of similar length, and advises judging click-through rate over the long term and by traffic source instead of reacting to small or early movements ([YouTube Help, audience retention, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en); [YouTube Help, impressions and click-through rate, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154?hl=en)). **[INFERENCE]** Every benchmark in this document is a starting reference for a new team. Once the department has its own data, the manager agent compares each piece against the company's own trailing output by platform and format and uses external figures only as a sanity check.
