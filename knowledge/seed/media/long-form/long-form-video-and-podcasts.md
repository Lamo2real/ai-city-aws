**When to use this:** When planning or editing a long-form video, webinar or podcast episode, reading the retention report, or cutting long-form into short-form.

**Source section:** 6. Long-form video and podcasts

### Structure of the opening

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The house-style opening for long-form is proof, promise, plan, as described in section 2 ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** YouTube measures the intro as the share of viewers still watching after the first 30 seconds, and its own advice for a weak intro is to align the title and thumbnail with the content or to experiment with the first 30 seconds ([YouTube Help, audience retention, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en)). **[INFERENCE]** The two fit together: the title and thumbnail make a promise, and the first 30 seconds must visibly keep it.

### Reading the YouTube retention report

**[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** YouTube's audience retention report is per video and data takes 1 to 2 days to process. "Top moments" are parts where almost nobody left; if they come late, YouTube advises moving strong material earlier. "Spikes" are parts that were rewatched or shared, which can signal interest or confusion. "Dips" are parts that were skipped or abandoned. A gradual decline is normal. Typical retention is compared against the channel's 10 latest videos of similar length. Key moments show only for videos at least 60 seconds long with at least 100 views ([YouTube Help, audience retention, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415?hl=en)).

**[INFERENCE]** Use the long-form retention graph as the clip-selection tool: spikes and rewatch points become short-form candidates, and dips mark material to cut from the next script. This matches the outside reading that Hormozi's channel treats long-form retention spikes as pre-validated clip moments ([OutlierKit, 10 Jul 2026](https://outlierkit.com/resources/alex-hormozi-growth-strategy/)).

### Video chapters

**[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** Manual chapters on YouTube are entered in the description. The first timestamp must be 00:00, there must be at least three timestamps in ascending order, and each chapter must last at least 10 seconds. Manual chapters override automatic chapters. Automatic chapters are allowed by default on new uploads and can be switched off per video or in upload defaults; not every video is eligible. Chapters are unavailable if the channel has active strikes or the content may be inappropriate for some viewers ([YouTube Help, video chapters, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en)).

**[INFERENCE]** Chapter checklist for the packaging agent: the description starts chapters at 00:00; three or more entries; none shorter than 10 seconds; ascending order; and a check after publishing that the chapters render.

### On-screen text and slides in long-form

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr-May 2024]** Hormozi says one of his best long-form videos is on-screen text for roughly 70 to 80% of its runtime, that visuals should clarify, and that slides versus no slides and camera versus stage made little difference in his data ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons); [Hormozi, "I Posted ONLY Business Content for 30 Days", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-posted-only-business-content-for-30-days-strategy-revealed)). These are his own unaudited observations.

### Cutting long-form into short-form

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand] [VENDOR]** The agency model in section 2 gives a yield of 4 to 5 mid-length clips and 10 to 15 short clips per 30 to 60 minute recording ([thinkdmg agency blog, 10 Mar 2025, updated Oct 2026](https://thinkdmg.com/the-alex-hormozi-content-strategy-how-to-repurpose-one-video-into-30-pieces/)). **[INFERENCE]** Treat the yield as a planning figure and not a quota: a clip earns publication only if it works as a complete content unit with its own hook and reward.

### Podcast audio files

**[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** Apple Podcasts accepts MP3 or AAC for RSS audio and strongly recommends AAC, at 128 to 256 kbps for stereo and 64 to 128 kbps for mono, at 44.1 or 48 kHz. Subscriber audio accepts WAV, FLAC or MP3; a single-channel WAV or FLAC is rejected, so a mono programme must be sent as two identical channels ([Apple Podcasts for Creators, audio requirements, read 11 Nov 2026](https://podcasters.apple.com/support/893-audio-requirements)). Loudness targets are in section 8.
