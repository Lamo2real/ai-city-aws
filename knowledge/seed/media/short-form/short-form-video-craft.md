**When to use this:** When scripting, cutting, testing or packaging a short-form video (Shorts, TikTok, Reels).

**Source section:** 5. Short-form video craft

### What is evidence and what is commentary

**[INFERENCE]** Most rules circulating about the first 3 seconds and pacing are vendor or commentator claims and not platform evidence. The strongest evidence in the notes concerns packaging: YouTube's native test decides on watch-time share, which supports accurate packaging over clickbait, and Hormozi independently reports that clear titles and message-first edits beat vague titles and heavy effects in his own data ([YouTube Help, A/B test titles and thumbnails, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en); [Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)).

### Hooks and pacing

**[VENDOR] [FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** An agency blog recommends a hook that states the payoff in the first 3 seconds, a pattern interrupt (B-roll, zoom, text or graphic) every 5 to 10 seconds, and always-on burned-in captions ([thinkdmg agency blog, 10 Mar 2025, updated Oct 2026](https://thinkdmg.com/the-alex-hormozi-content-strategy-how-to-repurpose-one-video-into-30-pieces/)). These are commentator rules with no platform citation. **[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Hormozi's own statements are narrower: about 80% of ad effort goes to the first five seconds and the headline (May 2024, [Hormozi, "13 Years of Marketing Advice in 85 Mins", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/13-years-of-marketing-advice-in-85-mins)), and hooks are studied in the first three seconds of organic content and competitors' ads (May 2025, [Hormozi, "14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes", auto-transcript, May 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/14-years-of-marketing-advice-in-35-minutes)).

**[GAP]** The notes contain no sourced guidance from YouTube on Shorts hooks and retention, no TikTok Creative Center statistics on first seconds, captions or sound-on viewing, no Instagram creator guidance and no independent study on captions and retention. Any figure for the share of viewers who watch without sound must be sourced before use. No evidence on series formats was found.

### Testing short-form hooks

**[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** YouTube's native title and thumbnail test cannot be used on Shorts ([YouTube Help, A/B test titles and thumbnails, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en)). **[INFERENCE]** Test Shorts hooks by publishing variants as separate posts and comparing retention, which matches the pattern an outside analysis attributes to Hormozi's channel, where Shorts act as cheap hook tests ([OutlierKit, 10 Jul 2026](https://outlierkit.com/resources/alex-hormozi-growth-strategy/)).

### Length for short-form

**[FAST-MOVING, 2 Nov 2026] [VENDOR]** YouTube Shorts run up to 3 minutes, and Instagram says reach to new audiences favours Reels under 3 minutes, as relayed by Sprout Social's compilation updated 2 November 2026 ([Sprout Social video specs guide, updated 2 Nov 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-video-specs-guide/)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** TikTok's in-feed ad page allows up to 10 minutes ([TikTok Ads Help, auction in-feed ads, read 11 Nov 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads)), and Sprout relays a recommendation of 21 to 34 seconds for TikTok feed ads ([Sprout Social video specs guide, updated 2 Nov 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-video-specs-guide/)). **[INFERENCE]** Keep a short under 60 seconds when one file must serve every vertical placement (section 7), and let the content unit, not a target duration, set the length.

### Thumbnails and covers for short-form

**[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** YouTube Shorts thumbnails are 2160×3840 pixels (9:16) with a minimum height of 640 pixels, in JPG or PNG. Custom Shorts thumbnails can be added only in YouTube Studio on a computer and only on a verified account. A vertical video with a 16:9 custom thumbnail gets an auto-generated 4:5 thumbnail on the home, explore and subscriptions pages ([YouTube Help, custom thumbnails, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2 Nov 2026] [VENDOR]** Sprout's compilation lists an Instagram Reels cover of 420×654 pixels ([Sprout Social video specs guide, updated 2 Nov 2026](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-video-specs-guide/)).
