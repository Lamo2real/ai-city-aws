**When to use this:** When passing work to or requesting work from another department (Marketing, Sales, Support, Product, Application, IT, Data and AI, Finance, Legal, Operations, Research).

**Source section:** 24. Handoffs between Media and the other departments

### How to read the handoff table

**[INFERENCE]** None of the handoffs below is sourced; they are the researchers' and the report writer's synthesis of the workflows described in this document. The one sourced idea behind the Marketing and Sales rows is Hormozi's: organic content tests hooks for paid ads, and questions heard on sales calls are raw material for content ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)). The table covers the departments the owner listed. Media appears in the list as the department itself, so its row describes the internal handoff between the manager agent and worker agents.

| Department | Media receives (inference) | Media hands over (inference) |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Briefs with audience, single promise, proof, call to action, platforms, deadline, approver and success metric; campaign calendar; paid-test results | Finished and packaged assets per platform; the winners register with top organic hooks for paid tests; rights-log status for each asset, including whether it is cleared for paid use; the disclosure and advertising-marker decision |
| Sales | Questions and objections heard on calls; requests for demo clips, case-study videos and presentation design | Sales collateral, demo and testimonial videos with consent records; clips matched to common questions |
| Customer Support | Recurring support questions; requests for how-to videos and screenshots | Captioned tutorials with transcripts; annotated images with alt text |
| Product | Roadmap and release dates; accurate product information; approved pack shots and interface captures | Launch videos, product imagery and demos checked against the real product; flags where a brief asks media to show something the product does not do |
| Application | Embedding requirements for the website and app; player and image-format constraints | Web-ready files (AVIF with fallbacks, .vtt captions, transcripts) and accessibility notes for the media player |
| IT infrastructure | Storage, backup, access control and tool provisioning; single sign-on and licence seats for approved tools | Storage and archive requirements; the approved tool list and the data each tool receives |
| Data and AI | Analytics pipelines, retention and conversion data joined to assets; model and tool evaluations | Asset metadata, prompts and performance tags; requirements for the paired metric (sign-ups or demo requests per 1,000 views) |
| Finance | Budgets and approval of tool subscriptions, licences, talent and agency fees | Licence and subscription records with plan tiers and renewal dates; cost per asset |
| Legal | Rulings on consent and release wording, vendor terms, disclosure decisions, comparative claims and complaints; confirmation of unverified legal points in this document | The rights log, consent records, vendor-terms versions, disclosure decisions and incident reports |
| Operations | Company calendar, events and facilities for shoots; process standards | Shoot schedules, notices to event attendees that images may be published, and turnaround reporting |
| Research | Customer and market research, verified statistics and sources for proof points | Requests for evidence behind claims; audience-response data from published media |
| Media (internal) | The manager agent receives briefs, classifies risk and assigns worker agents | Worker agents return drafts with QC results and rights-log entries; the manager agent routes them to the human approver |

### Rules that travel with every handoff

**[INFERENCE]** Three rules apply to every handoff. An asset never leaves Media without its rights-log status and disclosure decision attached. A claim, statistic or proof point is accepted into a script only with a source that Research, Product or Sales can document. A request that would break a rule in section 23 is refused and escalated to the human owner, whichever department it comes from.
