### The sourced cadence model

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The only sourced cadence model is Hormozi's: record long-form on a fixed marketing day, repackage per platform, and build in-house capability in phases using agencies as paid teachers (section 2; [Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed); [Hormozi, "I Gained 5,000,000+ Followers with this Secret Method", auto-transcript, Aug 2023](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-gained-5-000-000-followers-with-this-secret-method)). **[GAP]** No sourced material was found on turnaround standards, content-calendar practice or briefing templates, and no Sweden-specific working-time or holiday cadence considerations were researched.

### A proposed operating rhythm

**[INFERENCE]** A workable rhythm for a small team with AI agents is one recording session per week or fortnight with several pieces batched in one sitting, a fixed publishing calendar per platform, a weekly review of the retention and winners data, and a monthly review of the approved tool list, model deprecations, terms changes and the dated facts in this document. The batching idea follows a December 2021 Q&A in which about six videos per sitting was reported as Hormozi's suggestion ([Hormozi Q&A, "How I Put Out So Much Content Despite Being Part-Time", auto-transcript, Dec 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-put-out-so-much-content-despite-being-part-time)); the rest is the researchers' proposal.
