**When to use this:** When a question turns on Swedish law: copyright ownership and term, moral rights, adaptations, quotation, identifying marketing, truthful statements, GDPR with AI tools or utgivningsbevis. Orientation for a human to confirm.

**Source section:** 22. Swedish media law for a company's media department

### Scope and status

**[INFERENCE]** This section gathers the Swedish rules a media department meets daily. Each paragraph says whether the point was checked on a primary source. All of it is orientation as of 11 November 2026 and not legal advice. Likeness is in section 20, accessibility in section 10 and AI-image copyright in section 18.

### Copyright basics: who owns a work and for how long

**[DURABLE]** Checked on a primary source: under the Swedish Copyright Act (1960:729), consolidated through SFS 2026:1120, copyright belongs to the person who created a literary or artistic work, including film, photography, visual art and computer programs (1 §). The author has the exclusive right to make copies, including temporary copies, and to make the work available to the public in original or altered form (2 §). Protection lasts 70 years after the author's death (43 §), and photographic pictures are protected for 50 years from creation regardless of originality (49 a §) ([Riksdagen, upphovsrättslagen SFS 1960:729, read 11 Nov 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1960729-om-upphovsratt-till-litterara-och_sfs-1960-729/)).

### Moral rights

**[DURABLE]** Checked on a primary source: under 3 § the author must be named as good practice requires, and a work may not be changed, or presented in a form or context, that offends the author's reputation or individuality; the right can be waived only to a limited extent ([Riksdagen, upphovsrättslagen SFS 1960:729, read 11 Nov 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1960729-om-upphovsratt-till-litterara-och_sfs-1960-729/)). The practical consequences for editing and crediting are in section 9.

### Adaptations and new independent works

**[DURABLE]** Checked on a primary source: under 4 § a translator or adapter holds copyright in the adaptation but cannot exploit it against the original's copyright, and a new independent work created in free connection with another has its own copyright ([Riksdagen, upphovsrättslagen SFS 1960:729, read 11 Nov 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1960729-om-upphovsratt-till-litterara-och_sfs-1960-729/)).

### Quotation is narrow, and picture quotation does not cover marketing

**[DURABLE]** Checked on a primary source: under 22 § anyone may quote from published works in accordance with good practice and to the extent justified by the purpose. Under 23 § published artworks may be reproduced alongside text in a non-commercial scholarly presentation, in a critical presentation, and in a newspaper or periodical reporting a current event, with good practice and justified extent applying ([Riksdagen, upphovsrättslagen SFS 1960:729, read 11 Nov 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1960729-om-upphovsratt-till-litterara-och_sfs-1960-729/)). Under 52 p § users of certain online content-sharing services may make works available for quotation, criticism, review, caricature, parody or pastiche ([Riksdagen, upphovsrättslagen SFS 1960:729, read 11 Nov 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1960729-om-upphovsratt-till-litterara-och_sfs-1960-729/)).

**[INFERENCE]** The quotation right in 22 § is narrow for marketing use: quoting must serve a purpose such as illustration or criticism within your own content. 23 § does not cover commercial use of images, so picture quotation is not a basis for using others' images in marketing. Using clips, screenshots or music from third parties as a quote is therefore high risk in commercial content. **[GAP]** Whether the act now contains a general parody or pastiche exception beyond 52 p § was not seen, because the fetched text was cut at about two thirds of the act.

### Marketing must be identifiable as marketing

**[DURABLE] [FAST-MOVING, reviewed 4 Sep 2026]** Checked on a primary source: Konsumentverket, the Swedish Consumer Agency, states that posts a company publishes about its own products to promote sales fall under the ad-identification requirement. It must be clear at once, even on fleeting contact, that the content is marketing (court reference PMT 2054-18). Ad posts should be clearly marked with the word "reklam", and the assessment looks at word choice, graphic design (placement, size, colour and contrast) and whether vulnerable consumers are reached ([Konsumentverket, hidden marketing in social media, reviewed 4 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/dold-marknadsforing-i-sociala-medier-regler-for-foretag/)).

**[DURABLE] [FAST-MOVING, reviewed 4 Sep 2026]** Checked on a primary source: the company bears the main responsibility for influencers' posts about its products, and the influencer can be liable in parallel (PMT 5923-20). Liability cannot be contracted away (PMT 5093-23). Agreements can be oral or implied (PMT 2479-20, PMT 10572-20). A breach cannot be undone by editing the post afterwards. Companies should have a working ad-review function, and a platform's ad-label tool failing is no excuse (PMB 5086-23). Sanctions are prohibition, a conditional fine, and a market disruption fee of up to four percent of annual turnover ([Konsumentverket, hidden marketing in social media, reviewed 4 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/dold-marknadsforing-i-sociala-medier-regler-for-foretag/)).

**[GAP]** The Konsumentverket page read does not rule on wording such as "annons" or "i samarbete med" and does not address employees' own posts. Further Konsumentverket material, including the Nordic consumer ombudsmen's position, was not read ([Konsumentverket publication, Reklamidentifiering i sociala medier, unread](https://publikationer.konsumentverket.se/produkter-och-tjanster/reklam-och-marknadsforing/reklamidentifiering-i-sociala-medier)).

**[INFERENCE]** Company posts on its own channels count as marketing that must be identifiable. On a clearly branded company account this is normally evident from the sender, while paid partnerships, employee advocacy and influencer content need the explicit word "reklam" at the start.

### Marketing statements must be true, and AI is no excuse

**[DURABLE] [FAST-MOVING, 28 May 2026]** Secondary source only: a Swedish law firm summarises Marknadsföringslagen (2008:486) as requiring good marketing practice (5 §), commercial communication that is clearly identifiable as marketing and shows who is responsible (9 §), and statements that are true and not misleading (10 §), with contributory liability for others such as agencies (23 §). Enforcement is by Konsumentverket and the Patent and Market Court, with injunctions and market disruption fees ([CMS Sweden, 28 May 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/ai-in-advertising-key-aspects-for-advertisers-in-sweden)). The statute itself was not read.

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Secondary source only: in PMT 13029-25 (1 April 2026) the Patent and Market Court held that false information given by a mobile operator's AI chatbot was misleading marketing and that chatbot answers count as marketing. In matter 2503-36 (13 August 2025) Reklamombudsmannen held an education company responsible for false AI-generated information in its marketing; the ombudsman's decisions are not legally binding ([CMS Sweden, 28 May 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/ai-in-advertising-key-aspects-for-advertisers-in-sweden)).

### GDPR where AI tools process personal data

**[DURABLE]** Secondary source only: the same briefing notes that GDPR applies where AI tools process personal data in advertising, so a legal basis, transparency and safeguards for third-party AI providers and transfers outside the EEA are needed ([CMS Sweden, 28 May 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/ai-in-advertising-key-aspects-for-advertisers-in-sweden)). **[INFERENCE]** Uploading footage of identifiable people to a generative tool is a processing operation that needs its own assessment, in addition to the vendor-terms checks in section 18.

### Utgivningsbevis: what the publishing certificate does and does not do

**[DURABLE] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** Checked on a primary source: the Swedish Agency for the Media, Mediemyndigheten, describes the utgivningsbevis as a voluntary certificate for websites and databases that gives the constitutional protection mass media get automatically. Individuals, companies and organisations can apply. Conditions include that the database is publicly available and actively sought out by the visitor; is a clearly delimited, coherent product; can be changed only by the editorial staff, with comment fields clearly separated; has a name containing a domain name that is not confusable with another registered database; has a connection to Sweden; has a responsible publisher; and starts within six months. It is valid for ten years and renewable, an application fee applies, and it can be revoked. Its effects are special liability rules with the publisher responsible, source anonymity and no prior censorship ([Mediemyndigheten, utgivningsbevis och databaser, read 11 Nov 2026](https://mediemyndigheten.se/ansokan-och-registrering/utgivningsbevis-och-databaser/)).

**[DURABLE] [FAST-MOVING, read 11 Nov 2026]** Checked on a primary source: Mediemyndigheten states that the certificate does not in itself give a right to publish personal data and that each publication must be assessed by its purpose; it refers to a judgment of the Court of Justice of the EU from July 2026 holding that member states may not exempt processing from GDPR where the purpose is not journalistic, academic, artistic or literary. The authority has not issued certificates for Facebook or YouTube pages, because they are not the applicant's own databases ([Mediemyndigheten, utgivningsbevis och databaser, read 11 Nov 2026](https://mediemyndigheten.se/ansokan-och-registrering/utgivningsbevis-och-databaser/)). The judgment was not identified by case number and the fee amount was not found.

**[INFERENCE]** An utgivningsbevis is of little use to a SaaS marketing site: it does not cover social accounts and does not by itself lift GDPR. A company web or social channel is normally ordinary marketing and not constitutionally protected publishing, so marketing law, GDPR and Lag 1978:800 apply in full. That marketing law still applies to a certified database is the researchers' inference and is not stated on the page read.
