**When to use this:** When an asset shows another party's mark, a competitor's product, a building, outdoor art or a real location. Orientation for a human to confirm.

**Source section:** 21. Trade marks, products, locations and third-party material in media

### Generated images can carry other parties' marks

**[FAST-MOVING, Nov 2025]** Secondary source only: in Getty v Stability the UK High Court made limited, historic trade-mark infringement findings concerning Getty watermarks appearing in outputs of early model versions ([Taylor Wessing, Jan 2026](https://www.taylorwessing.com/de/insights-and-events/insights/2026/01/next-steps-for-getty-v-stability-why-has-permission-to-appeal-been-granted)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, 16 Oct 2026]** OpenAI's output indemnity excludes trade-mark claims arising from use of output in trade or commerce ([OpenAI Service Terms, updated 16 Oct 2026](https://openai.com/policies/service-terms/)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents should scan every generated frame for accidental third-party marks, watermarks, characters, artworks, distinctive product designs and packaging, because models reproduce them unprompted.

### Competitors' products

**[INFERENCE]** Depicting a competitor's product or mark is acceptable only within the comparative-advertising rules, which the researchers summarise from general EU and Swedish marketing-law principles as objective, verifiable, not denigrating and not taking unfair advantage; this was not verified. An AI-generated depiction of a competitor's product is likely to be inaccurate and therefore misleading, so use real, verified imagery or none, and get legal sign-off.

### Buildings and outdoor art in Sweden

**[DURABLE]** Checked on a primary source: under 24 § of the Swedish Copyright Act artworks permanently placed outdoors in a public place may be depicted, and buildings may be freely depicted. Artworks may also be depicted to advertise an exhibition or sale and in catalogues ([Riksdagen, upphovsrättslagen SFS 1960:729, read 11 Nov 2026](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1960729-om-upphovsratt-till-litterara-och_sfs-1960-729/)). **[GAP]** The fetched text indicates that some of these permissions do not extend to digital form; that limit comes from a machine summary and must be re-read in the statute before it is relied on.

### Real locations

**[INFERENCE]** Private property and distinctive interiors need a property release. Altering a real, identifiable place with AI can trigger the deepfake label, because the Commission's summary of the definition covers places ([European Commission, EU icons page, updated 13 Oct 2026](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-icons-labelling-ai-generated-content)).

### What was not verified here

**[GAP]** Not verified: Marknadsföringslagen 18 § on comparative advertising; Directive 2006/114/EC; Swedish case law on buildings and artworks in advertising; trade-mark use in imagery under the Swedish Trade Marks Act and the EU Trade Mark Regulation; and design-right or trade-dress risks from AI imagery.
