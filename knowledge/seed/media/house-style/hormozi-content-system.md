**When to use this:** As the default approach for planning, scripting, cutting and packaging content, after the rights, disclosure and evidence documents (section 3 precedence). Use the structures in the company's own voice, never his.

**Source section:** 2. House style: Alex Hormozi's published content-production system

### What "house style" means and how reliable the sources are

**[HOUSE STYLE]** This section describes Alex Hormozi's content-production system as the owner's chosen default where he has published it. It is a preference and not independent evidence. All of his figures are his own unaudited claims. The primary material was read as auto-generated transcripts of his videos on a third-party archive, so numbers and wording can be garbled; the hook-retain-reward unit and other book material rest on second-hand summaries because the text of *$100M Leads* was not read ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons); [Business Book Club summary of $100M Leads](https://businessbookclub.substack.com/p/free-book-summary-100m-leads-by-alex)). Agents apply the structures in the company's own voice under the precedence rules in section 3.

### Volume: what he claims to publish and spend

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Aug 2022]** In a video dated 2 August 2022 Hormozi claimed about 160 pieces of content a week across platforms for about $70,000 a month, estimated that buying the same impressions would cost about $2 million a month, and said content took about 18 months to produce deal flow for his firm ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)). These are his own unaudited claims.

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr 2024]** In a video dated 27 April 2024 he claimed that over roughly 40 months his team made about 35,000 pieces, spent about $4 million on team, equipment, vendors, studios and software, and that he spent about 1,000 hours on camera. He claimed growth to 2.23 million YouTube subscribers, 2.5 million on Instagram, 856,000 on TikTok, about 617,000 on X, 324,000 on LinkedIn, 25,000 on Facebook, 700,000 email subscribers, podcast downloads from 3,000 to 25 million, about 2 billion impressions and 7.8 million followers in total, and said he does not directly monetise the media ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). These are his own unaudited claims.

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [CONTESTED] [FAST-MOVING, Oct 2025]** In a video dated 18 October 2025 he is transcribed as saying his company posted about 35,000 pieces in that year, framing his volume as roughly 100 times one post a day, and reporting 32.7 million YouTube views that month ([Hormozi, "My Actual Social Media Strategy For 2026", auto-transcript, 18 Oct 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/my-actual-social-media-strategy-for-2026)). The 35,000 figure therefore appears both as a 40-month total (April 2024) and as a single-year figure (October 2025 transcript as extracted). The transcripts are auto-captions; neither figure should be quoted without checking the videos.

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand] [VENDOR] [FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Outside sources give further estimates that were not checked against Hormozi's own statements. A podcast by outside hosts dated 12 May 2025 says he publishes more than 300 pieces a week ([The Video Creatr Podcast, ep. 200, 12 May 2025](https://www.buzzsprout.com/1887347/episodes/17152925-how-alex-hormozi-posts-300-times-a-week)). A vendor analysis covering to April 2026 estimates more than 160 posts a week across three presenter brands and a media team of 15 to 25 or more, and labels its budget split as an estimate that reuses the 2022 figure of $70,000 a month ([Clipflow, undated, covers to Apr 2026](https://clipflow-ig-analysis.pages.dev/hormozi/)). A vendor snapshot dated July 2026 puts his YouTube channel at 4.3 million subscribers, 1.1 billion views and about 5,300 videos, with 91% of views from Shorts ([OutlierKit, 10 Jul 2026](https://outlierkit.com/resources/alex-hormozi-growth-strategy/)).

### Cadence: build regularity before volume, in five phases

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Aug 2022]** Hormozi describes five phases of content operations: post something; post on a regular cadence; post reliably on all platforms; push each platform to its maximum volume; then deliberately create and capture content with repurposing in mind. He says getting platform-specific packaging right took his team about six months ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)). **[INFERENCE]** For a small team the phases work as gates: do not add a platform until the current ones have held a steady cadence for a set number of weeks.

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Aug 2022]** His stated platform tolerances in 2022 were that short-form feeds can take 5 to 10 posts a day while newsfeed-style audiences tire at about 1 to 2 a day, and he said he does all of his marketing work on one day a week ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)). In a December 2021 Q&A a participant reported that Hormozi suggested recording about six videos in one sitting and recording when an idea from a real conversation is fresh ([Hormozi Q&A, "How I Put Out So Much Content Despite Being Part-Time", auto-transcript, Dec 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-put-out-so-much-content-despite-being-part-time)). These are his own claims; no platform source in the notes confirms the posting tolerances.

### Long-form recorded first, then cut into many short pieces

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Aug 2022]** Hormozi's workflow is that content is created, captured and then packaged: the same idea is repackaged per platform, so that a text post becomes a TikTok, a Short and a Reel. He says that beyond a few platforms one person cannot do this alone and leverage is required, first through vendors and then in-house ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, May 2024]** He says short and long pieces do different jobs. In his own data long-form drove most book sales, opt-ins and applications, while shorts are kept at a similar volume for top-of-funnel awareness and face recognition, which he says pays off later through retargeting ads. He also reports finding no evidence that shorts viewers convert into long-form viewers ([Hormozi, "I Posted ONLY Business Content for 30 Days", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-posted-only-business-content-for-30-days-strategy-revealed)). These are his own unaudited observations.

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Oct 2025]** In October 2025 he argued that 480 shorts at about 15 seconds each equal roughly two hours of long-form exposure, and said he planned more live, interactive formats ([Hormozi, "My Actual Social Media Strategy For 2026", auto-transcript, 18 Oct 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/my-actual-social-media-strategy-for-2026)). A LinkedIn post of his on his 2025 content strategy is described by an index page as long-form-first and repurpose-everything, but the post text was not readable ([eChai index entry linking his LinkedIn post](https://echai.ventures/how-founders-use-ai/r/my-4-000-000-content-strategy-for-2025--3236)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand] [VENDOR] [FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Third parties describe the cutting step in more detail than Hormozi does. One agency model has a single 30 to 60 minute recording yielding 4 to 5 mid-length clips of 5 to 10 minutes and 10 to 15 short clips of 30 to 90 seconds, plus a blog post, more than 10 text posts, more than 5 carousels and one newsletter ([thinkdmg agency blog, 10 Mar 2025, updated Oct 2026](https://thinkdmg.com/the-alex-hormozi-content-strategy-how-to-repurpose-one-video-into-30-pieces/)). A vendor analysis describes a five-step pipeline: capture 30 to 90 minute sessions; extract 10 to 15 high-energy clips of 30 to 90 seconds; rewrite hooks, captions and calls to action per platform; stagger publishing and amplify winners with paid; recycle winning hooks and re-edit evergreen pieces ([Clipflow, undated, covers to Apr 2026](https://clipflow-ig-analysis.pages.dev/hormozi/)). An outside reading of his channel says each Short gets its own hook and that retention spikes in long-form are treated as pre-validated clip moments ([OutlierKit, 10 Jul 2026](https://outlierkit.com/resources/alex-hormozi-growth-strategy/)). An archive summary of a February 2023 video says his team used AI transcription to find clip moments ([Rosetta topic index, Hormozi on content creation](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/topic/content-creation)).

### The hook-retain-reward content unit

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand]** Summaries of *$100M Leads* describe a content unit with three parts: a hook that earns the first seconds, a retain section that keeps attention through lists, steps or stories, and a reward that delivers what the hook promised ([Business Book Club summary of $100M Leads](https://businessbookclub.substack.com/p/free-book-summary-100m-leads-by-alex); [ADV Launch summary of $100M Leads](https://advlaunch.us/blog/hormozi-100m-leads-summary-community-operators)). A community post attributes the unit to a Hormozi video titled "The Content Unit" ([Skool, Accelerator University community post](https://www.skool.com/acceleratoruniversity/what-three-parts-make-up-an-effective-content-unit)), and his company's free course lists two modules named "Mozi Media Content Method" parts I and II, whose lesson content was not readable from the landing page ([acquisition.com/training/leads](https://www.acquisition.com/training/leads)). The book text was not read, so the details rest on summaries.

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand]** The same summaries report four supporting rules: spend as much time on the headline as on the rest of the piece; length is not the problem and boredom is; give roughly three pieces of value for every ask; and a first-person account of what you did is more credible than generic instruction ([ADV Launch summary of $100M Leads](https://advlaunch.us/blog/hormozi-100m-leads-summary-community-operators); [Business Book Club summary of $100M Leads](https://businessbookclub.substack.com/p/free-book-summary-100m-leads-by-alex)). The point that boring content, not long content, loses viewers also appears in his own August 2022 video ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)).

**[INFERENCE]** How agents use the unit: write the reward first (the single thing the viewer will be able to do or know), then the retain structure (list, steps or story), then several candidate hooks drawn from the strongest true element of the piece. A hook that the body does not pay off fails the unit and also fails the accuracy rules in sections 3 and 22.

### Clear titles and thumbnails

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr 2024]** Hormozi says he moved his titles and thumbnails from vague and curiosity-led to clear statements of what the video delivers, that he had previously believed in withholding information to create curiosity, and that clear promises converted better for him ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). This is his own unaudited claim.

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [CONTESTED] [FAST-MOVING, May 2025]** There is a tension inside his own material. In a May 2025 video about advertising he treats curiosity as the leading quality of a headline ([Hormozi, "14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes", auto-transcript, May 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/14-years-of-marketing-advice-in-35-minutes)). The researchers read this as two rules for two jobs: clear over clever for content packaging, curiosity for ad headlines. Agents default to clear packaging for organic content and treat ad-headline curiosity as Marketing's call, bounded by the accuracy rules in section 3.

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand] [VENDOR] [FAST-MOVING, 10 Jul 2026]** An outside analysis of his channel found a title pattern that pairs an audience situation introduced with "if" with a specific promise, performing at 3 to 6 times the channel median, with one 2026 example at 5.6 times ([OutlierKit, 10 Jul 2026](https://outlierkit.com/resources/alex-hormozi-growth-strategy/)). The analysis is a vendor's and the pattern is a structure to test, not a rule he has published.

**[GAP]** His thumbnail design rules beyond clear and matching the content were not found. A likely primary source, a 29 July 2024 video on building a brand, was not read ([Rosetta link to "Building a brand, but it's on easy mode", 29 Jul 2024, unread](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/building-a-brand-but-it-s-on-easy-mode-instead)).

### Proof-promise-plan intros

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr 2024]** After rewatching what he says were his top 35 educational videos, Hormozi reports a shared intro pattern: proof (why the viewer should trust the speaker), promise (what the viewer will get) and plan (how the video delivers it). The order can vary and he usually leads with proof. He adds that the presenter should be introduced in every piece, that the viewer should be told why to listen, and that inside references should be avoided so that nothing is assumed about what the viewer already knows ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). In a May 2025 video he says proof outperforms promise ([Hormozi, "14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes", auto-transcript, May 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/14-years-of-marketing-advice-in-35-minutes)).

**[INFERENCE]** How agents use the pattern: the first lines of a long-form script state one verifiable proof point the company can substantiate, one promise that matches the title, and a one-sentence plan. The proof must be true and documented, because Swedish marketing law requires statements to be true and not misleading (section 22).

### Message over effects, and pre-work over post-production

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr 2024]** In a 30-day review of his own Reels, Hormozi says 9 of his top 12 leaned on clear language while 8 of his bottom 12 leaned on effects and production. He recommends fewer vlog-style cuts and more lists, steps and stories, says visuals should clarify (for example charts showing scale or change over time) and not decorate, and says one of his best long-form videos is on-screen text for roughly 70 to 80% of its runtime ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). These are his own unaudited observations.

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr-May 2024]** He claims that shifting effort from post-production to pre-production cut his post-edit work by roughly 90 to 95% and allowed about four times the output, and that less post-production also let him publish more ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons); [Hormozi, "I Posted ONLY Business Content for 30 Days", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-posted-only-business-content-for-30-days-strategy-revealed)). He reports that slides versus no slides made little difference and that talking to camera performed about the same as a stage presentation ([Hormozi, "I Posted ONLY Business Content for 30 Days", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-posted-only-business-content-for-30-days-strategy-revealed)). In 2022 he said each platform needs its own packaging and that production does not need to be fancy ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)), and in May 2025 he argued that plain-looking pages and ads convert better than elaborate ones ([Hormozi, "14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes", auto-transcript, May 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/14-years-of-marketing-advice-in-35-minutes)). An archive summary of a 2020 video says he advises matching ad creative, colours and fonts to the target audience ([Rosetta topic index, Hormozi on content creation](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/topic/content-creation)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand] [VENDOR]** Third-party descriptions of the look, which do not come from Hormozi: plain, direct-to-camera delivery with captions, a whiteboard and credibility drawn from specific numbers ([OutlierKit, 10 Jul 2026](https://outlierkit.com/resources/alex-hormozi-growth-strategy/)); and a talking-head edit with burned-in word-by-word captions, jump cuts on breaths, a payoff-first hook in the first 3 seconds, a pattern interrupt every 5 to 10 seconds, a clean background, good light and a decent microphone ([thinkdmg agency blog, 10 Mar 2025, updated Oct 2026](https://thinkdmg.com/the-alex-hormozi-content-strategy-how-to-repurpose-one-video-into-30-pieces/)). A blog summary of his 2026 material adds that four weeks of research is worth more than four weeks of editing ([Postiz blog, 19 Apr 2026](https://postiz.com/blog/hormozi-2026-audience-growth-playbook)).

### Education-only positioning: the six shifts

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr 2024]** Hormozi lists six shifts in his content: from edutainment to education; from content made for his team to content made for the customer; from wide to narrow topics; from views to revenue as the goal; from shorts to longs as the priority; and from assuming knowledge to assuming nothing ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)). He says he had been making content his team liked instead of content for business owners ([Hormozi, "I Posted ONLY Business Content for 30 Days", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-posted-only-business-content-for-30-days-strategy-revealed)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Oct 2025]** In October 2025 he said content should be made for the ideal customer because the content itself does the targeting, framed influence as status, power, credibility and likeness, and said he values replies from respected business owners over view counts ([Hormozi, "My Actual Social Media Strategy For 2026", auto-transcript, 18 Oct 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/my-actual-social-media-strategy-for-2026)). He also recommends reposting the previous year's best piece because newer followers have not seen it ([Hormozi, "How I Gained 7.8 Million Followers In 40 Months", auto-transcript, 27 Apr 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-gained-7-8-million-followers-in-40-months-6-key-lessons)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, 2022-2024]** His stated philosophy is to give away the best material free, including things he could charge for, and to sell implementation and personalised help; he says about 99% of people who consume free content never buy, so free content sets reputation ([Hormozi, "13 Years of Marketing Advice in 85 Mins", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/13-years-of-marketing-advice-in-85-mins)). In 2022 he advised mostly giving value and adding calls to action sparingly ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)). A second-hand summary places the book's version of this idea in its lead-magnet section ([Business Book Club summary of $100M Leads](https://businessbookclub.substack.com/p/free-book-summary-100m-leads-by-alex)).

### Organic content as hook testing for ads

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Aug 2022]** Hormozi describes organic posts as a low-risk way of testing hooks and headlines: the top organic content shows which hooks to use in paid ads and cold email, where they are split-tested against a control ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)). He also recommends keeping a master spreadsheet of top-performing content together with the questions heard on sales calls, as raw material for future content and ads ([Hormozi, "1.2M Followers in 6 Months", auto-transcript, 2 Aug 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/1-2m-followers-in-6-months-my-content-marketing-strategy-revealed)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, May 2024]** His ad production routine, as he describes it: about 50 hooks a session, of which about 40 are built on past winners and 10 are new, with 3 to 5 body angles and 1 to 3 calls to action; then vary the look of winners (background, props, order, fonts, pacing, music) while keeping the message. He puts about 80% of ad effort into the first five seconds and the headline. His stated priority order is to do more of what works, then improve it, and try something new only when the first two are saturated, with about 90% of time on the first two ([Hormozi, "13 Years of Marketing Advice in 85 Mins", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/13-years-of-marketing-advice-in-85-mins)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, May 2025]** He advises studying the first three seconds of organic content and of competitors' ads for hooks, borrowing hook structures from unrelated industries, and keeping a winning hook until something beats it. He claims that moving click-through rate from 1% to 3% triples the people who see the rest of an ad, and that a headline change can lift results 2 to 5 times against 20 to 50% for a typical split test ([Hormozi, "14 Years of Marketing Advice in 35 Minutes", auto-transcript, May 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/14-years-of-marketing-advice-in-35-minutes)). These are his own unaudited claims. His iteration rule is to test fast, track data and not repeat the same mistake ([Hormozi, "I Posted ONLY Business Content for 30 Days", auto-transcript, May 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-posted-only-business-content-for-30-days-strategy-revealed)).

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand] [VENDOR]** A vendor analysis says viral organic posts get paid amplification, top text posts become video scripts, and his team saves winning hooks as reusable templates and re-edits evergreen pieces with new intros ([Clipflow, undated, covers to Apr 2026](https://clipflow-ig-analysis.pages.dev/hormozi/)). **[GAP]** No primary source documents a formal step in which an organic winner is converted into an ad; he describes using organic content for hook research and retargeting.

### Revenue signals over views

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Apr 2024]** Hormozi says he switched his primary metric from views to ad revenue per thousand views (RPM) and that the months with the highest RPM also had the most book sales, opt-ins and applications. ### The path from agency to in-house

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript] [FAST-MOVING, Aug 2023]** Hormozi describes a phased path. First hire a basic agency at about $3,000 to $5,000 a month for 3 to 6 months to establish a cadence, with three videos a week as his example. Then hire an advanced agency at about $15,000 to $30,000 a month for about six months to learn how it makes decisions. Then train the in-house team, downgrade the agency to consulting, and exit. He claims his in-house team produces five times an agency's output ([Hormozi, "I Gained 5,000,000+ Followers with this Secret Method", auto-transcript, Aug 2023](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-gained-5-000-000-followers-with-this-secret-method)). These are his own unaudited claims and prices in US dollars from 2023.

**[INFERENCE]** For a department staffed by AI agents the equivalent path is to treat any outside agency or freelancer as a paid teacher: capture its decision rules (how it picks clips, hooks, thumbnails and cadence) into the knowledge base, then bring the work in-house. Under Swedish marketing law as summarised by a law firm in May 2026, the advertiser stays liable for content whether it was produced in-house, by an agency or by AI, and agencies can be liable as contributors (secondary source only; [CMS Sweden, 28 May 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/ai-in-advertising-key-aspects-for-advertisers-in-sweden)), so the review gate in section 23 applies to agency work too.

### How a small SaaS team should scale the house style

**[INFERENCE]** Nine working rules follow from his primary statements. Every piece teaches one thing to the target customer. The title and thumbnail state the outcome plainly. The opening shows proof, then promise, then plan. The body is a list, steps or a story. Effects are minimal and visuals appear only where they explain. Research and outlining are heavy before the shoot and the edit is light after. Long-form is recorded first and cut into many shorts, each with its own hook. Winners are logged and top hooks go to paid tests. Content is ranked by revenue-linked signals and not by views.

**[INFERENCE]** His volume figures come from a multi-million-dollar team and several presenter brands. A small SaaS media team should copy the mechanism (one long recording, many native pieces, winners to ads) and not the absolute numbers. His 2022 advice on 5 to 10 short posts a day predates his 2024 shift toward long-form and narrow topics; where the two conflict, the 2024 to 2026 statements are the current house style.
