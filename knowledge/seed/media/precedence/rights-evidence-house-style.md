**When to use this:** Whenever two rules collide, before applying the house style, and before naming or citing Hormozi or using his numbers.

**Source section:** 3. Precedence rules: rights and disclosure first, evidence and official platform guidance second, house style third

### The order of precedence

**[INFERENCE]** When rules collide, rights and disclosure rules win over everything; evidence and official platform guidance win over house style; house style governs only what is left. A rights or disclosure rule is any requirement in sections 17 to 22: consent, copyright and licence scope, AI Act Article 50, platform AI-label rules, advertising identification, data protection and vendor terms. An evidence rule is a finding from a platform's own documentation, a standards body or audience research. The house style is section 2. The manager agent records which layer decided each contested choice.

### Agents apply his structures without imitating him or using his name or likeness

**[INFERENCE]** The house style is a set of structures (content unit, intro pattern, clear packaging, pre-work, long-to-short cutting, hook testing). It is not a persona. Agents must not imitate Hormozi's voice, catchphrases, appearance, on-screen look or delivery; must not use his name, image, voice or a lookalike or soundalike in any company media, prompt, thumbnail, title or ad; must not present his numbers as benchmarks or as the company's own results; and must not cite him in published content as an endorser. Internal documents may name him as the source of a framework.

**[DURABLE]** The legal ground for this rule in Sweden was checked on a primary source: under Lag (1978:800) a trader may not use another person's name or picture in marketing without that person's consent, and a designation that clearly points to a specific person counts as a name ([Riksdagen, Lag (1978:800) om namn och bild i reklam](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1978800-om-namn-och-bild-i-reklam_sfs-1978-800/)). **[PLATFORM CLAIM] [FAST-MOVING, Nov 2026]** Platform and vendor rules point the same way: TikTok bans AI content showing public figures endorsing or being endorsed, and OpenAI and ElevenLabs prohibit reproducing a person's likeness or voice without consent ([TikTok Support, AI-generated content, read Nov 2026](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content); [OpenAI Service Terms, updated 16 Oct 2026](https://openai.com/policies/service-terms/); [ElevenLabs Terms, updated 9 Oct 2026](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use)). This is orientation and not legal advice.

### Known collisions and which rule wins

**[INFERENCE]** The table lists the collisions the researchers identified between the house style and the other two layers. The resolutions are the researchers' synthesis; the sources in the right-hand column support the rule that wins, not the resolution itself.

| House-style move | Colliding rule | Resolution (inference) | Source for the winning rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum volume on every platform | No asset is published without a named human approver and a complete rights-log entry | Volume is capped by review capacity; never skip the gate to hit a cadence | Advertiser liable for AI output, secondary source only: [CMS Sweden, 28 May 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/ai-in-advertising-key-aspects-for-advertisers-in-sweden) |
| Lead with proof; proof beats promise | Marketing statements must be true and not misleading | Only documented, substantiated proof points; no invented results, testimonials or statistics | Secondary source only: [CMS Sweden, 28 May 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/ai-in-advertising-key-aspects-for-advertisers-in-sweden) |
| Curiosity-led ad headlines | YouTube rewards watch time and warns against clickbait; marketing must not mislead | Curiosity is allowed only where the content pays it off in full | [YouTube Help, A/B test titles and thumbnails, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en); [YouTube Help, impressions and click-through rate, read 11 Nov 2026](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154?hl=en) |

### His numbers are context, never targets or claims

**[INFERENCE]** Hormozi's figures are his own unaudited claims from a business unlike a Sweden-based SaaS company. Agents may use them internally to understand the mechanism. Agents must not set targets from them, must not report company performance against them, and must not repeat them in published content as facts.
