# House style sells; law and evidence govern

As of November 2026 the foundational knowledge for an AI sales department is a three-layer stack: compliance rules that always win, independent evidence that tunes tactics, and Alex Hormozi's sales system as the default structure underneath both. The house style supplies the working skeleton: answer leads within about a minute, run a question-led conversation (Clarify, Label, Overview, Sell the outcome, Explain away concerns, Reinforce), sort resistance into five buckets (time, money, fit, authority, avoidance), step down through payment terms instead of discounting the same thing, sequence attraction, upsell, downsell and continuity offers, and measure every stage through to cash collected; all of his performance numbers are his own unaudited claims. The evidence layer corrects it where deals get larger or buyers hesitate: classic closing techniques hurt large sales in Rackham's research, adding fear or discounts to an indecisive buyer made a loss more likely in 84% of cases in the JOLT dataset, the vendor a buying group prefers before contact wins roughly 77 to 80% of deals, and 67% of B2B buyers say they prefer a rep-free experience while 69% still want a rep to validate what AI told them. The compliance layer removes several house-style moves outright for a Sweden-based seller: a Swedish consumer contract from a trader-initiated call is invalid unless the consumer accepts in writing after the call, false urgency and scarcity are blacklisted under EU consumer law, cold email needs consent in Germany, Denmark and the Netherlands even for business recipients, AI systems must disclose themselves at first interaction under AI Act Article 50 (applicable since 2 August 2026), and a new Swedish consumer credit act enters into force on 20 November 2026. Current operating benchmarks are sobering and mostly vendor-sourced: new-business win rates near 19% with 91-day cycles against 45% and 52 days for expansion, median cold-email reply of 0.74% per contact, about one dial in ten connecting, only 32% of inbound demo forms receiving any reply, and no independent evidence that autonomous AI SDRs outperform humans. The practical conclusion is that agents win on speed, research depth, discipline and truthfulness, not on volume or pressure, and that pricing, discounts, contract terms, gifts and anything touching Swedish consumers by phone need hard gates outside the model. Where the research found no fetched source, this document says so instead of filling the hole from memory.

## How to read and split this document

This document is written to be cut into atomic knowledge-base entries. Every paragraph is meant to stand alone: it opens with one or more labels, names its subject in full, and keeps each number next to its date and source link. Section numbers group domains; sub-headings name the single topic of the paragraphs beneath them. Tables repeat their source link in every row so that a row can be split out as its own entry. When splitting, keep the label, the date and the link with the claim.

The labels mean the following. **[HOUSE STYLE]** marks Alex Hormozi's frameworks and rules, which the owner has chosen as the department's default style; it is a preference and not independent evidence, and every number attached to it is his own self-reported, unaudited claim unless stated otherwise. **[DURABLE]** marks a principle, definition, formula or long-standing legal rule that has held for years and is unlikely to age quickly. **[FAST-MOVING, date]** marks a figure, platform rule, survey result or legal status that will age, with the date of the source. **[VENDOR]** marks a figure or recommendation from a company with a commercial interest in the conclusion (tool vendors, training firms, agencies), including large platform datasets from such companies; treat it as correlational and self-selected. **[PLATFORM CLAIM]** marks a platform's or company's own statement about itself that nobody has independently verified. **[CONTESTED]** marks a point where sources disagree or where house style and evidence pull in different directions. **[INFERENCE]** marks a synthesis or convention written by the researchers that no single fetched source states; adopt it as a default and tune it against your own data. **[GAP]** marks something the research did not verify or could not find.

Three qualifiers are added inside labels where they matter. For house-style material, "primary transcript" means the point was read on a transcript of Hormozi's own video or on acquisition.com, and "second-hand summary" means it comes from a third-party summary (Shortform, SuperSummary, Deciphr, Summify, tubegraph, BAM) and was not checked against the original. For legal material, "checked on primary source" means the statute or regulator page was read during the research, "secondary source only" means a law firm, trade or vendor page was read and the primary text was not, and "unverified, not fetched" means the point comes from the researchers' background knowledge with a link to where it should be confirmed. For sales material, "unverified background knowledge" has the same meaning. Agents must not gate a live decision on an unverified legal point without confirmation.

Six caveats apply to the whole document. First, legal content is orientation as of 11 November 2026 and not legal advice. Second, the Hormozi transcripts were read through a third-party host of auto-generated transcripts and a summarising tool, so all of his material here is paraphrase, never his wording. Third, almost all benchmarks are US-weighted B2B SaaS; the only Nordic dataset with numbers is the Monterro benchmark, and no robust Nordic outbound benchmark exists. Fourth, conversation-intelligence numbers are large-sample but correlational, mostly from Gong, and several famous ones date from 2016 to 2018. Fifth, reply-rate figures from different email vendors use different denominators (per email, per contact, per sequence) and cannot be compared directly. Sixth, the research found essentially nothing research-grade on B2C high-ticket selling or B2C lead handling outside Hormozi's own material and the consumer-law rules, so consumer-sales guidance here is house style bounded by law.

## 1. Strategy overview: three layers, one funnel, speed and proof over pressure

### The three-layer rule that governs every agent decision

**[INFERENCE]** The department runs on three layers in strict order. Layer one is compliance: outreach consent rules, Swedish consumer contract rules, EU unfair-practice law, AI disclosure and data protection (sections 27 to 31). Layer two is independent evidence on what works in sales conversations and with 2025 to 2026 buyers (sections 13 to 16). Layer three is the house style, Hormozi's structures, which fills every space the first two layers leave open (sections 2 to 10). A worker agent picks the house-style move by default, checks whether an evidence rule changes it for this deal size or buyer state, and checks whether a compliance gate blocks it for this country, recipient type and channel. Section 11 lists the known collisions and says which rule wins in each.

### Design for how buyers actually buy in 2025 to 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Buyers do most of their work before and without sellers, then want a human for validation. Gartner's survey of 646 B2B buyers (fielded August to September 2025, published 9 March 2026) found 67% prefer a rep-free experience ([Gartner press release, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience)), and Gartner's related release on 645 buyers found 69% prefer to validate AI-generated insights with a sales rep ([Gartner press release, 20 May 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-survey-finds-sixty-nine-percent-of-b-two-b-buyers-turn-to-sales-reps-to-validate-ai-generated-insights)). 6sense's 2025 report on more than 4,000 buyers found 94% of buying groups rank preferred vendors before first seller contact and that the pre-contact favourite wins about 77 to 80% of the time ([6sense via Business Wire, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112018032/en/)). **[INFERENCE]** The strategic consequence is that the department earns revenue in three places: being fast and precise when a buyer raises a hand, being present and credible before the buying window opens (warm routes, proof that third parties can validate), and helping a hesitant buyer decide safely in the middle of the journey. Late persuasion of an account that already favours a rival has low odds.

### Choose the sales motion by deal complexity, not by fashion

**[DURABLE]** Sources agree that deal complexity (number of stakeholders, cycle length, customisation) determines method. Rackham's research on more than 35,000 sales calls found that small sales need little deliberation while large sales involve many people over months, and that closing pressure which works in small sales hurts in large ones ([Sales Society, Aug 2022](https://salessociety.substack.com/p/the-fascinating-science-of-spin-selling)). Practitioner guidance says heavy frameworks such as MEDDPICC are overkill for deals with one or two decision-makers and that hybrids are acceptable if the rules are written down ([Demodesk, Jul 2026](https://demodesk.ai/blog/sales-qualification-frameworks-how-to-choose); [MEDDICC.com framework comparison](https://meddicc.com/resources/meddicc-versus-other-qualification-frameworks-like-bant)). **[INFERENCE]** The house style is at its strongest in single-decision-maker, short-cycle sales (consumers, owner-operators, small businesses) and needs the adaptations in section 12 once a deal involves a buying committee.

### Run one funnel from first touch to expansion

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Winning by Design's Bowtie Standard defines one data model across product-led, AI-led and human-led motions: Awareness, Education, Prioritization, Selection, Mutual Commit, Onboarding, Retention and Adoption, and Expansion, each with a volume, a conversion and a time metric ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)). **[VENDOR]** Expansion deals win at 45% in 52 days against 19% in 91 days for new business in Ebsta and Pavilion's 2024 pipeline data on 655,000 opportunities ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). **[INFERENCE]** Post-sale stages therefore belong in the same pipeline, with owners and service levels, and the house style's Reinforce step and upsell timing slot into the right-hand side of that funnel.

### Map the manager agent and worker agents onto proven roles

**[INFERENCE]** The manager agent maps to Hormozi's sales director plus a revenue-operations function: it owns the scripts and playbooks, reviews transcripts against a rubric, tracks stage metrics, enforces gates and retires weak script variants. Worker agents map to setters or SDRs (speed to lead, qualification, booking, reminders), closers or account executives (discovery, offer, objections, close), a sales-engineer role (technical answers and demos) and a customer-success role (onboarding, health, renewal and expansion). The funnel to instrument end to end is leads, contacted, booked, showed, offered, closed and cash collected, extended on the post-sale side by onboarded, retained and expanded. This mapping is the researchers' synthesis of the Hormozi notes and the pipeline notes, not a sourced design.

### Compete on speed, relevance and truth, because volume is the losing game

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** The measurable openings for an AI department are on the inbound and warm side. In Clay's September 2026 test of 6,346 B2B demo and contact forms only 32% received any email reply and about 5% of replies arrived within five minutes ([Clay, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/claygent-experiment-speed-to-lead)). On the cold side the yields are low and falling: Smartlead's H1 2026 data on more than 850 million emails shows a median reply of 0.74% per contact ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)), and Belkins' managed campaigns fell from 0.50% reply per email in H1 2025 to 0.40% in H2 2025 ([Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents should spend their advantage on research depth per prospect and on response speed, keep cold volumes low, and treat every claim as something the buyer's own AI will check.

### Measure against your own trailing data first

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** The Bowtie paper states that self-benchmarking over the trailing 12 months is more accurate for operating decisions than industry benchmarks ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** Every benchmark in this document is a starting reference for a new team; once the department has its own stage data, the manager agent should compare against its own history by segment and motion and use the external figures only as a sanity check.

## 2. House style: the selling mentality the agents adopt

### What "house style" means and how far it reaches

**[HOUSE STYLE]** Sections 2 to 10 describe Alex Hormozi's sales system as the owner's chosen default. It is a preference. It is not independent evidence, no rigorous independent study of its effectiveness was found, and essentially every performance figure attached to it (close rates, show rates, lifts) is his own self-reported claim without audit ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [Summify summary of 'How to Close More Leads', 8 Mar 2026](https://summify.io/discover/how-to-close-more-leads/)). His own site carries a disclaimer that the results it shows are not typical or guaranteed ([acquisition.com/training, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/training)). Agents apply the structures in the brand's own voice and under the precedence rules in section 11.

### Selling is helping a person act on a goal they already hold

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Hormozi frames closing as helping a fearful person make a decision, a dance and not a fight, with the seller on the back foot as a helper (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). He teaches that the party who cares more about the prospect's outcome tends to win, and that the prospect comes before the sale ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). A second-hand summary of a September 2026 episode has him separating bad selling that leaves people feeling pressured from clear, structured communication that respects the buyer's decision ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). **[INFERENCE]** For agents this sets the default posture: diagnose first, recommend clearly, and treat a clear refusal as an answer.

### Conviction comes from proof, and no proof means do not sell it

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He teaches that conviction is built deliberately: read customer testimonials daily, ask whether you would sell the product to your own mother, and if the business has no testimonials, sell something else (2 June 2021, [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). Hard selling is only legitimate when the seller believes the product is worth it ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). A second-hand summary adds proof layering: for each major objection, give the correct view backed by examples, stories and statistics, and keep publishing testimonials ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). **[INFERENCE]** For AI agents, conviction translates into grounding every claim in real customer proof the agent can cite from the knowledge base; an agent without attributable proof for a claim does not make the claim.

### Tone carries the script

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He claims that tone carries most of the sale, putting it at about 90%, and that identical scripts produce different results depending on delivery (2 June 2021, [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). This is his own estimate, not a measurement. **[INFERENCE]** For text and voice agents, tone translates into pacing, brevity, warmth and certainty of wording.

### Full responsibility, no blaming leads, steady volume

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** His rules on responsibility are that reps do not blame leads, circumstances or customers and instead ask what to fix so the next customer succeeds ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework); [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Low-quality leads are to be treated as free practice ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Rejection is not personal, and he attributes burnout to self-respect lost by not doing the work and not to hearing a refusal ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). He favours sustained consistency over bursts and repeated asking ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** For a manager agent this means diagnosing conversion problems in the process and scripts before attributing them to lead quality, while still reporting lead quality back to marketing as its own metric.

### Skill matters for the middle 80%

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He claims that roughly 10% of prospects never buy, roughly 10% always buy, and sales skill decides the middle 80% (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). The split is his own heuristic and unaudited. **[INFERENCE]** It is a useful planning frame (do not over-invest in the clear refusals, do not credit skill for the easy wins) and nothing more.

### Profit and premium pricing serve the mission

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** In a September 2026 episode he is summarised as framing profit and premium pricing as the means to serve more people, not as something opposed to mission ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents do not apologise for price; they tie price to outcome and stay inside the pricing rules in sections 7 and 11.

## 3. House style: the CLOSER conversation framework

### What CLOSER is and when to use it

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** CLOSER is a six-step, question-led call structure that Hormozi taught consistently from 2020 to 2024: Clarify, Label, Overview, Sell the vacation, Explain away concerns, Reinforce ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process); [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). The same six steps are restated in his sales compilation of 31 July 2024, so the framework was unchanged at that date ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Use it as the default structure for any live sales conversation with a single decision-maker or a small buying unit. **[GAP]** No primary source sets out separate phone, in-person or chat versions; third-party pages that publish such mappings are the vendors' own. A claim that the framework came from about 4,000 calls and that his teams use a 31-row rubric appears only on a vendor page and is unverified ([11x guide, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.11x.ai/guides/alex-hormozi-sales-training-method)).

### C: clarify why they are there

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The first step asks what prompted the conversation, what the goal is, why it matters and what success looks like in twelve months. The stated purpose is that a rep who does not know the reason pitches blind; he says reps commonly talk product for minutes without knowing the prospect's pain or goal ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process); [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). What to avoid: any product description before the reason, the goal and the main pain are known ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### L: label the problem

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The second step restates what was heard, names the gap between where the prospect is and where they want to be as their problem, and gets the prospect to confirm it. The purpose is that the prospect admits and owns the problem; he compares it to diagnosis before cure (2 June 2021, [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)).

### O: overview past attempts

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The third step, which he calls the pain cycle, asks what the prospect has tried, for how long and how it went, keeps asking what else until the list is exhausted, then recaps. The seller shows that earlier failures were not the prospect's fault because a piece was missing; his fitness example is exercise without nutrition and accountability. The purpose is to build awareness of the cost of the problem and to gather material to reuse later in the conversation (2 June 2021, [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)).

### S: sell the outcome, not the process

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The fourth step pitches the destination and not the journey: outcomes, not modules, features or process, because process sounds like work. He recommends roughly three short stories or analogies of about 30 seconds each, tied to the pillars of the solution, and a whole pitch under about three minutes ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process); [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). His travel analogy is one destination reached at different service levels. **[INFERENCE]** This is consistent in direction with Gong's 2017 demo data, in which no winning demo had an uninterrupted pitch longer than 76 seconds ([Gong Labs demo study, Sep 2017](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/sales-demos/)); see section 15.

### E: explain away concerns

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The fifth step handles objections with memorised responses and stories and not improvised answers, walks the prospect through the decision with simple yes or no questions, asks for the main concern, resolves it and asks again. The seller does not ask for the sale until the pain has been clarified ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process); [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). The objection content is in section 4, and the limits on repeated asking are in section 11.

### R: reinforce the decision

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The sixth step sends something personal immediately after payment (a short founder video, a handwritten card or a small gift) to reduce buyer's remorse. He claims customers decide in roughly the first 48 hours whether they will stay, and that selling continues at onboarding and at the first check-in ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process); [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). The 48-hour figure is his own unaudited claim.

### Operating rules that sit on top of CLOSER

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Four rules govern delivery. A rep says only three kinds of thing: questions, restatements of what the prospect said, and short belief-changing stories ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). Every script step is written as a question, because questions need less skill and so scale across a team (3 December 2020, [Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). Reps are trained on the prospect's problem and not on the product ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). Top closers listen about twice as much as they talk, and preparation takes about 10% of call length, six minutes for a 60-minute call (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). The listening ratio is his claim; Gong's data in section 15 points the same direction with different numbers.

### Obstacles before the ask, objections after it

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He distinguishes obstacles, which arise before the ask, from objections, which arise after it, and teaches that obstacles are easier to handle, so partner, time and money concerns should be surfaced before price is mentioned (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** For agents this becomes a pre-offer checklist inside the Clarify and Overview steps: who else is involved in the decision, what timing constraints exist, and whether funds are accessible.

### The related AAA pattern for handling a concern

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** A summary of a December 2024 episode describes Acknowledge, Associate, Ask: repeat the concern neutrally, tie it to a past customer or credible figure who felt the same and went ahead, then ask for the sale again. The same summary describes answering a question with a question when no planned answer exists, and treating many objections as preferences about how to reach the goal ([Shortform summary of Ep 810, 20 Dec 2024](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2024-12-20-episode-summary-closing-made-easy-the-triple-a-framework-explained-ep-810)). Only the free preview of that summary was read. See section 11 for the limit on answering questions with questions.

### One-to-many selling and direct messages

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** For webinar selling he advised a consumer brand in September 2026 to shorten the presentation, open with a hook and a brief origin story, use an old-way and new-way structure, and answer the top three objections, ranked by how often they appear in direct messages, before the offer. In the same episode he suggested AI-suggested replies to recurring direct-message questions, reviewed by staff, and routing direct-message interest into a weekly webinar instead of one-to-one chats ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)).

### Adapting CLOSER to chat, phone and in-person channels

**[INFERENCE]** For chat and direct-message agents, CLOSER maps to one question per message: Clarify and Label as short questions plus a one-line restatement, Overview as a question about what has been tried, Sell as one or two brief customer stories, Explain using the responses in section 4, and Reinforce as an immediate personalised welcome. For phone the emphasis shifts to tone and recorded review; for in-person selling his own gym examples imply the same sequence with payment taken on the spot. These adaptations are the researchers' own and not his published guidance. **[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** For remote teams he recommends recording every call, naming Gong as a tool (3 December 2020, [Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)); recording rules are in section 29.

## 4. House style: objection categories and responses

### Five buckets, layered from circumstances to other people to self

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He sorts resistance into five buckets: time, money, fit, authority and avoidance. They run in layers, from blaming circumstances (time, money) to blaming other people (spouse, partner) to blaming self (avoidance), a layering he credits to psychologist Albert Ellis (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). A second-hand index confirms five categories and places value under money as relative value ([tubegraph objections index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/understanding_sales_objections_and_obstacles)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents code every objection into one of these five buckets and treat "not worth it" as a money or value objection that sends the conversation back to Clarify, Label and Overview, since his earlier teaching is that a price objection means the value was not conveyed ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)).

### Five beliefs that must be in place before the ask

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He lists five beliefs a prospect must hold before a close: they want the goal, they believe the offer gets them there, they believe it works the way they want, they believe others will support them, and they believe it will work for them personally ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** These five work as a diagnostic checklist: an objection names the missing belief.

### Expect a first refusal and stay curious

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He teaches reps to expect a first refusal, on the reasoning that if the prospect were already decided a salesperson would not be needed, and to respond with curiosity (asking the prospect to say more) instead of rebuttal ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[VENDOR]** This matches Gong's 2018 finding on 67,149 recordings that top reps pause, ask clarifying questions and stay interactive after an objection while average reps deliver a reflex monologue of about 21 seconds ([Gong Labs, Mar 2018](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/handling-sales-objections/)).

### Time objections

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** For the "busy season" form he asks whether the prospect will ever not be busy; if not, the skill to learn is doing it while busy. For "no time in my day" the response is that the fix is removing low-value activity, not adding hours. For "when I have X, I'll start" the response is that the programme is often what produces X, delivered with empathy and a similar personal experience and not as an argument ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Money and price objections

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** His money responses are: ask whether it is a lot of money to them, and if so, that is why the result matters; compare the price with the return (large in absolute terms, small against the outcome); point out that people pay either in money or in time; argue resourcefulness over resources; and anchor against costlier, slower alternatives ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Earlier teaching adds that the seller never competes on price and that a delayed close (card or deposit now, balance later) lowers resistance (2 June 2021, [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). Section 11 restricts the resourcefulness push and the deposit mechanics for consumers.

### Fit objections

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** For fit he uses identity (spending and time are votes for who the person becomes), a contrast between the pain of staying the same and the pain of changing, and a hypothetical question: if this were perfect, would the prospect do it, and what separates this from perfect. If nothing concrete separates them, he reads the real issue as trust in the rep ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Authority, spouse and decision-maker objections

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He raises the other decision-maker before the pitch where possible. If the partner is absent, the rep asks what the partner would object to and handles that; asks whether the partner wants the prospect to succeed; and reframes from seeking permission to seeking support. He says a decision-maker objection usually means the rep missed something earlier ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). He offers a short refund window of three days so the prospect can buy now and still talk at home, advice repeated in a second-hand summary from September 2026 ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). In 2021 he also mentioned acting first and seeking forgiveness on smaller tickets and telling buyers the agreement can be cancelled if the partner objects ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)); section 11 bars the first of those for agents.

### Avoidance and "I need to think about it"

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** The core move is to ask what the main concern is, or what the prospect is most afraid of, and offer to think it through together; his reasoning is that buyers fear a mistake and would prefer to let circumstances decide. He then uses a decision checklist of three or four yes or no questions, with wording that varies by video: will this help reach the goal, does the prospect trust the seller to deliver, will it work for them, can they access the funds. Other reframes: they have already been thinking about it for years; one bad past purchase should not cost them twice; not deciding is itself a decision; an informed decision is only possible from inside the programme, so use a trial or guarantee; decisions need to be directionally right, not perfect ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[CONTESTED]** The diagnosis (fear of a mistake) matches the JOLT research; several of the reframes add pressure, which the same research found counter-productive. Section 11 states which to follow.

### Looping: resolve, then ask again

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** After each refusal he teaches the rep to resolve the real concern and ask again, as often as needed, provided the concern is actually resolved ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The house rule for agents is narrower: loop only while a new, specific concern is being surfaced and resolved, and stop at a clear refusal. The legal reason is in section 11.

### Named closes circulating in 2026

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** An article of 11 June 2026 summarising one of his social videos lists eight closes; the names and steps were not checked against the original ([BAM, 11 Jun 2026](https://nowbam.com/8-closes-from-alex-hormozi-that-work-for-real-estate-agents/)). They are: the main-concern close (ask the concern, then the fear, then offer an exaggerated fear for the prospect to correct); the hypothetical close (if perfect, would you, and what is the gap); the zoom-out close (return to the goal named at the start); the one-to-ten close (rate readiness and ask what would make it a nine or ten); the comparison close (if both options cost the same, which would you pick and why); the good, fast, cheap close (pick two); the game-plan close (ask permission, then the decision questions); and the closer-or-further close (does this move you closer to or further from where you want to be in five years). **[GAP]** The official names or count of his all-purpose closes could not be confirmed on a primary transcript, and an older best-case, worst-case close exists only as an episode listing that was not read ([Ep 52 listing](https://mofibo.com/dk/podcasts/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-121932/reverse-risk-and-make-more-money-my-favorite-close-the-best-case-worst-case-close-its-made-me-a-ton-of-sales-and-helped-a-lot-of-people-take-the-next-step-ep-52-5433484)).

### False urgency is removed, even inside the house style

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** In one team turnaround he describes removing false urgency and scarcity from messaging as part of raising show rates (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The house style therefore does not require invented deadlines; the legal ban in section 29 and the house style point the same way.

## 5. House style: qualification, speed to lead, show rates and follow-up

### Qualification as he teaches it

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Setters qualify for the problem and for budget and do not quote price in a way that disqualifies people; closers do not pitch until they know why the prospect is there, the goal and the main pain ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[GAP]** No primary source was found in which he teaches BANT or a variant by that name. **[INFERENCE]** His implied qualification set is close to BANT without the label: need and pain (Clarify, Label), budget (the setter check and access to funds), authority (raising the partner question before price) and timing (the time obstacle). If the department uses an acronym checklist, present it as house terminology and not as his.

### Speed to lead: contact within about a minute

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** In a video of 8 March 2026 he is summarised as setting a target of contact inside 60 seconds, citing a 391% lift from fast contact, recommending one full-time person whose only job is calling leads as they arrive, and describing an owner closing about 55% of leads against a typical 10% ([Summify summary of 'How to Close More Leads', 8 Mar 2026](https://summify.io/discover/how-to-close-more-leads/)). These are his claims. **[VENDOR]** The 391% figure matches a 2012 vendor study by Velocify on about 3.5 million leads, which reported that calling within one minute raised conversion 391% ([Ainora study roundup, 2026](https://ainora.lt/blog/lead-response-time-statistics-every-study-2026)); treat it as directional. A second-hand index says he treated leads left for 30 minutes to several hours, and a failure to double-dial, as faults ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). Sections 11, 27 and 28 limit immediate calling by consent, register and calling-hour rules.

### Call new bookings immediately and be available when prospects are

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He teaches calling a new booking immediately to qualify and, if the prospect is free, running the sales conversation then. He says being available when prospects are, including evenings and weekends, was the strongest predictor of total sales across his companies (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). That statement is his own unaudited claim.

### Book close in time and pull appointments forward

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Same-day or next-day appointments show better than those booked five or more days out, and future appointments should be pulled forward into open same-day slots ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[VENDOR]** A single-company data point agrees: at Cognism, meetings booked more than four days out showed at about 60% in 2025 ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)).

### Personal reminders, not robotic ones

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Reminders should be a personalised voice memo or video from the setter or closer, including on the morning of the appointment, in preference to automated texts, with optional small personal pre-call touches ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** A second-hand index describes a three-way introduction in which a nurture specialist joined setter, closer and prospect to transfer rapport ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[INFERENCE]** For AI agents, "personal" means a reminder that references the prospect's stated goal and names who they will meet; AI-generated voice messages fall under the automated-calling and AI-disclosure rules in sections 27 and 31.

### Always book a meeting from a meeting

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** No conversation ends without a scheduled next step ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[VENDOR]** Gong's 2021 analysis of 8,382 deals found deals where next steps were never discussed closed at 5% against 20% where they were ([Gong Labs, Jul 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/driving-next-steps-isnt-enough-this-is-what-really-moves-deals-forward/)).

### Between-call work and the priority list

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He prescribes a separate off-call checklist for working leads, replacing non-responders with responsive leads, and keeping a visible priority list of high-value prospects to pursue ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Show-rate and setter benchmarks he uses

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** He is summarised as using about 70% as the show-rate benchmark for appointment businesses and about three booked appointments a day per setter, having raised one team's target from two to four ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** His case claims, unaudited: one team moved show rate from 49% to about 70%, close rate from 27% to 41%, upfront cash from 47% to 82% and monthly sales from 56 to 93 in about 60 days; a second moved first-call show from 74% to 91%, the share booking the close call from 53% to 94% and close-call show from 63% to 87% ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Lead quality is a marketing metric

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** He is summarised as telling teams to optimise advertising for cost per sale and not the cheapest lead, citing a team that was attracting the wrong age group ([tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[INFERENCE]** The manager agent reports cost per sale and disqualification reasons by lead source back to the marketing department.

### What was not found on follow-up and no-shows

**[GAP]** No verified Hormozi source gives a numeric follow-up cadence (attempts, days, channel mix) or a scripted no-show or no-sale follow-up sequence; a third-party video on show-rate tactics exists but its summary would not load ([Recapio listing](https://recapio.com/digest/24-ways-alex-hormozi-increased-show-up-rate-by-stefan-van-de-vlasakker)). A commonly repeated "four pillars of lead nurture" label (availability, speed, personalisation, volume) is consistent with the verified items but was not found on a primary page. The free training page lists no standalone lead-nurture playbook as of 11 November 2026 ([acquisition.com/training, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/training)). Use the evidence-based cadences in sections 24 and 25 for the numbers.

## 6. House style: scripts, team system and stage metrics

### Scripts are question-based, memorised and recorded

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Question-based scripts make performance transferable and auditable, and recording every call keeps reps on script and compliant (3 December 2020, [Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). Stories and obstacle responses are memorised, with flashcard drilling of objection responses ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework); [Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). **[INFERENCE]** For agents, the script is the playbook prompt and the retrieval set of approved stories and responses; "memorised, not improvised" becomes "retrieved from the approved set, not generated freely".

### Daily training and the daily huddle

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He describes daily training of about 60 minutes, five days a week: roughly 25 minutes reading the script aloud, a few minutes drawing out obstacles, then role-play, plus recording reviews (2 June 2021, [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). The daily huddle reads customer testimonials aloud to sustain belief through rejection ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)).

### Weekly one-to-one on three tagged calls

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** Each week the rep submits three tagged calls (best, worst, average) and the manager checks four things: whether the problem was clarified, whether past attempts and pain were overviewed, whether the sale was asked for, and whether objections were handled ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). He also reviews hot streaks, not only failures, and uses recordings to train new reps ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The manager agent's transcript rubric starts from those four checks, extended with the compliance checks in section 31.

### Competition, leaderboards and removing the bottom performers

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He runs a daily leaderboard, a close alert, six-week contests, three-person teams and a prize budget of around 25% of a month's pay per head, and removes the bottom 10% on a fixed rhythm, claiming roughly a 30% productivity jump each time ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). The productivity figure is unaudited. **[INFERENCE]** For agents, the analogue is retiring or retraining the lowest-converting prompt or script variants on a fixed cycle; the human-resources practice itself is outside this knowledge base.

### Setter then closer, with a video between calls

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** In his 2024 case the flow is a first call with a setter, then a close call booked the same or next day, with a video sales letter between calls covering benefits and pre-handling objections. Setters are paid on shows, not only bookings ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[GAP]** No verified statement was found of when he prefers a one-call close over a two-call close by price point.

### In-house over outsourced, and total sales cost under 10%

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He describes replacing an outsourced team paid 20% of revenue with an in-house team at about 9% all-in including the director; close rate fell from about 80% to 42% but total sales rose because far more prospects reached the close call. He targets total sales cost under 10% of revenue, pays setters and closers separately with closers paid more, and ratchets commission up with close rate, with illustrative tiers of 10%, 15% and 25% ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). All figures are his claims.

### Hire the director first and remove reps who will not follow the process

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He advises hiring one strong sales director first (experience in the same product or industry, metric-driven, coaching temperament), found by direct outreach or recruiter at a volume of hundreds to a thousand messages, who then builds the team; reps who will not follow the process are removed ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Stage metrics: show rate, offer rate, close rate, cash collected

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He tracks show rate, offer rate, close rate and cash collected, not close rate alone, and measures close rate against both shows and offers so that reps cannot inflate it by offering only to the easiest prospects. The best leads are routed to the best closers ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion)). **[INFERENCE]** The department's house funnel is leads, contacted, booked, showed, offered, closed and cash collected, with two close-rate views (per show and per offer) reported side by side.

### Diagnostic sequence for a broken sales process

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** His turnaround order is: secret-shop the process, find the constraint, anticipate objections, install the new process, then add recurring revenue ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The manager agent can run the same order on its own funnel: sample real conversations, find the stage with the worst conversion against history, fix that stage first.

### What the paid workshop says the system contains

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, checked 11 Nov 2026]** His 2026 Sales Workshop page describes four systems (who you hire, how you train, how you manage, what you feed the team) and a handbook with scripts, compensation structures, a ramp plan and tracking sheets, at $5,000 per seat for founders at $1 million or more in revenue ([acquisition.com Sales Workshop page, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/workshop-sales)). **[GAP]** That handbook is paid material and was not seen; no quota formula beyond setter daily appointment targets was verified.

## 7. House style: pricing and payment presentation at the close

### Anchor high, then step down through terms

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He sets the first number high enough to surprise and then steps down; his example is naming $100,000 before $10,000 (31 July 2024, [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[DURABLE]** Academic negotiation research supports opening first with a credible anchor and warns that extreme single numbers can read as greedy and damage trust ([Harvard Program on Negotiation, Mar 2026](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/anchoring-bias-negotiation-get-ahead-range-offer/)). Section 11 states the rule agents follow: the anchor must be a real, purchasable price or option.

### The payment ladder: full price, prepay, half down, plan

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He quotes the full price, then offers about 20% off for paying in full, about 10% off for half down, then a payment plan, and rolls the plan into automatic recurring billing at the end. His worked example is $99 a week for 45 weeks (about $4,500), about $3,600 prepaid and about $4,050 with half down ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** The point-of-sale order for agents is: anchor on the premium or full price, prepay incentive, half down, payment plan, feature downsell or conditional trial, then continuity. Consumer payment plans in Sweden fall under the credit rules in section 28, and automatic rollover falls under the renewal rules there.

### Never sell the identical thing for less

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** The rule summarised from his 2025 book is that a downsell changes terms or features and never sells the identical thing for less ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). **[VENDOR]** This is consistent with Ebsta and Pavilion's 2024 data, in which top performers cut negotiation time by 24% (49% on expansion deals) by not discounting ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)), and with the JOLT finding that discounting a wavering buyer tends to deepen indecision ([Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)).

### The diagnostic and prescription sale

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** For local businesses he describes a pre-sale questionnaire, collecting payment details up front, mapping current state, desired state and obstacle, then tying price to the outcome; he claims a lift in average customer value from about $200 to about $800 ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). The lift is unaudited. Collecting payment details before an agreement is restricted in section 11.

### Guarantees as a closing tool

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript and second-hand summary]** He uses a short no-penalty refund window of three days to defuse partner and "think about it" objections, and immediate onboarding to reduce remorse and refunds ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). **[VENDOR]** Gong's 2017 data associated risk-reversal language (opt-outs, guarantees, service levels) with a 32% higher win rate ([Gong, Apr 2017](https://www.gong.io/blog/winning-sales-conversations)). **[GAP]** No verified Hormozi policy on handling refund requests was found, and the guarantee types from his first book were not re-verified. Section 11 covers the interaction with the statutory 14-day withdrawal right.

### Presenting the offer stack

**[GAP]** Listing an offer's components and bonuses with their values before the price comes from his first book and was not re-verified on a primary source in this research, so it is not detailed here. Any stated value for a component must be truthful under the marketing rules in section 28.

## 8. House style: the money model of attraction, upsell, downsell and continuity offers

### What a money model is

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** A money model is a deliberate sequence of offers meant to recover customer acquisition cost fast; it is the subject of his third book, launched at a live virtual event on 16 August 2025 ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/); [Hormozi on X](https://x.com/AlexHormozi/status/1945911147045249104?lang=en)). All offer details below come from a third-party summary of the book and not from the book or the free course, and exact scripts for each offer were not verified. The sequencing advice in the same summary is to build one stage at a time, price new offers low to gather feedback and raise the price until revenue stops rising ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)).

### Attraction offers

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** Five attraction offers are named: win your money back; a giveaway; a decoy (a free or basic option beside a premium one); buy X, get Y free; and pay less now or pay more later ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). His gym example of the decoy pairs a free basic option with a $399 premium and claims an 80% take rate for the premium ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)); that rate is unaudited. Section 11 covers the EU rules on "free" claims, prize claims and bait offers that apply to these.

### Upsell offers

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** Four upsell offers are named: the classic upsell (solve the next problem); the menu upsell (unsell what the customer does not need, prescribe, offer a choice between two options instead of yes or no, and keep a card on file); the anchor upsell (show the premium first so the next option feels reasonable); and the rollover upsell (credit past spend toward a bigger offer) ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)).

### When to upsell

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** In September 2026 he is summarised as advising that the next offer be pitched at the halfway to two-thirds point of delivery, when enthusiasm peaks, and not at the end, citing conversion moving from about a third to 50 to 67%, and as recommending two clear checkout paths instead of an ambiguous menu ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). The conversion figures are his claims.

### Downsell offers

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** Three downsell offers are named: the payment plan; the trial with penalty (a free trial conditional on meeting terms, with a fee if they are not met); and the feature downsell (a lower price with features removed) ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)).

### Continuity offers

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** Three continuity offers are named: the continuity bonus; the continuity discount (free time in exchange for a longer commitment); and the waived fee (a setup fee waived for commitment). Billing every four weeks gives 13 billing cycles a year ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). **[VENDOR]** Independent platform data supports the commitment logic: annual plans show 10 to 20 points higher median net revenue retention than monthly plans in ChartMogul's data as of September 2025 ([ChartMogul, The AI churn wave, 10 Dec 2025](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/)).

### His own business as a worked example

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, checked 11 Nov 2026]** His own site offers free books, courses and audiobooks with no sign-up as the top of the funnel, a free personalised roadmap, then $5,000 two-day workshops for founders at $1 million or more in revenue, then partnership by application ([acquisition.com/training, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/training); [acquisition.com Sales Workshop page, checked 11 Nov 2026](https://www.acquisition.com/workshop-sales)). **[INFERENCE]** That is a live example of the attraction-then-upsell logic: give away the core information and sell implementation and access.

## 9. House style: price raises, retention reinforcement, referrals and affiliates

### The six-part price-raise letter

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** For raising prices on existing customers he describes a letter in six parts: the context and why now; the value already delivered, including hidden costs the business has absorbed; improvements that are coming; the value or promise the raise protects; the increase framed in small units such as per day or per week; and a closing note inviting genuine hardship cases to talk privately. He delivers it by video and mail, disables public comments, handles complaints one to one and does not back down (19 July 2021, [Hormozi price-raise video, transcript, 19 Jul 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-raise-your-prices-plus-the-letter-i-actually-sent-to-customers)).

### His price-raise numbers

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He claims that in his gym example he tripled the price, lost about a third of customers and roughly doubled revenue, and gives the margin arithmetic that at a 20% margin a 20% raise doubles profit ([Hormozi price-raise video, transcript, 19 Jul 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-raise-your-prices-plus-the-letter-i-actually-sent-to-customers)). The gym outcome is unaudited; the arithmetic holds if volume is unchanged. **[FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** For calibration, Nordic software companies raised prices by an average of 5.9% in 2025, down from 6.8% in 2024, in Monterro's survey of 236 companies ([Monterro Nordic Software Benchmark 2026](https://www.monterro.com/benchmark-report-2026/)).

### Retention as selling

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He treats retention as a continuation of the sale: re-close at onboarding and at the first check-in, and act within the first 48 hours, which he claims shape long-term retention ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). **[INFERENCE]** This is the house-style instruction behind an onboarding stage with an owner and a time-to-first-value target (section 22).

### Referrals: earn goodwill, then ask

**[HOUSE STYLE; primary transcript]** He asks who the prospect knows at the end of calls and claims referred leads close at 80 to 90% ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)); the rate is unaudited. **[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** Summaries of his 2023 book on leads add a growth test (the share of customers referring per month minus the share churning per month gives compounding growth, so referrals must outpace churn) and define goodwill as the gap between value received and price paid, to be widened by raising value and not by cutting price ([Deciphr summary of Ep 593](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-8--customer-referrals-ep-593)).

### Six ways to become more referable

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** The six are: sell to better-fit customers; set expectations the business can beat; get every customer doing what the best customers do; speed up the first win (he pads timelines by 50% to deliver early); reduce the effort required of the customer; and always give a next step ([Deciphr summary of Ep 593](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-8--customer-referrals-ep-593)).

### Referral ask mechanics

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** The mechanics named are: split the average customer acquisition cost between referrer and new customer; trade a negotiated discount for three introductions; a transferable gift card worth a third of the programme; and time-boxed referral events of one to four weeks ([Deciphr summary of Ep 593](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-8--customer-referrals-ep-593)). **[GAP]** The individual ways to ask were not all listed in the source read, and the book was not checked directly. **[INFERENCE]** Agents can operationalise this as a referral ask at the Reinforce step and at each verified customer win, and an offer of a discount in exchange for introductions during negotiation, subject to the gift and data rules in sections 30 and 11.

### Affiliates and partners in six steps

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** His affiliate build is: find businesses whose audience matches your customer; make them an offer framed as a new revenue stream; qualify them by requiring commitment; make them customers and certified experts, charging for onboarding; pay on a tiered scale capped at the allowable acquisition cost, in product where possible; and get them advertising through staged launches. He keeps affiliates active by embedding the product in their offer, preferably by letting them white-label lead magnets, judges the channel by lifetime gross profit against acquisition cost, and claims over $75 million sold through affiliates without advertising ([Deciphr summary of Ep 595](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-10--affiliates-and-partners-ep-595)). The sales figure is unaudited. **[VENDOR]** Independent platform data rates partner referrals as the most efficient pipeline source, at 1.3 times average efficiency in 2024 data ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

## 11. Precedence rules: compliance first, evidence second, house style third

### The order of precedence

**[INFERENCE]** Three rules settle every conflict. First, compliance always overrides house style and evidence: if a law, regulator rule, platform term or the department's own guardrail blocks a move, the move is not made, whatever its expected conversion effect. Second, where independent evidence contradicts a house-style tactic, the agent follows the evidence in the situations the evidence covers (typically larger, multi-stakeholder deals and hesitant buyers) and the house style elsewhere, and this document states both. Third, the house style is the default wherever the first two are silent. The paragraphs below apply these rules to each known collision; legal points carry their verification status and must be confirmed with counsel before an agent gates on an unverified one.

### Brand voice: structures yes, impersonation no

**[INFERENCE]** Agents apply Hormozi's structures (the conversation steps, the objection buckets, the payment ladder, the offer sequence, the stage metrics) in the brand's own voice. With customers and prospects they do not imitate him personally, do not use his name or likeness, do not present the company as affiliated with or endorsed by him, and do not use his catchphrases or named labels (for example the acronym, the step names or the named closes). Internally, playbooks may use the labels as shorthand. The reasons are that the owner chose the structures and not the persona, that marketing must be identifiable and not misleading about who is behind it under Swedish marketing law (9 § of the Marketing Act, checked on primary source, [Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)), and that his results are his own unaudited claims which the company cannot substantiate as its own.

### His performance numbers are never customer-facing proof

**[INFERENCE]** Because all of his performance figures are self-reported and unaudited ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [Summify summary of 'How to Close More Leads', 8 Mar 2026](https://summify.io/discover/how-to-close-more-leads/)), agents never quote them to prospects as evidence of what the company's product or process will achieve. Customer-facing proof comes only from the company's own attributable customers. EU law treats claiming that reviews come from real users without reasonable checks, and commissioning false reviews, as always unfair (Annex I points 23b and 23c of the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, checked on primary source, [Directive 2005/29/EC, consolidated 28 May 2022](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02005L0029-20220528)).

### Pressure closing and looping versus the research on closing techniques in large sales

**[CONTESTED]** House style: expect a first refusal, resolve the concern and ask again as often as needed, using memorised closes ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Evidence: Rackham's research found traditional closing techniques reduced success in larger sales, and that in large sales closing and objection-handling skill made no significant difference to success ([Sales Society, Aug 2022](https://salessociety.substack.com/p/the-fascinating-science-of-spin-selling)). Rule for agents: in small, single-decision sales (consumer, owner-operator, low-ticket SMB) the house-style ask-resolve-ask pattern is permitted while a new, specific concern is being surfaced and resolved. In multi-stakeholder or large deals, agents do not use closing techniques; they secure a dated next step that requires a buyer action (section 15). In every segment a clear refusal ends the attempt, because persistent unwanted solicitation by phone, email or other remote media is always unfair under Annex I point 26 of the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive (checked on primary source, [Directive 2005/29/EC, consolidated 28 May 2022](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02005L0029-20220528)) and Swedish regulator guidance expects sellers to respect a wish to end the call and give reasonable time to reflect (checked on primary source, [Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)).

### Reframes for "I need to think about it" versus the research on indecision

**[CONTESTED]** House style: answer hesitation with reframes such as years already spent thinking, not deciding being a decision, and another year of the status quo ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Evidence: in the JOLT research on about 2.5 million recorded sales conversations, preference for the status quo explained under half of no-decision losses and fear of making a mistake explained the rest; when buyers wavered, 73% of reps re-argued the case for change, and adding fear or discounts made a loss more likely in 84% of cases ([Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/); [CustomerThink interview with Matt Dixon](https://customerthink.com/the-problem-of-customer-indecision-and-how-to-get-over-it-interview-with-matt-dixon/)). This is vendor and author research, not independently replicated. Rule for agents: first diagnose. If the buyer disputes the problem or its size, the house-style case for change applies. If the buyer agrees but stalls, keeps asking for more information or cannot choose between options, agents follow the indecision playbook (judge the indecision, offer one recommendation, limit exploration, take risk off the table) and do not add urgency, fear or discounts. The house-style elements that fit both are kept: asking for the main concern, the short decision checklist, and the guarantee.

### Urgency and scarcity versus EU consumer law

**[CONTESTED]** House style: his money model includes pay-less-now-or-more-later offers and time-boxed events ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)), though he also describes removing false urgency and scarcity in a team turnaround ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Law: falsely stating that a product is available only for a very short time is always unfair (Annex I point 7), as are bait advertising and bait and switch (points 5 and 6) and false closing-down claims (point 15) (checked on primary source, [Directive 2005/29/EC, consolidated 28 May 2022](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02005L0029-20220528)); Sweden gives the blacklist direct effect through the Marketing Act, with a market disturbance fee from SEK 10,000 up to 4% of turnover (checked on primary source, [Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)). In the US, an FTC staff report of September 2022 lists fake countdown timers and false scarcity claims among deceptive designs (secondary source only, [Venable summary of FTC dark-patterns report, Sep 2022](https://www.venable.com/insights/blogs/2022/09/the-ftc-brings-more-light-to-dark-patterns-in-new)). Evidence: a practitioner's 72 A/B tests found scarcity and urgency won 2 of 10 tests, and the wins involved genuine scarcity (single practitioner, March 2026, [Atticus Li, Mar 2026](https://www.atticusli.com/blog/posts/behavioral-science-claims-vs-experiment-reality/)). Rule for agents: a deadline, limited quantity, price-rise date or "others are interested" statement is used only when a system of record confirms it is true at the moment of sending, and it is never extended or reset to manufacture pressure. This applies to business buyers as well as consumers as house policy.

### Accepting a consumer order during a call versus the Swedish written-confirmation rule

**[CONTESTED]** House style: close on the call, take payment on the spot, and call new bookings immediately to sell if the prospect is free ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Law: under the Swedish Distance Contracts Act (2 kap. 3 a §), when a trader calls a consumer on its own initiative the offer must be confirmed in writing or another durable form, and the contract is concluded only when the consumer accepts in writing after the call; otherwise it is invalid (checked on primary source, consolidated through SFS 2026:1018, [Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)). The Swedish Consumer Agency states that acceptance during the call, for example by BankID, makes the contract invalid, that the rule covers new and existing customers, and that it also applies when the trader prompted the consumer to call, though not when the consumer calls unprompted (checked on primary source, [Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/); [Konsumentverket, consumer page](https://www.konsumentverket.se/konsumentratt/telefonforsaljning-regler/)). Rule for agents: no voice or chat agent takes an order, a payment or a binding yes from a Swedish consumer during a trader-initiated or trader-prompted call. The compliant flow is call, written offer, the consumer's own written acceptance afterwards. **[GAP]** Whether a call placed in response to a consumer's own booking or callback request counts as trader-initiated was not examined in the research; treat it as covered until counsel says otherwise.

### Speed to lead, double-dialling and evening or weekend calling versus consent, registers and calling hours

**[CONTESTED]** House style: call within 60 seconds, double-dial, and be available evenings and weekends ([Summify summary of 'How to Close More Leads', 8 Mar 2026](https://summify.io/discover/how-to-close-more-leads/); [tubegraph lead-management index](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/challenges_in_lead_management_and_conversion); [Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Law and codes: Swedish consumer numbers must be checked against the NIX register before sales calls (checked on primary source, [Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/); [NIX-Telefon för företag](https://www.nixtelefon.org/for-foretag)); the Swedish industry code for consumer telephone sales limits calls to weekdays 08 to 21 and weekends 10 to 18, with at most two calls a day per number per campaign and 20 in 30 days (secondary source only, [Kontakta/SWEDMA ethical rules, 1 Jul 2021, PDF copy](https://telekomradgivarna.se/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/etiska-regler-for-forsaljning-och-marknadsforing-over-telefon-till-konsument-2021-07-01.pdf)); France restricts consumer cold-call hours and requires prior consent from 11 August 2026 (secondary source only, [HubSpot France](https://blog.hubspot.fr/sales/alternatives-demarchage-telephonique)); US rules add do-not-call scrubbing and calling windows (unverified, not fetched, [FTC, Complying with the Telemarketing Sales Rule, modified 8 Oct 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule)). Rule for agents: the 60-second target applies in full to written acknowledgement and booking links, and to phone only where the lead supplied the number and asked to be contacted, the country and recipient type permit the call, the register check is satisfied and the time falls inside permitted hours. Double-dialling a consumer number is capped by the applicable code.

### AI voice callbacks versus automated-calling rules

**[CONTESTED]** House style: a dedicated caller phones every new lead at once ([Summify summary of 'How to Close More Leads', 8 Mar 2026](https://summify.io/discover/how-to-close-more-leads/)). Law: in Sweden automated calling systems to natural persons need prior consent (19 § of the Marketing Act, checked on primary source, [Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)); the same holds in Germany and Norway ([UWG, English text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_uwg/englisch_uwg.html); [Forbrukertilsynet guidance, updated 8 May 2026](https://www.forbrukertilsynet.no/lov-og-rett/veiledninger-og-retningslinjer/forbrukertilsynets-veiledning-markedsforing-via-e-post-sms-o-l)); in the UK automated calls require specific consent (checked on primary source, [ICO, B2B marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)); in the US the FCC has confirmed that AI-generated human-sounding voices are artificial voices under the TCPA, so such calls generally need prior express consent (checked on primary source, [Federal Register, 10 Sep 2024](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/09/10/2024-19028/implications-of-artificial-intelligence-technologies-on-protecting-consumers-from-unwanted-robocalls)). Rule for agents: no synthetic-voice outbound call is placed without recorded prior consent that covers automated or AI calls for that person; without it, the instant response is written and the call is made by a human.

### Card on file, penalty trials and automatic rollover versus consumer protection

**[CONTESTED]** House style: collect payment details up front, keep a card on file for upsells, offer a free trial with a fee if conditions are not met, and roll payment plans into automatic recurring billing ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). Law: describing something as free when the consumer must pay more than unavoidable costs is always unfair (Annex I point 20), as is demanding payment for unsolicited products (point 29) (checked on primary source, [Directive 2005/29/EC, consolidated 28 May 2022](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02005L0029-20220528)); Swedish consumers have a 14-day withdrawal right on distance contracts, and from 19 June 2026 contracts concluded through an online interface need an easily reachable withdrawal function (checked on primary source for the statute, secondary source for the application date, [Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/); [CMS Wistrand, 15 Jun 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/new-withdrawal-button-required-on-swedish-e-commerce-platforms-from-19-june-2026)); for fixed-term consumer contracts that renew automatically the trader must send a written reminder at least one month before the last termination date, failing which the consumer may terminate immediately after renewal (checked on primary source, [Lag (2014:1449)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2014-1449.html)). Rule for agents: payment details are collected only with the terms, total price, renewal mechanics and withdrawal right stated in writing first; a conditional trial states the fee and conditions before sign-up and is never called free without that qualification; card-on-file charges for a new offer need a fresh, recorded consent; renewal reminders are scheduled automatically.

### Payment plans versus the Swedish consumer credit act

**[CONTESTED]** House style: offer a payment plan as the standard downsell ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). Law: the Riksdag adopted a new Consumer Credit Act on 27 May 2026, entering into force on 20 November 2026, implementing Directive (EU) 2023/2225 (checked on primary source, [Riksdagen, bet. 2025/26:CU26](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/_HD01CU26/)); the government's summary says it covers more credit types, including interest-free credit offered in stores by a third party, tightens creditworthiness assessment and requires more credit providers and brokers to be authorised (checked on primary source, [Regeringen, 30 Mar 2026](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/03/regeringen-foreslar-ny-konsumentkreditlag-for-starkt-skydd-for-konsumenter/)). **[GAP]** The act's exact scope for instalment and invoice arrangements, its marketing rules and cost caps were not confirmed. Rule for agents: any offer of instalments, deferred invoice or third-party financing to a Swedish consumer is routed to a human-run, legally reviewed process; agents may mention that payment options exist but do not arrange, price or approve credit.

### The three-day guarantee versus the statutory withdrawal right

**[INFERENCE]** House style uses a three-day no-penalty refund window to resolve partner and hesitation objections ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide); [Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). For Swedish consumer distance contracts the statutory withdrawal period is 14 days and never starts before withdrawal information is given (checked on primary source, [Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)). Rule for agents: a guarantee is offered only as something additional to statutory rights and is never worded so that a consumer could understand it as replacing or shortening them; for consumers the agent states the statutory right first. This reading is the researchers' inference and was not checked with counsel.

### Acting first and asking forgiveness on partner objections

**[INFERENCE]** House style in 2021 mentioned proceeding without the partner on smaller tickets ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). Rule for agents: this tactic is not used. Agents use the compliant parts of his authority handling instead: raise the other decision-maker early, ask what that person would want to know, offer written material for them, and state the withdrawal right. Swedish regulator guidance expects reasonable time to reflect ([Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)), and Swedish marketing law bans aggressive marketing (7 § of the Marketing Act, checked on primary source, [Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)).

### Resourcefulness pushes with financially stretched consumers

**[INFERENCE]** House style argues that people find money for what matters ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Rule for agents: when a consumer says they cannot afford the purchase, the agent does not argue that they can find the money and does not suggest borrowing; it may offer a lower-priced option with fewer features or end the conversation courteously. The basis is the aggressive-marketing ban ([Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)), the Swedish Consumer Agency's stated expectation of no pressure ([Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)), and the house-style rule itself that the prospect comes before the sale ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### High anchors versus truthful pricing and the evidence on anchoring

**[CONTESTED]** House style: name a number high enough to surprise before the real price ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). Evidence: anchoring is robust in negotiation research, but extreme anchors read as greedy and damage trust, and ranges that start at the target and stretch roughly 5 to 20% above it outperform single-point offers in Ames and Mason's 2015 experiments ([Harvard Program on Negotiation, Mar 2026](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/anchoring-bias-negotiation-get-ahead-range-offer/)). Law: a price-reduction claim to consumers in Sweden must show the lowest price applied in the 30 days before the cut (7 a § of the Price Information Act, checked on primary source, [Prisinformationslag (2004:347)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/prisinformationslag-2004347_sfs-2004-347/)), and an offer with a price must include main characteristics, price and trader identity (12 § of the Marketing Act, [Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)). Rule for agents: the anchor is always a real, purchasable price or package from the price list; agents never invent a "normal" price to discount from; in multi-issue B2B deals they present options with objective criteria instead of an extreme opener.

### Free, giveaway and money-back attraction offers versus the blacklist

**[INFERENCE]** House-style attraction offers include giveaways, free decoys and win-your-money-back offers ([SuperSummary of $100M Money Models](https://www.supersummary.com/100m-money-models/summary/)). The blacklist bans calling something free when the consumer pays more than unavoidable costs (point 20), false prize claims (point 31) and bait advertising (point 5) ([Directive 2005/29/EC, consolidated 28 May 2022](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02005L0029-20220528)). Rule for agents: every attraction offer is described with its full conditions in the same message as the headline, the free option must be genuinely available, and any prize or refund is paid exactly as stated.

### Answering a question with a question versus buyers' need for clear answers

**[CONTESTED]** House style (second-hand): when no planned answer exists, return a question ([Shortform summary of Ep 810, 20 Dec 2024](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2024-12-20-episode-summary-closing-made-easy-the-triple-a-framework-explained-ep-810)). Evidence: Gartner frames value clarity as the enablement priority and recommends content that buyers and their AI tools can validate ([Gartner press release, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience)); Forrester reports buyers validate answer-engine output with humans because it is incomplete ([Forrester press release, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/)). Rule for agents: factual questions about price, terms, capabilities, data handling and limitations are answered directly and accurately first; a clarifying question may follow. An agent that does not know says so and escalates.

### Comparison closes versus comparative-advertising rules

**[INFERENCE]** One of the named closes asks the prospect to compare the offer with a cheaper alternative ([BAM, 11 Jun 2026](https://nowbam.com/8-closes-from-alex-hormozi-that-work-for-real-estate-agents/)). Swedish law allows comparative advertising only if it is not misleading, compares objectively and does not discredit another trader (18 § of the Marketing Act, checked on primary source, [Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)). Rule for agents: the prospect may be asked to make their own comparison; any statement the agent makes about a competitor needs a dated, verifiable source in the knowledge base and may not disparage.

### Price-raise notices with comments disabled

**[INFERENCE]** House style delivers price rises firmly and disables public comments ([Hormozi price-raise video, transcript, 19 Jul 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-raise-your-prices-plus-the-letter-i-actually-sent-to-customers)). Rule for agents: the six-part structure is kept, but notice periods follow the contract and the renewal-reminder law for consumers ([Lag (2014:1449)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2014-1449.html)), the notice states how to terminate, and no agent suppresses or deletes customer complaints; complaints are routed to a human. Whether to disable comments on a public post is a brand decision for the marketing department.

### Recording every call versus notice and consent rules

**[INFERENCE]** House style records every call ([Hormozi sales-team video, transcript, 3 Dec 2020](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-i-built-a-20-sales-team-the-process)). Rule for agents: every call and meeting opens with an announcement that it is recorded and transcribed by an AI tool and for what purpose, and a refusal is honoured; the legal basis is in section 29.

### Referral incentives and gifts versus anti-bribery standards and data rules

**[INFERENCE]** House style uses two-sided referral rewards, gift cards and gifts after purchase ([Deciphr summary of Ep 593](https://www.deciphr.ai/podcast/100m-leads-audiobook-part-8--customer-referrals-ep-593); [Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). Rule for agents: rewards to consumers are stated openly in the offer; in B2B, no agent offers a gift, gift card, event ticket or personal reward to an individual at a customer or prospect without human approval, and never to public-sector or procurement contacts, in line with the Swedish business anti-corruption code (checked on primary source for existence and scope, [Institutet Mot Mutor, Näringslivskoden](https://www.institutetmotmutor.se/naringslivskoden/)). A referred person's contact details are personal data: the agent asks the referrer to make the introduction or confirms the lawful basis before first contact (section 30).

## 12. Mapping the house style onto a SaaS business and onto B2B deals of different sizes

### Why a mapping is needed

**[GAP]** No verified primary source gives SaaS-specific or enterprise-specific sales guidance from Hormozi; his examples are consumer and small-business services ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)). **[INFERENCE]** Everything in this section is the researchers' mapping of his structures onto SaaS and onto B2B deals of different sizes, using the sourced evidence in sections 13 to 22. The deal-size bands used are practitioner conventions that were not fetched from a source; tune them against the department's own data.

### What transfers to SaaS unchanged

**[INFERENCE]** Five parts of the house style transfer to SaaS without change: speed to lead on demo requests, trial sign-ups that meet the product-qualified threshold and pricing enquiries; the Clarify, Label and Overview sequence as the discovery structure; outcome-first presentation in demos; measuring every stage through to cash collected and beyond; and the Reinforce step as structured onboarding. **[VENDOR]** The supporting data: top inbound teams convert a median 62% of qualified form fills into booked meetings when a calendar is shown immediately ([RevenueHero, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.revenuehero.io/blog/speed-to-lead-benchmarks-for-saas)); winning demos open with the topic the buyer spent most time on in discovery ([Gong Labs demo study, Sep 2017](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/sales-demos/)); and feature-dumping correlates with win rates falling from 26% to 5% ([Gong Labs, Jul 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/driving-next-steps-isnt-enough-this-is-what-really-moves-deals-forward/)).

### Attraction offers become free trials, freemium and reverse trials

**[INFERENCE]** In SaaS the attraction offer is the free entry point: a time-boxed trial, a freemium tier or a reverse trial. The decoy offer maps to a basic tier displayed beside a recommended tier. The conditional trial maps most closely to a card-required trial, with the consumer-law limits in section 11. **[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Conversion data relayed from a ChartMogul, Growth Unhinged and ProductLed report on 200 B2B products: free-to-paid medians of 4 to 6% for trials without a card, 25 to 35% for trials with a card and 3 to 5% for freemium, and per 1,000 visitors about 10.5 payers for a card-required trial against 3.6 for a standard trial and about 5 for freemium ([Userpilot summary](https://userpilot.com/blog/free-trial-conversion-rate/)). The figures come through an aggregator page.

### Speed to lead becomes product-qualified lead routing

**[INFERENCE]** For trial and freemium users, the setter function is triggered by usage and not by a form: define a product-qualified threshold (fit, activation and intent) and have a seller or agent contact only those users. **[VENDOR]** ProductLed defines a product-qualified lead by fit, value reached and intent, and reports that such leads convert to paid at a 25% median against a 9% baseline in its survey of more than 600 SaaS companies ([ProductLed on PQLs, 2021, updated Jun 2024](https://productled.com/blog/how-to-define-pqls); [ProductLed benchmarks](https://productled.com/blog/product-led-growth-benchmarks)); the survey date is uncertain.

### The payment ladder becomes annual prepay versus monthly

**[INFERENCE]** His full-price, prepay-discount, half-down, plan ladder maps onto SaaS billing terms: list monthly price as the reference, annual prepay at a discount, then quarterly or monthly billing on an annual commitment, then month-to-month. His roughly 20% prepay incentive ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)) sits at the top of the commonly used annual-discount range, which is a practitioner convention of about 15 to 20% that was not sourced. **[VENDOR]** The retention case for pushing annual terms is sourced: annual plans show 10 to 20 points higher median net revenue retention than monthly plans ([ChartMogul, The AI churn wave, 10 Dec 2025](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/)).

### "Never the same thing for less" becomes tier and give-get discipline

**[INFERENCE]** The feature downsell maps to a lower tier or fewer seats; the terms downsell maps to a shorter commitment at a higher unit price. A discount on the identical package is given only in exchange for something (longer term, annual prepay, volume, a case study), under a written approval matrix. **[VENDOR]** One vendor's guardrail guidance says an AI sales agent should never negotiate custom pricing tiers, generate spontaneous discounts or alter standard payment terms ([YTT AI](https://www.ytt-ai.com/post/sales-ai-guardrails-what-your-agent-should-never-say)).

### Upsell offers become expansion motions

**[INFERENCE]** The classic upsell maps to cross-sell of an adjacent module; the menu upsell to a prescribed plan with unneeded add-ons removed; the anchor upsell to presenting the top tier first; the rollover upsell to crediting current spend toward an upgrade. His advice to pitch at the halfway to two-thirds point of delivery ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)) maps to expansion triggers such as usage approaching plan limits, a new team or use case, or a promoted champion. **[VENDOR]** Expansion supplies about 40% of new annual recurring revenue at the median in Benchmarkit's survey of 2024 data and wins at 45% against 19% for new business in Ebsta's ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf); [Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Continuity offers are the subscription itself

**[INFERENCE]** In SaaS, continuity is the core product. His continuity discount maps to multi-year or annual commitments, and the waived fee to waiving onboarding or setup fees for commitment. For consumers and sole traders the Swedish renewal-reminder rule applies ([Lag (2014:1449)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2014-1449.html)).

### Price raises become renewal uplifts

**[INFERENCE]** His six-part letter maps to a renewal notice: why now, value delivered (usage and outcomes from the customer's own account), what is coming, what the price protects, the increase in small units, and a route for hardship cases. **[FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** Nordic software companies averaged a 5.9% annual price increase in 2025 ([Monterro Nordic Software Benchmark 2026](https://www.monterro.com/benchmark-report-2026/)).

### Referrals and affiliates become partner programmes

**[INFERENCE]** His affiliate steps map to referral, reseller and integration partner programmes, with deal registration and separate tracking of partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue. **[VENDOR]** Partner referrals had the highest channel efficiency in Ebsta's 2024 data at 1.3 times the average ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Setter and closer become SDR and account executive, or one full-cycle role

**[INFERENCE]** His setter and closer split maps to the SDR and account-executive split; at low contract values or inbound-heavy volume a single full-cycle agent does both. **[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Full-cycle selling was at 45 to 46% adoption in Ebsta's report, and 38% of account executives had sole or shared ownership of renewals and expansion in the Bridge Group's 2026 survey ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en); [Bridge Group 2026 AE report, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2026-ae-models-motions-metrics)).

### Where the house style stops fitting: multi-stakeholder deals

**[CONTESTED]** The one-call close and the single-decision-maker objection model do not fit committee purchases. Forrester's 2026 survey of nearly 18,000 buyers reports an average of 13 internal and 9 external influencers, with procurement a decision-maker in 53% of buying cycles ([Forrester blog, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/state-of-business-buying-2026); [Forrester press release, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/)). **[INFERENCE]** In those deals the authority bucket stops being a partner objection and becomes stakeholder mapping; the avoidance bucket is handled with the indecision playbook; and closing techniques are replaced by mutual next steps (sections 13 to 15).

## 13. Established sales methodologies: what each is for and where it stops

### The methodologies do four different jobs

**[INFERENCE]** The named methodologies fall into four jobs: discovery and questioning (SPIN, Gap Selling, the Sandler pain funnel), insight and messaging (Challenger, Command of the Message), deal qualification and control (the MEDDIC family, Miller Heiman, NEAT), and late-stage decision support (JOLT). Only SPIN and the Challenger and JOLT work rest on large published observational datasets, and all of that research was run or commissioned by the firms that sell the training; none was found independently replicated. A practical mapping for the agent team: SPIN or Gap Selling question logic for discovery; MEDDPICC as the deal-record schema for anything multi-stakeholder; Miller Heiman roles as the stakeholder map; Challenger-style insight only at the "why change" stage and mainly for new customers; JOLT once the buyer has agreed in principle but hesitates; Sandler up-front contracts as the opening of every meeting.

### SPIN Selling: four question types for larger sales

**[DURABLE]** SPIN Selling by Neil Rackham rests on a study of more than 35,000 sales calls. Its four question types run in sequence: Situation (facts about the buyer's circumstances), Problem (pain points, which average sellers under-use), Implication (consequences and urgency of the problem) and Need-payoff (the buyer states how a solution would help). Top performers reportedly ask roughly ten times as many need-payoff questions as average performers; asking more questions correlated with success while open versus closed format made no difference; the most successful calls followed the SPIN pattern ([Sales Society, Aug 2022](https://salessociety.substack.com/p/the-fascinating-science-of-spin-selling); [Routledge listing](https://www.routledge.com/SPIN-Selling-1st-Edition/Rackham/p/book/9780566076893)). Use it for discovery in any deal with more than one decision-maker. **[GAP]** No primary Huthwaite source was fetched; details rely on a secondary newsletter. Unverified background knowledge: the book is usually dated 1988, the study is usually described as spanning 12 years and more than 20 countries, and Rackham distinguished implied from explicit needs.

### SPIN's finding on small versus large sales

**[DURABLE]** In Rackham's research small and large sales behaved differently: classic closing techniques hurt larger sales, and in large sales closing, probing and objection-handling skill made no significant difference to success ([Sales Society, Aug 2022](https://salessociety.substack.com/p/the-fascinating-science-of-spin-selling)). When to apply: any deal with a long cycle, several stakeholders and an ongoing relationship. What to avoid: transferring closing pressure from small-ticket selling into such deals.

### The Challenger Sale: teach, tailor, take control

**[VENDOR]** The Challenger Sale (Dixon and Adamson, 2011) is based on a study of more than 6,000 B2B reps and builds on provocation-based selling. It describes five rep profiles (Challenger, Hard Worker, Lone Wolf, reactive Problem Solver, Relationship Builder) and a three-part method: teach a commercial insight that exposes an unrecognised cost, risk or missed opportunity; tailor it to each stakeholder; and take control of the buying process, especially pricing and next steps, with constructive tension ([Corporate Visions on the Challenger model](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/challenger-sales-model/)). Commonly reported performance shares are that nearly 40% of star performers were Challengers, 54% in complex sales, and Relationship Builders 7% of high performers, from a secondary vendor summary that is internally inconsistent in places ([11x Challenger review](https://www.11x.ai/guides/challenger-sale-review)). Use it at the "why change" stage with new customers when the seller has real business expertise.

### Limits of the Challenger approach

**[VENDOR; CONTESTED]** A competitor's research claims provocative messaging to existing customers at renewal or expansion raised the risk of losing them by at least 10%, and that the model may suit only new-customer acquisition ([Corporate Visions on the Challenger model](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/challenger-sales-model/)). Other critiques: without real expertise it reads as preachy or arrogant, it assumes buyers welcome being challenged, the research focused on complex B2B and may not transfer to transactional selling, and it needs heavy investment in insight creation ([11x Challenger review](https://www.11x.ai/guides/challenger-sale-review)). The model's own lead author later found that re-running the case for change and adding fear at the decision stage usually backfires ([Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)). **[GAP]** No independent academic replication or critique of the Challenger research was retrieved.

### MEDDIC, MEDDICC and MEDDPICC: the deal-record schema for complex deals

**[VENDOR]** MEDDIC was created in 1996 at the software company PTC by studying won, lost and slipped deals. The letters are Metrics (quantified value), Economic Buyer (overall buying authority), Decision Criteria (how solutions are evaluated), Decision Process (how the buyer decides), Identify or Implicate Pain, and Champion (the internal person who drives the deal). MEDDICC adds Competition, defined as any alternative vendor, person or initiative including the status quo; MEDDPICC adds Paper Process, the path from decision to signature ([MEDDICC.com history page](https://meddicc.com/resources/who-created-meddic)). The vendor's claim that the method drove PTC's growth is its own and unsupported. Use it for multi-threaded enterprise deals where a trustworthy forecast is needed; it is overkill for simple deals with one or two decision-makers ([MEDDICC.com framework comparison](https://meddicc.com/resources/meddicc-versus-other-qualification-frameworks-like-bant); [Demodesk, Jul 2026](https://demodesk.ai/blog/sales-qualification-frameworks-how-to-choose)).

### Sandler: up-front contracts and the pain funnel

**[VENDOR]** The Sandler system has seven steps: bonding and rapport; up-front contracts; pain; budget; decision (stakeholders and process); fulfilment (presenting against stated needs); and post-sell (locking in and handing off). The up-front contract has both sides agree the meeting's purpose, time, topics and possible end states (fit, no fit, another meeting). The pain funnel is a series of questions that go beyond surface issues to test fit, money, commitment and consequences before any pitch ([Highspot on Sandler](https://www.highspot.com/blog/sandler-sales-methodology/)). Use up-front contracts at the start of every meeting; the method suits candid discovery and early disqualification and depends on sustained coaching. Unverified background knowledge: Sandler founded his training business in 1967.

### Gap Selling: current state, future state and the gap

**[VENDOR]** Gap Selling (Keenan, 2018) maps the buyer's current state, the desired future state and the gap between them; the bigger the gap, the stronger the case. Its discovery chart covers problems, root cause and impact (financial, operational, emotional). It fits consultative and complex sales, is weak for quick low-cost transactional sales, takes practice, and some buyers get defensive under probing ([HubSpot on Gap Selling](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/gap-selling)). **[INFERENCE]** The house style's Clarify and Label steps are a compact version of the same current-state, goal and gap logic.

### Command of the Message: a shared value-messaging framework

**[VENDOR]** Force Management's Command of the Message is a shared value-messaging framework used for discovery, sales conversations, manager coaching and AI-enabled workflows, positioned for complex technical deals with multiple decision-makers and often paired with MEDDPICC. Its results evidence is testimonial only ([Force Management](https://forcemanagement.com/sales-message)). **[GAP]** The vendor's page does not publish the framework's internal components; widely used terms such as value drivers and required capabilities are unverified here.

### NEAT Selling: a four-field qualifier

**[VENDOR]** NEAT (Harris Consulting Group with Sales Hacker) stands for Need (how serious the pain really is), Economic impact (the buyer supplies the numbers on the cost of the current state), Access to authority (whether the contact can reach decision-makers and who the biggest sceptic is) and Timeline (target date and the consequence of missing it). It is positioned as a replacement for BANT in non-linear buying and can be layered onto an existing methodology for new business, cross-sell and upsell ([GTMnow, Mar 2022](https://gtmnow.com/evolution-sales-welcome-neat-selling/)).

### The JOLT Effect: overcoming customer indecision

**[VENDOR]** The JOLT research (Dixon and McKenna) analysed about 2.5 million recorded sales conversations from several dozen companies, starting in spring 2020, across about 8,300 factors ([Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/); [CustomerThink interview with Matt Dixon](https://customerthink.com/the-problem-of-customer-indecision-and-how-to-get-over-it-interview-with-matt-dixon/)). Findings: 40 to 60% of deals are lost to no decision; preference for the status quo explains less than half of those and the larger share is the buyer's fear of making a mistake (people prefer errors of not acting to errors of acting); about 87% of opportunities show moderate or high indecision; win rate is about 30% at medium indecision and about 6% at high indecision ([CustomerThink interview with Matt Dixon](https://customerthink.com/the-problem-of-customer-indecision-and-how-to-get-over-it-interview-with-matt-dixon/); [Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)). The playbook: Judge the level of indecision and disqualify when needed; Offer your recommendation to reduce choice overload, even if the deal shrinks; Limit the exploration; Take risk off the table with realistic expectations and safety nets ([Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)).

### JOLT's performance comparison by buyer type

**[VENDOR]** In the same research, high performers against average performers won about 70% versus 39% with decisive buyers, 58% versus 26% with moderately indecisive buyers and 31% versus 6% with deeply indecisive buyers ([CustomerThink interview with Matt Dixon](https://customerthink.com/the-problem-of-customer-indecision-and-how-to-get-over-it-interview-with-matt-dixon/)). **[INFERENCE]** Challenger and JOLT are complementary: Challenger addresses status-quo preference, JOLT addresses fear of acting, and applying Challenger pressure to an indecision problem is the specific error the JOLT data warns about. **[GAP]** JOLT's sub-diagnosis of indecision types was not confirmed in the sources fetched.

### Miller Heiman Strategic Selling: the stakeholder map

**[VENDOR]** Strategic Selling (Miller and Heiman, 1985; now sold by Korn Ferry) defines four buying influences: the Economic buyer (final approval or veto), the User buyer (daily use, decides adoption), the Technical buyer (can block, usually cannot approve) and the Coach (wants you to win and tells you how decisions are really made). Each stakeholder is in one of four response modes: Growth and Trouble, which carry urgency, and Even Keel and Overconfident, which see no need to change. Each influence has a business result and a personal win; red flags mark missing information, uncovered influences and competitive or timing threats; the Blue Sheet records the single sales objective, influences, modes, win-results, red flags, strengths, competitive position and action plan ([Rework on Miller Heiman](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/pipeline-management/miller-heiman-strategic-selling)). Limits: no discovery-question guidance, no qualification score, heavy overhead for short single-decision sales, and first-party win-rate claims.

### Product-led sales: usage signals in place of form-fill qualification

**[VENDOR]** In product-led sales a product-qualified lead meets three tests: fit (matches the ideal customer profile), value (reached activation or a usage milestone within a time window) and intent (pricing-page visit, form, support request, hitting a usage limit). Leads meeting all three get a seller if deal potential is high and automated campaigns if low; fit-plus-intent leads need onboarding help; fit-plus-value leads should be nudged and not hard-sold; and leads are segmented by what they are ready to buy (free to paid, seat expansion, annual, enterprise agreement) ([ProductLed on PQLs, 2021, updated Jun 2024](https://productled.com/blog/how-to-define-pqls)). **[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Forrester reports that more than 60% of business buyers use a trial, 78% of buyers on purchases of $10 million or more trial first, and only just over a third planned to convert the trial with the same provider ([Forrester press release, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/); [Forrester blog, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/state-of-business-buying-2026)).

### Methodologies not sourced in this research

**[GAP]** Solution Selling, Value Selling and consultative selling were not sourced. Unverified background knowledge only: Solution Selling (Bosworth) centres on diagnosing pain before prescribing; Value Selling uses a qualified-prospect formula around business issue, problems, solution, value, power and plan; consultative selling traces to Mack Hanan's 1970 book. Deal-size thresholds for each methodology are not stated numerically in any source found.

## 14. Qualification frameworks: which one, when, and how to record unknowns

### Choose the framework by deal complexity

**[VENDOR]** Practitioner guidance from July 2026 gives five selection rules: define the job (short cycle and clear budget suits BANT or ANUM; a complex committee suits MEDDIC; challenge-first discovery suits CHAMP or SPICED); map complexity (customisation, stakeholder count, cycle length) and ignore fashion; pick what managers will actually coach and score; accept hybrids if the rules are written down; and record empty fields as unknown, especially the champion ([Demodesk, Jul 2026](https://demodesk.ai/blog/sales-qualification-frameworks-how-to-choose)). A common pairing is BANT at the top of the funnel and MEDDPICC from discovery to close ([MEDDICC.com framework comparison](https://meddicc.com/resources/meddicc-versus-other-qualification-frameworks-like-bant)). **[GAP]** No empirical study comparing win rates or forecast accuracy between frameworks was found; all comparisons are practitioner opinion.

### BANT and its gaps

**[VENDOR]** BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timing) originated at IBM and fits clear procurement paths and shorter cycles; it is weak when buying is collaborative and budget is created late. Its stated gaps are that authority is one checkbox although enterprise deals have many stakeholders, that it says nothing on decision criteria or process, has no champion concept and ignores competition including the status quo. It maps to MEDDPICC as Budget to Metrics, Authority to Economic Buyer plus Champion, Need to Implicate Pain, and Timing to Decision Process plus Paper Process; Decision Criteria and Competition have no BANT equivalent ([Demodesk, Jul 2026](https://demodesk.ai/blog/sales-qualification-frameworks-how-to-choose); [MEDDICC.com framework comparison](https://meddicc.com/resources/meddicc-versus-other-qualification-frameworks-like-bant)).

### The in-between frameworks: CHAMP, GPCTBA/C&I, ANUM, FAINT, SPICED

**[VENDOR]** CHAMP (Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritization) suits SaaS deals where the return-on-investment conversation matters more than a pre-set budget. HubSpot's GPCTBA/C&I (Goals, Plans, Challenges, Timeline, Budget, Authority, Consequences, Implications) is the most thorough and is heavy for transactional motions. ANUM (Authority, Need, Urgency, Money) reorders BANT. FAINT (Funds, Authority, Interest, Need, Timing) targets buyers with no predefined budget. SPICED (Situation, Pain, Impact, Critical Event, Decision; Winning by Design) ties outcomes to a deadline and works across stages ([Demodesk, Jul 2026](https://demodesk.ai/blog/sales-qualification-frameworks-how-to-choose)).

### A two-tier default schema for the agents

**[INFERENCE]** A reasonable default is a four-field quick qualifier (need, impact, access to authority, timeline) for inbound and small deals, expanding to the eight MEDDPICC fields once a deal passes a size or stakeholder-count threshold set from the department's own data. Budget as a gate is increasingly unreliable because buying groups are large and budgets are created during the process; pain, impact and decision process are better early gates. **[VENDOR]** Platform data supports three specific gates: a decision-maker involved in the first two stages is associated with a 50 to 55% higher win rate (the report is internally inconsistent on the figure), top performers are 24% more likely to disqualify deals outside the ideal customer profile early, and 76% of average-performer deals lack a critical event (2024 data on 655,000 opportunities, [Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Discovery: aim for interactivity, not a magic talk ratio

**[VENDOR; CONTESTED]** Gong's 2016 to 2017 analysis of 25,537 B2B SaaS first calls found the highest-converting talk-to-listen ratio was 43:57, with talking above 65% linked to lower conversion ([Gong, Mar 2017](https://www.gong.io/blog/turn-sales-conversations-to-exact-science)). Its 2025 re-analysis of 326,000 calls of ten minutes or more found average rep talk time of 60%, with won deals at 57% and lost deals at 62%; high performers hold a steady ratio win or lose while low performers swing from 54% on won deals to 64% on lost ones ([Gong talk-ratio analysis, Mar 2025](https://www.gong.io/blog/talk-to-listen-conversion-ratio); [Gong, Jan 2026](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-sales-insights-of-2025)). **[INFERENCE]** The two figures conflict on the surface and come from different samples and call types. The stable signal is direction and consistency: lost deals have more rep talk and longer monologues. Agents target frequent turn-taking and not a fixed percentage. The house-style claim that top closers listen about twice as much as they talk points the same way.

### Discovery: about fifteen purposeful questions, weighted to problems and implications

**[VENDOR]** In Gong's 2025 data won deals averaged 15 to 16 questions per call and lost deals about 20, so more questions was associated with losing ([Gong talk-ratio analysis, Mar 2025](https://www.gong.io/blog/talk-to-listen-conversion-ratio)). **[DURABLE]** Rackham found that question count correlated with success, that open or closed format did not matter, and that problem, implication and need-payoff questions distinguish successful large-sale calls ([Sales Society, Aug 2022](https://salessociety.substack.com/p/the-fascinating-science-of-spin-selling)). **[INFERENCE]** A working cap is roughly 15 purposeful questions in a live discovery call, weighted to problem, implication and need-payoff, with situation questions answered by pre-call research. **[GAP]** The often-quoted figures of 11 to 14 questions per discovery call and three to four problems discussed could not be confirmed on any Gong page fetched and must not be cited.

### Buying groups are large, and procurement is in from the start

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Forrester's survey of nearly 18,000 buyers reports an average of 13 internal and 9 external influencers per purchase; purchases with generative-AI features average 14 members against 7 without; 94% of buyers with groups of six or more report benefits; and procurement is a decision-maker in 53% of buying cycles, involved from the start, scrutinising features and not only price ([Forrester blog, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/state-of-business-buying-2026); [Forrester press release, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/)). **[VENDOR]** 6sense puts typical purchases at about 10 people (relayed by a secondary roundup, [Corporate Visions statistics roundup](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/b2b-buying-behavior-statistics-trends)); LinkedIn and Ipsos report that 40% of buyers see larger committees ([LinkedIn/Ipsos Trust Advantage, 2025](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/resources/the-trust-advantage)); LinkedIn's 2025 research on 750 senior buyers says hidden influencers such as legal, finance and procurement hold about half of decision power and that peer recommendation was about three times as influential as lower price or claimed performance ([The Drum, Jun 2025](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/06/18/linkedin-s-new-b2b-playbook-shows-ability-defend-bad-decisions-drives-b2b-buying)). **[GAP]** No Gartner 2025 to 2026 buying-group-size figure was retrieved.

### Multi-threading and team selling

**[VENDOR]** Gong's 2025 analysis of 1.8 million opportunities found 77% of deals involve multiple contacts, won deals have twice as many buyer contacts as lost ones, strategic enterprise deals average 17 contacts, multi-threading lifts win rate 130% in deals over $50,000, won deals have selling teams 67% larger, and enterprise win rates are up to 30% higher when a sales engineer joins ([Gong, Jan 2026](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-sales-insights-of-2025)). Older Gong data from 2021 on 10,332 deals showed win rates of about 15% solo against about 50% with three or more seller-side roles, with uplift by role of 204% for an executive, 119% for sales leadership, 98% for customer success and 32% for sales engineers ([Gong Labs, Sep 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/how-to-close-more-6-figure-deals-according-to-data/)). In the same 2021 data closed-won deal value rose from about $10,000 to about $200,000 as buyer contacts went from 3 to 25 ([Gong Labs, Sep 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/how-to-close-more-6-figure-deals-according-to-data/)). LinkedIn reports top performers are three times more likely to be connected to eight or more stakeholders per account and that 57% of sellers do not start multi-threading until after the first meeting ([LinkedIn/Ipsos Trust Advantage, 2025](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/resources/the-trust-advantage)). **[INFERENCE]** These effects are correlational, since larger deals naturally involve more contacts. The agent rule: begin stakeholder mapping in the first conversation and flag a single-contact deal above a size threshold as at risk.

### Champions, coaches and mutual plans

**[VENDOR]** MEDDIC's Champion drives the deal internally; Miller Heiman's Coach wants the seller to win and shares how decisions are really made, including bad news ([MEDDICC.com history page](https://meddicc.com/resources/who-created-meddic); [Rework on Miller Heiman](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/pipeline-management/miller-heiman-strategic-selling)). Sandler's up-front contract is described as functionally similar to a mutual action plan at meeting level ([Highspot on Sandler](https://www.highspot.com/blog/sandler-sales-methodology/)). **[GAP]** No quantitative evidence was found on mutual action plans or on champion-building tactics; guidance is practitioner opinion.

### Demos: lead with the buyer's top priority and keep segments short

**[VENDOR]** Gong's 2017 study of 67,149 recorded demos with CRM outcomes found winning demos averaged 47 minutes against 36 for unsuccessful ones; talk ratio barely differed (65:35 won, 66:34 lost) but interactivity did, with 21% more speaker switches per minute, rising 36% in the second half; the longest uninterrupted pitch reached 106 seconds in losing demos and never exceeded 76 seconds in winning ones; winning demos opened with the topic the buyer spent most time on in discovery; company overview stayed under about two minutes; pricing came between minutes 38 and 46; and winning demos spent 12.7% more time on next steps, about four minutes ([Gong Labs demo study, Sep 2017](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/sales-demos/); [Gong, Apr 2017](https://www.gong.io/blog/winning-sales-conversations)). The author notes this is correlation. **[GAP]** The dataset is from 2017 and SaaS-heavy; no 2025 to 2026 replication was found, and no evidence was found on written proposals or slide design.

### Demos: avoid feature-dumping

**[VENDOR]** In Gong's 2021 analysis of 8,382 deals, win rate fell from 26% to 5% as feature-dump duration rose from 0 to 500 seconds, and value topics drove more engagement than features ([Gong Labs, Jul 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/driving-next-steps-isnt-enough-this-is-what-really-moves-deals-forward/)). **[INFERENCE]** A defensible demo template: a recap of discovery and agenda check under two minutes; the highest-priority problem first; segments under roughly 75 seconds each followed by a check-in question; pricing late; about four minutes reserved for concrete next steps. Because 67% of buyers prefer rep-free experiences and trial use is heavy, agents should offer a self-guided trial alongside or before a demo.

### Demos and proof: what sellers and buyers say helps

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025]** In HubSpot's 2025 survey of 1,000 sellers, market research (35%) and product demos (32%) were the enablement content sellers said most helps close ([HubSpot State of Sales, Sep 2025](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/hubspot-sales-strategy-report)); this is seller perception. **[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Forrester advises designing trials to prove long-term business value, since more than 60% of buyers use them ([Forrester press release, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/)).

### Pricing conversations: late, and buyers bring it up three to four times

**[VENDOR]** Top reps discuss price around minutes 40 to 49 of a call while weaker reps scatter it, and three to four buyer pricing mentions correlate with the highest win rates (April 2017, [Gong, Apr 2017](https://www.gong.io/blog/winning-sales-conversations)). Pricing conversations have lengthened 62% since 2020 and pricing mentions rose 18% since 2022 (January 2025, [Gong, Jan 2025](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-sales-insights-of-2024)). **[INFERENCE]** The house-style rule of surfacing obstacles before price and presenting price after the outcome is consistent with this; the house-style setter rule of not quoting price early must give way when a buyer asks directly (section 11).

### Negotiation: anchor credibly, and consider a bolstering range

**[DURABLE]** Anchoring research (Tversky and Kahneman) shows an initial number pulls later estimates toward it, and negotiators who make the first offer often do better if the anchor is credible. Ames and Mason (2015) found across five experiments that bolstering range offers, with the seller's target as the floor and stretching upward, beat single-point offers and bracketing ranges; recipients inferred a higher reservation price, saw the offerer as more cooperative and conceded more. Limits: ranges that are too wide or start from an extreme lose effect, spans of roughly 5 to 20% above the base work best, and the approach suits price-focused one-off deals more than integrative, trust-building ones (academic summary, March 2026, [Harvard Program on Negotiation, Mar 2026](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/anchoring-bias-negotiation-get-ahead-range-offer/)).

### Negotiation: interests, options, criteria and your alternative

**[DURABLE]** Getting to Yes (Fisher and Ury) advises separating people from the problem, focusing on interests and not positions, generating multiple options, insisting on objective criteria, and knowing your best alternative to a negotiated agreement so that anything worse is rejected. Against hard tactics it advises naming the tactic, treating extreme demands as proposals to be judged on merit, verifying claims in writing and negotiating the ground rules (third-party summary, [Beyond Intractability summary of Getting to Yes](https://www.beyondintractability.org/bksum/fisher-getting)). **[GAP]** The primary Harvard page on principled negotiation was not retrieved; unverified background knowledge puts first publication in 1981.

### Negotiation: most sellers concede on price although buyers could pay more

**[VENDOR]** RAIN Group's study of 713 buyers and sellers, including 202 procurement professionals, reports that top negotiators open high and negotiate down, that their top differentiator is understanding each side's power and leverage, that most sellers give pure price concessions while most buyers admit they could pay more, and that procurement cites cost as a success metric 12 times more often than quality ([RAIN Group negotiation research](https://www.rainsalestraining.com/sales-research/negotiation)). The study date and detailed figures sit behind a download form. **[INFERENCE]** Discount discipline for agents: never concede on price alone; trade for term, volume, scope, payment timing or reference rights. Use a bolstering range only for simple price quotes; for multi-issue enterprise deals prefer multiple options built on the buyer's own numbers, prepared before the proposal because procurement is present from the start.

### Negotiation techniques that are not evidence-based here

**[GAP]** Chris Voss's techniques (mirroring, labelling, calibrated questions and staged offers) were not retrieved; as unverified background knowledge they derive from hostage-negotiation practice and lack controlled evidence in commercial sales, so they must not be described as evidence-based. No rigorous evidence was found on proposal format, number of pricing options or optimal discount ceilings.

### Contract friction is a controllable loss factor

**[VENDOR]** A smooth contracting experience makes about seven in ten buyers more likely to repurchase, according to 6sense's 2024 research as relayed by a secondary roundup ([Corporate Visions statistics roundup](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/b2b-buying-behavior-statistics-trends)). **[INFERENCE]** Together with the Paper Process field in MEDDPICC, this argues for mapping signature steps, legal review and procurement requirements early in the deal.

### Objections: pause, clarify, answer briefly, check

**[VENDOR]** In Gong's 2018 analysis of 67,149 demo recordings, top reps paused longer after an objection than in normal conversation, held a steady pace, responded with clarifying questions, kept speaker-switching up and checked understanding more often; average reps sped up and delivered a monologue of about 21 seconds ([Gong Labs, Mar 2018](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/handling-sales-objections/)). **[DURABLE]** Rackham found that in large sales objection-handling skill did not separate successful from unsuccessful calls ([Sales Society, Aug 2022](https://salessociety.substack.com/p/the-fascinating-science-of-spin-selling)). **[INFERENCE]** The agent protocol: pause, acknowledge, one clarifying question, a short answer (well under 20 seconds of speech or a few sentences of text), then check it landed. In text channels the pause means not replying with a wall of text. Frequent objections are a discovery-quality signal for the manager agent, because prevention through better discovery matters more than rebuttal skill. **[GAP]** Gong's article gives no pause length in seconds; circulating figures on pause multiples and question-response rates were not confirmed, and no data-backed frequency ranking of objection categories was found.

### Status quo versus indecision: diagnose before responding

**[INFERENCE]** Decision rule: if the buyer disputes the problem or its size, the issue is status quo, so build the gap and quantify the cost of inaction. If the buyer agrees but stalls, repeatedly asks for more information or cannot choose between options, the issue is indecision, so apply recommend, narrow and de-risk and do not add urgency or discount. **[VENDOR]** Supporting data: Miller Heiman's Even Keel and Overconfident modes describe stakeholders who see no reason to change ([Rework on Miller Heiman](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/pipeline-management/miller-heiman-strategic-selling)); LinkedIn's 2025 study found fear of being unable to defend a purchase the most decisive buying factor ([The Drum, Jun 2025](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/06/18/linkedin-s-new-b2b-playbook-shows-ability-defend-bad-decisions-drives-b2b-buying)); Forrester's 2024 data as relayed by a roundup says 86% of B2B purchases stall and 81% of buyers end up dissatisfied with the chosen provider ([Corporate Visions statistics roundup](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/b2b-buying-behavior-statistics-trends)); sellers surveyed by HubSpot in 2025 named no product fit (37%) and poor value for money (35%) as top deal-killers ([HubSpot State of Sales, Sep 2025](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/hubspot-sales-strategy-report)).

### Competitor objections: discuss competitors early

**[VENDOR]** Early discussion of competitors was associated with a 49% higher chance of closing than none in Gong's 2017 data, while late-stage competitor discussion came with slightly lower odds and smaller deals ([Gong Labs, May 2017](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/win-competitive-deals/)). The 2024 update found an early competitive mention raised enterprise win odds 32% and that competitive mentions rose 57% since 2022 ([Gong, Jan 2025](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-sales-insights-of-2024)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents ask about alternatives being considered, including doing nothing, during discovery, within the comparative-claims rule in section 11.

### Closing: next steps predict progress; closing techniques do not

**[VENDOR]** Deals where next steps were never discussed closed at 5% against 20% where they were, and value-based discussion precedes and fuels that momentum (2021, 8,382 deals, [Gong Labs, Jul 2021](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/driving-next-steps-isnt-enough-this-is-what-really-moves-deals-forward/)). Risk-reversal language at close was associated with a 32% higher win likelihood ([Gong, Mar 2017](https://www.gong.io/blog/turn-sales-conversations-to-exact-science)). At the decision point the JOLT guidance is a clear recommendation, limited further exploration and safety nets, not urgency ([Challenger Inc., Feb 2024](https://challengerinc.com/losing-to-customer-indecision/)). **[INFERENCE]** Agent rule: every interaction ends with a dated, mutual next step that requires a buyer action; a meeting that ends without one is flagged by the manager agent. Unverified background knowledge: Rackham's alternative to closing is the "advance", a specific buyer action that moves the sale forward. **[GAP]** No independent evidence was found on assumptive or now-or-never closes in modern B2B beyond Rackham and JOLT.

### Follow-up: add a call attempt and a voicemail to email

**[VENDOR]** In Gong's 2024 data on more than 300 million cold calls, a call attempt nearly doubled email reply rate (3.44% against 1.81%) even without a live connection, and leaving a voicemail lifted reply rate from 2.73% to 5.87% ([Gong, Jan 2025](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-sales-insights-of-2024)). LinkedIn reports top sellers are six times more likely to respond to buying signals within one hour ([LinkedIn/Ipsos Trust Advantage, 2025](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/resources/the-trust-advantage)). **[GAP]** No data was found on optimal follow-up cadence, number of touches or content for mid-deal follow-up, and the applicability of the call-plus-email finding to AI-agent outreach is untested.

### Sales psychology: what has support

**[DURABLE]** Anchoring is robust in negotiation research ([Harvard Program on Negotiation, Mar 2026](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/anchoring-bias-negotiation-get-ahead-range-offer/)). Loss aversion appears in buying as omission bias: people prefer the error of not acting to the error of acting, which drives no-decision outcomes ([CustomerThink interview with Matt Dixon](https://customerthink.com/the-problem-of-customer-indecision-and-how-to-get-over-it-interview-with-matt-dixon/)). **[VENDOR]** Social proof from peers has support in buyer surveys: peer recommendation about three times as influential as price or claimed performance ([The Drum, Jun 2025](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/06/18/linkedin-s-new-b2b-playbook-shows-ability-defend-bad-decisions-drives-b2b-buying)), and 56% of buyers, 71% in enterprise, consult existing users before buying (TrustRadius 2024, relayed by [Corporate Visions statistics roundup](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/b2b-buying-behavior-statistics-trends)). Risk reversal is associated with higher win rates, consistent with loss aversion ([Gong, Apr 2017](https://www.gong.io/blog/winning-sales-conversations)).

### Sales psychology: field effects are smaller than popular accounts imply

**[VENDOR]** One practitioner's 72 A/B tests of behavioural principles won only 29% of the time (anchoring 7 of 19, loss framing 2 of 13, scarcity and urgency 2 of 10, defaults 5 of 16), and the winning scarcity and loss-framing cases all involved genuine scarcity or a real imminent loss (March 2026, [Atticus Li, Mar 2026](https://www.atticusli.com/blog/posts/behavioral-science-claims-vs-experiment-reality/)). This is a single practitioner in a consumer conversion context with no raw data, who sells consulting. **[GAP]** No peer-reviewed meta-analysis of persuasion principles in B2B sales was retrieved; as unverified background knowledge, Cialdini's principles rest on a mix of field and lab studies and the wider behavioural-science literature has had replication problems.

### Ethical operating rules that the evidence supports

**[INFERENCE]** Five rules follow from the evidence and from the law in sections 28 and 29: scarcity and deadlines only when true; social proof only from real, attributable, relevant customers; loss framing limited to the buyer's own quantified cost of inaction during the case-for-change stage and never at the decision stage; no fabricated urgency, fake competitor claims or hidden terms; and disclosure of AI identity with no impersonation of a human reference. These are also the tactics the available data shows working.

## 16. Buyer behaviour in 2025 to 2026

### Buyers prefer to go without a rep, and the share is rising

**[FAST-MOVING, Mar 2026]** Gartner's survey of 646 B2B buyers, fielded August to September 2025 and published 9 March 2026, found 67% prefer a rep-free experience, 45% used AI in a recent purchase, and buyers with high decision confidence are twice as likely to report a high-quality deal. Gartner frames value clarity, a clear understanding of outcome improvement in the buyer's own role and context, as the enablement priority, and recommends deploying AI agents for buyer self-validation and seller value communication and breaking content into modular, agent-readable components ([Gartner press release, 9 Mar 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-09-gartner-sales-survey-finds-67-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-experience); [Digital Commerce 360, 17 Mar 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/17/gartner-b2b-buyers-rep-free-purchasing-ai-reshapes-sales/)). The prior year's figure was 61%, with 73% of buyers actively avoiding suppliers that send irrelevant outreach; the earlier release itself could not be retrieved and the figures come through a secondary roundup ([Gartner press release, 25 Jun 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-sales-survey-finds-61-percent-of-b2b-buyers-prefer-a-rep-free-buying-experience); [Corporate Visions statistics roundup](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/b2b-buying-behavior-statistics-trends)).

### Buyers still want a human to validate AI output

**[FAST-MOVING, May 2026]** Gartner's release of 20 May 2026 on 645 buyers reports an average of seven information sources per purchase, that 45% used generative AI mainly to gather vendor and product information, that 69% prefer to validate AI-generated insights with a sales rep, and that 51% say they are more likely to meet misleading information from generative AI against 49% from a sales rep; Gartner advises moving sellers from primary information source to source of validation and confidence ([Gartner press release, 20 May 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-survey-finds-sixty-nine-percent-of-b-two-b-buyers-turn-to-sales-reps-to-validate-ai-generated-insights)).

### AI use in buying is near-universal on the broadest definitions

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026; CONTESTED]** Forrester's State of Business Buying 2026 (nearly 18,000 buyers, published 21 January 2026) says 94% use AI during buying and generative-AI search is the starting point, but that answer engines give incomplete or unreliable information, so buyers validate with peers, product experts, analysts and human contact; buyers are risk-averse and want proof, and providers must show deep understanding of needs, make claims trusted third parties can validate and design trials that prove business value ([Forrester blog, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/state-of-business-buying-2026); [Forrester press release, Jan 2026](https://www.forrester.com/press-newsroom/forrester-2026-the-state-of-business-buying/)). 6sense and LinkedIn also report 94% ([6sense via Business Wire, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112018032/en/); [LinkedIn/Ipsos Trust Advantage, 2025](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/resources/the-trust-advantage)), against Gartner's 45%. **[INFERENCE]** The gap is most likely question wording (AI used in a recent purchase versus any AI use in the process) and sample; report it as a range.

### The pre-contact favourite usually wins

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Nov 2025]** 6sense's 2025 Buyer Experience Report (more than 4,000 buyers in North America, EMEA and APAC, released 12 November 2025) found 94% of buying groups rank a preferred vendor before first seller contact and that the favourite wins about 77 to 80% of the time; the pre-contact share of the journey shrank from about 70% to about 60%; cycle length fell from about 11 to 10 months; 58% engaged sellers earlier to clarify AI details; buyers averaged 16 interactions per person with the winning vendor; and 70% said economic anxiety influenced vendor choice, often favouring incumbents ([6sense via Business Wire, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112018032/en/)). The full report is gated; only press-release figures are cited.

### Trust in sellers is low, and regret is higher without them

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025]** LinkedIn and Ipsos (more than 900 buyers and 900 sellers in seven markets, 2025) report that nearly half of buyers use AI to analyse proposals and challenge claims, that 86% say expertise drives trust but only 45% trust sellers, that 88% value sellers most mid-journey (vendor comparison, pilot), that 75% would prefer to buy without a rep yet those who do report 23% higher regret, that shortlists are typically two to three vendors, and that three-quarters of sellers agree high-volume untargeted outreach erodes trust ([LinkedIn/Ipsos Trust Advantage, 2025](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/resources/the-trust-advantage)). LinkedIn's B2B Institute study of 750 senior buyers found defensibility of the decision outranks utility ([The Drum, Jun 2025](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/06/18/linkedin-s-new-b2b-playbook-shows-ability-defend-bad-decisions-drives-b2b-buying)).

### What sellers and their organisations report

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Feb 2026]** Salesforce's State of Sales 2026 (4,050 sales professionals in 22 countries, fielded August to September 2025) reports 87% of sales organisations use AI, 54% of sellers have used AI agents, reps spend 40% of their time selling, high performers are 1.7 times more likely to use prospecting agents, 51% of leaders say disconnected systems slow AI, and 79% of high performers prioritise data hygiene against 54% of underperformers ([Salesforce State of Sales announcement, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/)). HubSpot's 2025 survey of 1,000 sales professionals reports 74% believe AI makes buyer research easier, 40% are adding self-serve tools, and sellers see their main job as building buyer confidence (36%) or navigating internal buy-in (33%) ([HubSpot State of Sales, Sep 2025](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/hubspot-sales-strategy-report)); this is seller perception and not buyer data.

### Other buyer signals from secondary roundups

**[VENDOR]** A secondary roundup relays that only 9% of buyers consider vendor websites reliable (G2, 2024), that 56% consult existing users before buying and 71% in enterprise (TrustRadius, 2024), and that 77% prioritise integration capability (Demandbase, 2025) ([Corporate Visions statistics roundup](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/b2b-buying-behavior-statistics-trends)). The primaries were not opened.

### What this means for an AI sales department

**[INFERENCE]** The rep-free preference and the regret and validation data are compatible: buyers dislike low-value, pushy contact early but want expert help in the middle for comparison, validation and risk reduction. An AI sales agent should therefore be reactive and precise early (answer exactly, supply modular proof) and consultative mid-journey. Because most deals go to the pre-contact favourite, presence in AI answers, peer communities and third-party validation matters more than late persuasion. Because buyers use AI to interrogate proposals, every claim an agent makes must be verifiable and consistent with third-party sources. **[GAP]** Whether buyers accept, or know they are dealing with, AI sales agents was not covered by any source found.

## 17. Pipeline design and stage criteria

### Use the Bowtie Standard as the neutral stage model

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** The most current vendor-neutral stage framework read in primary form is Winning by Design's Bowtie Standard. Its stages in order are: Awareness (lead generation); Education (lead development, often demo or discovery); Prioritization (buyer and seller agree the impact to achieve, producing a qualified opportunity); Selection (stakeholder education, proof of concept, return-on-investment proposal); Mutual Commit (the close and the start of a multi-year relationship); Onboarding, called Activation in product-led motions (reaching first measurable impact); Retention and Adoption; and Expansion ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)). The paper gives no benchmark values and points to a public benchmark of more than 600 companies.

### Lead-status equivalents across product-led, AI-led and human-led motions

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** The Bowtie Standard defines volume metrics that line up three motions on one model. A prospect matches situation, pain and impact potential in all three. Expressed interest with shared contact details is a handraise (product-led), a marketing-qualified lead (AI-led) or a lead (human-led). Considering action is a product-qualified lead, a sales-qualified lead or an opportunity. Verified as a priority is a product-qualified account, a sales-accepted lead or a qualified opportunity. Mutual commitment is a sign-up or a win. The later metrics are revenue: committed revenue (wins multiplied by annual contract value), starting recurring revenue (committed revenue minus onboarding churn), recurring revenue and lifetime value ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)).

### Pick one ordering for SQL and SAL and write it down

**[CONTESTED]** In the Bowtie Standard a sales-qualified lead (considering action) comes before a sales-accepted lead (verified as a priority) ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)). Many CRMs use the older order of marketing-qualified, then sales-accepted, then sales-qualified. The two conventions conflict, so the knowledge base must pick one and define each status with its entry test. **[GAP]** The original demand-waterfall definitions and CRM vendors' lead-status definitions were not fetched.

### Conversion and time metrics per stage

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** The Bowtie conversion metrics are: awareness conversion (for example sign-ups over visitors); education conversion (the share of leads who enter a sales process); prioritization conversion (the share qualified on impact priority); win rate (commitments over qualified opportunities); revenue committed (list price minus discounts); onboarding conversion (one minus onboarding churn); gross revenue retention; and expansion (new recurring revenue from upsell as a share of post-churn cohort revenue). The time metrics measure waiting between actions: time to start a conversation; time until the buyer is ready to buy; time to qualify; sales cycle; time to set the customer up internally; time from purchase to first impact; contract length; and customer lifetime ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)).

### Default B2B opportunity stages with exit criteria

**[INFERENCE]** A default seller-side stage set, written from practitioner convention and not fetched from a source: stage 0, meeting booked or held, exits when a meeting is held with a contact who fits the ideal customer profile; stage 1, discovery or qualified, exits when pain, impact and a named champion are confirmed and the next meeting is dated; stage 2, solution or demo, exits when a decision-maker is engaged and decision criteria and process are known; stage 3, evaluation or proof (trial, proof of concept, security review), exits when success criteria are met and a mutual action plan is agreed; stage 4, proposal or negotiation, exits when commercial terms are agreed and the paper process and signer are known; stage 5 is closed won or closed lost, always with a reason code. Every exit criterion is a verifiable buyer action, never a seller activity.

### Default high-velocity pipeline for small deals and consumers

**[INFERENCE]** For high-velocity, small-business or consumer sales, the conventional pipeline is new lead, contact attempted, connected, qualified (need plus ability to buy), offer or quote sent or trial started, then won or lost: three to five stages, a cycle measured in days, and automated nurture for "not now". This is convention; no source was read for B2C or high-velocity stage designs. It lines up with the house-style funnel of leads, contacted, booked, showed, offered, closed and cash collected (section 6).

### Lead statuses, scoring and routing defaults

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional defaults, not fetched: statuses run lead or inquiry, marketing-qualified (fit plus engagement threshold), sales-accepted (accepted within the service level), sales-qualified (need confirmed after contact), then opportunity (with amount and close date), with reject and recycle statuses carrying reasons so that marketing gets feedback. Lead score is a fit score (industry, size, role, technology) plus an intent or behaviour score (pricing-page views, demo request, product usage, third-party intent) that decays over time; hand-raisers bypass scoring and route immediately. Routing options are round-robin, territory or segment, named-account owner match and capacity or performance weighting; match a lead to an existing account owner before any round-robin. **[GAP]** No evidence on routing models was retrieved.

### Marketing-to-sales service-level template

**[INFERENCE]** A conventional agreement, not fetched: marketing commits a volume and quality of qualified leads; sales commits a first-touch time, a minimum number of attempts (for example six to eight touches over about two weeks across channels) and a disposition on every lead within a fixed window; both sides review rejected leads weekly. Response-time targets are in section 25.

### Evidence for the qualification gates

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Ebsta and Pavilion's 2025 report (655,000 opportunities, $48 billion, 387 companies) supports gating early stages on three fields: a decision-maker involved in the first two stages is associated with a 50 to 55% higher win rate; top performers are 24% more likely to disqualify non-fit deals early; and 76% of average-performer deals lack a critical event. Eight stakeholders are engaged per new-business opportunity against five per expansion ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Slipped deals decay fast

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Win rate by slippage past the planned close date in the same dataset: 18% at one week, 13% at one month, 8% at three months, 5% at six months and 3% beyond six months, read from a chart and approximate ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). **[INFERENCE]** A pushed close date is a risk flag in its own right; a deal pushed more than twice should be re-qualified or closed.

### CRM contact coverage is poor by default

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** In Ebsta's data 44% of contacts sellers interact with are not in the CRM, 26% of those missing contacts are decision-makers, and 17% of CRM contacts have an outdated title or phone number ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents should write every contact they interact with to the record automatically.

### What the pipeline research did not find

**[GAP]** No primary source read gives stage-by-stage conversion benchmarks from lead through win; Ebsta reports only that marketing-qualified to sales-qualified conversion rose 32% year over year, not the level, and figures circulating on aggregator pages could not be traced to a primary dataset. Lead-to-opportunity and demo-to-close rates were not found in a primary 2025 to 2026 source.

## 18. Roles and team models

### The current shape of sales teams

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Recent data show a shift back toward full-cycle selling and account executives owning renewals and expansion. The Bridge Group's 2026 survey of 158 B2B companies (published 22 June 2026) reports account-executive ramp averaging 6.2 months, the highest in the study's history, experience required at hire of 3.7 years (2.7 in 2022), and 38% of account executives with sole or blended ownership of renewals and expansion, nearly double 2024 ([Bridge Group 2026 AE report, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2026-ae-models-motions-metrics)). Its 2025 SDR survey of 351 companies (78% North America, 83% SaaS) reports SDR ramp of 3.0 months, tenure of 1.9 years and a ratio of one SDR to 2.4 account executives ([Bridge Group 2025 SDR report](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)). Full-cycle adoption is 45 to 46% in Ebsta's report ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Performance is concentrated in a few sellers

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** In Ebsta's data 14% of sellers generate about 80% of revenue; top performers have a 43% higher win rate, a 42% shorter cycle, 76% higher contract value and 11 times the sales velocity; sellers average under two hours a day selling (21% of the day with customers) against about four hours for top performers ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### SDR handoff models and quotas

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025]** The Bridge Group distinguishes introductory, semi-qualified and fully qualified handoff models; median monthly SDR quota is 10 meetings held (16.0, 10.4 and 9.0 for the three models) and 6 converted to a stage-1 opportunity ([Bridge Group 2025 SDR report](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)).

### SDR cost and attrition

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, May 2026]** A vendor aggregation projects a fully loaded US SDR seat at $110,000 to $173,000 in 2026, attrition at 34 to 40% a year and median tenure of about 14 months, which conflicts with the Bridge Group's 1.9 years ([Glencoco, 2 May 2026](https://www.glencoco.com/2026-state-of-sales-development)); the 2026 figures are projections.

### Role fit by contract value

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional fit, not fetched: under about $5,000 a year, self-serve with sales-assist chat only; about $5,000 to $25,000, inside sales with full-cycle account executives or light SDR support; about $25,000 to $100,000, an SDR and account-executive split with a shared sales engineer and pooled customer success; over about $100,000, enterprise account executives with a dedicated sales engineer, named-account SDR and assigned account or success manager, where pods fit. A split is justified when account-executive time is the scarce resource and outbound is needed; full-cycle fits low contract values, inbound-heavy flow or early-stage teams. Account management owns commercial renewals and expansion; customer success owns adoption and health; under about $10,000 to $15,000 use pooled or digital success. **[GAP]** No measured data was found on pods or on sales-engineer ratios, and the full Bridge Group reports are gated.

### Nordic sales-cycle lengths by model

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** Monterro's survey of 236 Nordic software companies (Sweden 57%, Denmark 14%, Norway 13%, Finland 12%) reports enterprise sales cycles of about 9 months, inside-sales cycles of 4.3 months and 11.7 months for deals of EUR 100,000 or more ([Monterro Nordic Software Benchmark 2026](https://www.monterro.com/benchmark-report-2026/)).

## 19. Metrics, formulas and benchmarks

### Core funnel formulas

**[DURABLE; INFERENCE]** Standard definitions, written from general practice and not fetched: stage conversion rate is opportunities advancing from stage N to N+1 divided by opportunities that entered stage N, on a cohort basis. Win rate is closed-won divided by closed-won plus closed-lost; always state whether by count or value and from which stage, because win rate from qualified stage 1 and from all created opportunities differ widely. Average deal size is closed-won recurring revenue divided by won deals; annual contract value excludes one-off fees and total contract value covers the full term. Sales cycle length is average days from opportunity creation (or stage 1) to closed-won; report the median too. The Bowtie definition of win rate is commitments over qualified opportunities ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)).

### Pipeline velocity and coverage

**[DURABLE; INFERENCE]** Pipeline velocity is qualified opportunities multiplied by win rate multiplied by average deal size, divided by sales cycle length in days, giving revenue per day. Pipeline coverage is open pipeline for the period divided by quota minus closed-won to date. The folk target is about three times; the arithmetically correct target is one divided by win rate, so a 19% new-business win rate implies more than five times coverage on early-stage pipeline. **[GAP]** No primary benchmark for coverage was found. **[VENDOR]** Ebsta's per-deal channel efficiency index is win rate multiplied by contract value, divided by sales cycle ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Acquisition efficiency formulas

**[DURABLE; INFERENCE]** Customer acquisition cost is total sales and marketing cost divided by new customers acquired in the same period, or lagged by cycle length. Payback in months is acquisition cost divided by new monthly recurring revenue per customer multiplied by gross margin. The magic number is the change in quarterly revenue multiplied by four, divided by the prior quarter's sales and marketing spend. Lifetime value is average revenue per account multiplied by gross margin, divided by churn rate; the rule of thumb for lifetime value to acquisition cost is at least three to one. **[FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Benchmarkit's survey gives a median new-customer acquisition-cost ratio of $2.00 of sales and marketing spend per $1 of new recurring revenue (fourth quartile $2.82), an expansion ratio of $1.00 (against $0.61 to $0.69 in 2020 to 2022), magic-number thresholds of 0.75 minimum and above 1.0 ideal, and about 12 months as commonly cited good payback ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)). **[GAP]** The survey's stated fieldwork window is internally inconsistent and should be verified before dates are quoted.

### Retention formulas

**[DURABLE; INFERENCE]** Net revenue retention is starting recurring revenue plus expansion minus contraction minus churn, divided by starting recurring revenue, for a cohort over 12 months. Gross revenue retention is starting revenue minus contraction minus churn, divided by starting revenue, and cannot exceed 100%. Logo churn is customers lost divided by customers at start. Annual churn is one minus (one minus monthly churn) to the twelfth power. The Bowtie definitions agree: gross retention is recurring revenue kept after downgrades and cancellations, and expansion rate is expansion revenue over post-churn cohort revenue ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)).

### Response-time, attainment and activity metrics

**[DURABLE; INFERENCE]** Lead response time is the time from inbound form submission to the first human or qualified automated contact attempt; track the median and the share within the service level. Quota attainment is closed-won bookings over quota; participation rate is the share of reps at or above 100%. Activity metrics (dials, emails, social touches, conversations, meetings booked and held, show rate) are diagnostics, not targets.

### Win rate and cycle benchmarks

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Ebsta and Pavilion report a new-business win rate of 19% with a 91-day cycle and an expansion win rate of 45% with a 52-day cycle; the overall cycle was 9% shorter and contract value 54% higher than in the prior-year report; the base stage for win rate is not stated ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). **[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024-2025 data]** Gong's analysis of 7.1 million opportunities at 3,613 companies found average win rates and deal duration flat year over year in 2025 and average revenue growth of 16%, down three points for US organisations ([Gong, State of Revenue AI 2026](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-labs-state-of-revenue-ai-2026.pdf)). **[GAP]** Win rate and cycle by deal size from a primary 2025 to 2026 dataset were not found.

### Quota attainment benchmarks conflict by method

**[CONTESTED; FAST-MOVING, 2024-2026]** Self-reported surveys say roughly half of reps hit quota: 48% of account executives in the Bridge Group's H1 2026 survey (51% in 2024) ([Bridge Group 2026 AE report, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2026-ae-models-motions-metrics)), and a fall from 52% in 2024 to 46% in 2025 in Gong's survey ([Gong, State of Revenue AI 2026](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-labs-state-of-revenue-ai-2026.pdf)). Platform data say far fewer: 78% of sellers missed quota in Ebsta's 2024 data, up from 69% ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)), and 77.2% of account executives missed quota with median attainment of 61.7% in Everstage's commission data on 25,112 quota carriers, where attainment rises with quota size from 51.9% at a $126,000 median quota to 73.4% at $3.3 million ([Everstage 2026](https://www.everstage.com/resources/sales-quota-attainment-benchmarks-2026)). SDRs: 60% at quota, the lowest in the Bridge Group study's history ([Bridge Group 2025 SDR report](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)). **[INFERENCE]** For planning, assume that about 50% participation is already above the market and set the team plan at a discount to summed quotas.

### SDR productivity benchmarks

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025]** The Bridge Group reports median daily SDR activity of 112 (44 phone, 41 email, 19 LinkedIn, 8 other), 4.1 quality conversations a day (4.6 for phone-centric teams making 56 dials; 3.4 for email-centric teams), and pipeline sourced per SDR of $3.78 million a year, with the middle half between $1.9 million and $6.4 million ([Bridge Group 2025 SDR report](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)). **[INFERENCE]** The implied dial-to-quality-conversation rate for phone-centric teams is about 8% (4.6 over 56), which is the researchers' arithmetic and not a stated connect rate. **[VENDOR]** A vendor aggregation cites 8 to 10 qualified meetings per SDR per month and says agency-booked show rates of 40 to 60% are common; its worked example values are assumptions ([Glencoco, 2 May 2026](https://www.glencoco.com/2026-state-of-sales-development)).

### Free-trial and freemium conversion benchmarks

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** Free-to-paid conversion, median and 75th percentile, from a report on 200 B2B products relayed by an aggregator: trial without credit card 4 to 6% and 10 to 15%; trial with credit card 25 to 35% and 50 to 60%; freemium 3 to 5% and 8 to 12%; ungated freemium 7 to 9% and 8 to 12%; reverse trial 4 to 6% and 8 to 12%; AI-native products 6 to 8% and 15 to 20%; B2B products 6 to 10% and 15 to 20%; overall trial median 8%. Per 1,000 visitors: freemium yields 90 sign-ups and about 5 payers; a standard trial 45 and 3.6; ungated freemium 70 and 5.6; a card-required trial 35 and 10.5 ([Userpilot summary](https://userpilot.com/blog/free-trial-conversion-rate/)). An agency dataset on the same page shows much higher opt-in trial conversion (18.2%) and should be treated as a selected sample.

### Retention benchmarks depend on price point and sample

**[CONTESTED; FAST-MOVING, 2024-2025 data]** Benchmarkit's survey of venture-scale companies gives median net revenue retention of 101% (105% in 2021), 110% for hybrid subscription-plus-usage pricing, and median gross retention of 88% ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)). ChartMogul's platform data on about 3,500 software companies with at least $250,000 in recurring revenue (as of September 2025, published 10 December 2025) gives a B2B SaaS median net retention of 82% (upper quartile 97%), B2C SaaS 49%, and AI-native medians of 40% gross and 48% net; AI-native by price: above $250 a month 70% gross and 85% net, $50 to $249 a month 45% and 61%, under $50 a month 23% and 32% ([ChartMogul, The AI churn wave, 10 Dec 2025](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/)). **[INFERENCE]** The gap is mainly sample: surveys skew to funded, higher-priced companies and the platform data to small self-serve subscriptions. Pick the benchmark by price point.

### Older retention benchmarks by company size and price

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2022 data, possibly outdated]** ChartMogul's 2023 report on more than 2,100 companies gives top-quartile net retention of 94% at $1 to 3 million in recurring revenue, 99% at $3 to 15 million and above 105% at $15 to 30 million; for average revenue per account above $500 a month, top-quartile net retention of 109.3% and logo retention of 85.7%; below $10 a month, 65.1% and 63.1% ([ChartMogul SaaS Retention Report, 2023](https://chartmogul.com/saas-retention-report/)).

### Growth benchmarks

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025]** High Alpha and Growth Unhinged's survey of more than 800 companies (fielded August to September 2025) gives median growth for AI-native against other B2B SaaS companies by recurring revenue: under $1 million, 100% against 75%; $1 to 5 million, 110% against 40%; $5 to 20 million, 90% against 30%; $20 to 50 million, 60% against 35%; above $50 million, 40% against 15%. Companies with high net retention and low payback (13% of the sample) average 71% growth ([Growth Unhinged, 2025 SaaS Benchmarks](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2025-saas-benchmarks-report)). That group has a Rule of 40 score of 47, against 10% growth for companies with low retention and high payback, and best-in-class recurring revenue per employee is $350,000 at $20 to 50 million in recurring revenue ([Growth Unhinged, 2025 SaaS Benchmarks](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2025-saas-benchmarks-report)). Benchmarkit's median growth is 26% with a top quartile of 50% ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)).

### Nordic benchmarks

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** Monterro's Nordic survey (236 software companies) reports growth of 25% (32% in 2024), net revenue retention of 104.4% (116% for companies monetising AI), churn of 5.5%, acquisition-cost payback of 14.0 months (top quartile 6, bottom quartile 18), revenue per full-time employee of EUR 150,000 and an average net promoter score of 42 (47 in 2024) ([Monterro Nordic Software Benchmark 2026](https://www.monterro.com/benchmark-report-2026/)). **[GAP]** European data is thin: this is the only regional benchmark with numbers read, and it reports no win rates or quota attainment. A European SaaS benchmark from HubSpot exists but is gated ([HubSpot for Startups, European SaaS Benchmark 2025](https://offers.hubspot.com/european-saas-benchmark-2025)).

## 20. Forecasting, pipeline reviews, CRM hygiene and sales management

### Forecast confidence is low and published accuracy norms are missing

**[FAST-MOVING, Feb 2020, outdated]** The one traceable statistic is that only 45% of sales leaders and sellers had high confidence in their organisation's forecast accuracy in a Gartner survey released 12 February 2020 ([Gartner press release, 12 Feb 2020](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2020-02-12-gartner-says-less-than-50--of-sales-leaders-and-selle)). **[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Use of AI for forecasting rose 50% in 2025 in Gong's survey of 3,048 revenue leaders, with no accuracy figure given ([Gong, State of Revenue AI 2026](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-labs-state-of-revenue-ai-2026.pdf)); 40% of sales professionals with usage-based pricing say forecasting revenue is a top challenge ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)). **[GAP]** No current analyst or vendor forecast-accuracy benchmark was retrieved; frequently quoted statistics on the share of companies forecasting within 5% could not be traced.

### Forecasting methods to run side by side

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional methods, not fetched: a stage-weighted forecast sums amount multiplied by stage probability, with probabilities taken from the team's own historical stage-to-win conversion and not CRM defaults. A category forecast uses judgement in categories (closed; commit, expected to close; best case, possible if things go right; pipeline, early; omitted), with the call equal to closed plus commit plus a historical fraction of best case. A run-rate forecast suits high-velocity sales. A model-based forecast scores deals from activity and engagement signals. Run at least two and investigate gaps between them.

### Forecast accuracy formula and targets

**[INFERENCE]** Accuracy is one minus the absolute difference between forecast and actual, divided by actual, measured at fixed points such as week one and mid-quarter. A common practitioner target is within 10% early in the quarter and within 5% by mid to late quarter; no reliable published norm was found.

### Review cadence

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional cadence, not fetched: a weekly pipeline review per rep covering hygiene and next steps; a weekly forecast call covering commit changes only; a monthly or quarterly pipeline-generation review; and a quarterly business review. For an agent department the manager agent can run the weekly reviews continuously and reserve human attention for the exceptions.

### Deal-inspection signals with measured association to outcomes

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Signals associated with losing in Ebsta's data: slipped close dates (win rate from 18% at one week of slip to 3% beyond six months); late-stage deals slipping beyond two months; no decision-maker engaged in the first two stages; no critical event; and contacts missing from the CRM ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)).

### Hygiene rules to enforce automatically

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional rules, not fetched: every open opportunity has an amount, a close date in the future, a dated next step, stage-exit evidence fields, a primary contact and an economic buyer; flag opportunities with no activity in 14 days (small deals) or 30 days (enterprise), age above twice the average stage duration, or a close date pushed more than twice; closed-lost requires a reason; no stage is skipped without evidence. **[VENDOR]** 74% of sales teams with AI say they are prioritising data hygiene ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)).

### Compensation and quota norms

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Account executives: median on-target earnings of $200,000 ($190,000 in 2024, $167,000 in 2022), median quota of $960,000 ($875,000 for SaaS) and a quota-to-earnings ratio of 4.6 (4.2 in 2024) in the Bridge Group's 2026 survey ([Bridge Group 2026 AE report, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2026-ae-models-motions-metrics)). SDRs: median on-target earnings of $80,000 at a 68:32 base-to-variable split ([Bridge Group 2025 SDR report](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)). **[INFERENCE]** Conventional norms, not fetched: a 50:50 pay mix for account executives (60:40 for enterprise, 70:30 for SDRs and account management); uncapped commission with accelerators above 100%; short single-behaviour incentives used sparingly; clawbacks on early churn; quota at four to five times earnings; planning for 50 to 60% of reps at quota; ramp quotas stepping up over the ramp period. **[HOUSE STYLE]** His different rule of thumb is total sales cost under 10% of revenue with commission rising by close rate ([Hormozi 4-hour sales guide, transcript, 31 Jul 2024](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-sell-better-than-99-of-people-4-hour-ultimate-guide)).

### Coaching and onboarding

**[VENDOR]** Evidence on coaching effects is weak: 75% of reps say they are more likely to hit targets with a coach or mentor, which is perception ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)); assigning the right sellers to the right customers is associated with 90% higher quota attainment in Ebsta's data ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). Salesforce relays Gartner figures that 36% of sales teams with agents use them for coaching, that overwhelmed sellers are 45% less likely to attain quota and that sellers who partner with AI tools are 3.7 times more likely to meet quota, with samples not stated ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)). **[INFERENCE]** Conventional cadence, not fetched: a weekly one-to-one on pipeline plus one skill, one to two call reviews per rep per week against a scorecard, a monthly deal deep-dive and a quarterly development plan; onboarding on a 30-60-90 plan with certification gates and time to first deal as the ramp metric. **[HOUSE STYLE]** His cadence is daily script practice and a weekly review of three tagged calls (section 6). **[GAP]** No controlled or large-sample study of coaching hours against win rate was read.

### The agent analogues of coaching and compensation

**[INFERENCE]** For AI agents the analogue of coaching is transcript review against a playbook rubric followed by prompt and playbook updates, and the analogue of compensation is the objective function. Single-metric targets such as meetings booked can be gamed, so worker agents are measured on held meetings that convert to opportunities, on stage conversion and on compliance checks together.

### AI use and attainment are correlated, not proven causal

**[VENDOR]** 57% of reps in the highest AI-engagement third were at quota against 39% in the lowest in the Bridge Group's 2026 survey, an observational result ([Bridge Group 2026 AE report, 22 Jun 2026](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2026-ae-models-motions-metrics)); sellers who frequently use AI generate 77% more revenue than non-users in Gong's data ([Gong, State of Revenue AI 2026](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-labs-state-of-revenue-ai-2026.pdf)). Both are correlational and likely reflect selection effects.

## 21. SaaS go-to-market motions and pricing

### Motion follows contract value

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional mapping, not fetched: under about $1,000 to $5,000 a year, self-serve product-led growth with freemium or trial; about $5,000 to $25,000, product-led sales or inside sales with qualified users and demo requests routed to a rep; about $25,000 to $100,000, inside and mid-market sales with SDRs and sales engineers; above $100,000, enterprise field sales with proof of concept and procurement. **[FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Benchmarkit observes that deals of $10,000 to $50,000 often cost more to acquire per dollar of recurring revenue than deals of $50,000 to $100,000, and that deals above $250,000 pay back faster than those of $50,000 to $100,000, without exact values ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)); this is the difficult middle between self-serve and enterprise motions.

### Trial, freemium, reverse trial or demo

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional guidance, not fetched: freemium when marginal cost is near zero, the market is large and usage spreads virally; a time-boxed trial when value shows in days and usage has real cost; a reverse trial to combine both; demo-led when setup needs integration or data or several stakeholders. **[VENDOR]** The conversion data favour card-required trials for paid conversion per sign-up and freemium for sign-up volume (section 19, [Userpilot summary](https://userpilot.com/blog/free-trial-conversion-rate/)). AI inference cost makes free tiers expensive, consistent with a median target AI gross margin of about 50% in a 2026 survey ([Growth Unhinged, State of B2B Monetization 2026](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-state-of-b2b-monetization-in-2026)).

### Pricing models are moving from seats to hybrid

**[FAST-MOVING, Apr-May 2026]** Growth Unhinged's survey of more than 230 B2B SaaS and AI companies finds hybrid pricing the most common model at 37% (25% a year earlier); flat-fee subscription peaks at 37% among companies under $5 million in recurring revenue; seat-based is 29% among companies above $150 million; 29% offer multiple models; three in four changed pricing or packaging in the past year; and the top challenge is insufficient expansion revenue, especially for flat-fee and seat-based models ([Growth Unhinged, State of B2B Monetization 2026](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-state-of-b2b-monetization-in-2026)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Usage-based companies grew faster (44% against 25% median) and hybrid had higher net retention (110% against 101%) in Benchmarkit's survey ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)).

### Pricing AI features and agents

**[FAST-MOVING, Apr-May 2026]** In the same survey 29% use AI credits and 33% plan to add them within 6 to 12 months; internal cost and margin is the top AI pricing input (54%), then competition (36%) and productivity gains (30%); only 12% target AI gross margins of 80% or more; 70% say AI spend comes from existing software budgets; the author advises a premium edition 50 to 100% above base and usage limits for heavy AI users ([Growth Unhinged, State of B2B Monetization 2026](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-state-of-b2b-monetization-in-2026)). **[VENDOR]** Among companies monetising AI in High Alpha's 2025 benchmarks, 53% use subscription, 31% hybrid, 11% pure usage and 5% pure outcome pricing, with hybrid showing the highest median net retention at 105% ([High Alpha](https://www.highalpha.com/blog/how-saas-companies-are-monetizing-ai-and-5-predictions-for-2026)).

### Packaging and value metric

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional guidance, not fetched: charge on a unit that scales with customer value and is easy to predict (seats, usage, outcomes); offer three tiers plus an enterprise "contact us" option; use add-ons for features only a minority need.

### Discount policy

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional guidance, not fetched: publish a discount matrix with approval levels, require a give-get (term length, annual prepay, case study, volume) and prefer an annual-prepay discount over ad hoc cuts. **[VENDOR]** Ebsta's finding that top performers do not discount and close faster supports a strict policy ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). **[GAP]** No discount-rate benchmark was found. For agents the policy is enforced outside the model (section 31).

### Annual against monthly plans

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Sep 2025 data]** Annual plans have 10 to 20 points higher median net revenue retention than monthly plans ([ChartMogul, The AI churn wave, 10 Dec 2025](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/)). When to use: offer annual prepay as the default recommendation at the close for any customer above the lowest tier.

## 22. Post-sale revenue: onboarding, retention, expansion, advocacy and partners

### Why post-sale belongs in the sales knowledge base

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Expansion supplies roughly 40% of new recurring revenue at the median and 58% at $50 to 100 million in recurring revenue in Benchmarkit's survey ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)) and 52% of new revenue in Ebsta's data, where it wins at 45% against 19% in 52 against 91 days ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). The expansion acquisition-cost ratio is $1.00 against $2.00 for new customers, half the cost though up from about $0.65 ([Benchmarkit 2025 B2B SaaS Performance Metrics](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)).

### Onboarding as a pipeline stage

**[FAST-MOVING, Jan 2026]** The Bowtie model treats onboarding as a stage ending at first measurable impact, with onboarding conversion (one minus onboarding churn) and time from purchase to first impact as its metrics ([Winning by Design, The Bowtie Standard, Jan 2026](https://winningbydesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/The-Bowtie-Standard.pdf)). **[HOUSE STYLE]** His Reinforce step (a personal touch immediately after payment and re-closing at onboarding and first check-in) is the house-style content of this stage ([Hormozi CLOSER training, transcript, 2 Jun 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-taught-116-salesmen-my-closing-framework)). **[INFERENCE]** Time to value should have an owner and a service level, because onboarding churn is otherwise invisible in new-business numbers. **[GAP]** No quantitative benchmark was found for time to value or onboarding completion.

### Customer health scoring

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional components, not fetched: product usage (active users against licences, key-feature adoption, trend), outcomes achieved against the success plan, support tickets and severity, stakeholder engagement (champion still present, executive sponsor contact), commercial signals (payment delays, downgrade requests) and satisfaction scores. Weight usage trend and champion change most and validate the score against actual churn each quarter. **[GAP]** No benchmark for health-score predictive accuracy was found.

### Renewal process

**[INFERENCE]** Conventional process, not fetched: open a renewal opportunity 90 to 120 days before term end (180 for enterprise), run a risk review at 90 days, and give price-increase notice as the contract requires. For Swedish consumers on auto-renewing fixed terms a written reminder at least one month before the last termination date is a legal requirement (checked on primary source, [Lag (2014:1449)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2014-1449.html)).

### Expansion triggers and executive engagement

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024 data]** Ongoing executive engagement is associated with 189% more expansion opportunities, and likelihood to expand rises from about 18% at low engagement to about 35% at high engagement ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). **[INFERENCE]** Conventional triggers, not fetched: usage approaching limits, a new team or use case, and a champion's promotion. **[HOUSE STYLE]** His timing rule is to make the next offer at the halfway to two-thirds point of delivery ([Shortform summary of Ep 998, 10 Sep 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-09-10-episode-summary-if-you-hate-selling-watch-this-ep-998)). **[VENDOR; CONTESTED]** Provocative messaging to existing customers at renewal may raise the risk of loss, according to a competitor of the Challenger method ([Corporate Visions on the Challenger model](https://corporatevisions.com/blog/challenger-sales-model/)); with existing customers lead with their own results.

### Churn risk concentrates at low prices and in AI-native products

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Sep 2025 data]** Gross retention for AI-native products is 23% under $50 a month against 70% above $250 a month ([ChartMogul, The AI churn wave, 10 Dec 2025](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/)); companies reaching $20 million in recurring revenue raised net retention by about 12 points on the way ([Growth Unhinged, 2025 SaaS Benchmarks](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/2025-saas-benchmarks-report)).

### Advocacy and referrals

**[INFERENCE]** Ask for a referral after a verified outcome (first value milestone or a high satisfaction score); this is convention and matches the house-style rule of asking at each win (section 9). **[GAP]** No dated benchmark for referral conversion was retrieved.

### Partner and channel basics

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2024-2026]** 94% of sales teams say they use partner selling, up from 86% ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)), with 90% of those using dedicated partner tools and 40% of partners having full access to company AI tools ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)), and partner referrals have the highest channel efficiency at 1.3 times average, ahead of organic inbound at 1.2, outbound at 1.05, events at 0.78 and paid at 0.68 ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). A 2024 vendor compilation without sample sizes relays that partner-sourced opportunities have a 46% higher win rate and close 11% faster, among other figures whose definitions differ and should not be combined ([Crossbeam Insider, 9 Apr 2024](https://insider.crossbeam.com/entry/unleashing-the-power-of-nearbound-the-stats-you-need-to-know)). **[INFERENCE]** Conventional structure, not fetched and with unverified percentages: referral or affiliate partners paid a commission; resellers who own the contract and buy at a discount; agencies and integrators who implement and influence; technology partners; cloud marketplaces; and embedded partners. Operating rules: deal registration with time-limited protection, partner-sourced and partner-influenced tracked as separate fields, a written channel-conflict rule and partner tiers. **[GAP]** No primary source was fetched on partner commission norms or marketplace share.

## 23. Prospecting foundations: who to pursue, when, and through which warm route

### Account selection matters more than outreach volume

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Nov 2025]** Because 94% of buying groups rank preferred vendors before first seller contact and the favourite wins about 80% of deals ([6sense via Business Wire, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112018032/en/)), prospecting is as much about reaching the shortlist before the buying window as about booking a meeting now. **[INFERENCE]** This argues for a tiered model: a small tier 1 of named accounts with best fit and an active trigger, worked with research-heavy, human-reviewed, multi-threaded plays on phone, email and LinkedIn; a tier 2 of good-fit accounts with one verified trigger, worked with templated-plus-research messages; and a tier 3 of fit-only accounts covered in small batches of under about 50 recipients per campaign. The tier definitions are the researchers' synthesis.

### Build the ideal customer profile from existing customers

**[VENDOR; 2021, possibly outdated]** A Nordic data vendor advises building the ideal customer profile from existing customers and concentrating on segments where the product creates most value before expanding ([Vainu, 2021](https://www.vainu.com/blog/expand-your-business-to-sweden/)). **[GAP]** No sourced method for total addressable market list building was retrieved.

### Narrow lists and few contacts per company reply more

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** Hunter's data on 31 million emails shows sequences with 21 to 50 recipients got 6.2% reply against 2.4% for 500 or more, and contacting one or two people per company got 5.1% against 3.5% for three or more ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)). **[INFERENCE]** Multi-threading is better done across channels (phone or LinkedIn to additional stakeholders) than by emailing many people at one company at once.

### Smaller companies and less senior titles reply more

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** Belkins' dataset of 7.53 million emails shows reply per email of 0.72% for companies of 0 to 10 employees, 0.49% for 11 to 50 and 0.22% for 10,000 or more; founders and owners 0.57%, C-level 0.42%, vice presidents 0.32% ([Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md)). Gong's analysis of more than one million executive sales cycles found C-level executives 30.2% less likely to reply than non-executives (29 January 2026, [Gong, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.gong.io/blog/do-execs-really-reply-to-cold-email-here-s-what-the-data-says)). **[INFERENCE]** Identical sequences should not be benchmarked across segments; expect two to three times the reply rate from micro and small companies as from large enterprises.

### Bounce rate is the cheapest test of data quality

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Smartlead reports typical bounce of 1.54%, a danger line of 2% and a worst quartile above 3.21%; Hunter reports an average bounce of 3.6%; Instantly says under 2% is ideal ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks); [Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email); [Instantly benchmark report 2026](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)). On the phone, wrong-person or wrong-number outcomes were 13.5% of non-booking call outcomes in Belkins' 175,000-dial dataset ([Belkins cold calling benchmarks, 26 Jun 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks.md)). **[INFERENCE]** Verify addresses before sending and stop any list whose bounce rate exceeds 2%. **[GAP]** No sourced benchmark for data-provider accuracy by vendor was found.

### Trigger events that justify outreach

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, May 2026]** One vendor's recommended triggers are a new budget cycle or funding round, a champion promoted or departed or a new decision-maker joining, a merger or acquisition, a technology change (competitor replaced, legacy tool retired) and restructuring; it argues that signal and not elapsed time should drive timing ([UserGems, 22 May 2026](https://www.usergems.com/blog/closed-lost-re-engagement-playbook)).

### Third-party intent data is a weak signal

**[GAP]** No independent, methodologically transparent study of third-party intent data accuracy was retrieved. One 2026 article asserts precision of 30 to 40% and a seven-month median lag in LinkedIn title updates without tracing either figure to a study ([leadgen-economy.com, 2026](https://leadgen-economy.com/blog/ai-sdr-cancellation-wave-failure-forensics/)); treat both as unverified. **[INFERENCE]** Use intent data to prioritise within a fit list, never as a trigger on its own.

### Check warm routes before any cold touch

**[INFERENCE]** Warm paths in likely order of yield: existing customers who changed jobs, closed-lost contacts, customer referrals and introductions, partner-shared accounts, event and webinar attendees, community members, then cold. **[VENDOR]** The supporting single-company data: callbacks succeed at 26.85% against about 2.3% for cold calls (Cognism, 2025, [Cognism cold calling statistics, 2025 report](https://www.cognism.com/blog/cold-calling-statistics)); warm account-executive emails got 28.38% reply against 8.98% for SDR cold emails at the same company ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)); messaging existing LinkedIn connections got 12.2% replies against 7.9% for connect-then-message campaigns ([Belkins LinkedIn outreach study, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study.md)). The gap of three to ten times is directionally reliable; exact multiples do not transfer. **[GAP]** No dated benchmarks were retrieved for referral, community, event or webinar lead conversion, and named practitioner sources other than Jason Bay's work were not fetched.

### Re-engaging closed-lost and no-decision deals

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, May 2026]** Guidance is consistent but unquantified: re-engage on a new signal and not on a timer; review recordings and objection history to find the original blocker and address it. One vendor's example sequence is five touches over two to three weeks: a signal-triggered email referencing the earlier conversation; a proof point tied to the original use case; a resource addressing the stalled objection; a direct ask for a 15-minute call with an agenda; and an optional final touch to a different stakeholder. Metrics to track are re-engagement reply rate against cold, meeting conversion, closed-lost pipeline as a separate CRM source, deal velocity and cost per opportunity ([UserGems, 22 May 2026](https://www.usergems.com/blog/closed-lost-re-engagement-playbook)). Another vendor advises pausing a sequence after five touches with no engagement and re-engaging after 60 to 90 days on a new trigger ([Apollo, 6 May 2026](https://www.apollo.io/insights/whats-the-ideal-cadence-for-a-multi-channel-outbound-sequence)). **[GAP]** No sourced benchmark for win-back or nurture conversion was retrieved.

### Loss reasons decide the re-engagement trigger

**[INFERENCE]** Record a structured loss reason (timing, budget, competitor, no decision, fit) and the contacts involved at close. Budget losses re-open on a new fiscal year or funding; competitor losses on the contract renewal date; no-decision losses on a new stakeholder or priority change; poor-fit losses are not recycled. With buying cycles of about 10 months ([6sense via Business Wire, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251112018032/en/)), a quarterly light-touch nurture plus signal monitoring is proportionate, and a former champion moving company creates two leads. Monitoring signals across the whole closed-lost base is work agents do well and humans rarely do.

### Segment and regional differences in prospecting

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Suggested sequence scale from one vendor without sample sizes: small-business or high-intent accounts 6 to 10 touches over 10 to 14 days on email and phone; mid-market 10 to 12 touches over four to six weeks adding social; enterprise 12 to 18 touches over 8 to 12 weeks adding direct mail ([Apollo, 6 May 2026](https://www.apollo.io/insights/whats-the-ideal-cadence-for-a-multi-channel-outbound-sequence)). Phone connect and reply rates look higher outside the US in small samples: a UK live-contact rate of 14.7% and positive rate of 2.84% against the US at 9.0% and 0.55%, with non-US samples described as directional ([Belkins cold calling benchmarks, 26 Jun 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks.md)); cold-email reply per email of 0.73% in Denmark against 0.51% in the US ([Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md)); LinkedIn reply of 10.5% in the Netherlands against 6.0% in the US ([Belkins LinkedIn outreach study, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study.md)). **[GAP]** No robust Nordic outbound benchmarks exist in the sources read.

### Nordic buyer norms

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2026]** A vendor guide describes Nordic buyers as favouring direct, clear, structured communication; Denmark as having flat hierarchies and fast decisions; Sweden as consensus-driven and slower, with refusals rarely blunt; Norway as hierarchical and data-driven with strict work-life boundaries; Finland as prepared and agenda-bound. English works, but localised materials convert better and customers expect account management and support in their own language; trade shows are described as still effective where cold email is consent-restricted ([Leadfeeder Nordics guide, 2026](https://www.leadfeeder.com/guides/complete-go-to-market-guide-nordics/)). **[VENDOR; 2021, possibly outdated]** One vendor's experience entering Sweden: local-language meetings had slightly higher hit and conversion rates, lacking a local entity or phone number created doubt, Swedish deals involved more stakeholders, and Sweden needed 50 to 100% more calls or emails per meeting than Finland ([Vainu, 2021](https://www.vainu.com/blog/expand-your-business-to-sweden/)). **[INFERENCE]** For Nordic prospecting the default channel order is phone (after register checks) and LinkedIn first, with email where consent, an existing relationship or a confirmed lawful basis exists; use local language and numbers; avoid July and the main holidays; and remember that small markets are exhausted quickly by low-quality automated outreach.

## 24. Outbound channels: cold email, cold calling, LinkedIn and multichannel sequences

### Cold email: mailbox-provider rules are the hard floor

**[FAST-MOVING, checked Nov 2026]** Gmail requires SPF or DKIM for all senders and SPF, DKIM and DMARC for bulk senders, a spam rate in Postmaster Tools below 0.10% and never reaching 0.30%, and one-click unsubscribe headers plus a visible link on marketing and subscribed messages; non-compliant mail can be marked as spam or rejected. Gmail's volume guidance is to send at a consistent rate, start low with engaged recipients, increase slowly and cut volume if bounces or deferrals start ([Google email sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126?hl=en)). Microsoft's consumer mail services require domains sending more than 5,000 emails a day to pass SPF and DKIM and publish DMARC from 5 May 2025, and reject non-compliant mail ([Microsoft Tech Community](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/outlook/strengthening-email-ecosystem-outlook%E2%80%99s-new-requirements-for-high%E2%80%90volume-senders/4399730)). A vendor summary modified 3 November 2026 says Google, Yahoo and Microsoft all set the bulk threshold at 5,000 or more messages a day per domain, that Yahoo expects unsubscribes honoured within two business days and that Apple requires authentication for bulk senders ([EasyDMARC, modified 3 Nov 2026](https://easydmarc.com/blog/google-yahoo-microsoft-icloud/)). **[GAP]** The enforcement start dates for Gmail and Yahoo and Yahoo's own sender page were not verified in the research. A vendor item dated 9 November 2026 headlined a brief Microsoft rejection of mail from domains without DMARC; only the headline was seen ([EasyDMARC, modified 3 Nov 2026](https://easydmarc.com/blog/google-yahoo-microsoft-icloud/)).

### Cold email: infrastructure and sending volume

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Smartlead's data (more than 850 million emails, H1 2026) shows a typical sender at 13.9 emails per mailbox per day, the heaviest 10% at 42, a stated safe limit of 50, and authenticated senders needing 108 contacts per reply against 120 for unauthenticated ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)). The same dataset shows a typical account running 20 mailboxes and a typical campaign list of 499 people, and contacts per reply of 90 for Gmail senders, 120 for Outlook and 179 for custom servers ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)). Hunter's 2025 data shows 20 to 49 emails per account per day at 5.7% reply against 4.5% overall, a custom domain at 5.2% against 2.5% for free mail, and disabling open tracking at 7.4% against 4.4% with tracking ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)). Instantly advises warming new mailboxes at 5 to 10 emails a day and increasing over four to six weeks ([Instantly benchmark report 2026](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)). It also says consistent senders see 15 to 20% more replies ([Instantly benchmark report 2026](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)), and Hunter reports Google Workspace senders at 5.9% reply against just over 4% for Microsoft 365 ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)). Smartlead excludes open rate from its benchmarks because automatic image loading inflates opens, and Belkins disabled open tracking for all 2025 campaigns ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks); [Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md)).

### Cold email: operating thresholds for agents

**[INFERENCE]** Derived thresholds: authenticate every sending domain even far below 5,000 a day; warm for four to six weeks; cap at roughly 20 to 30 cold emails per mailbox per day with 50 as a ceiling; pause a mailbox when bounce exceeds 2% or complaint rate approaches 0.1%; keep open tracking off; include an easy opt-out in every message. Sending from separate domains and not the primary corporate domain is universal practitioner advice. **[GAP]** No primary source was retrieved for the separate-domain practice or for a safe number of mailboxes per domain.

### Cold email: reply and meeting benchmarks, with denominators

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Per contact, Smartlead: median sender 0.74% (135 contacts per reply), top quarter 1.43%, top tenth 2.63%, top twentieth 3.85% ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)). Per email sent, Belkins' managed campaigns: 0.45% average in 2025 on 7,530,489 emails, 0.50% in H1 and 0.40% in H2, with more than 1,200 appointments ([Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md)). All replies over all emails sent, Instantly: 3.43% average, top quarter 5.5% and above, top tenth 10.7% and above ([Instantly benchmark report 2026](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)). Per sequence, Hunter: 4.5% average but 3% for sales use cases, with 1.6% unsubscribe ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)). Gong Labs with Jason Bay on more than 28 million cold emails: the average rep needs 344 cold emails to book one meeting, and top performers book 8.1 times more meetings ([Gong Labs cold email study, 24 Jul 2025](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/does-cold-email-even-work-any-more-heres-what-the-data-says/)). Single-company and coached-population figures are higher: Cognism's own team at 8.98% reply and 1.12% opt-out ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)) and Lavender's users around 5% for well-scored emails ([Lavender, 4 Feb 2026](https://lavender.ai/blog/the-cold-email-benchmark-report)). Further detail from the same datasets: Smartlead's bottom tenth need 625 contacts per reply ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)); Belkins' reply rate peaked in February at 0.54% and bottomed in December at 0.35% ([Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md)); Hunter's non-sales use cases reply at 6.2% (marketing), 7.5% (recruiting) and 13% (digital PR), and a third of its surveyed decision-makers replied to no cold email in the past year while 32% replied to five or more ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)); in Lavender's sample of 231,818 emails, well-scored emails to technical buyers got 5.2% and only 6.1% of emails to finance earned its top score ([Lavender, 4 Feb 2026](https://lavender.ai/blog/the-cold-email-benchmark-report)).

### Cold email: planning yield

**[INFERENCE]** For forecasting use the per-contact figures: roughly one reply per 100 to 200 contacts and one meeting per several hundred emails for average execution, with top-decile execution three to eight times better. Belkins' totals imply about one appointment per 6,000 emails sent, which is the researchers' arithmetic on its published totals. The platform averages from Instantly and Hunter are higher mainly because of definitions and user mix.

### Cold email: length, subject and content

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Gong and Bay: emails of 100 words or fewer get more replies, best at three to four sentences; pitching the product cuts reply rates by as much as 57%; buzzwords and numbers cut open rates by up to 17.9%; subject lines should avoid selling language, jargon, numbers and questions ([Gong Labs cold email study, 24 Jul 2025](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/does-cold-email-even-work-any-more-heres-what-the-data-says/)). For executives: subject lines of one to four words naming their problem, bodies of 50 to 100 words, and a call to action that offers something of value such as a peer benchmark instead of asking for a meeting ([Gong, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.gong.io/blog/do-execs-really-reply-to-cold-email-here-s-what-the-data-says)). Smartlead: first emails under 50 words got 1.47% reply ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)). **[CONTESTED]** Hunter disagrees on details: a subject sweet spot of five to six words, both 61 to 80 and 181 to 200 word bodies performing comparatively well, and no measurable effect from images, attachments, emojis or day of week ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)).

### Cold email: personalisation and why emails fail

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025 data]** Hunter found two custom attributes in the body gave 5.6% reply against 3.6% with none and manually edited emails 5.2% against 4.4% fully automated. Its decision-maker survey (respondent count not stated) says cold emails fail because they are too pushy (65%), irrelevant (61%) or generic (48%), and that preferred asks are low-pressure offers of more information ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)).

### Cold email: follow-ups and timing

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Hunter: three messages gave 6.8% total reply against 3.3% for one, the first message drives most replies, and its recommendation moved from four messages to three ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)). Instantly: step one produces 58% of replies, with three to four days between touches and reply-style follow-ups outperforming formal ones by about 30% ([Instantly benchmark report 2026](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)). Smartlead: email one gets 1.18% reply and email seven 0.33%; 64% of replies arrive within one hour and 88.5% within 24 hours ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks)). Timing findings vary: peak around 7am recipient time at Smartlead, 8am to noon at Belkins, Tuesday to Wednesday at Instantly ([Smartlead benchmarks, H1 2026](https://www.smartlead.ai/benchmarks); [Belkins cold email response rates, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md); [Instantly benchmark report 2026](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)). **[INFERENCE]** Default sequence: three to four emails over 10 to 14 days, three to four days apart, a first email of 50 to 80 words, later emails written as replies in the same thread and each adding something new. Agents must be ready to answer replies within the hour.

### A first-touch message template and lint rules

**[INFERENCE]** A first-touch template consistent with the evidence: a subject of one to four words naming the prospect's problem; one line showing why this person and why now, based on a verified trigger; one line on the problem as peers experience it; one specific proof point, only if true and permitted; one soft question offering something useful; under 80 words, with no feature list, no mention of AI as a selling point and no meeting link. Lint rules for agent output: more than 100 words; a product-first opening; unverifiable personalisation; multiple asks; superlatives and buzzwords; statistics in the subject line; fake familiarity on a first touch; identical copy at scale. **[GAP]** No controlled evidence compares problem-led with product-led messaging beyond Gong's correlational data.

### Cold calling: connect and meeting benchmarks

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Belkins' 2025 data on more than 175,000 dials: a per-dial connect rate of 9.9%, per-prospect connect rate of 24.5%, connect-to-conversation of 58%, conversation-to-meeting of 4.6%, roughly 370 dials per meeting and three attempts per prospect ([Belkins cold calling benchmarks, 26 Jun 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks.md)). Cognism's 2026 report on more than 200,000 calls: a success rate (calls resulting in a meeting) of 2.7%, up from 2.3% and down from 4.82% in 2024; its own team at 11.3%; average call 82 seconds ([Cognism Cold Calling Report 2026](https://www.cognism.com/reports/cold-calling-report-2026)). Cognism's own SDR calls had a 13.3% answer rate in 2025 ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)). Cognism also reports 1.55 calls on average to reach a prospect (2.9 the year before) ([Cognism Cold Calling Report 2026](https://www.cognism.com/reports/cold-calling-report-2026)), an average SDR call of two minutes and about 70% of SDR calls coming from sequences ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)). One vendor states connect rates typically range from 3% to 10% ([Orum blog, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.orum.com/blog/how-to-improve-sales-connect-rates-in-2026)). **[INFERENCE]** The two headline numbers use different denominators; report both with the denominator. **[GAP]** Cognism's regional figures are inconsistent across its own pages and should be treated as unreliable.

### Cold calling: attempts and timing

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025]** 93% of conversations happen by the third call attempt and more than 98% by the fifth ([Cognism cold calling statistics, 2025 report](https://www.cognism.com/blog/cold-calling-statistics)). **[CONTESTED]** Best-time findings conflict: Cognism finds 10 to 11am and 2 to 3pm with Thursday strongest and Friday weakest ([Cognism Cold Calling Report 2026](https://www.cognism.com/reports/cold-calling-report-2026)); Belkins finds Wednesday then Monday, 3 to 4pm and 6pm local, with meeting rates highest in February (8.9%) and May (8.5%) and lowest in July to September at about 3% ([Belkins cold calling benchmarks, 26 Jun 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks.md)). **[INFERENCE]** Stop after about five attempts, test timing by segment, and expect Friday and the summer months to be weak.

### Cold calling: openers and call structure

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Jul 2024]** In Gong's analysis of more than 300 million cold calls, asking whether it is a bad time scored 2.15%, about baseline; asking how the day is going 7.6%; a permission-based opener (lead with context, own that it is a cold call, ask for time) 11.18%; and asking whether the prospect has heard the company's name 11.24%. If they have heard of you, ask what they heard; if not, explain how you work with their peers ([Gong Labs openers study, 24 Jul 2024](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/the-best-and-worst-cold-call-openers-backed-by-data-from-300m-calls/)). The page does not define success and was edited in 2026, so quote it cautiously. Meeting-booking calls averaged 3.7 minutes in Belkins' data ([Belkins cold calling benchmarks, 26 Jun 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks.md)). **[INFERENCE]** Script skeleton: name and context; acknowledge that it is a cold call and ask for 30 seconds; a one-sentence problem statement tied to peers; a question; then the ask for a meeting.

### Cold calling: who answers and what they say

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** By title in Belkins' data: managers and leads had an 11.1% live-contact rate and vice presidents 7.3%; C-level calls produced follow-up requests more than bookings; directors booked the most meetings. Non-booking conversations ended in hard rejection 31%, warm follow-up 19%, wrong person or number 13.5%, gatekeeper 13.4% and a request for email 9% ([Belkins cold calling benchmarks, 26 Jun 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks.md)). The top objections per Cognism are not interested, other priorities, too busy so send an email, tried similar before and already have a solution; its suggested response to "not interested" is to ask whether that is because of existing tools or timing and budget ([Cognism Cold Calling Report 2026](https://www.cognism.com/reports/cold-calling-report-2026)). **[INFERENCE]** Prepare responses to these five in advance, phrased as a clarifying question and not a rebuttal.

### Cold calling: number reputation

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Aug 2026]** One vendor guide lists spam-label triggers for outbound numbers: high volume from one number with low engagement, low answer rates, short call durations, complaints and blocks, recycled numbers and mismatched caller identity; rotation alone does not fix it; warm new numbers gradually, track volume, answer rate and duration per number, and keep suppression lists current ([Kixie, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.kixie.com/sales-blog/your-2025-guide-to-caller-id-reputation-management/)). **[GAP]** No sourced data on voicemail was retrieved in the prospecting research, carriers publish no calls-per-number threshold, and no independent comparison of dialler types was found.

### Cold calling: older and unverified vendor claims

**[VENDOR; 2018, outdated]** Gong's 2018 analysis of 90,380 connected cold calls found successful calls were almost twice as long, with reps talking more in longer monologues (37 against 25 seconds), recommended stating the reason for the call early, and put baseline success as low as 1.48% ([Gong Labs, 2018](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/is-cold-calling-dead/)). **[VENDOR]** One dialler vendor says early morning and 4 to 6pm are better for reaching decision-makers and that its dialler connects reps faster than parallel diallers, with no independent test; its large gated report was not readable ([Orum blog, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.orum.com/blog/how-to-improve-sales-connect-rates-in-2026); [Orum report page](https://www.orum.com/reports-and-guides/the-state-of-cold-calling)). Older survey figures relayed by a vendor say 57% of C-level and vice-president buyers prefer phone contact and 82% of buyers accept meetings at least occasionally from sellers who reach out; the date is not shown ([Cognism cold calling statistics, 2025 report](https://www.cognism.com/blog/cold-calling-statistics)). A combined 2026 outreach report from one agency publishes no combined-channel numbers on its landing page ([Belkins State of B2B Outreach 2026](https://belkins.io/b2b-outreach)).

### Phone-centric teams have more conversations

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025]** Phone-centric SDR teams have 4.6 quality conversations a day against 3.4 for email-centric teams ([Bridge Group 2025 SDR report](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)). **[INFERENCE]** That is about 35% more, supporting a phone-first mix where calling is lawful.

### LinkedIn: acceptance and reply benchmarks

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, May 2025-Apr 2026]** Expandi's data on 13,302 accounts and 13.2 million connection requests: acceptance of 28.5%, reply to the connection note of 3.0% and reply to messages of 10.4%; connection-note reply fell from 3.5% in May 2025 to 2.2% in April 2026 while request volume rose about 13% ([Expandi, May 2025-Apr 2026 data](https://expandi.io/?p=20007)). Belkins and Expandi on 15.1 million contacts in 2025: an average connection rate of 26% and overall reply rate of 7.2%; requests with a note got 25.3% acceptance against 27.6% without, but 8.2% reply against 5.3%; in Belkins' own client funnel 18.7% accepted, 17.6% of connected prospects replied and 1.3% of connected prospects booked a meeting ([Belkins LinkedIn outreach study, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study.md)). AI-assisted messages got 7.3% reply against 7.0% without AI, effectively no difference ([Belkins LinkedIn outreach study, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study.md)). By campaign type, messaging existing connections got 12.2% reply, connection-then-message 7.9% and open InMail 7.5% on only 13,600 contacts; by recipient role, HR and talent 10.9%, C-level 7.0% and founders 6.4% ([Belkins LinkedIn outreach study, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study.md)). By sender industry in Expandi's data, staffing and recruiting saw 36.5% acceptance and computer software 27.5%; 82% of volume comes from senders at companies with 200 or fewer employees ([Expandi, May 2025-Apr 2026 data](https://expandi.io/?p=20007)).

### LinkedIn: limits and restriction triggers

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, Jul 2026]** A vendor guide mixing official and community figures gives official limits of 50 InMail credits a month on Sales Navigator Core rolling over to 150, 30,000 first-degree connections and a 300-character connection note, and community-reported limits of about 100 invitations per rolling seven days and 10 to 30 first messages a day. Restriction triggers are exceeding limits, rapid repeated actions, identical copy at scale, device or location churn and scraping tools ([Overloop, 8 Jul 2026](https://overloop.com/blog/linkedin-limits)). LinkedIn's user agreement prohibits scraping and automated messaging (unverified, not fetched, [LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement)). **[GAP]** The roughly 100-a-week limit is community-reported and not confirmed by LinkedIn.

### LinkedIn: how agents should use it

**[INFERENCE]** With about 100 invitations a week, 26 to 29% acceptance and a 1.3% connected-to-meeting rate, one profile yields on the order of one meeting every three weeks from cold connection campaigns alone. LinkedIn is best used as a recognition and warming layer in a multichannel sequence and for messaging existing connections. A blank request followed by a relevant message after acceptance is a defensible default. All benchmark datasets come from automation tools whose use LinkedIn's terms prohibit, so the safer pattern is for agents to draft and queue while a human or a compliant integration sends. **[VENDOR]** 50.5% of decision-makers in Hunter's survey named LinkedIn as their preferred outreach channel against 25% for email ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)).

### Multichannel sequences

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** Cognism's 2025 data on 196,470 prospects: an average of 3.36 touches per prospect, best results at three to five touches, a recommended opening of a call, then an email, then a LinkedIn touch within the first five days, and a task mix of 57% calls, 27% LinkedIn and 15% email ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)). Apollo's 12-touch mid-market template over 28 days, with no sample: day 1 call plus intro email; day 3 email with a self-serve asset; day 5 call plus voicemail; day 8 social touch; day 10 email with case study; day 14 call; day 18 email with a low-friction ask; day 22 social; day 28 call plus a final email ([Apollo, 6 May 2026](https://www.apollo.io/insights/whats-the-ideal-cadence-for-a-multi-channel-outbound-sequence)). **[INFERENCE]** A default consistent with the per-channel evidence: three to four emails, three to five call attempts and two to three LinkedIn touches, giving 8 to 12 touches in three to four weeks, front-loaded in the first five days, then stop and recycle on a new trigger after 60 to 90 days. Measure completed touches per prospect and not designed sequence length. Channel choice follows the consent rules in section 27. **[GAP]** No controlled or large-sample study quantifies the lift from combining channels, and no evidence on SMS, messaging apps or direct mail in B2B sequences was retrieved.

## 25. Inbound lead handling

### Most companies respond slowly or not at all

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Sep 2026]** In Clay's field experiment published 22 September 2026, 6,346 B2B demo and contact forms were submitted across 100 countries under one fictional identity: 2,013 (32%) received an email reply; about 5% of replies arrived within five minutes; average speed to lead was 15 to 24 hours overall and 26 to 40 hours for human replies; only 100 companies called; 36% of replies were from a person; and 75.8% of human replies were personalised against 4 of 1,281 automated replies. The authors note that non-replies may reflect disqualification, bot suspicion or spam filtering ([Clay, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/claygent-experiment-speed-to-lead)). **[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Mar 2024]** An earlier audit of 1,000 B2B SaaS demo requests found 63.5% never replied ([RevenueHero, Mar 2024](https://www.revenuehero.io/blog/b2b-lead-response-times), relayed by [Ainora study roundup, 2026](https://ainora.lt/blog/lead-response-time-statistics-every-study-2026)).

### The classic speed-to-lead studies are old but point one way

**[DURABLE; outdated primaries, relayed by an aggregator]** A 2007 study of six companies and more than 15,000 leads found the odds of contacting a lead fall about 100 times and of qualifying about 21 times when the first call is at 30 minutes instead of 5 ([MIT/InsideSales study PDF, 2007](https://25649.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na2.net/hub/25649/file-13535879-pdf/docs/mit_study.pdf)). A 2011 Harvard Business Review audit of 2,241 US companies found an average response of 42 hours among responders, 37% responding within an hour and 23% never responding, and that firms contacting within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify the lead as those waiting one more hour ([Harvard Business Review, Mar 2011](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)). A 2012 vendor study reported that calling within one minute raised conversion 391% and that 93% of converted leads were reached by the sixth call. All were read through one aggregator, which also notes that the widely repeated claim about buying from the first responder has no traceable primary source ([Ainora study roundup, 2026](https://ainora.lt/blog/lead-response-time-statistics-every-study-2026)). **[GAP]** No peer-reviewed replication since 2011 was found, and whether instant AI-agent responses convert as well as human responses is unknown; treat the multipliers as directional only.

### Qualified-to-booked benchmarks

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Jul 2026]** RevenueHero's data on more than one million inbound form submissions across B2B SaaS: a median qualified-to-booked rate of 62%, 78% or more at the 90th percentile and 88% for the best performers, against 30 to 40% framed as typical without instant scheduling. By segment: small business 63.2% with 21.8% disqualified; mid-market 61.2% with 28.1% disqualified; enterprise 70.1% with 71.2% disqualified. Form length barely mattered (77% with two fields, 76% with thirteen). Top-decile companies show a calendar immediately after qualification with no manual review ([RevenueHero, 15 Jul 2026](https://www.revenuehero.io/blog/speed-to-lead-benchmarks-for-saas)).

### Service levels for inbound

**[INFERENCE]** Recommended service levels: acknowledge and offer booking in under five minutes around the clock, which automation can do in under a minute; a personalised, human-quality reply within one business hour; a phone attempt within the same hour where a number was given and calling is lawful; at least five to six follow-up attempts over two weeks before recycling to nurture. This is the evidence-layer version of the house-style 60-second rule (section 5), bounded by section 11. Qualify on the form or by enrichment and not by making the lead wait: route qualified leads straight to a calendar and disqualified leads to self-serve resources.

### Show rates

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025]** At Cognism, show rates rose from about 60% to 72% after adding a pre-meeting nurture process and are now 80 to 82%; meetings booked more than four days out showed at about 60%; the overall held rate was 85.94% (34,336 of 39,679) ([Cognism State of Outbound 2026](https://cognism.com/reports/state-of-outbound-2026)). **[VENDOR; partly outdated]** One scheduling vendor claims an outbound no-show rate under 5% with three reminders (24 hours, one hour and one minute before) and recommends restricting booking to a two-week window, suggesting specific times, sending an agenda and pre-call content, and an automated re-booking sequence for no-shows ([Chili Piper, updated Jan 2026](https://www.chilipiper.com/blog/improve-your-show-rates)). **[INFERENCE]** Show-rate playbook: book within two to five days, send the invite immediately, confirm with an agenda, remind at 24 hours and one hour, add an SMS reminder where consent exists, and re-book no-shows the same day. Expect about 60% without process and 80% or more with it; the house-style benchmark is about 70% (section 5). **[GAP]** No independent scheduling dataset on no-show rates by lead time was retrieved.

### Trials and freemium as inbound

**[VENDOR]** ProductLed's survey of more than 600 SaaS companies gives medians of 5% visitor-to-trial, 12% visitor-to-freemium, 9% free-to-paid and 25% for product-qualified leads converting to paid, with only about a quarter of companies using such leads ([ProductLed benchmarks](https://productled.com/blog/product-led-growth-benchmarks)). The publication date is ambiguous and the data may be from 2022.

### AI voice agents on inbound

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Mar 2026]** One CRM vendor judges AI voice agents useful for inbound intake, after-hours qualification and routing with a human handoff path, notes typical agent latency of 1,100 to 1,500 milliseconds against a natural conversational gap of about 200, and advises disclosing that the caller is speaking with an AI ([Close, 5 Mar 2026](https://www.close.com/blog/do-ai-voice-agents-work)). **[GAP]** No sourced data on chat or conversational bots, on routing models or on B2C lead handling was retrieved.

## 26. AI in sales: what is supported and what is not

### Adoption is near-universal by vendor surveys; outcomes are not proven

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** 87% of sales organisations say they use AI ([Salesforce State of Sales announcement, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/); [Gong, State of Revenue AI 2026](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-labs-state-of-revenue-ai-2026.pdf)), yet quota attainment fell to 46% in the same Gong survey; organisations with AI as a core strategy were 65% more likely to raise win rates than limited pilots, a correlational vendor finding ([Gong, State of Revenue AI 2026](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-labs-state-of-revenue-ai-2026.pdf)). 94% of sales leaders with agents call them essential in a survey by a company that sells agents ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)). AI was used to automate sales tasks at 34% of companies in early 2025, with automating manual work the top use case ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). Top performers at AI-enabled companies spend 43% of their time with customers ([Ebsta/Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks](https://benchmarks.ebsta.com/hubfs/V3%202025%20Benchmark%20Report/gtm_benchmarks_digital_report.pdf?hsLang=en)). In Gong's survey, 59% say their teams use personal general-purpose AI tools, seven in ten revenue leaders regularly use AI to inform decisions, and the three-year outlook is job transformation without cuts for 43%, cuts for 28% and job creation for 21% ([Gong, State of Revenue AI 2026](https://www.gong.io/files/gong-labs-state-of-revenue-ai-2026.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** The fall in attainment alongside near-universal adoption means adoption alone guarantees nothing.

### Autonomous AI SDRs: the independent record is mostly negative

**[FAST-MOVING, Mar 2025]** TechCrunch reported on 24 March 2025, from nearly two dozen sources, that an AI SDR vendor listed as customers companies that said they were not, that employees described 70 to 80% of customers being lost and annual recurring revenue being overstated, and that hallucinations and weak results were reported; the vendor disputes much of this and states its retention is 79% ([TechCrunch, 24 Mar 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have/)). **[VENDOR; secondary, treat with caution]** A blog citing named sources says one large data vendor found its trial of that product performed worse than its human SDRs, puts AI SDR tool churn at 50 to 70% a year, and cites tests finding hallucination rates above 20% for one agent and factual errors in 12 to 18% of another vendor's emails ([leadgen-economy.com, 2026](https://leadgen-economy.com/blog/ai-sdr-cancellation-wave-failure-forensics/)). The same blog's estimates of AI against human reply and meeting-conversion rates are unsourced and must not be presented as fact. **[GAP]** No independent quantitative 2026 study comparing AI SDR and human SDR pipeline was found.

### A regulator has acted against exaggerated AI sales claims

**[FAST-MOVING, Mar 2026; secondary source only]** The FTC filed a complaint against an AI sales-calling company in August 2025 and announced a settlement on 24 March 2026 banning it and its owners from marketing business opportunities, with an $18 million judgment, largely suspended, and a requirement to substantiate AI capability claims, as reported by a secondary blog ([leadgen-economy.com, 2026](https://leadgen-economy.com/blog/ai-sdr-cancellation-wave-failure-forensics/)). The primary documents were not read.

### AI-written outreach is roughly at parity with templated human outreach

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025]** AI-assisted LinkedIn campaigns got 7.3% replies against 7.0% without AI ([Belkins LinkedIn outreach study, 2025 data](https://belkins.io/blog/linkedin-outreach-study.md)); manually edited emails got 5.2% against 4.4% for fully automated ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email)). **[CONTESTED]** Hunter's 2026 report says 69% of US decision-makers are bothered if AI wrote the email unless it feels genuinely human, while its November 2025 article framed the same survey as 67% not minding and found recipients identified fewer than four of nine AI-written emails ([Hunter, State of Email Outreach 2026](https://hunter.io/the-state-of-cold-email); [Hunter blog, 24 Nov 2025](https://hunter.io/blog/the-cold-email-confidence-gap-why-senders-fear-ai-more-than-decision-makers)). **[INFERENCE]** Recipients punish generic, formulaic text and not AI use as such; AI drafting saves cost and does not by itself raise response.

### Where AI agents are supported by the evidence

**[INFERENCE]** The supported uses are research, enrichment, drafting, sequencing, inbound response, meeting notes and CRM updates, call scoring, forecast risk flags, proposal drafting and renewal-risk detection, with humans owning judgement-heavy conversations. **[VENDOR]** One email platform says AI agents handle about 80% of research and sequencing for its top senders ([Instantly benchmark report 2026](https://instantly.ai/cold-email-benchmark-report-2026)); in Clay's experiment only 4 of 1,281 automated first replies were personalised ([Clay, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.clay.com/blog/claygent-experiment-speed-to-lead)), which is the gap a well-grounded agent can close.

### Design rules for an AI sales department

**[INFERENCE]** Rules that follow from the evidence: ground every personalised statement in a retrieved, logged source and block sending when no source exists, because fabricated personalisation is the documented failure mode; keep volume low per mailbox and list; keep a human or a strict review agent on first-touch copy for tier 1 accounts and on reply handling beyond scheduling; do not use synthetic voice for cold calls; disclose AI and never invent customer names or proof; and measure agents on held meetings that convert to opportunities. Gong and Bay advise not mentioning AI as a selling point in outreach copy ([Gong Labs cold email study, 24 Jul 2025](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/does-cold-email-even-work-any-more-heres-what-the-data-says/)).

### The sales technology stack

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Feb 2026]** Sellers use an average of 8 tools, 42% feel overwhelmed by them, reps spend 60% of their time on non-selling tasks, and 51% of sales professionals say data-security concerns halt AI initiatives ([Salesforce sales statistics, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/state-of-sales/sales-statistics)). **[INFERENCE]** Stack layers, from general knowledge and not fetched: a CRM as system of record; sales engagement and sequencing; conversation intelligence; enrichment and intent data; scheduling and routing; quoting and billing; e-signature; forecasting; and a customer-success platform. Product choices were not researched and none is endorsed here.

### Headline claims that could not be read

**[GAP]** A Gartner press release of 28 July 2026 on AI agents outnumbering sellers was seen only as a headline. No evidence was found on the accuracy of AI research and enrichment tools beyond vendor statements, and voice-agent results for outbound in Europe were not researched.

## 27. Compliance: outreach rules by country and channel

### Two legal layers apply to every send

**[DURABLE]** Two layers apply to every outreach message. Data-protection law (GDPR) governs holding and using the contact's data: legitimate interest is usable for prospecting, with an absolute right to object. National electronic-privacy and marketing law decides whether the email, SMS or call may be sent at all, and that layer differs sharply by country for business recipients. Everything in sections 27 to 31 is orientation as of 11 November 2026 and not legal advice.

### GDPR: legitimate interest, documentation and the absolute right to object

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Swedish data protection authority states (page updated 15 June 2026) that processing for direct marketing can be a legitimate interest, that the assessment must be documented, that the person's reasonable expectations weigh in the balance, and that the controller must always stop if the person objects ([IMY, Intresseavvägning, updated 15 Jun 2026](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/rattslig-grund/intresseavvagning/)); the right to object to direct marketing is absolute ([IMY, Rätt att göra invändningar](https://www.imy.se/privatperson/dataskydd/dina-rattigheter/att-gora-invandningar/)). **[GAP]** The European Data Protection Board's guidelines on legitimate interest appear on its site only as a 2024 consultation version; no final version was found ([EDPB Guidelines 1/2024 consultation page](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/documents/public-consultations/2024/guidelines-12024-processing-personal-data-based_en)).

### GDPR: notice when data comes from a third party

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** Under GDPR Article 14, when data is not obtained from the person (broker, enrichment, scraping), the controller must give the required information (identity, purposes, legal basis, categories, source, rights) within a reasonable period, at the latest one month, or at the first communication if the data is used to contact the person; under Article 21 the objection right must be brought to the person's attention at the latest at first communication ([GDPR on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)). **[FAST-MOVING, Jun 2026; checked on primary source]** The French authority confirms the same logic for B2B: people must be informed and able to object, including when data came from third parties ([CNIL, updated 10 Jun 2026](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-prospection-commerciale-par-courrier-electronique)). **[FAST-MOVING, autumn 2025; secondary source only]** Finnish guidance requires that when contact details come from a third party, the data source, the controller's identity and where to find more information be given during the first call ([DLA Piper, Finland, modified 13 Feb 2026](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=electronic-marketing&c=FI&c2=NL)).

### Pending change: the EU Digital Omnibus is not law

**[FAST-MOVING, Oct 2026; secondary source only]** The GDPR and ePrivacy part of the EU Digital Omnibus is not law. The Council agreed its position on 21 October 2026; the Parliament has not; three-way negotiations are not expected before March 2027. The Council deleted the proposed redefinition of personal data and reinstated the existing right on automated decisions ([Addleshaw Goddard, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/en/insights/insights-briefings/2026/data-protection/eu-digital-omnibus-european-council-adopts-its-position/)).

### Sweden: what the Marketing Act says about electronic outreach

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Section 19 of the Swedish Marketing Act requires prior consent of the natural person for email, fax and automated calling, with an exception where the trader obtained the address in a sale of its own similar products, the person did not object and can opt out free and easily. Section 20 requires every marketing email to carry a valid stop address, also when aimed at legal persons. Section 21 allows other individual distance methods unless the natural person has clearly objected ([Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)). **[INFERENCE]** Cold email to a limited company's addresses about a business-relevant offer is not caught by the section 19 opt-in, but sole traders are natural persons and should be treated as opt-in; GDPR still applies to any named contact. **[GAP]** Whether section 19 covers a named employee's work address at a legal person was not confirmed on a primary source, and amendments after SFS 2022:656 were not checked.

### Norway, Germany, France and the Netherlands: details that bite

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Norway: consent must be an active, documentable step, the existing-customer exception is read narrowly and every message needs a free, simple opt-out ([Forbrukertilsynet guidance, updated 8 May 2026](https://www.forbrukertilsynet.no/lov-og-rett/veiledninger-og-retningslinjer/forbrukertilsynets-veiledning-markedsforing-via-e-post-sms-o-l)). Germany: the soft opt-in needs all four statutory conditions; consumer phone consent must be documented and kept five years; fines reach EUR 300,000 for phone or automated-call advertising without consent and EUR 50,000 for documentation failures ([UWG, English text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_uwg/englisch_uwg.html)). France: each message must identify the sender and offer a simple refusal route ([CNIL, updated 10 Jun 2026](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-prospection-commerciale-par-courrier-electronique)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026; secondary source only]** France's law of 30 June 2025 makes consumer telephone canvassing opt-in from 11 August 2026, ending the former opt-out register, with an exception for calls to existing customers about the contract, permitted hours Monday to Friday 10 to 13 and 14 to 20, fines up to EUR 375,000 and contracts concluded after unlawful canvassing void; B2B phone stays on a lighter opt-out regime ([HubSpot France](https://blog.hubspot.fr/sales/alternatives-demarchage-telephonique); [Scrap.io](https://scrap.io/gdpr-cold-email-b2b)). Dutch fines reach EUR 900,000 per violation ([DLA Piper, Netherlands, modified 18 Jan 2024](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/index.html?t=electronic-marketing&c=NL)).

### United Kingdom: treat uncertain recipients as individuals

**[FAST-MOVING, Feb 2026; checked on primary source]** The UK regulator advises that if a sender is unsure whether a recipient is a corporate or individual subscriber it should treat them as individual; UK GDPR applies to named business contacts; objectors go on a suppression list instead of being deleted; automated calls need specific consent ([ICO, B2B marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)). Fines under the electronic-marketing rules now reach GBP 17.5 million or 4% of global turnover, and the main provisions of the Data (Use and Access) Act commenced on 5 February 2026 ([ICO statement, Feb 2026](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/02/statement-on-the-commencement-of-the-data-use-and-access-act-duaa)).

### United States: email

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The CAN-SPAM Act is an opt-out regime with no exception for business-to-business email: accurate headers, non-deceptive subject lines, disclosure that the message is an advertisement, a physical postal address, and a working opt-out honoured within 10 business days; penalties run up to USD 53,088 per email, and the sender stays liable for its vendors ([FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business)).

### United States: calls, AI voices and texts

**[FAST-MOVING, Oct 2026; checked on primary source]** Under the Telemarketing Sales Rule most B2B calls are exempt (with an exception for sales of nondurable office or cleaning supplies), caller ID must be transmitted, records kept, and penalties run to USD 53,088 per violation ([FTC, Complying with the Telemarketing Sales Rule, modified 8 Oct 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule)). The FCC has confirmed that the TCPA's artificial or prerecorded voice restrictions cover AI technologies that generate human-sounding voices, including voice cloning, so such calls generally need prior express consent ([Federal Register, 10 Sep 2024](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/09/10/2024-19028/implications-of-artificial-intelligence-technologies-on-protecting-consumers-from-unwanted-robocalls)). **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The national Do Not Call registry must be scrubbed at least every 31 days and the calling window is 8:00 to 21:00 recipient local time ([FTC, Complying with the Telemarketing Sales Rule, modified 8 Oct 2026](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-telemarketing-sales-rule)). **[FAST-MOVING, Aug-Oct 2026; secondary source only]** The one-to-one consent rule was vacated and removed in November 2025; a revoke-all rule is delayed to 31 January 2027; an FCC order released 1 October 2026 requires senders to honour stop, quit, end, revoke, opt out, cancel and unsubscribe; no final rule on AI-generated calls existed as of August 2026 ([Gryphon regulatory report, Aug 2026](https://gryphon.ai/regulatory-report-august-2026/); [Inside Global Tech, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.insideglobaltech.com/2026/10/02/update-fcc-adopts-final-order-and-further-notice-on-tcpa-consent-revocation/)).

### United States: state telemarketing laws and SMS registration

**[FAST-MOVING, Jul-Oct 2026; secondary source only]** State laws add stricter rules: Florida and Oklahoma require written consent for automated selection or dialling, limit hours to 8:00 to 20:00 and calls to three per 24 hours on one subject, with private suits of USD 500 to 1,500; Texas covers texts from 1 September 2025 and requires registration and a USD 10,000 security unless exempt; Oregon's law applies from 1 January 2026; Virginia requires opt-outs kept 10 years; Pennsylvania narrows calls to 9:00 to 19:00 with no Sundays from 18 October 2026 ([Tychron, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.tychron.com/guides/state-texting-laws/); [Enzo Dialer tracker](https://enzodialer.com/resources/compliance/state-mini-tcpa-laws); [Gryphon regulatory report, Aug 2026](https://gryphon.ai/regulatory-report-august-2026/)). Maryland's law applies from 1 January 2024, and in July 2026 the Seventh Circuit held that texts are not telephone calls under the TCPA's do-not-call provision ([Gryphon regulatory report, Aug 2026](https://gryphon.ai/regulatory-report-august-2026/)). Anyone sending application-to-person SMS to US numbers over ten-digit long codes must register a brand and a campaign, or face carrier fees and heavier filtering ([Twilio docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc)).

### Canada

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Canada's anti-spam law requires express consent (provable, no pre-ticked boxes) or implied consent (two years after a purchase, six months after an inquiry), sender identification and a mailing address valid for 60 days, and unsubscribes processed within 10 business days. The B2B exemption applies only where the two organisations already have a relationship and the message concerns the recipient's activities. It applies to messages sent from abroad to Canada, with penalties up to CAD 10 million per violation for businesses ([CRTC FAQ](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm)).

### The default send gate for agents

**[INFERENCE]** A safe default is that the strictest applicable recipient-country rule wins. Before any send, an agent checks recipient country, recipient type (consumer, sole trader, named employee, generic company address), channel, and consent and suppression status. Cold B2B email into Germany, Denmark and the Netherlands is blocked unless consent is on file. Where cold email to individuals needs consent, phone (after register checks) and LinkedIn carry more of the sequence.

## 28. Compliance: Sweden in detail

### Consumer telephone sales: identity, purpose and written acceptance

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Under the Swedish Distance Contracts Act (2 kap. 3 a §, consolidated through SFS 2026:1018), when the trader calls on its own initiative the caller must state at the start the trader's identity, the purpose, the caller's name and relation to the trader; the offer must be confirmed in writing or another durable form; and the contract is concluded only when the consumer accepts in writing after the call, failing which it is invalid ([Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)). The Consumer Agency (page reviewed 6 September 2026) adds that acceptance during the call, for example by BankID, makes the contract invalid; the rule covers new and existing customers; hidden-number calls breach good marketing practice; the principal is primarily liable and a hired call centre can be liable as accessory; phone sales of pension-related services are banned; and a consumer may access an existing audio file of the call ([Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)). The written-acceptance rule also applies when the trader prompted the consumer to call, and not when the consumer calls unprompted ([Konsumentverket, consumer page](https://www.konsumentverket.se/konsumentratt/telefonforsaljning-regler/)).

### The NIX register

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Companies calling consumers for sales, marketing or fundraising should scrub lists against the NIX register first; a cleared number may be called for two months from the register version date; existing customers need not be checked, though the register suggests removing them anyway; web scrubbing costs 17 öre per number ([NIX-Telefon för företag](https://www.nixtelefon.org/for-foretag)). **[INFERENCE]** Consumer cold calling in Sweden is opt-out today; B2B calls sit outside the register, which defines consumers as natural persons acting outside business, but the B2B industry code and the good-practice standard still apply.

### The Swedish industry code for consumer telephone sales

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** The code issued by Kontakta and SWEDMA (1 July 2021) sets calling hours of weekdays 08 to 21 and weekends 10 to 18 with none on Christmas, Easter or Midsummer Eve; a maximum of two calls a day per number per campaign and 20 in 30 days; no silent calls; honest identification with a callable number; offer and acceptance recorded with consent and kept six months; the contract sent within five working days; and an existing-customer exception covering customers of the past 12 months ([Kontakta/SWEDMA ethical rules, 1 Jul 2021, PDF copy](https://telekomradgivarna.se/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/etiska-regler-for-forsaljning-och-marknadsforing-over-telefon-till-konsument-2021-07-01.pdf)). Kontakta also publishes rules for remote customer communication and, with a trade federation, a B2B telemarketing code (checked on primary source for existence only, [Kontakta](https://www.kontakta.se/etiska-regler-for-forsaljning-och-marknadforing-over-telefon-till-konsument); [SWEDMA rules index](https://www.swedma.se/radgivning-2/regler-riktlinjer/)). **[GAP]** The B2B telemarketing code's content was not opened.

### Conduct the Consumer Agency expects on calls

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Consumer Agency expects sellers to state the sales purpose first with no survey pretext, respect a wish to end the call, give reasonable time to reflect and make no exaggerated claims; its 2023 review of credit brokers found all of these breached. It ties false "free" claims to blacklist point 20 and payment demands without a valid order to point 29 ([Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)).

### A ban or opt-in for consumer telephone sales is under inquiry

**[FAST-MOVING, Jul 2026; checked on primary source]** On 9 July 2026 the government appointed an expert to assess strengthening the blocking-register model, a prior-consent requirement, and a full or sector-limited ban on consumer telephone sales initiated by the trader; the report is due 15 January 2027 ([Regeringen press release, 9 Jul 2026](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/07/forbud-mot-telefonforsaljning-ska-utredas/); [Regeringen committee directive, Jul 2026](https://regeringen.se/rattsliga-dokument/kommittedirektiv/2026/07/ett-starkt-konsumentskydd-vid-telefonforsaljning/)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2024-2026; secondary source only]** The Consumer Agency proposed in summer 2025 either a total ban or a French-style opt-in, the register covers about 3 million numbers, and the agency earlier called for a ban on phone sales of credit ([Realtid, 12 Aug 2026](https://www.realtid.se/juridik/telefonforsaljning-kan-forbjudas-i-sverige-frankrike-har-redan-gjort-det/); [SVT, 12 Oct 2024](https://www.svt.se/nyheter/inrikes/konsumentverket-vill-se-hardare-tag-mot-telefonforsaljning)). **[GAP]** The inquiry's outcome is unknown until January 2027.

### Marketing Act: aggressive marketing, identification and price offers

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Section 7 bans aggressive marketing, and the practices in Annex I of Directive 2005/29/EC are always unfair. Section 9 requires all marketing to be identifiable as marketing and to show who is responsible. Section 12 requires an offer with a price to include main characteristics, price, trader identity and address, and withdrawal information. Section 18 allows comparative advertising only if it is not misleading, is objective and does not discredit. Section 20 a bars emails that urge visits to sites whose marketing breaches section 9. The market disturbance fee runs from SEK 10,000 up to 4% of turnover ([Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)).

### Distance contracts: pre-contract information and the 14-day withdrawal right

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Distance Contracts Act requires pre-contract information (identity, main characteristics, total price and fees, payment and delivery, withdrawal right and who pays returns, duration and termination terms). The withdrawal period is 14 days; for services it runs from the contract date; it never starts before withdrawal information is given and ends at the latest one year after it would otherwise have expired. Exceptions include custom goods, fully performed services with express consent, digital content started with consent, and dated leisure services ([Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)).

### The online withdrawal function

**[FAST-MOVING, Jun 2026; statute checked on primary source, date from secondary source]** A new provision (2 kap. 10 a §) requires that for contracts concluded through an online interface the trader provide a withdrawal function that is easy to reach for the whole period and clearly labelled, with a confirmation step and a receipt without delay ([Lag (2005:59) om distansavtal](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-200559-om-distansavtal-och-avtal-utanfor_sfs-2005-59/)). It applies from 19 June 2026 under Directive (EU) 2023/2673, may sit behind a login, creates no new withdrawal right, and its absence can be treated as unfair marketing ([CMS Wistrand, 15 Jun 2026](https://cms.law/en/swe/legal-updates/new-withdrawal-button-required-on-swedish-e-commerce-platforms-from-19-june-2026)).

### Automatic renewal of consumer contracts

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** For fixed-term consumer contracts that renew unless terminated, the trader must send a written reminder at least one month before the last termination date; without it the consumer may terminate immediately after renewal. The rule does not apply if the consumer can exit within three months after renewal, and it protects consumers only ([Lag (2014:1449)](https://data.riksdagen.se/dokument/sfs-2014-1449.html)). **[INFERENCE]** B2B auto-renewal clauses are enforceable subject to the general reasonableness clause of contract law; long silent renewals to small businesses carry adjustment risk.

### The new Consumer Credit Act from 20 November 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, Nov 2026; checked on primary source]** The Riksdag adopted the new act on 27 May 2026; it and its consequential amendments enter into force on 20 November 2026 and implement Directive (EU) 2023/2225 ([Riksdagen, bet. 2025/26:CU26](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/_HD01CU26/)). The government's summary of 30 March 2026: more credit types are covered, including interest-free credit offered in stores by a third party; creditworthiness assessment is stricter; the withdrawal period starts only when withdrawal information is received; lenders must offer forbearance options before collection; and more credit providers and brokers need authorisation ([Regeringen, 30 Mar 2026](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/03/regeringen-foreslar-ny-konsumentkreditlag-for-starkt-skydd-for-konsumenter/)). **[GAP]** The act's statute number, marketing rules, cost caps and exact scope for deferred invoices were not confirmed.

### Price information and the 30-day prior-price rule

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Price Information Act applies to traders offering products to consumers. Price means the final price including VAT and other taxes; extra fees are stated separately; goods also need a unit price. Under section 7 a, a price-reduction claim must show the lowest price applied in the 30 days before the cut. Breaches are handled under the Marketing Act ([Prisinformationslag (2004:347)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/prisinformationslag-2004347_sfs-2004-347/)).

### Consumer sales, consumer services and contract law basics

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** These points come from background knowledge and were not checked on the statutes. Consumer Sales Act (2022:260): mandatory in the consumer's favour; the trader is liable for defects existing at delivery for three years; defects appearing within two years are presumed original; digital content is covered ([Konsumentköplag (2022:260)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konsumentkoplag-2022260_sfs-2022-260/)). Consumer Services Act (1985:716): professional performance, a duty to advise against work that does not benefit the consumer, a reasonable price if none is agreed, and approximate quotes exceeded by at most 15% ([Konsumenttjänstlag (1985:716)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konsumenttjanstlag-1985716_sfs-1985-716/)). Contracts Act (1915:218): an offer binds the offeror during the acceptance period; an acceptance with changes is a rejection plus a new offer; section 36 allows unreasonable terms to be adjusted; sections 30 and 33 cover fraud and bad faith ([Avtalslag (1915:218)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1915218-om-avtal-och-andra-rattshandlingar_sfs-1915-218/)).

## 29. Compliance: recording, and EU law on sales tactics

### Call and meeting recording in Sweden

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** In Sweden recording a conversation one takes part in is not unlawful interception and there is no two-party consent rule, but a company that records is processing personal data: it needs a lawful basis (commonly legitimate interest with a documented balancing test, or contract), must inform before or at the start, and must set a retention period with automatic deletion. No general maximum retention exists; quality and training purposes usually justify days to a few months ([Tacerto, updated 4 Feb 2026](https://www.tacerto.se/blogg/spela-in-kundsamtal-gdpr); [Telink](https://telink.se/funktioner/vad-sager-lagen-om-samtalsinspelning/)). Financial-markets rules require at least five years for in-scope calls ([Tacerto, updated 4 Feb 2026](https://www.tacerto.se/blogg/spela-in-kundsamtal-gdpr)), and the Swedish industry code asks for recorded offer and acceptance to be kept six months ([Kontakta/SWEDMA ethical rules, 1 Jul 2021, PDF copy](https://telekomradgivarna.se/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/etiska-regler-for-forsaljning-och-marknadsforing-over-telefon-till-konsument-2021-07-01.pdf)). Transcription vendors may move audio outside Sweden, and a processor agreement is needed for AI call tools ([Lynes](https://lynes.io/blogg/spela-in-samtal-gdpr); [Sono](https://callsono.com/sv/blog/far-man-spela-in-kundsamtal/)). **[GAP]** No regulator guidance page specifically on call recording or AI note-takers was found; these points rest on vendor blogs.

### Call recording in the United States and the United Kingdom

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; secondary source only]** US federal law requires one party's consent, but all-party consent states include California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania and Washington, with Connecticut and Nevada for phone calls and Oregon for in-person conversations. California has applied its rule to an out-of-state recorder, with a penalty of USD 5,000 per violation ([Scribbl 50-state table 2026](https://scribbl.co/post/do-you-have-to-tell-people-youre-recording); [Brass Transcripts 2026 guide](https://brasstranscripts.com/blog/state-by-state-recording-laws-2026-guide)). Delaware and Michigan are described as unsettled ([Scribbl 50-state table 2026](https://scribbl.co/post/do-you-have-to-tell-people-youre-recording)). **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** In the UK a participant may record and businesses rely on 2018 regulations plus transparency duties ([SI 2018/356](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2018/356/contents/made)).

### The recording rule for agents

**[INFERENCE]** Treat every call as requiring all-party consent. One announcement at the start, stating that the call is recorded and transcribed by an AI tool, for what purpose and where details are, with the option to decline, satisfies the Swedish notice duty and most all-party statutes. Feeding transcripts to conversation-intelligence tools is a new purpose that the balancing test and privacy notice must cover. **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The AI Act bans emotion-recognition AI in the workplace from 2 February 2025, which touches sentiment scoring of employees' calls ([Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)).

### The EU blacklist of always-unfair practices

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Annex I of the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive (consolidated 28 May 2022) bans outright, among others: bait advertising (point 5); bait and switch (point 6); falsely stating a product is available only for a very short time (point 7); undisclosed paid ranking in search results (point 11a); false closing-down claims (point 15); describing something as free when the consumer pays more than unavoidable costs (point 20); claiming reviews are from real users without reasonable checks (point 23b); submitting or commissioning false reviews (point 23c); persistent unwanted solicitation by phone, email or other remote media (point 26); demanding payment for unsolicited products (point 29); and false prize claims (point 31). How a trader verifies reviews is material information ([Directive 2005/29/EC, consolidated 28 May 2022](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02005L0029-20220528)). Sweden gives the blacklist direct effect through its Marketing Act ([Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/)).

### Agent rules that follow from the blacklist

**[INFERENCE]** An agent must not generate countdowns, low-stock statements, same-day deadlines or statements that other buyers are looking unless a system of record confirms them as true at send time. Follow-up cadences need a hard stop after a refusal, because repeated contact after a refusal maps to point 26. Testimonials and reviews are used only when attributable and verified.

### Related EU instruments

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The Omnibus Directive (EU) 2019/2161 added the review, ranking and price-reduction rules, personalised-pricing disclosure and fines of at least 4% of turnover for cross-border infringements ([Directive (EU) 2019/2161](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/2161/oj)). The Digital Services Act bars online platform providers from designing interfaces that deceive or manipulate users ([Regulation (EU) 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj)).

### The Digital Fairness Act is a draft

**[FAST-MOVING, Nov 2026; secondary source only]** The Digital Fairness Act was listed as a fourth-quarter 2026 initiative ([PinMeTo, 22 Jul 2026](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/eu-digital-fairness-act-scope-split-2026/)). A draft reported on 9 November 2026 covers dark patterns, addictive design, influencer marketing, personalised pricing and advertising and dynamic-pricing disclosure, with fines up to 6% of worldwide turnover, and its presentation was expected within days ([MLex, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/articles/2547809); [PYMNTS, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.pymnts.com/cpi-posts/eu-draft-bill-targets-unfair-digital-practices-with-fines-of-up-to-6/)). **[GAP]** Whether the Commission adopted the proposal on 11 November 2026 could not be confirmed; even if proposed, application is years away.

### United States: fake urgency as a deceptive design

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** An FTC staff report of September 2022 lists fake countdown timers and false scarcity or demand claims among designs that induce false beliefs, and the FTC called such designs an enforcement priority ([Venable summary of FTC dark-patterns report, Sep 2022](https://www.venable.com/insights/blogs/2022/09/the-ftc-brings-more-light-to-dark-patterns-in-new)).

## 30. Compliance: contracting, anti-bribery, competition and data handling

### A quote can bind the company

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** Under the Swedish Contracts Act offers bind, a late or modified acceptance counts as a new offer, and the reasonableness clause also applies between businesses, though more restrictively ([Avtalslag (1915:218)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1915218-om-avtal-och-andra-rattshandlingar_sfs-1915-218/)). **[INFERENCE]** An agent that sends a quote can commit the company. Agents label drafts as non-binding indications unless a human has approved the quote, and every quote carries a validity date and a reference to the standard terms.

### Data processing agreements, e-signatures and late payment

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** A written contract with defined content is required whenever a processor handles personal data for a controller (GDPR Article 28, [GDPR on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)). An electronic signature cannot be denied legal effect solely for being electronic, and only a qualified electronic signature equals a handwritten one ([Regulation (EU) No 910/2014](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj)). On late payment, Swedish law implementing Directive 2011/7/EU requires express agreement for payment terms between businesses over 30 days, sets statutory interest at the reference rate plus eight points and provides fixed compensation of SEK 450 ([Directive 2011/7/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/7/oj); [Räntelag (1975:635)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rantelag-1975635_sfs-1975-635/)). **[FAST-MOVING, Jul 2024; secondary source only]** The Commission's 2023 proposal for a strict 30-day cap stalled and was reported as likely to be withdrawn ([GTR, 24 Jul 2024](https://www.gtreview.com/news/europe/exclusive-eu-to-scrap-late-payment-reforms-after-industry-backlash/)). **[GAP]** Its current status and any Swedish bill capping B2B payment terms were not confirmed.

### Sanctions and export control

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** EU restrictive measures bind all EU persons and bar making funds or economic resources available to listed parties; dual-use items fall under Regulation (EU) 2021/821; US rules can reach non-US sellers of US-origin items or dollar transactions ([EU Sanctions Map](https://www.sanctionsmap.eu/); [Regulation (EU) 2021/821](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2021/821/oj); [OFAC search](https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/)). **[INFERENCE]** A sanctions and ownership screen belongs in the lead-qualification gate, before a quote, not at invoicing.

### Gifts, hospitality and anti-bribery

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The Swedish business anti-corruption code is issued by the Swedish Anti-Corruption Institute, complements the legislation, requires preventive work and gives guidance on benefits and intermediaries; the current version was adopted on 10 June 2020 and applies from 14 August 2020 ([Institutet Mot Mutor, Näringslivskoden](https://www.institutetmotmutor.se/naringslivskoden/)). **[DURABLE; secondary source only]** The 2020 revision added expectations of management-led prevention and third-party management and calls for special care toward the public sector ([Tidningen Balans](https://tidningenbalans.se/nyheter/imm-uppdaterar-naringslivskoden-korruptionsrisker-finns-i-alla-organisationer/)). **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The Swedish Criminal Code covers giving and taking bribes and trading in influence ([Brottsbalk (1962:700)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/brottsbalk-1962700_sfs-1962-700/)); the UK Bribery Act creates strict corporate liability for failing to prevent bribery by associated persons and applies to any organisation carrying on business in the UK ([Bribery Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/23/contents)); the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act bars corrupt payments to foreign officials, with third-party agents a main risk ([DOJ FCPA unit](https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-fraud/foreign-corrupt-practices-act)). **[GAP]** The code's section on permissible benefits was not opened and no value thresholds were confirmed.

### Competition law limits on sales talk

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** Swedish and EU competition law ban price-fixing, market sharing, bid rigging and exchange of competitively sensitive information, with fines up to 10% of turnover; resale price maintenance imposed on distributors is a hardcore restriction ([Konkurrenslag (2008:579)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/konkurrenslag-2008579_sfs-2008-579/); [Article 101 TFEU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:12008E101)). **[INFERENCE]** Agents never discuss prices, customers or bids with competitors, must not ingest or relay competitors' non-public prices, and need a dated, verifiable source for any claim about a competitor.

### Bought, enriched and scraped lead data

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Publicly available contact data still requires data-protection compliance, and objectors go on a suppression list ([ICO, B2B marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)); the balancing test must be documented ([IMY, Intresseavvägning, updated 15 Jun 2026](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/rattslig-grund/intresseavvagning/)). **[DURABLE; secondary source only]** Dutch regulators have fined organisations for buying marketing lists without verifying the consents in them ([iGDPR](https://www.igdpr.eu/en/gdpr-email-marketing-consent/)). **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The French authority fined a contact-data vendor EUR 240,000 in December 2024 for harvesting LinkedIn contact details that users had restricted ([CNIL on Kaspr](https://www.cnil.fr/en/data-scraping-kaspr-fined-eu240000)); LinkedIn's user agreement prohibits scraping and bots regardless of what data-protection law permits ([LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement)).

### Lead-record fields, retention and transfers

**[INFERENCE]** Each lead record should carry source, collection date, lawful basis, Article 14 notice date, country and recipient type so that agents can gate on them. A workable retention default is to delete or anonymise prospects with no engagement after a fixed, documented period while keeping a minimal suppression record indefinitely. **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** GDPR sets no statutory CRM period; the controller must set and justify one; international transfers need an adequacy decision or standard clauses with an impact assessment ([GDPR on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj); [European Commission, EU-US data transfers](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/eu-us-data-transfers_en)). **[GAP]** No regulator-set retention period for B2B prospects was found for Sweden, and the current status of the EU-US framework was not checked.

## 31. AI-agent guardrails

### Disclose that the agent is an AI at first interaction

**[FAST-MOVING, Aug 2026; secondary source only]** The AI Act's Article 50 duties became applicable on 2 August 2026. Providers must design systems so that people know they are interacting with AI unless that is obvious to a reasonably well-informed person; synthetic audio, image, video and text must carry machine-readable marking; the information must be clear and given at the latest at first interaction; generative systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2 December 2026 for marking; and fines reach EUR 15 million or 3% of turnover ([Goodwin, Aug 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force); [artificialintelligenceact.eu, Article 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/)). The same sources say the AI Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 July 2026) moved high-risk deadlines to 2 December 2027 without moving the chatbot disclosure duty, that the Commission adopted transparency guidelines on 20 July 2026, that deployers must disclose deepfakes, and that deployers of emotion-recognition systems must inform the people exposed ([Goodwin, Aug 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force); [artificialintelligenceact.eu, Article 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/)). **[INFERENCE]** Every outbound agent message on a new thread, chat or call opens with an AI disclosure; "obvious from context" is too uncertain to rely on in email or voice. **[GAP]** The amending regulation and the Commission's guidelines were not opened on the official site, and who enforces Article 50 in Sweden today was not confirmed; an inquiry proposed the telecoms regulator as main market surveillance authority ([Regeringen, 6 Oct 2025](https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2025/10/utredning-foreslar-forbud-mot-vissa-ai-system-och-sanktioner-for-bristande-dokumentation-av-hogrisk-ai/)).

### AI voices need prior consent

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** In the US, AI-generated and cloned voices are artificial voices under the TCPA ([Federal Register, 10 Sep 2024](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/09/10/2024-19028/implications-of-artificial-intelligence-technologies-on-protecting-consumers-from-unwanted-robocalls)). In Sweden, Germany and Norway automated calling systems to natural persons need prior consent ([Marknadsföringslag (2008:486)](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/marknadsforingslag-2008486_sfs-2008-486/); [UWG, English text](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/englisch_uwg/englisch_uwg.html); [Forbrukertilsynet guidance, updated 8 May 2026](https://www.forbrukertilsynet.no/lov-og-rett/veiledninger-og-retningslinjer/forbrukertilsynets-veiledning-markedsforing-via-e-post-sms-o-l)), and in the UK automated calls require specific consent ([ICO, B2B marketing](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/)). **[GAP]** No Swedish regulator statement on cloned voices in sales was found.

### Automated decisions about individuals

**[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** GDPR Article 22 gives a right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects unless an exception applies, with human-intervention safeguards; the EU Court of Justice held in December 2023 that producing a score on which a third party draws strongly can itself be such a decision ([GDPR on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj); [CJEU C-634/21](https://curia.europa.eu/juris/liste.jsf?num=C-634/21)). **[INFERENCE]** Lead scoring that only prioritises outreach is unlikely to be such a decision, but automated individual pricing, credit or eligibility refusal for natural persons likely is, so those need human review and an explanation path.

### The company answers for what its agent says

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** In Moffatt v. Air Canada (14 February 2024) a Canadian tribunal held an airline liable for negligent misrepresentation after its chatbot misstated a fare policy, rejecting the argument that the chatbot was a separate entity and holding that correct information elsewhere on the site was no defence ([American Bar Association, Feb 2024](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/resources/business-law-today/2024-february/bc-tribunal-confirms-companies-remain-liable-information-provided-ai-chatbot/)). Swedish marketing law makes the principal primarily responsible and helpers liable as accessories (checked on primary source, [Konsumentverket, rules for companies, reviewed 6 Sep 2026](https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/telefonforsaljning-regler-for-foretag/)). **[INFERENCE]** Together with the offer-binding principle of Swedish contract law, a price or term stated by an agent is likely to bind or create liability, which is the legal reason for human approval of pricing, discounts and contract terms. **[GAP]** No Swedish or EU case on contracts concluded by an AI agent was found.

### Platform terms that constrain agents

**[FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026; secondary source only]** From 15 January 2026 the WhatsApp Business interface bars providers whose main offering is a general-purpose AI assistant, while businesses using AI to serve their own customers are unaffected ([TechCrunch, 18 Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/)). US SMS needs brand and campaign registration ([Twilio docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc)). LinkedIn bans bots and automated messaging (unverified, not fetched, [LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement)). Mailbox-provider rules are in section 24.

### Security risks specific to language-model agents

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025 names prompt injection, sensitive information disclosure, improper output handling, excessive agency, system prompt leakage and misinformation among the top risks ([OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/)). **[INFERENCE]** Inbound emails, web pages and CRM notes are untrusted input; worker agents must not be able to change prices, terms, recipients or tools on the strength of text found in them.

### Guardrail patterns documented by vendors

**[VENDOR]** One support-agent vendor describes four guardrail types (input, output, action, escalation), threshold limits such as refunds above a set value needing human approval, escalation on explicit request, negative sentiment or unresolved intent with full history handed over, and logging of every block, escalation and approval ([Fin.ai](https://fin.ai/learn/ai-guardrails-customer-service)). A CRM vendor advises defining data-access permissions, action authorisation, topic scope and escalation protocols, requiring human sign-off on high-stakes decisions and logging everything ([Salesforce on AI guardrails](https://www.salesforce.com/welcome-to-the-agentic-enterprise/ai-guardrails/)). A third says an AI sales rep should never negotiate custom pricing tiers, generate spontaneous discounts or alter standard payment terms ([YTT AI](https://www.ytt-ai.com/post/sales-ai-guardrails-what-your-agent-should-never-say)).

### The hard gates for this department

**[INFERENCE]** Gates enforced outside the model, drawn from sections 11 and 27 to 31: a send gate keyed on country, recipient type, channel, consent and suppression; a register and calling-hours gate for phone; a block on in-call acceptance and payment for Swedish consumers; a block on synthetic-voice outbound without recorded consent; human approval for quotes, discounts, non-standard terms, credit, gifts and public-sector benefits; a truth gate that blocks urgency, scarcity, proof and competitor claims without a system-of-record source; a hard stop after a refusal; a recording announcement on every call; an AI disclosure at first interaction; sanctions screening before a quote; and a log of what was said and why each gate fired.

### A compact code of conduct for agents

**[INFERENCE]** Drawn from the Swedish codes and regulator expectations: identify yourself and that you are an AI; state the commercial purpose first; tell the truth about product, price, scarcity and competitors; take the first clear refusal as final; do not pressure; do not exploit vulnerability; offer a human on request; log what was said. **[DURABLE; unverified, not fetched]** The International Chamber of Commerce's marketing code (11th revision, 2024) covers direct marketing and AI use and is applied in Sweden by the advertising ombudsman ([ICC Advertising and Marketing Communications Code](https://iccwbo.org/news-publications/policies-reports/icc-advertising-and-marketing-communications-code/)). **[GAP]** No international professional sales code was researched.

## Conclusion

The research changes the starting assumption in one respect that matters for design: the house style and the best independent evidence agree far more often than they collide. Both say diagnose before pitching, talk less than the buyer, keep pitches short, raise obstacles before price, protect price by trading terms, always leave with a next step, respond fast and keep selling after the sale. The collisions are concentrated in a narrow band of tactics (repeated asking, pressure reframes, manufactured urgency, taking payment on the call, penalty trials and card-on-file charges) and in a narrow set of situations (multi-stakeholder deals, hesitant buyers, Swedish and EU consumers). That makes the department buildable: the conversation structures, objection taxonomy, payment ladder, offer sequence and stage metrics can be loaded as defaults, and a small number of hard gates outside the model remove the risky band entirely.

Three things should be revisited on a schedule, because the knowledge base will be wrong about them first. The legal layer is moving: the Swedish telephone-sales inquiry reports on 15 January 2027, the Consumer Credit Act applies from 20 November 2026, the AI Act marking deadline falls on 2 December 2026 and US consent-revocation rules change through January 2027. The benchmarks are vendor-sourced and US-weighted, so the department's own trailing data should replace them within two or three quarters. And the central open question for an AI sales team has no evidence yet in either direction: nobody has published whether buyers respond to a disclosed AI agent as they do to a human when the agent is fast, specific and truthful. The department is positioned to measure that itself, and held meetings that convert to opportunities is the metric that will answer it.
