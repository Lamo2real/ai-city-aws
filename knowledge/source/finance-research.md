# Law rules the books; cash-first designs them

As of 11 November 2026 the foundational knowledge for an AI finance department serving a Swedish SaaS aktiebolag is a three-layer stack: law and accounting rules that always win, independent evidence and SaaS benchmarks that tune targets, and Alex Hormozi's cash-first money models as the default design style underneath both. The legal layer is concrete and mostly verified: book cash events by the next working day, keep records seven years, recognise subscription revenue straight-line over the service period under K3 point 23.26 or K2 point 6.11 however the customer pays, hold the AGM within six months and file within seven or pay SEK 7,500, test equity against half the share capital at every close, charge 25% VAT at home, reverse-charge EU business customers after a VIES check, switch to destination VAT through OSS once EU consumer sales pass EUR 10,000 (SEK 99,680), pay 20.6% corporate tax and 31.42% employer contributions. The evidence layer says the median private B2B SaaS company grew about 25 to 26% in 2024 with gross revenue retention near 88 to 90%, net revenue retention near 101 to 104%, about $2.00 of sales and marketing per $1.00 of new-customer ARR, and a Nordic average CAC payback of 14 months, while AI product gross margins averaged 45% in 2025 and public SaaS traded near 3.2 times trailing revenue in Q2 2026. The house style supplies the design questions those benchmarks do not ask: does a new customer's first 30 days of cash cover the cost of winning and serving them, is lifetime gross profit at least three times CAC, is there an attraction, upsell, downsell and continuity sequence with a prepay option, is price raised before spend, and is effort aimed at the single constraint; every number he attaches is his own unaudited claim. The layers collide in predictable places: prepaid cash is a liability and not revenue, his 30-day payback sits far from typical SaaS payback, a 10% prepay discount is expensive financing at roughly 26% annualised by the researchers' calculation, and consumer payment plans and money-back offers need a legal check that the research did not perform. For agents, the COSO 2026 generative-AI guidance points to a simple division of labour: agents prepare, reconcile, forecast and recommend; named humans approve payments, filings, prices and master-data changes; configuration rights stay separate from approval rights; everything is logged. Large areas remain unverified, including transfer pricing, permanent establishment, pensions, the 13-week cash forecast method, treasury and FX policy, consumer-credit rules and the 2027 budget bill due on 12 November 2026, and this document says so where it applies instead of filling the holes from memory.

## How to read and split this document

This document is written to be cut into atomic knowledge-base entries. Every body paragraph is meant to stand alone: it opens with one or more labels, names its subject in full, and keeps each number next to its date and source link. Section numbers group domains; sub-headings name the single topic of the paragraphs beneath them. Tables repeat their source link in every row so that a row can be split out as its own entry. When splitting, keep the label, the date and the link with the claim.

The labels mean the following. **[DURABLE]** marks a principle, definition, formula or long-standing legal rule that has held for years. **[FAST-MOVING, date]** marks a rate, amount, deadline, survey result or legal status that will age, with the date of the source or the period it covers. **[VENDOR]** marks a figure from a party with a commercial interest in the conclusion (investors, lenders, software vendors, brokers, benchmark publishers that sell to the segment); treat it as self-reported, self-selected and correlational. **[PLATFORM CLAIM]** marks a statement a platform or tool maker makes about its own product. **[CONTESTED]** marks a point where sources disagree, or where house style and evidence pull in different directions. **[INFERENCE]** marks a synthesis, calculation or convention written by the researchers that no single source states; adopt it as a default and tune it against the company's own data. **[GAP]** marks something the research did not verify or could not find. **[HOUSE STYLE]** marks Alex Hormozi's published frameworks, which the owner has chosen as the department's default style where they exist; it is a preference and not independent evidence, and every number attached to it is his own self-reported, unaudited claim.

Three qualifiers are added inside labels where they matter. For legal, tax and regulatory material, "checked on primary source" means the statute, Skatteverket page, Riksbank page, government document, European Commission page or standard-setter page was read during the research; "secondary source only" means an audit firm, FAR Online's reference series, a software vendor, a trade body or the press was read and the primary text was not; "unverified background" means the point comes from the researchers' recollection, was not checked, and is given only with a pointer to where it should be confirmed. For house-style material, "own site" means Acquisition.com was read directly, "transcript" means a third-party auto-generated transcript of Hormozi's own video or podcast was read (his words, with possible transcription errors), and "second-hand summary" means a third party's summary was read and not checked against the original. For finance method, "unverified working knowledge" means ordinary practice recalled by the researchers with no source opened.

Seven caveats apply to the whole document. First, this is orientation as of 11 November 2026 and not legal, tax, accounting or financial advice; a qualified accountant, auditor or tax adviser confirms anything that goes into a filing. Second, every source was read through a summarising fetch tool, so even a point marked "checked on primary source" should be re-read at the cited paragraph before it is relied on. Third, the research ran under search and fetch limits, so coverage is uneven: Swedish company law, VAT invoicing, employer contributions, SaaS benchmarks, Hormozi's models and the COSO guidance are well sourced, while FP&A statistics, cash and treasury method, classic controls detail, pensions, transfer pricing and funding terms other than Almi are thin. Fourth, the Swedish budget bill for 2027 is handed to the Riksdag on 12 November 2026, the day after this document's status date, with the framework vote on 9 December 2026 ([regeringen.se, press release 9 Nov 2026](https://www.regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/11/presentation-av-budgetpropositionen-for-2027/); [regeringen.se, Finance Minister's slides, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.regeringen.se/globalassets/regeringen/dokument/finansdepartementet/ppt-till-presstraffar/presentationsbilder-fran-presstraff-med-finansministern-den-27-oktober-2026.pdf)); everything marked "proposed for 2027" must be re-checked after those dates. Fifth, SaaS benchmarks are mostly US-weighted and self-reported; the only Nordic dataset with numbers is Monterro's, published by an investor in the segment. Sixth, all Hormozi material is paraphrase from transcripts and summaries; the 2025 book *$100M Money Models* was not read directly. Seventh, where a later research pass corrected an earlier one, only the corrected claim appears here; the corrections that matter most are the 2026 income base amount (SEK 83,400, not SEK 80,600), the income tax return dates (one date per year-end group), the continued existence of växa-stöd as a refund, and the confirmed late-filing fee scale.

## 1. Overview: what the finance department is for and how it decides

### The three-layer rule that governs every finance decision

**[INFERENCE]** The department runs on three layers in strict order. Layer one is law and accounting rules: Bokföringslagen, Årsredovisningslagen, Aktiebolagslagen, K2 or K3, VAT, tax and payroll law, and consumer and credit law where the company sells to individuals (sections 4 to 11 and 21). Layer two is independent evidence and SaaS benchmarks: metric definitions, peer medians, practitioner method and control guidance (sections 12 to 18). Layer three is the house style, Hormozi's cash-first money models, which fills the space the first two layers leave open and supplies the default design questions (section 2). A worker agent takes the house-style design by default, checks whether a benchmark or evidence rule changes the target for this business model, and checks whether a legal or accounting rule blocks or reshapes the move. Section 3 lists the known collisions and states which rule wins in each.

### The ten jobs the department does

**[INFERENCE]** The department's work divides into ten jobs, each of which can be a worker agent under one manager agent: bookkeeping and accounts payable; invoicing, receivables and collections; VAT and tax compliance; payroll; month-end and year-end close with statutory reporting; planning and forecasting; SaaS metrics and unit economics; cash and treasury; controls and risk; and pricing and funding advice. The manager agent owns the calendar, assigns work, enforces the precedence rule, routes anything that needs human approval, and reviews one worker's output with a different worker or a person before it is relied on. This role map is the researchers' design and no source prescribes it; it follows the segregation logic in the COSO guidance, where assistance is kept apart from authoritative decisions ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).

### The recurring rhythm in one paragraph

**[INFERENCE]** A workable rhythm for a Swedish SaaS aktiebolag is: daily booking of cash events and bank matching; weekly cash forecast roll-forward and collections review; a month-end close in the first working days followed by the tax-account payment date around the 12th; a monthly actual-versus-forecast review and equity test; quarterly OSS returns, a new forecast quarter and a pricing and discount review; and an annual cycle of budget, annual report, audit, AGM, Bolagsverket filing and income tax return. The dates behind this rhythm are in section 11 and the close checklist in section 6; the rhythm itself is a synthesis and not a sourced standard.

### What is verified and what is not

**[INFERENCE]** Agents should treat the document as three confidence bands. The first band, safe to use as a working default subject to re-reading the cited paragraph, covers Swedish bookkeeping and filing duties, the capital-shortfall rules, VAT periods and invoice content, OSS mechanics, employer contribution rates, växa-stöd, holiday pay, late-payment interest, corporate tax dates, SaaS metric formulas and benchmark values with their dates. The second band, usable as method with the label showing it is practitioner opinion or researcher synthesis, covers rolling forecasts, the close checklist, the cash-payback view and the agent guardrails. The third band, not to be loaded as fact, covers everything labelled [GAP] or "unverified": transfer pricing, permanent establishment, pensions, withholding mechanics, BAS account numbers, SIE, the 13-week cash forecast, treasury and FX policy, classic control matrices, venture debt terms and consumer-credit rules.

### Measure against the company's own trailing data first

**[INFERENCE]** Benchmark medians differ by sample: Benchmarkit and SaaS Capital skew to slower-growing, smaller, bootstrapped or private-equity-backed companies with median growth near 25%, while High Alpha and ICONIQ skew to venture-backed and AI-native companies. An agent should pick the peer set that matches the company's funding model, ARR band and region before judging performance, state the definition next to every metric, and prefer the company's own trailing trend to any external median. The underlying samples are described in section 13.

## 2. House style: Alex Hormozi's money models as cash-flow design

### What "house style" means in finance and how far it reaches

**[HOUSE STYLE] [INFERENCE]** The owner has chosen Alex Hormozi's published money models as the department's default style wherever he has published one. In finance that style is cash-first: design the first 30 days of a customer relationship (attraction offer, upsell, downsell, continuity, prepay) so that cash collected exceeds acquisition plus delivery cost, keep lifetime gross profit to CAC at 3:1 or more, raise price before anything else, and fix the single constraint. It is a set of design rules for cash conversion, not an accounting method and not independent evidence. Law and accounting rules override it first and evidence second, as section 3 sets out. All of his figures below are his own unaudited claims, and his company's site states that results are not typical ([Acquisition.com workshop page](https://www.acquisition.com/workshop-3)).

### Provenance of the house-style material

**[HOUSE STYLE] [GAP]** The house-style content rests on three kinds of source. Transcripts are third-party auto-generated transcripts of his own videos and podcast hosted on Rosetta and PodScripts, so the words are his but figures can contain transcription errors. Second-hand summaries are jxnl.co, Shortform and stormy.ai, with stormy.ai the least reliable. His own site was read for the Acquisition.com workshop page only. The 2025 book *$100M Money Models* was not read directly, so every statement attributed to the book is second-hand, and his "levels of customer-financed acquisition" material from the Lost Chapters audiobook of November 2025 could not be opened. Videos from 2021, 2022 and 2023 are older material and are dated where used.

### Client-financed acquisition: let early customer cash fund the next customer

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 11 Oct 2021]** Client-financed acquisition means customers' early payments cover marketing and acquisition cost. The aim he describes is a negative acquisition cost, where cash collected in the first 30 days covers both acquisition and fulfilment and leaves money over to acquire the next customer. He adds that this removes the acquisition bottleneck but not operational or hiring limits ([Rosetta transcript, "How to get unlimited funding…", video dated 11 Oct 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-get-unlimited-funding-to-build-your-business-in-30-days)). Use it when designing onboarding offers, billing terms and checkout flows; do not use it as a description of revenue or profit.

### The 30-day cash rule and its three published variants

**[HOUSE STYLE] [CONTESTED]** Three statements of the 30-day rule exist and they differ. In the 2021 video the minimum is cash collected in 30 days greater than two times the sum of acquisition cost and fulfilment cost, with the example of $100 acquisition plus $100 fulfilment requiring $400 of 30-day cash, and higher multiples described as better ([Rosetta transcript, "How to get unlimited funding…", video dated 11 Oct 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-get-unlimited-funding-to-build-your-business-in-30-days)). In the January 2026 video the targets are to recover CAC within 30 days and for 30-day cash collected to exceed COGS plus CAC ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). A second-hand summary of the 2025 book gives 30-day gross profit greater than two times CAC plus COGS, with the surplus funding the next customer ([jxnl.co summary of $100M Money Models, 20 Aug 2025](https://jxnl.co/writing/2025/08/20/the-100m-money-models-the-complete-report)). The researchers note that the book-summary wording nets COGS twice if gross profit is already after COGS. The department's default is the wording from his own videos, with the variant named whenever the rule is quoted.

### Why 30 days: the card-float argument

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 11 Oct 2021]** His reason for the 30-day window is that card credit is typically interest-free for about 30 days, so acquisition spend placed on a card can be repaid from customer cash before interest accrues and the credit line reused ([Rosetta transcript, "How to get unlimited funding…", video dated 11 Oct 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-get-unlimited-funding-to-build-your-business-in-30-days)). **[INFERENCE]** For this department the window is a design target for cash conversion; funding advertising on card float is a treasury decision that needs an approved limit and human sign-off, and no source in the research assesses it for a Swedish company.

### A worked example attributed to the book

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary]** An example attributed to the book contrasts a gym trial model that costs about $300 per member and breaks even after three months with a $500 prepaid challenge that leaves the business about $200 cash-positive on day one ([jxnl.co summary of $100M Money Models, 20 Aug 2025](https://jxnl.co/writing/2025/08/20/the-100m-money-models-the-complete-report)). The figures are his claims as relayed by a third party and were not checked against the book.

### LTGP:CAC as the unit-economics ratio

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 9 Jan 2026]** Lifetime gross profit (LTGP) is the gross profit a customer generates over the whole relationship, and he uses it as lifetime value. His shortcut is annual payment divided by annual churn: a $100-a-year customer is worth about $200 at 50% retention and about $500 at 80% retention ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). **[INFERENCE]** The shortcut ignores gross margin unless "payment" is read as gross profit, ignores expansion and discounting, and becomes unstable at low churn; when it drives a decision, cap the assumed lifetime at a fixed horizon such as three to five years and state the cap.

### LTGP:CAC targets rise with the number of people in delivery

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 9 Jan 2026]** His ratio targets depend on how many humans sit in delivery and sales: none (fully scalable, for example SaaS) about 3:1; one about 6:1; two about 9:1; three about 12:1. The extra margin pays for hiring and ramp-up ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). **[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary, July 2026]** A podcast summary expresses the same ladder by automation of lead generation, sales and delivery: all three automated about 3:1, two about 6:1, one at least 9:1, none above 12:1. The same summary says he prefers annual back-of-envelope calculations, includes commissions and sales labour in CAC, and expects today's CAC to be the lowest it will be because costs rise with scale ([Shortform summary of The Game Ep 985, 7 July 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-07-07-episode-summary-the-only-two-numbers-that-decide-if-your-business-survives-ep-985)). Apply the 3:1 floor to self-serve SaaS and a higher rung where onboarding, sales or support need people.

### His retention and margin targets

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 9 Jan 2026]** He states targets of at least 80% annual retention for B2B and at least 80% gross margin for service businesses ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). **[INFERENCE]** The 80% retention floor is below the SaaS medians in section 13 (gross revenue retention about 88 to 90%), so for a SaaS company it works as an alarm level and not as a goal.

### The money model: a deliberate sequence of four offer types

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary, Aug 2025]** A money model is a deliberate sequence of offers; the "$100M" version is one where a new customer spends more than the cost of acquiring and serving them early enough to fund further acquisition. The four offer types are attraction (a free or discounted first purchase), upsell (an immediate add-on, upgrade or larger quantity), downsell (an alternative after a refusal: a different payment structure, a smaller quantity or fewer features) and continuity (a recurring subscription or membership) ([jxnl.co summary of $100M Money Models, 20 Aug 2025](https://jxnl.co/writing/2025/08/20/the-100m-money-models-the-complete-report)). For finance the sequence is a cash-flow design: each offer is modelled for cash timing, margin, take rate and refund exposure before it is launched.

### Examples of offers attributed to the book

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary, Aug 2025]** Examples attributed to the book include a prepaid challenge where the customer can win the money back as credit toward membership, a "pay less now or pay more later" choice, "buy X, get Y free", and a free trial that converts to a paid subscription. He claims that doubling customer value, customer count and payment speed compounds to eight times ([jxnl.co summary of $100M Money Models, 20 Aug 2025](https://jxnl.co/writing/2025/08/20/the-100m-money-models-the-complete-report)). **[HOUSE STYLE] [VENDOR; low-reliability marketing blog]** Further sub-types and take rates appear on a third-party blog and were not verified against the book: a feature downsell from $5,000 to $2,000, required add-on upsells with claimed take rates of 80 to 90%, and 20% of gym members prepaying $2,000 for a year ([stormy.ai blog, 2026](https://stormy.ai/blog/alex-hormozi-money-models-2026-strategy)). Do not use the blog figures as planning assumptions.

### Why the first 30 days carry the weight

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 11 Oct 2021]** He notes that upsells, downsells and continuity do not help an owner who is short of cash now, which is why his attention goes to the first 30 days ([Rosetta transcript, "How to get unlimited funding…", video dated 11 Oct 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-get-unlimited-funding-to-build-your-business-in-30-days)). The finance reading is that lifetime value does not pay this month's payroll, so the cash forecast and the unit-economics view must both be shown.

### Prepay offers and his take-rate claims

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 9 Jan 2026]** For prepayment he suggests at least 10% off for payment in full; he claims a "buy 10, get 2" style offer is taken by about 15 to 20% of customers, and that a moderate discount combined with bonuses such as priority, a guarantee or concierge service gets about 30 to 40% to prepay ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). These percentages are his unaudited claims from service and membership businesses; section 14 gives the researchers' calculation of what such a discount costs as financing, and section 5 gives the accounting treatment.

### Payment plans as a downsell

**[HOUSE STYLE; second-hand summary, Aug 2025]** Changing the payment structure after a refusal, such as offering a payment plan, belongs to the downsell category ([jxnl.co summary of $100M Money Models, 20 Aug 2025](https://jxnl.co/writing/2025/08/20/the-100m-money-models-the-complete-report)). **[GAP]** The finance research contains no source on consumer-credit law for payment plans; section 3 treats any instalment or deferred-payment offer to a consumer as blocked until Legal has confirmed the applicable rules.

### Price raises: his close-rate heuristic for underpricing

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 9 Jan 2026]** His heuristic reads the close rate as a price signal: a close rate of 80% or more suggests the offer is underpriced by about three to four times; 60 to 80% by two to three times; 50 to 60% by 1.5 to 2 times; 40 to 50% by 1.25 to 1.5 times; 30 to 40% is about right; and below 30% the fix is targeting or sales process before any price cut. He states an exception for software with unlimited scale, where a lower price can increase growth, and his general order is to raise price before considering lowering it ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). Because he carves out scalable software himself, a SaaS finance agent uses the heuristic as a prompt to test price and not as a multiplier to apply.

### Price raises for supply-constrained businesses

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 3 June 2025]** For supply-constrained businesses he suggests raising prices by 50 to 100% and accepts that losing some customers can still leave more profit and time; his example is a consultant who doubles price, loses about 30% of clients and earns more. A framing example is to present a weekend surcharge as a weekday discount ([Rosetta transcript, "How to Grow Your Business So FAST…", video dated 3 June 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-grow-your-business-so-fast-it-makes-your-accountant-nervous)). **[INFERENCE]** The advice is framed around service businesses with capacity limits; for SaaS, test price changes on new customers and cohorts first and measure the effect on gross revenue retention before touching the installed base.

### Reinvestment versus taking profit

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 3 June 2025]** He says not to pour money into growth when margins are too thin and to fix price or offer first; higher prices can fund marketing in demand-constrained businesses. He cites a software company spending about 5% of revenue on marketing against competitors' 20 to 30% as under-investing ([Rosetta transcript, "How to Grow Your Business So FAST…", video dated 3 June 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-grow-your-business-so-fast-it-makes-your-accountant-nervous)). **[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 14 Feb 2022]** On his own exit he compared sale proceeds reinvested elsewhere with what the retained stake could be worth in five years, and says he had taken about $45M in owner distributions from the two businesses before selling ([Rosetta transcript, "I Sold My Company at 31", video dated 14 Feb 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-sold-my-company-at-31-my-detailed-breakdown-of-the-decision)). **[GAP]** No primary source was captured for a general Hormozi rule on reinvesting versus distributing, on "more, better, new", or on a method for raising prices on existing customers.

### Constraint thinking: fix the bottleneck, nothing else

**[HOUSE STYLE] [DURABLE; transcript, video dated 24 May 2023]** A business grows to its constraint and stops, and owners often work on what they enjoy instead of the bottleneck. His funnel example has steps converting 50%, 5% and 50%: lifting the 5% step to 15% triples output, while small gains elsewhere add about 20% ([Rosetta transcript, "3 Simple Fixes that Grow Any Business", video dated 24 May 2023](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/3-simple-fixes-that-grow-any-business)). For finance agents this means every analysis ends by naming the single constraint the numbers point to.

### Supply-constrained or demand-constrained: the doubling test

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 3 June 2025]** A business is usually either supply-constrained or demand-constrained. His test is to imagine doubling ads or outreach: if that would create chaos, the constraint is supply; if it would double sales, the constraint is demand. Constraints move once solved, and improving a non-bottleneck adds potential but no throughput ([Rosetta transcript, "How to Grow Your Business So FAST…", video dated 3 June 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-grow-your-business-so-fast-it-makes-your-accountant-nervous)). **[HOUSE STYLE; own site]** Acquisition.com's workshop is built around finding the constraint limiting the business; the site claims a $250M-a-year portfolio, targets businesses with $1M to $100M revenue, prices the two-day workshop at $5k per seat, and states that results are not typical and that the Hormozis do not invest in every business they work with ([Acquisition.com workshop page](https://www.acquisition.com/workshop-3)).

### His view of valuation and exits

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, episode date not shown, older material]** In the podcast episode on selling a business he says businesses under about $1M of annual profit are hard to sell, cites average multiples of about 3.75 times below $5M of profit and about 6.75 times above it, says only about 20% of companies that want to sell do, and lists deal components (cash, seller financing, earn-out and rollover equity) and the difference between financial and strategic buyers. His example is $5M EBITDA at five times giving $25M of enterprise value ([PodScripts transcript, The Game Ep 350](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-game-with-alex-hormozi/how-to-sell-your-business-ep-350)). The scraped transcript mixed episode content with generic items, so the figures need checking against the audio. **[INFERENCE]** His EBITDA-multiple lens fits profitable owner-run businesses; SaaS is usually valued on ARR or revenue multiples adjusted for growth and retention (section 16), and the two lenses should not be mixed in one scenario.

### His own exit figures

**[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 14 Feb 2022]** He states that Gym Launch and Prestige Labs sold at a $46.2M valuation, that he sold majority stakes in three companies for over $50M in total and kept roughly a third. His reasons to sell included focus, a belief that buyers with M&A experience could grow the business further, and funding Acquisition.com; his reasons to keep included tax drag and compounding distributions. He reports profit falling from about $17M to $13.4M while revenue rose from about $26M to $37M, which he partly attributes to price cuts ([Rosetta transcript, "I Sold My Company at 31", video dated 14 Feb 2022](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/i-sold-my-company-at-31-my-detailed-breakdown-of-the-decision)). **[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 11 Oct 2021]** His 2021 claim of growing to about $85M a year of portfolio revenue with no outside capital is the bootstrapping case behind client-financed acquisition ([Rosetta transcript, "How to get unlimited funding…", video dated 11 Oct 2021](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-get-unlimited-funding-to-build-your-business-in-30-days)). **[HOUSE STYLE; transcript, video dated 24 May 2023]** He also claims average founder ownership at IPO is about 12.5%, without giving a source ([Rosetta transcript, "3 Simple Fixes that Grow Any Business", video dated 24 May 2023](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/3-simple-fixes-that-grow-any-business)).

### How to apply the house style in a SaaS company: two parallel views

**[INFERENCE] [HOUSE STYLE]** To apply client-financed acquisition in SaaS without breaking the accounting, report two views side by side. The cash view shows cumulative cash collected per new customer by day 30, 90 and 365 against fully loaded CAC plus cost to serve. The accrual view shows CAC payback using the Benchmarkit formula in section 13. Annual-upfront billing, paid onboarding or implementation fees, prepaid credit packs and add-ons at checkout are the SaaS equivalents of his attraction, upsell and prepay offers; they improve the cash view immediately while revenue is still recognised over the service period under the rules in section 5.

### Where a 30-day cash payback is realistic in SaaS

**[INFERENCE] [HOUSE STYLE]** A 30-day cash payback is realistic mainly for self-serve, low-CAC, annual-prepay or paid-onboarding motions. For sales-led B2B with accrual payback of 12 to 30 months it should be treated as a design aspiration and not as a threshold for judging the business. The benchmark evidence behind this reading is in section 3.

## 3. Precedence: law first, evidence second, house style third

### The precedence rule stated once

**[INFERENCE]** When sources of guidance conflict, the order is fixed. Law and accounting rules always override the house style and the benchmarks: statutes, K2 or K3, VAT, tax and payroll rules, consumer and credit law, and the auditor's reading of them. Independent evidence and SaaS benchmarks come second and override the house style where they show a house-style target does not fit the business model. The house style comes third and is the default wherever the first two layers are silent. An agent that cannot tell which layer a point belongs to treats it as layer one and escalates.

### Collision: prepaid cash is not revenue

**[DURABLE; secondary source only for K2 and K3, checked on primary source for IFRS 15]** His prepay and 30-day cash measures are cash measures. Under K3 point 23.26 and K2 point 6.11 a service contract covering an unspecified number of activities over an agreed period is recognised straight-line over that period ([FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: uppdrag med ett obestämt antal aktiviteter](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/u/rr_uppdragmedettobestamtantalaktiviteter/)), and IFRS 15 recognises service revenue over time as the performance obligation is satisfied ([IFRS Foundation, IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/)). Cash collected up front on a subscription is therefore a liability released to revenue as service is delivered; on a 24-month deal one twenty-fourth moves from deferred revenue to revenue each month ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)). The accounting rule wins: a prepaid annual plan improves the cash view on day one and changes revenue by one-twelfth a month.

### Collision: 30-day payback versus SaaS payback benchmarks

**[CONTESTED] [HOUSE STYLE] [VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025-2026]** His payback target is 30 days ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). SaaS benchmark sources report $2.00 of sales and marketing per $1.00 of new-customer ARR at the median for calendar 2024 ([Benchmarkit 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics Benchmarks, CY-2024 data (PDF)](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)), KeyBanc and Sapphire respondents expected account-executive payback to shorten to 18 months by 2026 ([Sapphire Ventures, 2025 KeyBanc and Sapphire SaaS Survey page](https://info.sapphireventures.com/2025-keybanc-capital-markets-sapphire-ventures-saas-survey)), and Monterro's Nordic sample averaged a CAC payback of 14.0 months in 2025 with the top quartile at about six months ([Monterro Nordic B2B Software Benchmark 2026, 19 April 2026 (PDF)](https://www.monterro.com/media/2finidot/monterro-nordic-software-benchmark-report-2026_web.pdf)). Evidence wins on the target: judge a sales-led SaaS motion against the peer payback range and use the 30-day rule to design billing terms and onboarding offers that pull cash forward.

### Collision: the 3:1 ratio agrees, the LTV definition is stricter

**[CONTESTED] [HOUSE STYLE]** His 3:1 LTGP:CAC floor for fully scalable businesses matches the conventional SaaS rule of thumb noted in the podcast summary ([Shortform summary of The Game Ep 985, 7 July 2026](https://www.shortform.com/podcast/episode/the-game-w-alex-hormozi-2026-07-07-episode-summary-the-only-two-numbers-that-decide-if-your-business-survives-ep-985)). The a16z definition of lifetime value is stricter: it uses contribution margin, warns that using revenue or gross margin in its place is a common error, and advises measuring realised 12-month and 24-month value when history is short instead of projecting a lifespan ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)). Evidence wins on the definition: compute the ratio on contribution or gross profit, on cohort data, with a capped lifetime.

### Collision: "raise price first" versus SaaS pricing evidence

**[CONTESTED] [HOUSE STYLE] [VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, April 2026]** He advises raising price before anything else and by 50 to 100% where supply is constrained ([Rosetta transcript, "How to Grow Your Business So FAST…", video dated 3 June 2025](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/how-to-grow-your-business-so-fast-it-makes-your-accountant-nervous)), and he himself excepts scalable software, where a lower price can increase growth ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). Monterro's Nordic B2B software sample reports an average price increase of 5.9% in 2025 and 6.8% in 2024 ([Monterro Nordic B2B Software Benchmark 2026, 19 April 2026 (PDF)](https://www.monterro.com/media/2finidot/monterro-nordic-software-benchmark-report-2026_web.pdf)). SaaS Capital's definition counts price increases inside net revenue retention and excludes them from gross revenue retention ([SaaS Capital, retention benchmarks, 18 Sept 2025](https://www.saas-capital.com/research/saas-retention-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-companies/)). **[GAP]** The research found no sourced data on churn after price increases in B2B SaaS. The working rule is to test larger increases on new customers first, hold installed-base increases near the peer range unless a test supports more, and report NRR with and without the price effect.

### Collision: payment plans and consumer-credit rules

**[GAP; unverified, not researched]** The owner's brief names consumer-credit rules for payment plans as an example of law overriding house style. The finance research did not open any source on Swedish or EU consumer-credit law, so this document cannot state the rule, its thresholds or its current status. The operating rule is therefore a hard gate: no instalment plan, deferred payment, "pay later" option or financing offer is made to a consumer until Legal has confirmed in writing which credit, information and creditworthiness rules apply and how the offer must be worded. For business customers, the verified constraint is narrower and sits in Räntelagen (section 7).

### Collision: guarantees, "win your money back" and refunds

**[INFERENCE]** Prepaid, refundable or guarantee-backed offers create refund liabilities and variable consideration, so revenue and cash forecasts need an explicit refund assumption for each such offer. Under EU consumer rules the consumer versions may also carry withdrawal rights; the research did not verify those rules, so a legal check is required before launch. IFRS 15's model includes estimating variable consideration when setting the transaction price ([IFRS Foundation, IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/)).

### Collision: prepay discounts are financing with a price

**[INFERENCE] [HOUSE STYLE]** His guidance is at least 10% off for payment in full ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). By the researchers' own calculation, comparing one upfront payment with twelve equal monthly payments in advance, a 10% discount costs about 2.0% a month (about 26% annualised), 15% about 3.1% a month (about 44%), and 20% about 4.3% a month (about 67%). The discount is expensive financing unless it also reduces churn, collection cost or failed-payment loss. **[GAP]** No sourced SaaS norm for annual-prepay discounts and no sourced annual-versus-monthly retention difference was found, so the comparison must use the company's own cohorts and cost of capital.

### Collision: distributions and reinvestment versus Swedish company law

**[INFERENCE; partly checked on primary source]** His reinvest-or-distribute reasoning is a business judgement. For a Swedish aktiebolag it sits beneath three legal constraints: the board's duty to assess the company's financial situation continuously and the capital-shortfall rules ([Aktiebolagslag (2005:551), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:783](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/)), the restricted development fund that reduces distributable equity when development costs are capitalised ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)), and the owner-level 3:12 rules for closely held companies (section 9). **[GAP]** Aktiebolagslagen chapters 17 and 18 on value transfers and the prudence rule for dividends were not read, so any dividend proposal goes to a human with the auditor or adviser.

## 4. Bookkeeping law and standards for a Swedish aktiebolag

### The legal backbone: BFL, ÅRL and BFN's K2 and K3

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source for the statutes, secondary source only for K2 and K3]** The legal backbone for keeping the books of a Swedish aktiebolag is Bokföringslagen (1999:1078) and Årsredovisningslagen (1995:1554), filled out by the Swedish Accounting Standards Board's K2 (BFNAR 2016:10) and K3 (BFNAR 2012:1) frameworks ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/); [Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/); [KPMG on BFN's K2 and K3 changes decided 16 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html)). An aktiebolag must always close its books with an annual report and make it public under Bokföringslagen 6 kap. 1 § ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)). General principle for any company: know which statute and which accounting framework govern before automating a single entry.

### Booking deadlines: cash by the next working day

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Cash receipts and payments must be booked no later than the next working day, and other transactions as soon as possible; later booking is allowed where there is reason and it is consistent with good accounting practice (Bokföringslagen 5 kap. 2 §). Companies with normal annual net turnover up to SEK 3 million may wait to book until payment, but must book all unpaid receivables and payables at year-end ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)). For agents this makes daily bank ingestion and matching a legal requirement and not a convenience.

### What every verification must contain

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Every business transaction needs a verification stating when it was compiled, when the transaction occurred, what it concerns, the amount, the counterparty, references to supporting documents where relevant, and a verification number or other identifier that links it to the booked entry (Bokföringslagen 5 kap. 6 to 7 §§). Similar transactions may share one verification. Corrections must show when and by whom they were made ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)). An agent that posts or corrects an entry must therefore record its own identity, the time and the human approver on the entry.

### System documentation and processing history

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The company must keep system documentation, meaning an overview of how the bookkeeping system is organised, and processing history, so that the treatment of each entry can be followed (Bokföringslagen 5 kap. 11 §) ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)). **[INFERENCE]** Where agents prepare or post entries, the logs of their prompts, sources, outputs and model versions plausibly fall within this requirement; this link is the researchers' reading and not a statement by the Accounting Standards Board.

### Financial year rules

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The financial year is twelve calendar months; the first year, or a year in which the financial year is changed, may be shortened or extended to at most 18 months; an aktiebolag may use a broken financial year; changing the financial year needs Skatteverket's permission with some exceptions; and group companies generally share a financial year (Bokföringslagen 3 kap.) ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)).

### Archiving: seven years, stored in Sweden as the main rule

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Accounting records may be kept on paper or electronically, provided they are readable with technical aids and can be printed immediately, and they must be kept in the form and with the content they had when received or compiled (Bokföringslagen 7 kap. 1 §). They are retained through the seventh year after the calendar year in which the financial year ended, stored in Sweden in an orderly, secure and clear way, with the equipment and systems needed to print them available in Sweden for the whole period (7 kap. 2 §) ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)).

### Storing electronic records in another EU country

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Electronic records may be stored in another EU member state if the company notifies Skatteverket of the location and any change, gives immediate electronic access on request for control purposes, and can print the records in Sweden on demand (Bokföringslagen 7 kap. 3 a §). Skatteverket may permit storage elsewhere for special reasons (7 kap. 4 §) ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)). **[INFERENCE]** Cloud bookkeeping hosted in an EU data centre outside Sweden fits the rule only if Skatteverket has been notified; hosting outside the EU needs a permit or an exception that was not verified. Agents should keep a register of where each system of record physically stores data.

### Paper originals may be destroyed after safe digitisation

**[FAST-MOVING, in force 1 July 2024; checked on primary source for the rule, secondary source only for the date]** Originals may be destroyed once records have been transferred to another form, provided the transfer carries no risk of altering or losing information given technical and organisational measures (Bokföringslagen 7 kap. 6 §) ([Bokföringslag (1999:1078), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2024:342](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/bokforingslag-19991078_sfs-1999-1078/)). The change took effect on 1 July 2024 and removed the earlier rule that a paper original had to be kept three years after scanning; the seven-year retention of the digital record is unchanged ([Spiris, arkivering av bokföring](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/foretagsredovisning/arkivering-bokforing)).

### What counts as accounting records in a SaaS company

**[INFERENCE]** Payment-provider reports, bank files, subscription-billing exports and contract PDFs that support entries are accounting records and fall under the seven-year rule. This follows from the statute's definitions, but the definition paragraph itself was not read, so confirm before deleting any such data.

### Size classes: "större företag" and consolidated accounts

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** A "större företag" is a company that is listed or that exceeds more than one of the following in each of the two latest financial years: more than 50 employees on average, a balance sheet total above SEK 40 million, net turnover above SEK 80 million. Everything else is a "mindre företag". The same thresholds define a larger group, with alternative gross limits of SEK 48 million and SEK 96 million without eliminations (Årsredovisningslagen 1 kap. 3 §). A parent in a smaller group need not prepare consolidated accounts (7 kap. 3 §) ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)).

### The 2026 K2 and K3 update and when it applies

**[FAST-MOVING, decided 16 June 2025, applies to years starting after 31 Dec 2025; secondary source only]** The Accounting Standards Board decided amendments to K3 and K2 on 16 June 2025; they apply to financial years beginning after 31 December 2025. Companies that started operations after 30 June 2025 with an extended first year ending 31 December 2026 or later are caught earlier ([KPMG on BFN's K2 and K3 changes decided 16 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html)). A second audit-firm summary lists no change to K3 chapter 23 on revenue or chapter 18 on intangibles in this update ([BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)).

### Who may no longer use K2: option programmes push a SaaS company toward K3

**[FAST-MOVING, applies from financial years starting 1 Jan 2026; secondary source only]** Under the updated K2 the following may not apply K2: companies with foreign branches; companies using share-based compensation such as option programmes; companies with debt instruments that can be settled in equity or similar hybrids; companies holding crypto-assets (incidental transactions and indirect holdings excepted); companies where buildings generate at least 75% of net turnover; and companies with a significant deferred tax liability. The last two limits do not apply to companies not subject to audit ([KPMG on BFN's K2 and K3 changes decided 16 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html)). **[CONTESTED; secondary source only]** A relief rule lets a company already on K2 stay there despite the new exclusion criteria if it meets at most one of the audit-size tests (3 employees, SEK 1.5 million balance sheet, SEK 3 million turnover) in the last two years ([BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** A SaaS aktiebolag with an employee option or warrant programme or a foreign branch should plan on K3 for financial years starting 1 January 2026 or later, with the caveat that a very small company already on K2 may fall under the relief rule; the framework choice is confirmed with the auditor.

### Other K2 changes that affect the close

**[FAST-MOVING, applies from financial years starting 1 Jan 2026; secondary source only]** The updated K2 raises the threshold for not accruing recurring items and prepayments to SEK 7,000, requires shareholder contributions to be committed by the balance sheet date, allows errors to be corrected by restating the opening balance with a note, and no longer allows fixed-price contracts with positive and negative balances to be netted ([KPMG on BFN's K2 and K3 changes decided 16 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html)).

### K3 changes that affect groups and corrections

**[FAST-MOVING, applies from financial years starting 1 Jan 2026; secondary source only]** The updated K3 clarifies that share-based compensation rules cover awards granted by another group company (for example a parent granting options to subsidiary staff, recorded by the subsidiary), rewrites contingent consideration by removing the one-year window, has material errors corrected by restating comparatives or the opening balance, and lets larger companies changing principles on transition skip restating comparatives if they disclose the lack of comparability ([KPMG on BFN's K2 and K3 changes decided 16 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html)).

### When IFRS applies

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** IFRS as adopted by the EU is compulsory only for the consolidated accounts of companies covered by Article 4 of Regulation (EC) 1606/2002, meaning companies with securities on a regulated market (Årsredovisningslagen 7 kap. 32 §). Other companies may choose to prepare consolidated accounts under the international standards (7 kap. 33 §) ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; secondary source only]** IFRS 18 on presentation applies from 1 January 2027, and RFR 2 updates published in December 2025 apply from January 2026 and give legal entities exemptions from IFRS 18, which matters only to IFRS and RFR 2 reporters ([BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)). Most of FAR's RedU statements have become RedR recommendations with unchanged names and content ([BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)).

### BAS chart of accounts: only the revenue mapping is sourced

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** In the BAS chart of accounts net sales sit in account class 3, other operating income in account group 39, and financial income in groups 80 to 83 ([FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: inkomst](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/i/rr_inkomst/)). **[GAP; unverified background]** The rest of BAS was not researched. The researchers' unverified recollection is that BAS is a de facto standard and not law, uses four-digit accounts, with class 1 for assets, 2 for equity and liabilities, 3 for revenue, 4 for cost of goods, 5 to 6 for other external costs, 7 for personnel and depreciation and 8 for financial items and tax, and that deferred revenue and accruals commonly use accounts 2970, 2990 and 1790; confirm all of it at [bas.se](https://www.bas.se/).

The unverified recollection is that SIE is the Swedish format for exchanging accounting data, with types 1 to 4 (type 4 carrying transactions) and an XML-based SIE 5, maintained at [sie.se](https://sie.se/). ## 5. Revenue recognition for SaaS and capitalised development

### Subscriptions are recognised straight-line over the service period

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** Under K3 point 23.26, revenue from a service assignment with an unspecified number of activities over a fixed period is recognised on a straight-line basis over the period, unless another method better reflects the stage of completion; if one specific activity is much more significant than the others, revenue is recognised when that activity is performed. K2 point 6.11 gives the same result for smaller companies where the assignment is not commission-based or on time and materials, covers an unspecified number of activities within an agreed period, and the fee relates to that period ([FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: uppdrag med ett obestämt antal aktiviteter](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/u/rr_uppdragmedettobestamtantalaktiviteter/)). A SaaS subscription is this kind of contract, so the invoice date drives VAT and receivables while revenue follows the service period.

### Prepaid annual subscriptions are a liability released monthly

**[INFERENCE; rests on secondary source for the paragraph references]** A prepaid annual SaaS subscription is booked as a liability (förutbetald intäkt) and released one-twelfth a month under K3 point 23.26 or K2 point 6.11 ([FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: uppdrag med ett obestämt antal aktiviteter](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/u/rr_uppdragmedettobestamtantalaktiviteter/)). Under K2, small prepayments below SEK 7,000 need not be accrued across financial years from 2026 ([KPMG on BFN's K2 and K3 changes decided 16 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html)). This is the rule that overrides any house-style habit of treating prepayment as income.

### General income and recognition conditions under K2

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** K2 point 6.2 with its commentary says income is what the company receives or will receive for its own account, owner contributions are not income, and a transaction may be split into parts when needed to reflect economic substance and the parts and amounts can be identified separately. K2 point 6.6 sets the recognition conditions: significant risks and rewards have passed, the amount can be measured reliably, and the economic benefits are probable; only the part of income belonging to the financial year is that year's revenue ([FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: inkomst](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/i/rr_inkomst/)).

### IFRS 15's five-step model

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** IFRS 15's core model is: identify the contract; identify distinct performance obligations; determine the transaction price including estimates of variable consideration; allocate by relative stand-alone selling prices; and recognise revenue when or as each obligation is satisfied, meaning when the customer obtains control. Services are typically recognised over time using a measure of progress. The standard has applied to periods beginning on or after 1 January 2018, clarifications were issued in April 2016, and the post-implementation review is listed as completed in 2024 ([IFRS Foundation, IFRS 15](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/)). It binds only IFRS reporters, but its logic is a useful general reference for any company.

### Set-up and onboarding fees

**[INFERENCE]** K3 and K2 have no SaaS-specific rule for set-up or onboarding fees. The route is the component-separation principle: if onboarding has no stand-alone substance, treat it as part of the subscription and spread it over the contract period; if it is a separately identifiable service, recognise it when performed. This is the researchers' reading of K2 point 6.2 and the dominant-activity exception in K3 point 23.26 ([FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: inkomst](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/i/rr_inkomst/); [FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: uppdrag med ett obestämt antal aktiviteter](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/u/rr_uppdragmedettobestamtantalaktiviteter/)), not a quoted rule, and the policy should be agreed with the auditor.

### Usage-based fees and prepaid credits

**[INFERENCE]** Usage-based fees are earned as usage occurs and are accrued at month-end if unbilled; this follows from the general accrual rule in K2 point 6.6 and IFRS 15's fifth step, and no usage-specific paragraph was read. Unused prepaid credits are a contract liability until used or expired, and the treatment of expiry and breakage should be confirmed with the auditor. **[GAP]** No sourced accounting guidance specific to prepaid AI credits and breakage was found.

### Sales commissions

**[GAP; unverified]** The researchers could not verify any contract-cost asset rule in K3, so the cautious default under K3 is to expense sales commissions as incurred. IFRS 15's capitalisation of incremental costs of obtaining a contract, with its one-year practical expedient, was not on the page read and remains unverified background.

### Bookings, billings and revenue are three different numbers

**[DURABLE]** Bookings are the value of a signed contract; revenue is recognised as the service is delivered, ratably for subscriptions; letters of intent and verbal agreements count as neither, and treating a booking as revenue is a common error. Billings equal revenue in the period plus the change in deferred revenue and are described as a better forward-looking indicator than revenue, with the caveat that a company can hold revenue steady for a time by drawing down its backlog ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)).

### Capitalising own development: allowed by statute, with a restricted fund

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Årsredovisningslagen allows own development work to be recognised as an intangible asset; an aktiebolag that capitalises its own development costs must transfer the same amount from free equity to a restricted development fund (fond för utvecklingsutgifter); formation, share-issue and administration costs may not be capitalised (4 kap. 2 §) ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)). **[INFERENCE]** The fund requirement reduces distributable equity by the capitalised amount, so capitalising development affects dividend capacity and interacts with the capital-shortfall test in section 6.

### K3's choice between expensing and capitalising, and the six criteria

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** K3 point 18.7 lets a company choose between the expensing model and the capitalisation model for internally generated intangible assets. Under K3 point 18.12 development-phase expenditure is capitalised only if the company can show all six of the following: it is technically feasible to complete the asset; it intends to complete and use or sell it; it is able to use or sell it; future economic benefits are probable, demonstrated with the value-in-use method of chapter 27; adequate technical, financial and other resources exist; and the expenditure can be measured reliably ([FAR Online, Redovisa Rätt: aktiveringsmodellen](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/redovisa-ratt/a/rr_aktiveringsmodellen/)).

### Gaps in revenue and development accounting

**[GAP]** Not verified: K3 points 23.3 to 23.8 on measurement and separating components and 23.17 to 23.22 on stage of completion; K3 amortisation rules for capitalised development, including the often-quoted five-year presumption; that K2 prohibits capitalising own development; IFRS 15 guidance on contract costs, contract liabilities and licences; and the tax treatment of capitalised development.

## 6. Close, annual report, audit and board duties

### The statutory chain from year-end to filing

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** For an aktiebolag the statutory chain is: the annual report goes to the auditors at least six weeks before the AGM (Årsredovisningslagen 8 kap. 2 §); the AGM is held within six months after the end of the financial year, with the board presenting the annual report and audit report (Aktiebolagslagen 7 kap. 10 §); and certified copies of the annual report and audit report reach Bolagsverket within one month after the AGM adopted the balance sheet and income statement (Årsredovisningslagen 8 kap. 3 §) ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/); [Aktiebolagslag (2005:551), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:783](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/)).

### Late-filing fees: SEK 7,500, 7,500 and 15,000 for a private company

**[FAST-MOVING, wording from Lag (2024:1115), in force 1 Jan 2025; checked on primary source]** A late-filing fee is charged if the documents have not arrived within seven months of the financial year-end, or nine months where stated extension conditions are met: SEK 7,500 for a private aktiebolag and SEK 15,000 for a public one (Årsredovisningslagen 8 kap. 6 §). A new fee of SEK 7,500 (public: SEK 15,000) follows if the report is still not filed two months after notice of the first decision, and a further SEK 15,000 (public: SEK 30,000) after another two months (8 kap. 6 a §). The current wording comes from Lag (2024:1115), in force 1 January 2025, and first applies to financial years beginning after 31 December 2024. No fee may be decided once a bankruptcy decision has been registered (8 kap. 7 §) ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)). **[GAP]** The exact conditions of the nine-month variant were not extracted.

### Personal liability at fifteen months

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** If the annual report and audit report have not been sent within 15 months of year-end, board members and the CEO become jointly liable for the company's obligations unless they show they were not negligent (Årsredovisningslagen 8 kap. 12 §) ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)). An agent tracking the filing calendar treats this date as a board-level alarm.

### Digital filing is allowed, not mandatory

**[FAST-MOVING, status November 2026; checked on primary source]** Årsredovisningslagen 8 kap. 3 a §, introduced by Lag (2024:347) and in force 1 July 2024, says the documents may be transferred electronically and the adoption certificate may be e-signed; only companies that must prepare a sustainability report are required to publish in the uniform electronic format (8 kap. 3 b §). The text consolidated through SFS 2026:780 contains no general mandatory digital-filing rule ([Årsredovisningslag (1995:1554), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:780](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/arsredovisningslag-19951554_sfs-1995-1554/)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2023-2024; secondary source only]** A Ministry memorandum from February 2023 proposed compulsory digital filing for about 95% of aktiebolag (roughly 587,000), in force 1 January 2026, exempting financial, insurance and IFRS-reporting or listed companies; about 326,000 companies filed digitally in 2023, and reports must be in XHTML (iXBRL) from supporting software ([EY, Feb 2024](https://www.ey.com/sv_se/insights/assurance/krav-pa-digital-inlamning-av-arsredovisning-kan-bli-verklighet); [Syna, Aug 2024](https://upplysningar.syna.se/nyheter/nyhet/digital-arsredovisning-ska-bli-obligatorisk/)). **[INFERENCE]** The proposal's start date passed with the statute still saying "may", and a 2026 audit-firm review does not mention a mandate ([BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)), so the mandate has not been enacted as far as these sources show; Bolagsverket's own site was not read, so confirm at [bolagsverket.se](https://bolagsverket.se/).

### Mechanics of digital filing and annual-report formalities

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** Digital filing has been possible for K2 and K3 reports since 2018, needs software that supports it, is possible only after the AGM has adopted the accounts, and is signed by a board member or the CEO with e-identification ([Driva Eget, digital filing of the annual report](https://driva-eget.se/artiklar/lamna-in-arsredovisningen-digitalt-fragor-och-datum); [Realtid on digital filing](https://www.realtid.se/bors-finans/digital-inlamning-av-arsredovisningar-lagkrav-fran-1-juli/)). **[FAST-MOVING, financial years starting after end of June 2024; secondary source only]** The annual report must be dated when its final content is fixed, and each signatory's signing date must be shown ([BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)).

### Worked calendar for a December year-end

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2028; secondary source and inference]** For 2025 accounts of a calendar-year company, one audit firm's calendar gives books to the auditor around 19 May 2026 as practice, the AGM by 30 June 2026 and filing by 31 July 2026 ([RSM, financial calendar for December year-ends](https://www.rsm.global/sweden/sv/news/hall-koll-pa-din-ekonomiska-kalender-viktiga-datum-privatpersoner-och-aktiebolag-med-bokslutsdatum-sista-december-0)). **[INFERENCE]** For a company closing 31 December 2026 the same arithmetic gives the annual report to the auditor by about 19 May 2027 if the AGM is on 30 June 2027, the AGM by 30 June 2027, filing by 31 July 2027 to avoid the fee, and the personal-liability date on 31 March 2028; weekend adjustments were not checked.

### Audit requirement: the 3 / 1.5 million / 3 million test

**[FAST-MOVING, unchanged as of April 2026; checked on primary source]** A private aktiebolag may opt out of having an auditor in its articles unless it exceeds more than one of these in each of the two latest financial years: more than 3 employees on average, a balance sheet total over SEK 1.5 million, net turnover over SEK 3 million. The test uses group figures for a parent, and a company with a registered auditor at year-end must have that year audited (Aktiebolagslagen 9 kap. 1 §) ([Aktiebolagslag (2005:551), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:783](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/)). **[FAST-MOVING, March to April 2026; secondary source only]** The thresholds have not been adjusted since 2010; a business organisation argued in March 2026 for raising them and cites SOU 2021:60, which suggested investigating an exemption up to 9 employees and SEK 20 million ([Företagarna, March 2026](https://www.foretagarna.se/politik-paverkan/rapporter/2026/hojd-grans-for-revisionsplikt-skulle-spara-foretagen-miljardbelopp/)), and an audit firm's guide dated 14 April 2026 lists the same thresholds with no pending change ([EY, 14 April 2026](https://www.ey.com/sv_se/insights/private-business/nar-maste-ett-bolag-ha-revisor)).

### Enforcement of the audit requirement has tightened

**[FAST-MOVING, 2024-2026; secondary source only]** Bolagsverket has been responsible for checking the audit requirement since April 2024, identified about 4,000 aktiebolag in breach in May 2025, received extra resources from 1 January 2026, and non-compliance can end in compulsory liquidation ([Balans/FAR, April 2026](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/balans/2026/april/balans_2026040802/); [EY, 14 April 2026](https://www.ey.com/sv_se/insights/private-business/nar-maste-ett-bolag-ha-revisor)).

### Board and CEO responsibilities for finance

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The board is responsible for the organisation and management of the company, must continuously assess its financial situation, and must organise it so that bookkeeping, fund management and finances are controlled reliably; delegation requires continued follow-up (Aktiebolagslagen 8 kap. 4 §). The CEO runs day-to-day management under board guidelines and must ensure the bookkeeping complies with law and that funds are managed securely (8 kap. 29 §). Minimum share capital for a private aktiebolag is SEK 25,000 (1 kap. 5 §) ([Aktiebolagslag (2005:551), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:783](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/)). An AI finance department works under this delegation and never replaces it.

### Kontrollbalansräkning: the capital-shortfall procedure

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Under Aktiebolagslagen 25 kap. 13 to 20 a §§ the board must immediately prepare a control balance sheet and have the auditor review it when there is reason to believe equity is below half of the registered share capital, or when enforcement shows the company cannot pay a judgment debt. If the shortfall is confirmed, the board must promptly call a first control meeting to consider liquidation. If liquidation is not decided, a second control meeting must be held within eight months with a new audited control balance sheet, and equity must then be restored to the full registered share capital; if it is not, the board must apply to the district court for liquidation within two weeks ([Aktiebolagslag (2005:551), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:783](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/)).

### Personal liability under the capital-shortfall rules

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Board members who fail the capital-shortfall duties are jointly and personally liable for obligations the company incurs during the failure unless they show they were not negligent, and a shareholder who knowingly votes to continue shares that liability. Claims lapse three years after the obligation arose or one year after its due date ([Aktiebolagslag (2005:551), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:783](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/)).

### Monthly equity test for loss-making SaaS companies

**[INFERENCE]** With the minimum share capital of SEK 25,000 the trigger level is equity below SEK 12,500. A loss-making, venture-funded SaaS company with small share capital and large share-premium reserves can reach it quickly once accumulated losses consume the premium. Equity against half the registered share capital should be tested at every month-end close and any breach or near-breach flagged to the board the same day; this rule is the researchers' reading of Aktiebolagslagen 25 kap. 13 § and 8 kap. 4 § ([Aktiebolagslag (2005:551), riksdagen.se, consolidated through SFS 2026:783](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/aktiebolagslag-2005551_sfs-2005-551/)).

## 7. Invoicing, receivables, late payment and payment-provider reconciliation

### Mandatory content of a full invoice

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** A full invoice must contain the issue date; a unique sequential number; the seller's VAT number; the buyer's VAT number when the buyer is liable for the VAT; both parties' names and addresses; the quantity and nature of goods or the extent and nature of services; the date of supply or of advance payment if different from the invoice date; the taxable amount per rate or exemption, the unit price excluding VAT and any discounts; the VAT rate; and the VAT amount ([Skatteverket, momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momslagensregleromfakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html)). A billing agent validates every invoice against this list before sending.

### Reverse-charge wording and the EU B2B invoice deadline

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Where the buyer accounts for the VAT, the invoice must state "Omvänd betalningsskyldighet" or "Reverse charge". An invoice for an EU business-to-business sale must be issued by the 15th of the month after the goods were delivered or the service supplied; services are reported in VAT-return box 39 and goods in box 35 ([Skatteverket, momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momslagensregleromfakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html)).

### Simplified invoices

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026 amounts; checked on primary source]** A simplified invoice is allowed when the total is SEK 4,000 or less including VAT, for credit notes, where trade practice or technology makes a full invoice hard, or when the seller is exempt because turnover is SEK 120,000 or less; it may not be used for an EU buyer who must self-account. Minimum content is the date, seller identification, nature of the supply, and the VAT amount or the data to compute it ([Skatteverket, momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momslagensregleromfakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html)).

### Foreign-currency invoices need the VAT amount in SEK

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** If Swedish VAT is due and the books are kept in SEK, the VAT amount must also be shown in SEK, using the ECB rate or the Nasdaq OMX Stockholm mid rate, normally the latest known rate on the day of supply or advance payment ([Skatteverket, momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momslagensregleromfakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html)). **[INFERENCE]** Monthly subscription billing in EUR or USD to Swedish customers therefore needs a SEK VAT amount on each invoice.

### Credit notes and invoice retention

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Credit notes must refer unambiguously to the original invoice, normally by number, and carry the required invoice details. Issued and received invoices must both be kept, on paper or electronically, provided the content stays readable and unchanged ([Skatteverket, momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momslagensregleromfakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html)).

### Default payment term between businesses: 30 days

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Under Räntelagen 2 a § a business-to-business or business-to-public-body claim for goods or services is due 30 days after the creditor demands payment; a longer term is valid between businesses only if the creditor has explicitly accepted it ([Räntelag (1975:635), riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rantelag-1975635_sfs-1975-635/)). The consolidated texts read date from SFS 2013:55 and 2013:56, in force 16 March 2013, which implement the 2011 EU Late Payment Directive ([Räntelag (1975:635), riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rantelag-1975635_sfs-1975-635/)).

### When late interest starts to run

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Interest runs from the due date when a due date was fixed in advance (Räntelagen 3 §); otherwise from 30 days after an invoice or demand was sent, and between businesses the invoice need not state that interest will be charged (4 §). In business dealings, contract terms that remove the right to late interest or set a lower rate are ineffective against the creditor (4 a §, 6 §) ([Räntelag (1975:635), riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rantelag-1975635_sfs-1975-635/)).

### Statutory late-payment interest is 10.00% in the second half of 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 July to 31 December 2026; checked on primary source]** The statutory late-interest rate is the Riksbank reference rate plus eight percentage points (Räntelagen 6 §), and the Riksbank sets the reference rate each half-year, rounded up to the nearest half point (9 §) ([Räntelag (1975:635), riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rantelag-1975635_sfs-1975-635/)). The reference rate is 2.00% from 1 July 2026 for the period to 31 December 2026; its history is 2.00% from 1 January 2026, 2.00% from 1 July 2025, 3.00% from 1 January 2025, 4.00% from 1 July 2024 and 1 January 2024, 3.50% from 1 July 2023 and 2.50% from 1 January 2023 ([Riksbank, reference rate](https://www.riksbank.se/en-gb/statistics/interest-rates-and-exchange-rates/reference-rate/)). **[INFERENCE]** Statutory late-payment interest is therefore 10.00% a year for 1 July 2025 through 31 December 2026 and was 11.00% in the first half of 2025; the rate for 1 January 2027 is set around year-end and must be re-read.

### Reminder fee, collection fee and the SEK 450 compensation

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Under Lag (1981:739) a fee for a written reminder may be charged only if it was agreed no later than when the debt arose; the caps are SEK 60 for a reminder, SEK 180 for a debt-collection demand and SEK 170 for an instalment plan. Between businesses, and business to public authority, a creditor entitled to late interest may also claim a flat late-payment compensation of SEK 450, and reminder and collection fees are payable only to the extent they exceed that amount (4 a §, 5 §) ([Lag (1981:739) om ersättning för inkassokostnader, riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1981739-om-ersattning-for-inkassokostnader_sfs-1981-739/)).

### Invoice and terms template for late payment

**[INFERENCE]** A practical template states the due date, a reference to late interest under Räntelagen or an agreed rate, and a reminder-fee clause in the contract or terms so the SEK 60 fee is chargeable; on overdue business invoices the SEK 450 compensation can be claimed without prior agreement. This is the researchers' reading of the two statutes ([Räntelag (1975:635), riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/rantelag-1975635_sfs-1975-635/); [Lag (1981:739) om ersättning för inkassokostnader, riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/lag-1981739-om-ersattning-for-inkassokostnader_sfs-1981-739/)).

### Stripe: reconcile payout by payout

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; DURABLE]** Stripe's payout reconciliation report matches each bank payout to the batch of charges, refunds, disputes and fees it settles and works only with automatic payouts; with manual or instant payouts the Balance report is used and the Stripe balance treated like a bank account. The itemised CSV gives one row per balance transaction with gross, fee and net, the payout ID, the reporting category, and optionally invoice ID and number, customer and subscription IDs and custom metadata for matching to the ledger ([Stripe docs, payout reconciliation report](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/payout-reconciliation)).

### Stripe timing differences at month-end

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; DURABLE]** The payout reconciliation report groups payouts by estimated arrival date and not by the date the bank posts the deposit; an ending-balance reconciliation section lists transactions not yet paid out at period end; and report data can lag the payout ([Stripe docs, payout reconciliation report](https://docs.stripe.com/reports/payout-reconciliation)).

### Bookkeeping pattern for a payment provider

**[INFERENCE]** Post gross sales to revenue or receivable per invoice, provider fees to a bank-charges account, and carry a clearing balance-sheet account that must equal the provider's ending balance at each month-end; payouts in transit over month-end are a reconciling item because the provider dates them by expected arrival. **[GAP]** The VAT treatment of provider fees (reverse charge on a purchased foreign service, or exempt financial service, depending on fee type) was not verified.

### Collections practice

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** No sourced data on days sales outstanding, dunning recovery rates or Swedish SaaS payment terms was found. Ordinary practice recalled by the researchers is reminders before the due date, staged dunning, a named owner per overdue account, automated retry for failed card payments, and stop-service rules agreed with Sales.

**[GAP]** Not researched or verified: Inkassolagen (1974:182); the status of the EU's 2023 proposal for a Late Payment Regulation with a strict 30-day cap; and Bankgiro, Swish, SEPA and Autogiro. The unverified recollection is that Bankgiro with OCR references allows automatic payment matching, that Swedish payment infrastructure is migrating to ISO 20022 formats, that SEPA transfers and direct debits are euro-only, and that SEPA Instant became mandatory for euro-area providers during 2025.

## 8. VAT (moms) for a SaaS company selling in Sweden, the EU and beyond

### VAT rates: 25% is the general rate

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; secondary source for the rate list, primary for food]** Swedish VAT rates are 25% general, 12% (for example restaurant services) and 6% (for example passenger transport and books) ([Fortnox, belopp och procentsatser 2026, updated 7 Jan 2026](https://www.fortnox.se/fortnox-foretagsguide/skatter-och-moms/belopp-och-procentsatser)); food is at 6% from 1 April 2026 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)), stays there through 2027 and returns to 12% from 2028 under current law ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)). **[INFERENCE]** SaaS and other electronically supplied services fall under the 25% general rate because no reduced-rate category covers them; Skatteverket's rate page at [Skatteverket, momssatser och undantag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momssatserochundantagfranmoms.4.58d555751259e4d66168000409.html) was not opened.

### VAT return periods by turnover

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The reporting period depends on taxable turnover: up to SEK 1 million, annual, with monthly or quarterly optional; up to SEK 40 million, quarterly, with monthly optional; above SEK 40 million, monthly only ([Skatteverket, när ska jag deklarera moms](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html)).

### VAT return due dates

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The return is filed and payment booked on the tax account on the same day. Quarterly filers: the 12th of the second month after the quarter, 17th in August, which gives 12 May for Q1, 17 August for Q2, 12 November for Q3 and 12 February for Q4. Monthly filers with turnover up to SEK 40 million: the 12th of the second month after the period, 17th in January and August. Monthly filers above SEK 40 million: the 26th of the month after the period, 27th in December ([Skatteverket, när ska jag deklarera moms](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html)). **[INFERENCE]** Crossing SEK 40 million moves the VAT deadline from the 12th of the second month to the 26th of the following month, so the close calendar must change in the same month.

### Annual VAT returns

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Annual VAT for an aktiebolag with EU trade is due the 26th of the second month after the period; without EU trade it follows the income tax return, and the VAT page states for year-ends in September to December 12 July on paper and 17 August digitally, for May to June year-ends 27 December and 17 January, for July to August year-ends 12 March and 12 April, and for January to April year-ends 12 November and 12 December ([Skatteverket, när ska jag deklarera moms](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html)). These are annual VAT return dates and must not be confused with the income tax return dates in section 9.

### Late VAT return fee: two Skatteverket pages disagree

**[CONTESTED; checked on primary source, two pages]** Skatteverket's VAT deadlines page gives a late fee of SEK 625 for a late VAT return and says a filing extension must be applied for before the due date and does not itself extend payment ([Skatteverket, när ska jag deklarera moms](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html)). Skatteverket's 2026 amounts page lists a late fee in the category "other" of SEK 1,250, which the researchers assume, without confirmation, covers VAT and employer returns ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). One of the two pages is likely out of date or the fee was doubled; check before quoting either figure.

### Business customers in other EU countries: reverse charge and VIES

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** For services sold to a business in another EU country Swedish VAT is usually not charged and the buyer accounts for VAT at home. The seller must check that the buyer's VAT number is valid on the day of sale using the Commission's VIES service, and Skatteverket does not accept other checking services. VAT-free EU sales of goods and certain services must be reported in a periodic summary (periodisk sammanställning) ([Skatteverket, försäljning till andra EU-länder](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/forsaljningtillandraeulander.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80004737.html)).

### Invoice template for an EU business SaaS sale

**[INFERENCE]** The template is: no Swedish VAT, both VAT numbers, the words "Reverse charge", issue by the 15th of the month after the service period, the sale in box 39 and in the periodic summary, and a dated VIES validation record kept per customer ([Skatteverket, momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momslagensregleromfakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html); [Skatteverket, försäljning till andra EU-länder](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/forsaljningtillandraeulander.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80004737.html)).

### Consumers in other EU countries: the EUR 10,000 threshold

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The threshold is EUR 10,000, expressed as SEK 99,680 in Sweden, for the sum of telecom, broadcasting and electronic services plus intra-EU distance sales of goods to non-taxable persons in other EU countries; sales to Swedish buyers do not count. Swedish VAT applies up to the sale that breaches the threshold, and from that sale on the buyer country's VAT applies ([Skatteverket, omsättningströskel vid distansförsäljning inom EU](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/handelmedandralander/omsattningstroskelviddistansforsaljninginomeu.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80005545.html)). The Commission states the test on the current and preceding calendar year, excluding VAT ([European Commission, One Stop Shop portal](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en)).

### Opting in to destination VAT below the threshold

**[CONTESTED; checked on primary source, two sources]** A seller below the threshold may choose destination-country VAT. Skatteverket says such a decision applies until further notice ([Skatteverket, omsättningströskel vid distansförsäljning inom EU](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/handelmedandralander/omsattningstroskelviddistansforsaljninginomeu.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80005545.html)); the Commission's portal says the option binds for two calendar years ([European Commission, One Stop Shop portal](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en)). Treat two years as the minimum.

### Above the threshold: register locally or use the Union OSS scheme

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Above the threshold the seller must either register for VAT in each buyer country or use the Union scheme and report in OSS; applying for OSS is voluntary and possible on a mere intention to sell. The Union scheme is for EU-established sellers and covers cross-border consumer services and intra-EU distance sales; it requires a seat or fixed establishment in Sweden to use Sweden as the member state of identification. A third-country scheme exists for sellers with no EU establishment and an import scheme (IOSS) for consignments up to EUR 150 ([Skatteverket, OSS](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/mossredovisningavmomspadigitalatjanster.4.3aa8c78a1466c5845876a05.html)). Supplies in a member state where the supplier has an establishment go in that state's domestic return ([European Commission, One Stop Shop portal](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en)).

### OSS timing rules

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The OSS scheme applies from the first day of the calendar quarter after Skatteverket receives the application, or from the date of the first sale if the application is made by the 10th of the month after that sale. Changes must be reported by the 10th of the following month; to leave, notify at least 15 days before the end of the quarter; a final return is required on exit ([Skatteverket, OSS](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/mossredovisningavmomspadigitalatjanster.4.3aa8c78a1466c5845876a05.html)). OSS returns are quarterly and VAT is paid with the return to the member state of identification, which distributes it ([European Commission, One Stop Shop portal](https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en)).

### Monitoring rule for EU consumer sales

**[INFERENCE]** A Swedish SaaS company invoicing consumers in other EU countries should track the EUR 10,000 combined threshold continuously in the billing system; once it is passed, or by option, it charges the customer country's VAT rate and reports through OSS at Skatteverket instead of registering in each country ([Skatteverket, omsättningströskel vid distansförsäljning inom EU](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/handelmedandralander/omsattningstroskelviddistansforsaljninginomeu.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80005545.html); [Skatteverket, OSS](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/mossredovisningavmomspadigitalatjanster.4.3aa8c78a1466c5845876a05.html)).

### Other EU-trade thresholds and services

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source]** The purchase threshold in EU trade is SEK 90,000, and Skatteverket's e-service for refund of foreign EU VAT closes on 31 December 2026, with a replacement expected in spring 2027 ([Skatteverket, handel med andra länder](https://skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/sarskildamomsregler/handelmedandralander.4.361dc8c15312eff6fd1cf39.html)).

### E-invoicing today: mandatory only toward the public sector

**[FAST-MOVING, status 2026; secondary source only]** E-invoicing is mandatory in Sweden only for suppliers to the public sector, since 1 April 2019 in the EN 16931 format, and public buyers had to be reachable in Peppol from 1 December 2019 ([VATCalc on Swedish e-invoicing](https://www.vatcalc.com/sweden/sweden-progresses-mandatory-e-invoicing/); [Forvis Mazars on e-invoicing under ViDA](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyhetsbrev-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/implementering-av-e-fakturering-enligt-vida)). Skatteverket, Bolagsverket and Digg had earlier asked the government to investigate mandatory business-to-business e-invoicing based on EN 16931 and possibly Peppol, with a possible threshold to spare small businesses ([VATCalc on Swedish e-invoicing](https://www.vatcalc.com/sweden/sweden-progresses-mandatory-e-invoicing/)).

### ViDA: the EU timeline to 2035

**[FAST-MOVING, adopted 11 March 2025; checked on primary source]** The VAT in the Digital Age package was adopted on 11 March 2025 (Directive (EU) 2025/516, Regulation 2025/517, Implementing Regulation 2025/518), published 25 March 2025 and in force 14 April 2025. From 2025 member states may impose domestic mandatory e-invoicing under conditions; 1 January 2027 brings minor OSS and IOSS clarifications; 1 July 2028 brings platform deemed-supplier rules for short-term accommodation and passenger transport, single VAT registration reforms and mandatory reverse charge for non-identified suppliers; 1 July 2030 brings e-invoice-based digital reporting for cross-border business transactions; and by 1 January 2035 national real-time reporting systems must align with the EU model ([European Commission, VAT in the Digital Age](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en)). The 2028 platform rules do not target SaaS vendors as such ([European Commission, VAT in the Digital Age](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en)).

### ViDA start date: use the Commission's 1 July 2030

**[CONTESTED]** One vendor page says EU cross-border e-invoicing starts in 2028 ([VATCalc on Swedish e-invoicing](https://www.vatcalc.com/sweden/sweden-progresses-mandatory-e-invoicing/)); the Commission's own page gives 1 July 2030 ([European Commission, VAT in the Digital Age](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en)). Use the Commission date. One audit-firm page also names a Dutch system as a Swedish entry point, which looks like an error and is not used here.

### Sweden's ViDA inquiry reports in November 2027

**[FAST-MOVING, March 2026; secondary source only]** A government inquiry was set up in March 2026 under directive 2026:9 to work out the law changes ViDA needs; it reports in November 2027 ([Företagarna, 14 April 2026](https://www.foretagarna.se/nyheter/riks/2026/april/kommer-pappersfakturan-forbjudas/)). **[INFERENCE]** Sweden has no domestic business-to-business e-invoicing mandate in November 2026 and none can realistically start before the inquiry reports; being able to send and receive Peppol BIS and EN 16931 invoices is sensible preparation for 2030.

### Proposed VAT change for mixed activities

**[FAST-MOVING, proposed for 1 Jan 2027, not enacted; secondary source only]** A new statutory method for splitting input VAT on shared costs in mixed activities, by turnover, was at referral stage for 1 January 2027 ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)). It depends on the 2027 budget process and should not be loaded as a rule.

## 9. Corporate tax, owner taxation and R&D incentives

### Corporate income tax is 20.6%

**[FAST-MOVING, income year 2026; checked on primary source]** The corporate income tax rate for legal entities is 20.6% for income year 2026 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). ### Income tax return deadlines by financial year-end

Skatteverket's page shows one date per year-end group and does not distinguish paper from electronic filing ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)). ### Late fee for the company income tax return

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** The late fee for the company income tax return is SEK 6,250, and up to three fees can be charged depending on the delay; non-filing can lead to an estimated assessment, a tax surcharge and loss of F-tax approval ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)).

### Extra payments to avoid interest

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source]** The last dates for an extra payment to the tax account to avoid cost interest are, for amounts over SEK 30,000 and up to SEK 30,000 respectively: year-ends January to April 2026, 12 June 2026 and 3 September 2026; May to June 2026, 12 August 2026 and 3 November 2026; July to August 2026, 12 October 2026 and 4 January 2027; September to December 2026, 12 February 2027 and 3 May 2027 ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)). ### Final tax notices and residual tax

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source]** For year-ends September to December 2025 the final tax notice comes on 7 to 9 December 2026 digitally and residual tax must be on the tax account by 12 March 2027; for year-ends January to April 2026 the residual tax date is 12 July 2027; for May to June 2026 it is 13 September 2027. Roughly 90 days are given after a decision ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)).

### Periodiseringsfond: defer up to 25% of profit for six years

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026 rate; checked on primary source]** A legal entity may allocate up to 25% to a periodiseringsfond (30% for individuals). Notional income equals the government borrowing rate (2.55% at 30 November 2025) times the fund balance at the start of the tax year, with a floor of 0.50% ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). Each allocation must be reversed to taxation no later than the sixth year after the allocation year, earlier reversal is allowed, and for tax years ending in 2025 the notional rate was 1.96% ([Skatteverket brochure SKV 294, edition 24](https://www.skatteverket.se/download/18.6491ed82199c4722daef0c/1761209691103/skatteregler-for-aktie-och-handelsbolag-skv294-utgava24.pdf)). **[INFERENCE]** For a tax year ending in 2026 the notional rate should be 2.55%, following the rule as described; the pages read do not state this in so many words.

### Tax losses carry forward without time limit

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Tax losses are carried forward to the next year's return with no time limit as long as the company exists; after an ownership change an amount cap (beloppsspärr) or a group-contribution block (koncernbidragsspärr) can restrict their use ([Skatteverket brochure SKV 294, edition 24](https://www.skatteverket.se/download/18.6491ed82199c4722daef0c/1761209691103/skatteregler-for-aktie-och-handelsbolag-skv294-utgava24.pdf)). **[GAP; unverified background]** The cap formula (commonly cited as 200% of acquisition cost) and the five-year block were not verified, which matters before any funding round or sale that changes control.

### Group contributions and dividends between companies

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Group contributions are deductible for the giver and taxable for the recipient and must be reported openly in both returns for tax years with the same filing date. Dividends received on business-related shares (näringsbetingade andelar) are tax-free for an aktiebolag, listed shares held under one year excepted. A company paying dividends to individuals must file a control statement, and only CSD-registered companies withhold 30% tax, which closely held companies normally are not ([Skatteverket brochure SKV 294, edition 24](https://www.skatteverket.se/download/18.6491ed82199c4722daef0c/1761209691103/skatteregler-for-aktie-och-handelsbolag-skv294-utgava24.pdf)). **[GAP; unverified background]** The more-than-90% ownership test for group contributions and withholding tax on dividends to foreign shareholders were not verified.

### Base amounts for 2026 and 2027

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027; checked on primary source for 2026, secondary source only for 2027]** For 2026 the price base amount (prisbasbelopp) is SEK 59,200, the raised price base amount SEK 60,500 and the income base amount (inkomstbasbelopp) SEK 83,400; the state income tax threshold is SEK 643,000 at 20% and the breakpoint SEK 660,400 for people under 66 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)). **[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Deductible representation excluding VAT in 2026 is SEK 0 for meals, SEK 60 for simple refreshments, SEK 180 for events such as theatre or a staff party, and SEK 300 for representation and advertising gifts ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)).

### 3:12 rules for owner-managers: reformed from income year 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, in force 1 Jan 2026; secondary source only]** The Riksdag adopted new 3:12 rules for closely held companies on 26 November 2025; they entered into force on 1 January 2026 and first apply to tax years starting after 31 December 2025 ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna)). All 3:12 figures in this document rest on audit-firm and reference-site summaries; Skatteverket's own page was not reached.

### 3:12 grundbelopp: SEK 322,400 for 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, income year 2026; secondary source only]** The grundbelopp is four income base amounts, stated as SEK 322,400 for 2026, and replaces the old simplification rule. An owner of shares in several closely held companies gets one grundbelopp in total, split in proportion to holdings, and within a company it is split across the shares ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna)). **[INFERENCE]** SEK 322,400 equals four times SEK 80,600, which is evidently the prior-year (2025) income base amount, since Skatteverket lists the 2026 income base amount as SEK 83,400 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)); an earlier research inference that the 2026 income base amount was SEK 80,600 was wrong and is not used.

### 3:12 wage-based allowance and its SEK 644,800 deduction

**[FAST-MOVING, income year 2026; secondary source only]** The wage-based allowance is 50% of the wage base allocated to the owner's shares, after a standard deduction of eight income base amounts (SEK 644,800 for 2026) per owner; it is capped at 50 times the owner's or a close relative's own salary, and spouses are calculated jointly. The owner's own minimum-salary requirement and the 4% capital-share requirement are abolished, so every owner of qualified shares can use the wage-based allowance ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna); [Ekonomifakta, 3:12-reglerna, updated 8 Jan 2026](https://www.ekonomifakta.se/sakomraden/skatt/skatt-pa-foretagande-och-kapital/312-reglerna_1210959.html)). **[INFERENCE]** An owner-managed SaaS company needs total group cash wages above SEK 644,800 per owner before any wage-based allowance arises; below that only the grundbelopp gives 20%-taxed dividend room.

### Other 3:12 changes

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026-2029; secondary source only]** Saved dividend allowance is no longer uplifted with interest, and an interest-based addition is given only on the part of the shares' cost base above SEK 100,000. The waiting period for shares to stop being qualified is cut from five to four years, first for owners whose period began in 2023 or later, and one source lists the four-year periods as applying from income year 2027. The 2026 limit amount, built on 2025 wages, is computed under the new rules and reported on form K10 in spring 2027. Wages in a company owned through an alternative investment fund are excluded from the wage base, the special subsidiary definition is replaced by the Companies Act definition, and old index and capital-base rules for pre-1990 and pre-1992 shares end on 1 January 2029 ([Forvis Mazars, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.forvismazars.com/se/en/insights/nyheter-artiklar/forvis-mazars-newsletter/riksdagen-har-beslutat-de-nya-3-12-reglerna); [Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)).

This is arithmetic on an assumed lag convention that was not read. The ceiling amounts for employment-income taxation and the interest rate on the cost base above SEK 100,000 were not found.

### R&D deduction on employer contributions: the current rule

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Under the current rule (lag 2023:747 as described in a government bill draft of 13 August 2026) employer contributions and the general payroll fee are reduced by 20% of the contribution base for staff working with R&D; the contributions may not fall below the old-age pension fee of 10.21%; the cap is SEK 3 million per calendar month per group; and the employee must have worked with R&D at least half of working time and at least 15 hours in the month ([regeringen.se, lagrådsremiss on a new R&D tax incentive, 13 Aug 2026](https://regeringen.se/contentassets/a57a22d3f04c4a10b86509f88c90b529/ett-nytt-skatteincitament-for-forskning-och-utveckling.pdf)). Skatteverket's rate table lists the deduction as minus 20% for 2026 ([Skatteverket, arbetsgivaravgifter](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/arbetsgivaravgifter.4.233f91f71260075abe8800020817.html)).

### R&D deduction: a monthly payroll control

**[INFERENCE]** The R&D deduction is claimed monthly in the employer return and depends on each employee's share of working time, so time-allocation evidence per employee per month is a payroll control and not a year-end exercise. For a SaaS company with qualifying developers the saving is material, which justifies a monthly sign-off by the engineering lead.

### R&D proposals for 2027 are not law

## 10. Payroll, employer contributions, holiday pay and employee equity

### Employer contributions are 31.42% in 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Standard employer contributions in 2026 are 31.42% for employees born 1959 or later, made up of sickness insurance 3.55%, parental insurance 2.00%, old-age pension 10.21%, survivor's pension 0.30%, labour-market fee 2.64%, work-injury fee 0.10% and general payroll fee 12.62%. The total is unchanged from 2020 to 2025 but the split changed, with the general payroll fee up from 11.62%. For employees born 1938 to 1958 the rate is 10.21% (old-age pension fee only) and for those born 1937 or earlier 0%. Contributions are due once total pay to a person reaches SEK 1,000 in a year ([Skatteverket, arbetsgivaravgifter](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/arbetsgivaravgifter.4.233f91f71260075abe8800020817.html)).

### Youth reduction from 1 April 2026 to 30 September 2027

**[FAST-MOVING, 1 Apr 2026 to 30 Sep 2027; checked on primary source]** For employees born 2003 to 2007 the rate is 20.81% on pay up to SEK 25,000 per calendar month (the old-age pension fee plus half of the other fees) and the full 31.42% on pay above that, for pay from 1 April 2026 through 30 September 2027 ([Skatteverket, arbetsgivaravgifter](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/arbetsgivaravgifter.4.233f91f71260075abe8800020817.html); [Skatteverket news, lower employer contributions for young people, 2026](https://skatteverket.se/omoss/pressochmedia/nyheter/2026/nyheter/lagrearbetsgivaravgifterforungdomar.5.70685bee19c85dd5dd02b10.html)). The reduction ends for pay after 30 September 2027 unless a new decision is taken ([Spiris, skattenyheter, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.spiris.se/blogg/skatter/skattenyheter)). **[INFERENCE]** Payroll systems needed a rule from April 2026 pay that splits monthly pay at SEK 25,000 for those birth years, and need another change after September 2027.

### Special payroll tax on pension costs and other rates

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Special payroll tax on pension costs is 24.26% and is reported in the income tax return. The rate table lists a regional support deduction of minus 10%. Foreign employers without a fixed establishment in Sweden pay 18.80% (born 1959 or later), 10.21% (born 1938 to 1958) and 14.50% (born 2003 to 2007 from 1 April 2026). Posted-worker rates vary by social-security agreement, for example 24.39% for a Swedish employer posting staff to the USA, Canada, India, South Korea or Japan ([Skatteverket, arbetsgivaravgifter](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/arbetsgivaravgifter.4.233f91f71260075abe8800020817.html)). The employee's general pension fee is 7%, at most SEK 47,100 ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)).

### Växa-stöd still exists, as a refund since 1 January 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Växa-stöd is still available in 2026 with no end date stated. From 1 January 2026 the employer pays full contributions and must apply for a refund; an approved refund is credited to the tax account no earlier than the filing deadline for that period's employer return. Only the old-age pension fee of 10.21% remains on pay up to the cap, and the rest (21.21 points of the 31.42%) is refunded. The cap is SEK 35,000 a month for employment started after 30 April 2024 and SEK 25,000 for earlier hires. It covers the first and second employee, for up to 24 consecutive calendar months each ([Skatteverket, regler för växa-stöd](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/vaxastod.4.361dc8c15312eff6fd37447.html)).

### Växa-stöd conditions and state-aid limit

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** The employer must be a sole trader, aktiebolag or partnership with no employee, or at most one, since 1 January 2024, ignoring people paid SEK 5,000 or less in total. Employment must be for at least three months and 20 hours a week; the person must not have worked for the employer or a related party in the current or three preceding years; no support is given for partners, managers or their relatives in an aktiebolag; only one business per owner qualifies; and companies under common control count as one. It is de minimis aid: total de minimis aid may not exceed EUR 300,000 over three years, the application must list other de minimis aid and the NACE code, and aid is entered in a central register ([Skatteverket, regler för växa-stöd](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/vaxastod.4.361dc8c15312eff6fd37447.html)).

### Växa-stöd is a cash-flow item

**[INFERENCE]** Because full contributions go out with the monthly return and the refund returns later, a first-hire budget shows 31.42% paid and a receivable of 21.21% of capped pay. The switch from a reduced rate to a refund explains why växa-stöd no longer appears in Skatteverket's 2026 rate table.

### Employer return (AGI): monthly, with nil returns

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** The employer return is filed the month after pay is paid, a nil return is required for months without pay, and per-employee absence data is reported with it and forwarded to Försäkringskassan. With turnover of SEK 40 million or less, contributions and withheld tax are paid by the last filing date; above SEK 40 million, payment is due on the 12th (17th in January) while the return is normally filed on the 26th; a date on a weekend or holiday moves to the next weekday. Corrections are made in the month concerned, and withheld tax can be raised but normally not lowered after reporting ([Skatteverket, arbetsgivare](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare)). **[GAP; unverified background]** The exact filing date for employers at or below SEK 40 million (commonly cited as the 12th, 17th in January and August), the withholding mechanics by municipal tax table and the 30% rate on side income were not on the page read; the late fee is subject to the SEK 625 versus SEK 1,250 conflict in section 8.

### Holiday entitlement under Semesterlagen

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Semesterlagen gives 25 days of annual leave per holiday year (4 §), or 5 days if employment starts after 31 August. The holiday year runs 1 April to 31 March and the earning year is the preceding twelve months (3 §). Paid days equal employment days in the earning year less unpaid absence, divided by days in the year, times 25, rounded up (7 §). Days above 20 may be saved for up to five years (18 §); at least four consecutive weeks fall in June to August unless agreed otherwise (12 §); sickness counts as holiday-qualifying up to 180 days a year (17 §); and on termination holiday compensation is paid without undue delay and at the latest one month after employment ends (28 to 30 §§) ([Semesterlag (1977:480), riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/semesterlag-1977480_sfs-1977-480/)).

### Holiday pay: the same-pay rule and the percentage rule

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Under the same-pay rule (16 a §) holiday pay is current salary plus a supplement per paid day of 0.43% of monthly salary (1.82% of weekly pay), plus 12% on variable pay. Under the percentage rule (16 b §) it is 12% of pay earned in the earning year ([Semesterlag (1977:480), riksdagen.se](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/semesterlag-1977480_sfs-1977-480/)). **[INFERENCE]** The month-end accrual per salaried employee under the same-pay rule is unused days times daily salary plus 0.43% of monthly salary, plus employer contributions; under the percentage rule it is 12% of earned pay plus contributions. Holiday-pay and bonus accruals carry 31.42% employer contributions or the individual's reduced rate, and pension premiums carry 24.26% special payroll tax.

### Allowances, mileage, gifts and meal benefits for 2026

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026; checked on primary source]** Tax-free domestic traktamente in 2026 is SEK 300 for a full day, SEK 150 for a half day, SEK 210 a day after three months, and SEK 150 night allowance. Mileage is SEK 25 per 10 km for an own car, SEK 12 for a company car including plug-in hybrids and SEK 9.50 for a fully electric company car. Tax-free gifts to employees including VAT are SEK 600 for a Christmas gift, SEK 1,800 for an anniversary gift and SEK 15,000 for a memorial gift. Meal benefit values are SEK 62 for breakfast, SEK 124 for lunch or dinner and SEK 310 a day for full board ([Skatteverket, belopp och procent 2026](https://www.skatteverket.se/privat/skatter/beloppochprocent/2026)).

### Qualified employee stock options: company conditions

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** Under Inkomstskattelagen chapter 11 a, after the change at the 2021/2022 year-end, the company must have fewer than 150 employees and group net turnover or balance sheet total of at most SEK 280 million; not be listed on a regulated market; have public bodies controlling at most 25%; have a business not older than ten years; not carry on excluded activities such as banking and finance, insurance, coal and steel, trading in property, commodities or financial instruments, long-term letting, or legal, accounting or audit services; not be obliged to prepare a control balance sheet, in reorganisation or insolvent; and have total options outstanding of at most SEK 75 million ([FAR Online, Rätt Skatt: kvalificerade personaloptioner](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/ratt-skatt/k/rs_kvalificeradepersonaloptioner/)).

### Qualified employee stock options: holder conditions and tax

**[DURABLE; secondary source only]** Options are exercised no earlier than three and no later than ten years after grant; a holder may receive at most SEK 3 million of options at grant; an employee must work at least 30 hours a week and earn at least 13 income base amounts during the vesting period; board members and deputies qualify with pay of at least 1.5 income base amounts; and the holder with related parties owns at most 5%. There is no tax at grant or exercise, the gain is taxed as capital income at sale, no employer contributions arise, and the company gets no deduction for the cost. Skatteverket's view is that warrants cannot themselves be qualified options, though an option may be used to acquire a warrant (HFD 2020 ref. 39) or a share in another group company. The rules originally entered into force on 1 January 2018 ([FAR Online, Rätt Skatt: kvalificerade personaloptioner](https://www.faronline.se/dokument/rattserien/ratt-skatt/k/rs_kvalificeradepersonaloptioner/)).

Granting options also has an accounting consequence: the updated K2 excludes companies with share-based compensation, subject to the relief rule in section 4 ([KPMG on BFN's K2 and K3 changes decided 16 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/se/en/insights/newsletters/finansiell-rapportering/bokforingsnamndens-nyheter-for-k2-och-k3-regelverken.html); [BDO, Inför bokslutet 2025](https://www.bdo.se/getmedia/788f5945-3a3a-44b8-afd8-abf5a2841a5a/Infor-bokslutet-2025-Nyheter-inom-redovisning-och-finansiell-rapportering-ver-1-0.pdf)). **[GAP]** FAR's page indicates that an upcoming wording of chapter 11 a exists without detail, so a pending amendment cannot be ruled out; Skatteverket's own page and the reporting duties were not read.

## 12. Planning, budgeting and forecasting

### Keep the annual budget and add a rolling forecast

**[DURABLE; practitioner opinion, 2018-2019]** Practitioner guidance says rolling forecasts supplement annual budgets and do not replace them: the budget still translates strategy into numbers and remains the performance yardstick ([AFP, Carl Seidman, 20 Sept 2019](https://www.financialprofessionals.org/training-resources/resources/articles/Details/8-steps-for-creating-a-rolling-forecast)). The horizon is four to six quarters (12 to 18 months), matched to the business cycle, with a re-forecast monthly or quarterly; one author found that adding new periods at quarter-end worked better than monthly because preparers resisted monthly extensions ([FP&A Trends, Elena Kiristova, 18 April 2018](https://fpa-trends.com/article/best-practices-rolling-forecasts)). These sources are practitioner opinion from 2015 to 2019 and give no adoption or accuracy statistics.

### AFP's eight steps for building a rolling forecast

**[DURABLE; practitioner opinion, 20 Sept 2019]** The eight steps are: define the purpose and users; set the horizon and period length, accepting that longer horizons give insight at the cost of accuracy and that there is no rule of thumb; set the level of detail by the cost of a wrong decision; choose accountable, unbiased contributors; identify the few value drivers instead of every ledger account; vet data sources; build scenarios and sensitivities; and track actuals against forecast, explaining what happened, why, and what to do ([AFP, Carl Seidman, 20 Sept 2019](https://www.financialprofessionals.org/training-resources/resources/articles/Details/8-steps-for-creating-a-rolling-forecast)). The fuller AFP guide sits behind a registration wall and was not read ([AFP guide page](https://www.financialprofessionals.org/training-resources/resources/guides/fp-a-guides/Detail/rolling-forecast)).

### Keep forecasts and targets separate

**[DURABLE; practitioner opinion, 18 April 2018]** Forecasts and targets should be independent, because merging them invites sandbagging. Capital and strategic projects are planned separately. Accuracy is reviewed through post-mortems aimed at learning and not blame. The completed rolling forecast is the baseline for scenario analysis. Spreadsheets are described as a personal productivity tool that weakens once versions, scenarios and variance analysis multiply ([FP&A Trends, Elena Kiristova, 18 April 2018](https://fpa-trends.com/article/best-practices-rolling-forecasts)).

### Driver-based modelling practices

**[DURABLE; practitioner opinion, 17 Dec 2015]** Practices drawn from AFP interviews: focus on line items that are significant, volatile and hard to forecast; keep models simple; agree drivers with operations and validate relationships across functions; back-test continuously; hold clean, defined data in one place; run the model as a closed loop inside the planning process and not as a side model; and do not simply trend history ([AFP, Nilly Essaides, 17 Dec 2015](https://www.financialprofessionals.org/ideas-inspiration/topics/articles/Details/best-practices-in-driver-based-modeling)).

### Working design for a small SaaS company

**[INFERENCE]** Combining the three practitioner sources gives a working design: an annual budget fixed as the yardstick; a driver-based rolling forecast of 12 to 18 months refreshed monthly, with a new quarter added each quarter-end; drivers limited to the handful that move ARR and cash (new ARR, churn, expansion, price, headcount, collections timing); a monthly actual-versus-forecast review with written causes and actions; and a logged accuracy record per driver.

### Plans run above trailing actuals, so keep a separate base case

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025]** In the Benchmarkit sample the median planned growth for 2025 was 35% against a 2024 actual median of 26% (planned N=71, actual N=149) ([Benchmarkit 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics Benchmarks, CY-2024 data (PDF)](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)), and KeyBanc and Sapphire respondents projected ARR growth rising from 15% in 2024 to 20% in 2025 ([Sapphire Ventures, 2025 KeyBanc and Sapphire SaaS Survey page](https://info.sapphireventures.com/2025-keybanc-capital-markets-sapphire-ventures-saas-survey)). **[INFERENCE]** Company plans in these samples sit above trailing actuals, so a forecasting agent should treat management plans as optimistic by default and keep a separate base case anchored on trailing run-rate.

### AI cost lines are hard to forecast

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Q2 2026]** In ICONIQ's survey of roughly 300 executives building AI products, 92% said the true cost of internal AI is hard to predict, and token spend was the top source of budget overruns; one workflow budgeted at $0.10 per run exceeded $1.50 once agents retried ([ICONIQ, 2026 State of AI, July 2026](https://www.iconiq.com/growth/insights/state-of-ai-2026); [SaaStr summary of ICONIQ 2026](https://www.saastr.com/the-builders-economy-10-metrics-from-iconiqs-newest-2026-state-of-ai-report)). Forecast any AI or inference cost line from usage drivers with an explicit retry and failure assumption, and review it monthly.

## 13. SaaS metrics: formulas, mistakes and 2025 to 2026 benchmarks

### State the definition next to every metric

**[INFERENCE]** Formula definitions differ between sources, so every reported metric carries its definition, period and source. The mistakes the sources imply are: mixing bookings, billings and revenue; counting one-off fees in ARR; reporting NRR without GRR; presenting blended CAC as paid CAC; computing payback on revenue instead of gross margin; building LTV from a churn rate measured on a young, small cohort; and ignoring inference and token costs in COGS when claiming a SaaS-like gross margin.

### ARR and what it excludes

**[DURABLE]** ARR equals MRR times 12 in ChartMogul's glossary, which calls ARR "annual run rate" ([ChartMogul SaaS Retention Report, Nov 2023, 2022 data](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-report/)). ARR counts recurring revenue only and excludes one-time and professional-service fees; common errors are including non-recurring fees and using bookings in place of recurring revenue ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)).

### NRR, GRR and churn

**[DURABLE]** Net revenue retention is current-period recurring revenue from customers who were customers 12 months earlier, divided by total recurring revenue 12 months earlier, and includes upsell, cross-sell and price increases. Gross revenue retention uses the same formula excluding upsell, cross-sell and price increases, so it cannot exceed 100%. Churn is approximately one minus GRR, and retention should be measured by following a cohort ([SaaS Capital, retention benchmarks, 18 Sept 2025](https://www.saas-capital.com/research/saas-retention-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-companies/)). Monthly logo churn is customers lost divided by prior-month customers; gross MRR churn is MRR lost divided by opening MRR; net churn subtracts upsell MRR, and relying on net churn alone understates losses ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)). **[INFERENCE]** Because price increases inflate NRR and not GRR, report both, and show NRR with and without the price effect in any period where a price rise lands.

### CAC ratios, payback and related efficiency formulas

**[DURABLE; VENDOR definitions]** Benchmarkit's definitions are: blended CAC ratio equals total sales and marketing divided by new-customer ARR plus expansion ARR; new CAC ratio equals total sales and marketing divided by new-customer ARR; expansion CAC ratio equals sales and marketing plus customer-success cost allocated to expansion, divided by expansion ARR; CAC payback in months equals sales and marketing divided by new-customer ARR times subscription gross margin, times 12; CLTV:CAC equals average ARR per account times gross margin divided by churn rate, divided by CAC per new customer; magic number equals current-quarter revenue minus prior-quarter revenue, divided by prior-quarter sales and marketing; burn multiple equals net burn divided by net new ARR over the last twelve months; ARR per FTE equals period-end ARR divided by period-end employees; ARR to capital equals period-end ARR divided by lifetime capital raised; and expansion ARR share equals expansion ARR divided by new plus expansion ARR ([Benchmarkit 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics Benchmarks, CY-2024 data (PDF)](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)). A magic number of 0.75 is described as the low-water mark and above 1.0 as ideal ([Benchmarkit 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics Benchmarks, CY-2024 data (PDF)](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)).

### LTV and CAC as a16z defines them

**[DURABLE]** In a16z's definition LTV equals monthly contribution margin times one divided by monthly churn; the stated common error is using revenue or gross margin where contribution belongs, and with little history a company should measure realised 12-month and 24-month LTV instead of projecting a lifespan. Blended CAC across all new customers, including organic, hides whether paid acquisition is profitable, so paid CAC is shown separately by channel, and CAC includes referral fees, credits and discounts ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)).

### Burn, runway and the burn multiple

**[DURABLE]** Gross burn is monthly cash outlays, net burn is gross burn less incoming cash, and investors use net burn for runway ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)). The burn multiple is net burn divided by net new ARR; its originator's text reference points are that about 2x is reasonable for an early-stage company, 3x or more signals heavy spend for the growth achieved, and 5x is poor. **[FAST-MOVING, April 2020]** Those thresholds were published in April 2020 and may be dated, and the full threshold table is an image that was not readable ([David Sacks, The Burn Multiple, April 2020](https://sacks.substack.com/p/the-burn-multiple-51a7e43cb200)). Benchmarkit reports a burn multiple target below 1.0 at $25 to 50M ARR (N=130) ([Benchmarkit 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics Benchmarks, CY-2024 data (PDF)](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)).

### Rule of 40

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** The Rule of 40 is commonly computed as revenue or ARR growth percentage plus a profit margin percentage (EBITDA or free cash flow, stated explicitly). No page read defines it; the benchmark sources use it without a formula. State the margin measure whenever it is reported.

## 14. Cash, working capital, runway, treasury and currency

### Cash and revenue are tracked as separate truths

**[DURABLE]** Cash collected up front on a subscription is a liability released to revenue as service is delivered ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)), and runway is judged on net burn, which is gross burn less cash received ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)). A finance department therefore keeps two ledgers of truth in every report: what was earned and what was collected.

### Annual prepayment as a working-capital lever, with its financing cost

**[INFERENCE] [HOUSE STYLE]** Annual-upfront billing is the main working-capital lever in SaaS and the direct equivalent of Hormozi's prepay offer, for which he suggests at least 10% off ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). By the researchers' own calculation, comparing one upfront payment with twelve equal monthly payments in advance, a 10% discount costs about 2.0% a month (about 26% annualised), 15% about 3.1% a month (about 44%) and 20% about 4.3% a month (about 67%). Approve a prepay discount when the annual cohort's better retention, lower collection cost or fewer failed payments justify that cost against the company's actual cost of capital. **[GAP]** No sourced SaaS norm for annual-prepay discounts and no sourced annual-versus-monthly retention data was found.

### Grants need bridging cash

**[DURABLE; PLATFORM CLAIM]** Vinnova states that funded costs must appear in the organisation's accounts during the project period and that it normally does not fund all costs ([Vinnova, Apply for funding](https://www.vinnova.se/en/apply-for-funding/)). Grant projects therefore need bridging cash, and a grant never enters the base-case cash forecast until it is awarded.

### Växa-stöd, extra tax payments and VAT as cash-timing items

**[INFERENCE]** Three Swedish items belong in every cash forecast: the växa-stöd refund, which arrives after full contributions have been paid ([Skatteverket, regler för växa-stöd](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/arbetsgivaravgifterochskatteavdrag/vaxastod.4.361dc8c15312eff6fd37447.html)); the extra tax payment dates of 12 February and 3 May 2027 for December 2026 year-ends ([Skatteverket, deklarera åt ett aktiebolag](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/inkomstdeklaration/juridiskapersoner.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)); and the VAT and employer-return payment dates in section 11.

### The 13-week cash forecast

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** No sourced template or method for the 13-week cash-flow forecast was obtained. Ordinary practice as the researchers recall it is a direct-method forecast with weekly columns: opening bank balance; receipts by source (subscription collections by ageing bucket, card and processor settlements, grants, VAT refunds); disbursements (payroll with Swedish employer contributions and preliminary tax, VAT, suppliers, cloud and inference, rent, debt service); and a closing balance against a minimum-cash threshold, rolled forward every week with a forecast-versus-actual line and a reconciliation to bank.

### Runway and the "default alive" view

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** Runway in months is commonly computed as cash divided by average net burn, shown on trailing three-month burn and on the forecast, alongside a view of whether forecast revenue growth reaches breakeven before cash runs out. The burn definitions are sourced ([a16z, 16 Startup Metrics](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/)); the presentation convention is not.

### Working-capital levers and collections

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** Unsourced ordinary practice lists the SaaS working-capital levers as annual upfront billing, shorter payment terms, automated dunning for card failures, invoice accuracy, tracking of days sales outstanding by segment, and supplier terms. No sourced norms for days sales outstanding or dunning recovery were found; the verified Swedish late-payment rules are in section 7.

### Treasury policy for a small company

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** No treasury-policy source was opened. The unsourced outline is: objectives ranked safety, liquidity, then yield; permitted instruments and maximum tenor; counterparty limits and bank diversification; minimum operating cash; dual approval for transfers; and no speculative positions. The Swedish deposit-guarantee amount and whether it protects a company's balance were not verified and must be checked at Riksgälden.

### Currency exposure for a Swedish company billing in EUR, USD and SEK

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** No currency-hedging guidance or 2026 SEK volatility data was sourced. The unsourced outline is: map exposure by currency across revenue, costs, cash and receivables; use natural hedging first by paying same-currency costs from same-currency receipts and holding currency accounts; consider forwards only for committed, material exposures under a written policy; and report ARR at constant currency alongside reported currency so that currency moves are not mistaken for growth or churn. The one verified currency rule is the SEK VAT amount on foreign-currency invoices in section 7.

## 15. Pricing and packaging from a finance view

### AI builders now blend pricing models

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Q2 2026]** Among AI builders surveyed by ICONIQ in Q2 2026, 57% use subscription pricing, 42% consumption-based (up from 35% six months earlier) and 23% outcome-based (up from 18%), with 1.7 pricing models blended on average (up from 1.5). Among companies using consumption pricing, 15% absorb 100% of inference cost and 84% pass at least part of it to customers ([SaaStr summary of ICONIQ 2026](https://www.saastr.com/the-builders-economy-10-metrics-from-iconiqs-newest-2026-state-of-ai-report); [ICONIQ, 2026 State of AI, July 2026](https://www.iconiq.com/growth/insights/state-of-ai-2026)).

### Bessemer's taxonomy of AI pricing models

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Feb 2026]** Bessemer distinguishes seat-based pricing; consumption pricing per API call or token, with predictable margin but hard budgeting for non-technical buyers; workflow-based pricing per completed task; outcome-based pricing per successful result, where the vendor carries cost variability; and hybrid pricing with a base subscription plus usage or outcome tiers, which it recommends under uncertainty ([Bessemer, AI pricing and monetization playbook, Feb 2026](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-ai-pricing-and-monetization-playbook)).

### Bessemer's hybrid construction

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Feb 2026]** Bessemer's suggested hybrid is a platform fee of about twice the calculated delivery cost plus outcome credits, with the example of a $12k annual fee including 100 resolutions and then $5k per additional 100. It names prepaid credits, usage caps and tiers as tools for balancing variable compute cost against predictable revenue, warns that unit economics which fail at 10 customers will also fail at 1,000, and says reliable AI pricing benchmarks are scarce. Its named examples are Intercom Fin at $0.99 per resolution and Leena AI per auto-closed ticket with minimums ([Bessemer, AI pricing and monetization playbook, Feb 2026](https://www.bvp.com/atlas/the-ai-pricing-and-monetization-playbook)).

### Pricing model and performance: correlation only

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, CY-2024 data]** In Benchmarkit's sample, usage-based companies grew a median 44% against 25% for subscription, usage-based GRR was 92% against 88%, and hybrid NRR was 110% against 101% overall, on samples of 149 to 228 ([Benchmarkit 2025 SaaS Performance Metrics Benchmarks, CY-2024 data (PDF)](https://www.hibob.com/wp-content/uploads/2025-SaaS-Performance-Metrics-Benchmarks.pdf)). High Alpha reports that a common packaging approach is AI credits bundled into subscription plans, with customers expected to buy more as they see a return ([High Alpha webinar recap, late 2025](https://www.highalpha.com/blog/webinar-recap-unpacking-the-saas-benchmarks-report-2025)). These are correlations in self-selected samples and do not show that changing the pricing model causes the difference.

### Finance checklist for an AI or credit-priced plan

**[INFERENCE]** Before approving any AI or credit-priced plan, finance checks: cost per unit of the value metric, including retries and failed runs (the $0.10 to $1.50 overrun example comes from [ICONIQ, 2026 State of AI, July 2026](https://www.iconiq.com/growth/insights/state-of-ai-2026)); gross margin at median and heavy-usage customers; a cap, overage price or fair-use limit on anything described as unlimited; the credit expiry and breakage policy; and how unused prepaid credits are recognised, which is a contract liability until used or expired, to be confirmed with the auditor.

### Forecast usage revenue separately

**[INFERENCE]** Usage-based revenue is harder to forecast than seat subscriptions. Forecast it from usage drivers by cohort and present committed revenue (minimums and platform fees) separately from variable revenue in ARR reporting.

### Price increases: where house style and evidence meet

**[CONTESTED] [HOUSE STYLE] [VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, April 2026]** The house style says to raise price before lowering it and reads a high close rate as a sign of underpricing, with an explicit exception for scalable software ([Rosetta transcript, "The Mathematics of Business, Explained", video dated 9 Jan 2026](https://rosetta.to/u/alexhormozi/the-mathematics-of-business-explained)). The Nordic evidence is an average annual increase of 5.9% in 2025 ([Monterro Nordic B2B Software Benchmark 2026, 19 April 2026 (PDF)](https://www.monterro.com/media/2finidot/monterro-nordic-software-benchmark-report-2026_web.pdf)), and price increases count inside NRR and outside GRR ([SaaS Capital, retention benchmarks, 18 Sept 2025](https://www.saas-capital.com/research/saas-retention-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-companies/)). The department's default, by inference, is an annual price review, increases tested on new customers first, installed-base increases sized against the peer range, and GRR watched for three to six months after each change.

### Discount governance and value-metric selection

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** No source was opened on discount norms or governance. The unsourced outline is a discount-approval matrix by discount size and deal value; discounts traded for something (term, prepay, volume, case study); time limits written into the order form; average discount and the share of deals above threshold tracked as KPIs; and a quarterly review of list-price integrity. Unsourced value-metric criteria are that the metric scales with customer value, is easy to understand and measure, is predictable for the buyer and aligns with cost to serve.

### Gaps on pricing

**[GAP]** Not found: data on the share of companies changing pricing and typical price-increase size from pricing specialists; sourced outcomes of price increases in B2B SaaS, including churn impact; discount norms; and accounting guidance specific to prepaid AI credits and breakage.

### SaaS valuation multiples compressed sharply into 2026

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Q1-Q2 2026; secondary compilation dated Aug 2026]** One compilation reports the SEG SaaS Index at 3.2 times enterprise value to trailing revenue in Q2 2026, down from 5.7 times in Q2 2025, and the Aventis public index at 3.4 times in March 2026. For private SaaS M&A it reports an SEG median of 4.0 times in Q2 2026 (average 6.2 times) and an Aventis median of 3.1 times in Q1 2026 against a 2015 to 2026 median of 4.5 times (quartiles 2.4 and 8.1 times); by size, about 3.3 times for $0 to 5M revenue against about 6.2 times for $500M and above ([Peony, SaaS valuation multiples, Aug 2026](https://www.peony.ink/blog/saas-valuation-multiples)). The SEG and Aventis figures were read through this compilation and not in the original reports.

### The Rule of 40 premium and the AI-native premium

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Among public SaaS companies in the Aventis 2026 data, the median multiple is 4.8 times for companies clearing the Rule of 40 against 2.7 times for those missing it, roughly 1.1 turns per 10 points. A broker quoted 8 to 15 times revenue for well-positioned AI-native companies against 4 to 6 times for traditional private SaaS in April 2026; that is a broker's stated range and not a dataset, and the same compilation notes that no primary data house publishes the commonly repeated tiered medians ([Peony, SaaS valuation multiples, Aug 2026](https://www.peony.ink/blog/saas-valuation-multiples)).

### Older multiples for comparison only

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Jan 2025, year-end 2024 data, now dated]** SaaS Capital reported a public index median of 7.0 times run-rate revenue and modelled private multiples of 4.8 times ARR for bootstrapped and 5.3 times for equity-backed companies; the inputs to its private valuation estimate are the public multiple, ARR growth and NRR ([SaaS Capital, private SaaS valuations, Jan 2025](https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/private-saas-company-valuations-multiples/)). **[INFERENCE]** With public medians near 3 times and private medians at 3 to 4 times revenue in 2026, a planning agent should not assume 2021-style or early-2025 multiples in cap-table or exit scenarios; use a range and state the date and source of the multiple.

### Bootstrapped versus equity-backed: similar growth, different profitability

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Q1 2025 survey]** Bootstrapped and equity-backed SaaS companies show similar median growth (23% against 25%), but 85% of bootstrapped companies are at or near breakeven against 46% of equity-backed ([SaaS Capital, Benchmarking Private SaaS Company Growth Rates 2025 (PDF)](https://www.saas-capital.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/RB33WS5-2025-B2B-Benchmarking-Private-SaaS-Company-Growth-Rates.pdf)). In late 2025 US venture deal value was about $80B per quarter, similar to 2021, while deal count had not recovered and average deal size was about $20M ([High Alpha webinar recap, late 2025](https://www.highalpha.com/blog/webinar-recap-unpacking-the-saas-benchmarks-report-2025)). **[INFERENCE] [HOUSE STYLE]** This supports the house-style preference for customer-financed growth: model non-dilutive routes (customer prepayment, Almi loans, Vinnova or EIC grants) before equity.

### Almi loans: eligibility and products

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Almi lists six loan products: Företagslån, Grönt lån, Tillväxtlån, Innovationslån, Exportlån and Mikrolån. General eligibility is a company registered in Sweden with up to 249 employees and turnover up to EUR 50 million or a balance sheet up to EUR 43 million; private individuals cannot apply; and processing normally takes 3 to 4 weeks from a complete application ([Almi, lån och finansiering](https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/)). Almi also offers risk capital and advisory, co-operates with banks, and requires BankID for applications; a company need not be registered to apply but must be before payout ([Almi, lån](https://www.almi.se/vara-tjanster/lan/)).

### Almi Tillväxtlån: up to SEK 10 million

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, 2026]** The Tillväxtlån is for innovative, established SME limited companies with a growth plan, up to SEK 10 million. Almi normally covers 30 to 50% of the capital need with co-financing from owners, banks or others. Interest is set individually, typically about 2 to 3 percentage points above bank rates and lower than Almi's other loans thanks to a European Investment Fund guarantee under InvestEU. The term is 12 to 84 months, an initial amortisation-free period is offered (length not stated), and security is usually a floating charge plus a limited personal guarantee. Application documents are a budget, the latest annual accounts, interim figures and prior-year comparatives where available ([Almi, Tillväxtlån](https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/tillvaxtlan/)).

### Almi Företagslån

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, 2026]** The Företagslån has no fixed upper amount; Almi normally finances 30 to 50% of the need, often alongside a bank; interest is set individually, normally about 2 to 3 points above banks; the term is normally 2 to 5 years; and security is usually a floating charge plus a limited personal guarantee ([Almi, Företagslån](https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/foretagslan/)). **[INFERENCE]** Almi debt is priced as risk capital and not as bank debt, and the 30 to 50% share means a company cannot fund a plan with Almi alone; it is a top-up to a bank loan or an equity round.

### Vinnova and Tillväxtverket

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Vinnova funds only through calls, each with its own terms; aid intensity is set per beneficiary under state-aid rules and normally does not cover all costs; on average about 30% of applicants are funded; decisions cannot be appealed; and only organisations that are not in financial difficulty are eligible ([Vinnova, Apply for funding](https://www.vinnova.se/en/apply-for-funding/)). Tillväxtverket provides its financial support through calls for applications ([Tillväxtverket, sök finansiering](https://tillvaxtverket.se/tillvaxtverket/sokfinansiering.html)). **[INFERENCE]** Grant funding is slow, competitive and co-financed, so it stays out of the base case until awarded.

### EIC Accelerator: grant up to EUR 2.5 million plus equity

**[PLATFORM CLAIM; FAST-MOVING, 2026-2027]** The EIC Accelerator offers a grant of up to EUR 2.5 million (technology readiness levels 6 to 8) and equity of EUR 1 million to EUR 10 million through the EIC Fund as direct equity or quasi-equity, in blended, grant-only (once per Horizon Europe period) or equity-only form. The process is a short proposal at any time (a 12-page form, a 10-slide deck and a 3-minute video, with feedback in about 4 to 6 weeks), then a full proposal, a jury interview, and award with due diligence. Listed full-proposal cut-offs are 20 January, 17 March, 1 September and 4 November 2027; 2027 budgets are EUR 428 million (Open) and EUR 150 million (Challenges); and proposals scoring 13 of 15 or more without an interview receive a Seal of Excellence ([European Innovation Council, EIC Accelerator](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)).

### Swedish tax incentives that act as funding

## 17. Financial controls and fraud risk

### Payment fraud is common

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, 2025 data, published 2026]** In AFP's 2026 Payments Fraud and Control Survey (22nd edition, 465 corporate practitioners, US-centric), 76% of organisations experienced attempted or actual payments fraud in 2025, slightly down on 2024; checks were the most targeted method at 58% of organisations, ahead of ACH and wires; business email compromise is described as still among the most common forms; and AI and deepfake fraud was examined for the first time, with the figures behind a paywall ([AFP, 2026 Payments Fraud and Control Survey page](https://www.financialprofessionals.org/training-resources/resources/survey-research-economic-data/details/payments-fraud)).

### Occupational fraud: controls shorten detection, and two red flags are cheap to act on

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** The ACFE's 2026 report covers 2,402 cases from 143 countries and finds that anti-fraud controls are associated with lower losses and faster detection. Tips arrive mainly through web forms (46%), email (34%) and phone (23%), and median losses are higher where red flags are present, for example an unusually close relationship with a vendor or customer ($300,000) or refusal to take holidays ($250,000) ([ACFE, Occupational Fraud 2026: A Report to the Nations](https://www.acfe.com/fraud-resources/report-to-the-nations)). **[INFERENCE]** Two cheap small-company controls follow: enforce holiday cover or rotation for anyone who handles payments, and separate ownership of the vendor relationship from the vendor master and from payment approval.

### COSO's framework and its guidance list

**[DURABLE]** COSO's 2013 Internal Control–Integrated Framework has five components, monitoring being one, and superseded the 2006 guidance for smaller public companies on 15 December 2014. COSO's guidance list includes 2024 guidance on robotic process automation and the 2026 guidance on generative AI ([COSO, Guidance on Internal Control](https://www.coso.org/guidance-on-ic)). The 2026 paper refers to five components and 17 principles ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)). **[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** The component names (control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, monitoring) and the idea that a small company applies them through a short risk-and-control matrix are standard descriptions that the research did not quote from a COSO page beyond the 2026 paper's component headings.

### Fewer people in G&A raises the weight of designed controls

**[VENDOR; FAST-MOVING, Q2 2026]** G&A headcount is planned to contract at AI-building software companies in ICONIQ's 2026 survey ([ICONIQ, 2026 State of AI, July 2026](https://www.iconiq.com/growth/insights/state-of-ai-2026)). **[INFERENCE]** With fewer people, separation of duties has to be designed into systems and agent permissions instead of relying on headcount.

### Classic controls: segregation, approval matrix, purchase-to-pay, order-to-cash

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** No primary source for classic control design was obtained, so the following is unsourced ordinary practice. Segregation to preserve even in a team of two or three: whoever creates or edits a vendor or bank detail does not approve the payment; whoever prepares a payment does not release it; whoever reconciles the bank cannot pay; payroll changes are approved by someone other than the processor. Compensating controls where headcount prevents separation: owner review of bank statements, dual authorisation in the bank, system audit logs, and a monthly review of vendor-master changes. Approval matrix: thresholds by amount and type (operating cost, capital spend, contracts with a term or auto-renewal, hiring, discounts, credit notes, refunds), named approvers, and nobody approves their own spend. Purchase-to-pay: vendor onboarding with company registration and VAT check, sanctions screen and bank-detail verification by call-back to an independently sourced number; purchase approval before commitment; three-way match where relevant; duplicate-invoice check; scheduled payment runs with dual release. Order-to-cash: signed order form, pricing and discount approval, invoice accuracy, credit-note approval, cash application, revenue-recognition review and dunning.

### Payment-fraud defences

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** Unsourced ordinary practice: any request to change bank details or make an urgent or secret payment is verified out-of-band through a known number and never through the channel it arrived on; no payment is made on the strength of a video or voice call alone, because of deepfake risk; bank-level allow-lists, limits and dual authorisation are used; and staff are trained with simulated business email compromise. A widely reported 2024 deepfake video-conference case is the usual illustration, but no source for it was opened, so its facts are not stated here.

### Software spend management

**[GAP; unverified working knowledge]** Unsourced ordinary practice for software spend is a single system of record for subscriptions, a named owner per tool, a renewal calendar with notice dates, card controls and a quarterly licence-utilisation review. No sourced expense-policy or procurement benchmarks were found.

### Gaps on controls

**[GAP]** Not captured: AFP's detailed figures on business email compromise share, deepfake incidence, recovery rates and control effectiveness (paywalled); the ACFE 2026 median loss, share of revenue lost and small-organisation findings; Swedish fraud statistics and Swedish payment controls around Bankgirot and BankID.

## 18. AI in the finance function: what the 2026 guidance says

### COSO's 2026 generative-AI paper: scope and stance

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** COSO's "Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI" (copyright 2026; authors Scott Emett, Marc Eulerich, Jason Guthrie of EY, Jason Pikoos of Meta and David A. Wood; commissioned by COSO) adapts the 2013 Integrated Framework instead of creating a new one, focuses on risks unique to or amplified by generative AI, and excludes rule-based automation and robotic process automation ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)). Its exact publication month is not stated in the PDF.

### Risks COSO names

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** The paper names hallucinations; prompt injection; opaque reasoning; model drift and vendor updates; fast-changing configurations such as prompts, thresholds and retrieval connectors; data exfiltration and privacy; bias; third-party opacity; data provenance gaps; "shadow AI"; and fraud through deepfakes and synthetic records ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).

### COSO's eight capability types

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** The eight capability types are data extraction and ingestion; data transformation and integration; automated transaction processing and reconciliation; workflow orchestration and autonomous task execution; judgment, forecasting and insight generation; AI-powered monitoring and continuous review; knowledge retrieval and summarisation; and human–AI collaboration. An appendix profiles each with risks, controls by component, metrics and artefacts ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)). A finance department can use the eight types as the taxonomy for its agent inventory.

### COSO's foundational characteristics

**[DURABLE]** The paper's foundational points are that output is probabilistic and should be treated as a claim to validate; systems change, so risk assessment and monitoring must be ongoing; AI scales errors as well as quality; low barriers to entry mean building and using it needs governance; and AI can help control AI, for example through multi-model cross-checks ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).

### COSO's controls by component

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Control environment: named owners and a RACI for prompts, retrieval data and transformation rules, an acceptable-use policy, training, and board visibility through a cross-functional committee. Risk assessment: objectives and success criteria per use case, including whether generative AI is the right tool, AI-specific fraud schemes, and reassessment triggered by vendor or model change. Control activities: human-in-the-loop review scaled to risk, separation of AI assistance from authoritative decisions, and models, configurations, embeddings and retrieval indexes treated as configuration items with an "AI bill of materials". Information and communication: capture prompts, inputs, outputs, sources, model versions and confidence scores. Monitoring: KPIs and KRIs, adversarial testing, a deficiency and remediation log, and multi-metric tolerances instead of pass or fail ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).

### COSO's specific controls

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** Specific controls in the paper include confidence thresholds that route low-confidence items to a person; separating configuration rights from approval rights; version history, approval workflow and rollback for model and prompt changes; provenance tagging, schema validation, pre- and post-reconciliations and citation requirements; and vendor-change notification with validation before deployment ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).

### COSO on agents

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** The paper's definitions include models that plan and execute multi-step tasks through tools. Under fraud risk it flags excessive agency, authorisation risk and insecure interfaces, and its controls include documented expected routing logic, simulation before go-live, multi-party approval of configuration changes and audit of routing outcomes ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).

### COSO's reliance test

**[DURABLE]** When management uses AI output as evidence for internal control over financial reporting, that use needs the same evidence standard as any other such control; uses that are not relied on can carry lighter evidence ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)). For agents this means the review burden follows what the output is used for, not how impressive it looks.

### COSO's six-step roadmap

**[FAST-MOVING, 2026]** The roadmap is: establish a governance structure; inventory use cases, including scans for shadow AI; assess risk by component; design and map controls; implement and communicate; and monitor and adapt, then repeat. The paper offers no separate small-entity guidance and says governance should fit size, complexity and AI usage ([COSO, Achieving Effective Internal Control Over Generative AI, 2026 (PDF)](https://www.coso.org/_files/ugd/719ba0_08f358f2c8f946fa9d26bd51d37b7117.pdf)).

### The UK FRC's guidance on generative and agentic AI

**[FAST-MOVING, March 2026]** The UK Financial Reporting Council published "Generative and Agentic AI Guidance" in March 2026. It is addressed to audit firms' central technical teams, is the FRC's second AI publication, is framed as good practice and not a response to failings, and leaves responsibility with the human auditor ([ICAS, 30 March 2026](https://icas.com/news-insights-events/news/audit-assurance/frc-issues-new-guidance-to-support-audit-firms-using-emerging-ai-technologies/); [FRC, Generative and Agentic AI Guidance, March 2026 (PDF)](https://media.frc.org.uk/documents/Generative_and_Agentic_AI_Guidance_crosPeBU.pdf)). It defines agentic AI as systems that orchestrate and carry out multiple tasks toward a goal with some autonomy over how, without step-by-step instruction, and a human in the loop as a person who directs, authorises or reviews actions or outputs at runtime ([FRC, Generative and Agentic AI Guidance, March 2026 (PDF)](https://media.frc.org.uk/documents/Generative_and_Agentic_AI_Guidance_crosPeBU.pdf)).

### FRC risk taxonomy and mitigations

**[FAST-MOVING, March 2026]** The FRC's risk taxonomy covers deficient output (model errors such as hallucination, omission and faulty reasoning; orchestrator errors; failures in data, rules or tool components; combination risk where errors amplify across steps; human input errors in task specification and configuration; poor source information), misuse of output, and non-compliant methodology. Mitigations fall into four groups: system design (workflow design, output schemas, scope and authority boundaries, prompt libraries, model ensembles); certification testing (default, edge and diverse cases, recertification triggers, limited deployment); staff education and governance; and human review at final output and at intermediate control points. Confidence is proportional to reliance: how much assurance a tool needs depends on how its output is used, and the file should record that AI was used and how output informed conclusions ([FRC, Generative and Agentic AI Guidance, March 2026 (PDF)](https://media.frc.org.uk/documents/Generative_and_Agentic_AI_Guidance_crosPeBU.pdf)).

### What the FRC guidance means for a Swedish company

**[INFERENCE]** The FRC text binds only UK audit firms. The auditor of a Swedish aktiebolag may be expected to follow similar thinking when relying on AI-prepared evidence, so the department should be able to show, for any figure an agent prepared, what the agent did, from which sources, with which model version, and who reviewed it. **[GAP]** No guidance on AI in finance functions from FAR, Revisorsinspektionen, IAASB or PCAOB was found; one unverified headline says the FRC is the only audit regulator with published AI guidance ([LearnSignal](https://www.learnsignal.com/blog/frc-ai-audit-guidance-what-it-requires/)); and the EU AI Act's application to finance workflows was not researched.

### Measured results of AI in finance are not sourced

**[GAP]** No sourced adoption rates or measured results for AI in accounts payable, close, reconciliation or forecasting were obtained, and no incident data on AI-agent errors in finance processes. Commonly cited automation targets (invoice capture and coding, bank and intercompany reconciliation, accrual and flux-analysis drafting, collections prioritisation, forecast baselines, expense audit) are unverified working knowledge, and vendor-reported time savings should be labelled as claims until measured locally. The nearest sourced data point is Nordic: 97% of Monterro's sample report positive internal effects from AI but only 27% report measurable cost reductions ([Monterro Nordic B2B Software Benchmark 2026, 19 April 2026 (PDF)](https://www.monterro.com/media/2finidot/monterro-nordic-software-benchmark-report-2026_web.pdf)).

### The EU Omnibus narrows CSRD

**[FAST-MOVING, Feb 2025 to July 2026; checked on primary source]** At EU level the February 2025 Omnibus proposed limiting CSRD to companies with more than 1,000 employees; an April 2025 directive postponed waves two and three; a July 2025 "quick fix" froze wave-one requirements for 2025 to 2026; Parliament and Council reached political agreement on Omnibus I on 9 December 2025; on 3 July 2026 the Commission adopted a delegated act with voluntary standards for companies protected by the value-chain cap; and a July 2025 Recommendation sets a voluntary SME standard (VSME) ([European Commission, corporate sustainability reporting](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/capital-markets-union-and-financial-markets/company-reporting-and-auditing/company-reporting/corporate-sustainability-reporting_en)). **[INFERENCE]** A typical growing SaaS company is outside mandatory sustainability reporting; its practical exposure is customer questionnaires, for which the VSME and value-chain-cap standards are the reference. **[GAP]** Formal adoption, final thresholds and the transposition deadline of the Omnibus directive were not confirmed.

### UK VAT on digital services to consumers

**[DURABLE; checked on primary source]** Digital services supplied to UK consumers are liable to UK VAT, and a business outside the UK making such supplies needs to register for UK VAT. Where the customer gives a valid VAT number the supply is treated as business-to-business and the customer accounts for the VAT. Location is where the consumer usually lives, evidenced by billing address, IP address, bank details or similar ([GOV.UK, VAT rules for digital services to private consumers](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-vat-rules-if-you-supply-digital-services-to-private-consumers)). **[INFERENCE]** The text implies no small-seller threshold for overseas suppliers, meaning registration from the first sale, though the page did not say so in those words. **[GAP]** The UK rate and the exact threshold position were not on the page read.

### DAC7, Pillar Two, US sales tax and other non-EU regimes

**[GAP; unverified background]** None of these was researched. The unverified recollection is: DAC7 obliges platform operators connecting sellers with buyers to report sellers' income annually, so a SaaS vendor selling its own software is normally not in scope unless it adds a marketplace feature; Pillar Two sets a 15% minimum effective tax for groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, implemented in Sweden by the top-up tax act, and matters to a small SaaS company only as a subsidiary of a large group; US states impose economic nexus for sales tax since a 2018 Supreme Court decision, commonly at USD 100,000 of sales into a state, with SaaS taxability varying by state; and Norway, Switzerland and other non-EU digital-services VAT regimes exist. Each needs a specialist check before the first sale into the jurisdiction.

## 22. Conclusion: what this changes about running finance with agents

**[INFERENCE]** The material turns the house style from a slogan into a design discipline with a boundary. Hormozi's models tell the department what to ask of every offer: when the cash arrives, whether it covers the cost of winning and serving the customer, and which constraint the next krona should go to. Swedish accounting rules then decide how the answer is recorded, and the benchmarks decide whether the answer is good for a SaaS company of this size and funding model. The most useful single artefact is the two-view unit-economics report: cash payback by day 30, 90 and 365 next to accrual payback, with deferred revenue shown between them. A department that produces it monthly can follow the house style at full strength without ever misstating revenue.

**[INFERENCE]** The second shift is that an AI finance department's reliability rests less on the model than on what surrounds it: a statutory calendar, code-level reconciliation, logs kept as accounting documentation, and a strict split between who configures, who prepares and who approves. The research also shows where the department is still blind. Transfer pricing, permanent establishment, pensions, treasury, classic control design and consumer-credit law were not verified, and several 2027 rules hang on a budget bill presented the day after this document's status date. The honest operating posture is to run the verified parts with confidence, treat every [GAP] as a question owed to a named human or to Research, and re-date the fast-moving figures at each half-year.
