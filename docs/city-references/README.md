# City references (the owner, 2026-11-12)

The owner's photos of Manhattan, the base point for how the VAIC City should look and feel. They are inspiration
only: the City stays in its own pixel style, and no logo, brand, billboard or building is copied from them.

| File | What to take from it |
|---|---|
| `112.jpg` | A street canyon: brick, limestone and glass towers shoulder to shoulder, a yellow cab in the middle |
| `113.jpg` | A narrow downtown street: stone arches, brick walk-ups with fire escapes and flags, glass towers behind |
| `114.jpg` | A wide avenue between dark glass curtain walls, a planted island, cabs everywhere, an old tower closing the view |
| `115.jpg` | A busy square in the centre: lit signs and screens on every building, glass towers above |
| `116.jpg` | The skyline: dense mid-rises with water towers on their roofs, tall glass towers rising out of them, setbacks |
| `117.jpg` | A brick alley between walk-ups, black fire escapes zig-zagging down the facades |

The direction they set (decision 273): towers tightly packed, tallest and densest in the middle around HQ and thinning
outward; not a street between every building; glass curtain walls next to brick and limestone, setbacks, water towers,
fire escapes, a few lit signs in the centre; houses outside the town where some people live and take a cab in.
