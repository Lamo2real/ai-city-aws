# Pricing and packaging

- Find the value metric: what grows as the customer gets more value (seats, orders, usage, locations). Price on it.
- Floor: cost to serve per unit (see unit-economics) plus target margin. Ceiling: the value to the customer and the closest alternatives' prices (cite them with dates).
- Three tiers at most to start: an entry plan that lets people succeed, the plan most should buy, and one for larger customers. Name each by who it is for.
- For every feature, decide: in every plan, a reason to upgrade, or add-on. Don't put the core value behind the top tier.
- Annual discount, trial or free plan only with a reason tied to how customers buy.
- A test plan: what to change, for which new visitors, for how long, and which number decides it.
- Proposals only; changing live prices is the owner's call.
