# KPI definitions

- At most eight KPIs, each tied to a goal the owner has stated. One north-star metric on top.
- For each: name, plain-language meaning, exact formula (numerator, denominator, filters), data source and table, time window, time zone, who owns it, how often it is reviewed, target and why.
- Write down edge cases: refunds, test accounts, internal users, trials, currency.
- Mark leading vs lagging, and pair each vanity number with one that shows quality (sign-ups with activation).
- Keep a change log: any change to a definition gets a date and the reason.
