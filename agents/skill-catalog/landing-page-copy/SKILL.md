# Landing page copy

- One page, one audience, one action. Name all three before writing.
- Headline: the outcome the visitor wants, in their words. Sub-headline: how, in one sentence.
- Above the fold: the call to action and one piece of proof (a number, a logo, a quote the owner can stand behind).
- Three benefits, each with a short line on how it works. Benefits before features.
- Answer the top objections (price, time, trust, switching) in a short FAQ.
- Repeat the same call to action at the end. Every button says what happens ("Start free trial", not "Submit").
- Give two headline options to test. Plain words, short sentences, no claims you can't back up.
