# Weekly status report

- Gather the week from the city's own record: finished and running jobs, decisions, spend, and anything waiting for the owner.
- Open with three lines: the week in one sentence, the most important result, and the one thing that needs the owner (or "nothing needs you").
- Done: bullets with the outcome and where to find it, not the activity ("Pricing page live", not "Worked on pricing").
- Next week: the three to five things that will move, with who does them.
- Stuck: what, why, and what would unblock it.
- Numbers that matter for the business (revenue, leads, spend) with last week beside them.
- Under one page. No filler, no praise, no blame.
