# Discovery call prep

- One page. The company: what it does, size, recent news, who they sell to (public sources only, each with its link).
- The person: role, how long in it, what they likely care about. Nothing private or personal.
- Three likely problems we solve for a company like this, each with a question that tests it.
- Eight open questions in order: their situation, the problem, its cost, what they've tried, who decides, timing, budget, next step.
- What a good outcome of the call is (a second meeting, a trial, a proposal) and how to ask for it.
- Two likely objections and an honest answer to each.
