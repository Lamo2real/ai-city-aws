# Monthly financial update

- One page. Headline first: cash, runway, revenue this month and its change from last month and the same month last year.
- A small table: revenue, gross margin, operating costs, net burn or profit, cash at month end, with last month beside it.
- Three to five plain sentences on why the numbers moved; name the cause, not the account code.
- What went better and worse than planned, with the size of the gap.
- Next month: the decisions or risks that matter, and any ask (an introduction, a hire, a decision).
- Use the books as the source; a figure that isn't closed yet says "estimate". Never round a loss into a profit.
- Drafts only; the owner sends it.
