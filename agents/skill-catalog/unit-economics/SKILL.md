# Unit economics

- Define the unit first (a customer, an order, a seat) and keep it the same throughout.
- Gross margin per unit: price minus everything it takes to deliver one (payment fees, hosting, shipping, support time, refunds).
- CAC: sales and marketing spend in a period divided by the new customers it brought, per channel when the data allows.
- LTV: margin per period × expected lifetime (1 ÷ churn); cap the lifetime at 3 years unless the owner has longer data.
- Payback: months of margin to earn back CAC. Under 12 months is healthy for most small businesses; say where this one sits.
- Show every input with its source and date; mark estimates as estimates.
- End with the one lever that moves the result most (price, churn, a channel's CAC) and what a 10% change in it is worth.
