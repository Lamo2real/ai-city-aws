# Design critique

- Start with the goal of the screen and who it's for. Judge everything against that.
- First look: where does the eye go first, second, third? Is that the right order?
- Clarity: can someone tell what this is and what to do in five seconds?
- Consistency with the rest of the product: spacing, type, colour, components, wording.
- States: empty, loading, error, long text, many items, phone width.
- Say what works, then the top three changes in order of impact, each with a concrete suggestion. Taste notes last and marked as taste.
