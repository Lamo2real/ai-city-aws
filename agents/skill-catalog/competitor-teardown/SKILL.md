# Competitor teardown

- Public sources only (their site, docs, pricing page, changelog, reviews, job posts, filings), each with its link and the date read.
- Positioning: who they say they are for and their main promise, in their words.
- Product: main features, what's missing, how it's sold (self-serve, sales-led), integrations.
- Pricing: plans and prices as published; note what's hidden behind "contact us".
- Customers: who uses them and what reviews praise and complain about (quote short phrases, count themes).
- Signals: hiring, funding, recent launches.
- End with where we win, where they win, and one move for each of product, pricing and messaging. Never copy their copy or assets.
