# Win/loss review

- Pull the closed deals for the period from the CRM: won, lost and gone quiet, with size, source, length and stated reason.
- For each lost deal, separate the stated reason from the likely one (price often means value wasn't clear).
- Patterns: by source, deal size, industry, competitor and sales step where deals stall.
- Wins: what the buyers had in common and what they said tipped it.
- Three to five findings, each with the evidence (deal count, quotes) and one change to try.
- Small numbers: say when a pattern rests on fewer than five deals.
