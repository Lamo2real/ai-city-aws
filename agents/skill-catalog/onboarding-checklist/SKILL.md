# Onboarding checklist

- Ask first who is being onboarded and what success looks like at day 7, 30 and 90.
- Steps in order, grouped Before day one, Day one, Week one, First month. Each step: what, who does it, by when, and how you know it is done.
- Accounts and access: list what they need and the least access that does the job; note when to remove it.
- Customers: kickoff, setup, first value moment, check-in, review. Name the first value moment explicitly.
- Hires and contractors: contract, equipment, accounts, introductions, first task, a check-in on day 30.
- Keep it as a reusable template plus the filled-in copy for this person.
