# Brand voice check

- Read the brand profile in the knowledge base first (voice, words to use and avoid, audience). If there is none, say so and ask for three examples the owner likes.
- Mark each issue with the sentence, why it's off-voice and a rewrite. Group as Must fix, Should fix, Fine either way.
- Check names, product terms and capitalisation against the brand's own spelling.
- Flag claims that need proof (numbers, "best", "only", health, money or legal claims) and missing disclaimers.
- Keep the writer's meaning; change as little as needed.
- End with a one-line verdict: ready, ready after fixes, or rewrite.
