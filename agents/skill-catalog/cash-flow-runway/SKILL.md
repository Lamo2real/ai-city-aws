# Cash flow and runway

- Start from today's cash in every bank and payment account (read from the accounting system or the owner's numbers; never guess a balance).
- Week by week for 13 weeks: money in (invoices due, by how reliably each customer pays; recurring revenue) and money out (payroll, rent, subscriptions, suppliers, tax dates, loan payments).
- Show three lines: expected, cautious (late payers slip two weeks, 10% less new revenue) and the lowest week's balance in each.
- Runway = months until cash falls below one month of costs, on the cautious line. Say it in one sentence first.
- List the five biggest assumptions and what would change the answer most.
- Flag any week that goes negative, with the cheapest fixes (chase an invoice, move a payment, cut a subscription).
- A forecast only; never move money or change a payment date. Changes are proposals for the owner.
