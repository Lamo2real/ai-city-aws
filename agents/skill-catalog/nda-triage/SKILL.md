# NDA triage

- Not legal advice; the owner or a lawyer decides. Say so once at the top.
- Facts: parties, mutual or one-way (and which way), purpose, term of the agreement and of confidentiality, governing law.
- Check the usual risks: confidential information defined too broadly, no standard exclusions (public, already known, independently developed, received from others), no end date, non-solicit or non-compete tucked in, IP assignment or licence, residuals clause, one-sided remedies, unusual jurisdiction.
- Verdict: Green (standard, sign), Yellow (sign after these small changes), Red (needs a lawyer), with the clause and the suggested wording for each change.
- Never sign or send anything; drafts only.
