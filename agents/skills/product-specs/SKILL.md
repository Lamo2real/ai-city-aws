# Product specs

Structure (one page when possible): problem and who has it; evidence (research, analytics, tickets, revenue); goal and success metric; the solution in user stories; out of scope; acceptance criteria; designs (**Figma** links); risks and open questions; launch plan.

- Tickets in **Linear** or **Jira** that Application can pick up, each with acceptance criteria.
- Review with Application for effort and with Support and Sales for customer impact before the owner approves.
