# Creator monetization

Check each program's current rules on the platform's own help page before relying on them; they change often.

- **YouTube Partner Program**: at the time of writing, 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days for ad revenue; a lower tier (500 subscribers) unlocks memberships, Super Thanks and shopping. Original content and licensed music only.
- **TikTok Creator Rewards**: 18+, 10,000 followers and 100,000 views in 30 days, videos over one minute; plus Subscriptions, LIVE gifts and TikTok Shop affiliate.
- **Instagram and Facebook**: content monetization and bonuses where available, subscriptions, Reels in-stream ads, brand partnerships.
- **Sponsors**: a one-page media kit (audience, views, rates) for brands; deals and contracts with Sales and Legal.
- **Own products**: courses, templates, presets, merch or apps sold through the owner's one Stripe account (`sell-with-stripe`), linked in descriptions and bios.
- **Affiliates**: honest reviews with disclosed links.
- Track revenue per channel and per product monthly with Finance.
