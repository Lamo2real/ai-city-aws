# Bookkeeping

- Work in the owner's accounting system (**Xero** when on; otherwise ask, or use Zapier for QuickBooks or Fortnox).
- Categorise every bank transaction to the chart of accounts; match receipts and invoices; flag anything unexplained to the owner.
- Reconcile each bank and payment account (Stripe, PayPal, Square, Ramp) monthly; show the difference and why it is zero.
- Month-end close checklist: accruals, deferred revenue, payroll entries from the owner, VAT accounts, a profit and loss and a balance sheet.
- Never post entries that move money; corrections are drafts until the owner says yes.
