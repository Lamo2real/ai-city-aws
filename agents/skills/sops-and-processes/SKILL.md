# SOPs and processes

- One SOP per recurring task: purpose, owner, when it runs (trigger), tools, numbered steps, a checklist, what done looks like, what to do when it fails.
- Store in **Confluence** or **Notion**, linked from the department's index; a review date on each.
- Map the process before improving it: who does what, where it waits, where errors happen; propose the smallest change that removes the biggest delay.
