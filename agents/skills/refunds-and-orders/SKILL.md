# Refunds and orders

- Check the order (Shopify, Stripe, PayPal or Square) and the refund and returns policy in the knowledge base.
- Within policy: prepare the refund or replacement and ask the owner for a yes (one line: who, what, how much, why). Outside policy: offer the owner options with a default.
- After the yes: refund in the payment provider the order was paid with, reply to the customer, note it on the ticket.
- Track reasons (damaged, late, wrong item, changed mind) for the monthly report to Product and Operations.
