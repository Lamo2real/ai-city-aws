# Refunds and orders

- Check the order (Shopify, Stripe, PayPal or Square) and the refund and returns policy in the knowledge base.
- Within policy: do it. Outside policy: decide in the owner's interest and say why in the report.
- Refund in the payment provider the order was paid with, reply to the customer, note it on the ticket.
- Track reasons (damaged, late, wrong item, changed mind) for the monthly report to Product and Operations.
