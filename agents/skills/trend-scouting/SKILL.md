# Trend scouting

- Launches and reactions: **Product Hunt**, Hacker News, Reddit and X threads (via web search).
- Money: **Crunchbase** funding rounds and new companies in the space; app store top charts; Etsy, Gumroad and Shopify niches; creators' income streams (video channels, courses, templates, newsletters, digital products).
- Demand: search interest (Google Trends via web), forum questions, reviews complaining about current tools.
- For each trend: what it is, the evidence (numbers and links, dated), who pays today and how much, how crowded it is, and whether it fits the owner's skills and the city's departments (IT can build software, Marketing can make videos and ads, Sales can sell B2B).
