# Research papers

## Find
- New AI and ML work: **Hugging Face** daily papers and trending models; arXiv listings (cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.CV, cs.LG …) through **Jina** or **Exa**.
- Peer-reviewed evidence: **Consensus** and **OpenAlex** (citations, venues, authors).
- The labs' own pages: IBM Research, Google DeepMind and Research, Meta AI, Microsoft Research, OpenAI, Anthropic, NVIDIA, Apple ML, MIT, Stanford, Berkeley, ETH, KTH. Read them with WebFetch, **Jina** or **Firecrawl**.

## Judge each paper
- What problem, what method, what result (numbers, compared with what), on which data.
- Strength of evidence: peer-reviewed or preprint, venue, citations, replication, code and weights released.
- Licences: the paper's code and model licences (MIT, Apache-2.0, CC-BY-NC, research-only) and any patent notes; whether commercial use is allowed.
- Readiness: a lab result, an open model anyone can run, or a product already.

## Write
For each paper: title, authors and lab, date, link, a three-line plain summary, why it matters, the commercial angle, and the licence. Rank the set by commercial potential and say why.
