# Budgets and forecasts

- Start from actuals (accounting system, Stripe, Ramp): revenue by product, costs by category.
- Assumptions written down (growth, price, hires, ad spend) and easy to change.
- 12-month cash-flow forecast: cash in, cash out, the lowest point and when; alert the owner when runway is under 6 months.
- Monthly: actual versus budget, the three biggest variances and why.
- Deliver as a spreadsheet the owner can open (Airtable or a shared file) plus a one-page summary.
