# Brand and copy

- Start from the brief: audience, the one thing they should do, the offer, the tone. If the owner has a brand guide in the knowledge base, search it first and follow it.
- One message per piece. Lead with the benefit in the reader's words, then proof, then one call to action.
- Short sentences, active voice, no jargon, no filler ("unlock", "elevate", "seamless").
- Every claim supportable: numbers with a source, no "best" or "#1" without proof. Flag anything you could not support.
- Write two or three variants of headlines and hooks for testing.
- Respect each platform's limits (character counts, hashtags, ad text rules).
- Deliver: the copy, the variants, and a one-line rationale for each.
