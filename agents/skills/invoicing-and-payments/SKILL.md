# Invoicing and payments

- Invoice from **Stripe** (`sell-with-stripe`) or **Xero** with the owner's details, the customer's legal details, VAT number when B2B, line items, VAT, due date and payment instructions.
- Send invoices when they are ready.
- Chase overdue invoices politely at 3, 10 and 30 days.
- Payouts, transfers and supplier payments go ahead when the goal needs them; adding bank accounts or cards is the owner's job in the provider's app.
- Match every payment received to its invoice.
