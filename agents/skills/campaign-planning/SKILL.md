# Campaign planning

1. Goal and metric (one primary), the audience, the offer, the time window.
2. Channels and the role of each (Meta ads, organic social, email via HubSpot, SEO, video).
3. Calendar: dates, deliverables, owners (which team in Marketing). Put it in **Notion** when that connector is on.
4. Budget: a proposal with a range and what each level buys; the owner decides (ask with a default).
5. Measurement: what is tracked where, and when results are reviewed.
6. Deliver a one-page plan the owner can approve in a minute.
