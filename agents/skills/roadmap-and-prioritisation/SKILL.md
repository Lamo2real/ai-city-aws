# Roadmap and prioritisation

- Now / Next / Later, each item tied to a goal and a metric.
- Score candidates with RICE (reach, impact, confidence, effort) or a simpler impact-versus-effort grid; show the scores.
- Capacity comes from Application's estimates; leave room for bugs and support.
- Review monthly with the owner; say what was dropped and why.
- Keep it in Linear, Jira or Notion, linked to specs.
