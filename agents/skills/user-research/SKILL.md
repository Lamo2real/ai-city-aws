# User research

- Start from the decision the research must inform; write 3–5 research questions.
- Interviews: 5–8 people per segment, open questions about past behaviour (not hypotheticals), recorded only with consent (**Fireflies** or **Granola** notes).
- Surveys (**Typeform**, **SurveyMonkey**): short, one question per screen, no leading questions.
- Usability tests: 5 users per round, tasks not instructions, note where they struggle.
- Synthesis: themes with evidence counts and quotes, what it means for the decision, confidence level.
