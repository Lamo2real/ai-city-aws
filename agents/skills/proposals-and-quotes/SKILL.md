# Proposals and quotes

1. Gather: the customer's problem, scope, timeline, decision makers (from call notes and the CRM).
2. Use the owner's template and price list from the knowledge base or **PandaDoc**; never invent prices or discounts.
3. Structure: their goal, the solution, deliverables and timeline, price and payment terms, proof, next steps.
4. Create the document in **PandaDoc** or **Docusign** and send it for signature; the owner signs.
5. On acceptance: update the deal to won and ask Finance for the invoice or a Stripe payment link (`sell-with-stripe`).
