# Video and voice

Connectors: **Higgsfield** (AI video and images), **HeyGen** (presenter/avatar videos), **ElevenLabs** (voice-overs, sound).

1. Script first: hook in the first two seconds, one message, a call to action; 15–30 s for social, 60–90 s for explainers. Get the script approved by your manager before generating.
2. Choose the tool: product or scene shots → Higgsfield; a talking presenter → HeyGen; narration or voice → ElevenLabs (pick a voice that fits the brand; never clone a real person's voice without their documented consent).
3. Formats: 9:16 for Reels, Stories, TikTok and Shorts; 1:1 or 4:5 for feeds; 16:9 for YouTube and the web. Burn in captions: most social video plays muted.
4. Check the cost of a generation before running it when the tool shows one; keep within the budget hint.
5. Deliver the links, the script, and the settings used so it can be regenerated.
