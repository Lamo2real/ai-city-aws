# Policies and terms

- Start from the business's real practice (ask Operations, Finance, Support) and the owner's existing texts in the knowledge base.
- Consumer sales in the EU: right of withdrawal (14 days), clear prices with VAT, delivery terms, complaints, the online dispute resolution link where required.
- Plain language, numbered sections, a date and a version.
- Internal policies (acceptable use, security, expenses, remote work): short and practical.
- Publish nothing; drafts for the owner, flagged for a lawyer where the risk is high.
