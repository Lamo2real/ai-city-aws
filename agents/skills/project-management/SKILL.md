# Project management

- Kick-off: goal, scope, success measure, milestones, owners per department, dependencies.
- Plan in the owner's tool (**Asana**, **monday.com** or **ClickUp**; Jira for IT-heavy work): tasks with one owner and a due date.
- Weekly status in three lines: on track or not, what changed, what needs a decision; generated from the tool.
- Risks and blockers raised the day they appear, with options and a recommended default.
- Close: what was delivered, lessons, follow-ups.
