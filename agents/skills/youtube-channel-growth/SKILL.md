# YouTube channel growth

- **Niche and promise**: one audience, one promise; the channel banner and about text say it.
- **Ideas from demand**: **vidIQ** or **TubeBuddy** keyword scores, competitors' outliers (videos far above their channel average), comments and questions.
- **Title + thumbnail first**: a curiosity gap or a clear outcome, under 60 characters; thumbnail with one face or object, 3 words at most, high contrast (**Ideogram** or **Canva**, 1280×720).
- **Retention**: hook in 15 s, pattern breaks every 30–60 s, pay off the title; check audience retention and fix the drop points.
- **SEO**: description with the keyword in the first lines, chapters, 3–5 relevant tags, end screens and cards to the next video.
- **Shorts** feed the channel: 3–5 a week cut from the long videos.
- **Cadence**: a schedule the team can keep (for example one long video and four Shorts a week).
- Report weekly: views, watch time, click-through rate, average view duration, subscribers, revenue.
