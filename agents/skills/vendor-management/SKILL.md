# Vendor management

- Requirements first (must-have, nice-to-have, budget), then compare at least three options with prices, terms, reviews and data protection.
- Recommend one with the reasons and the total cost for the first year.
- Contracts go through Legal (`contract-review`); purchases go ahead when the goal needs them; report the cost.
- Track every vendor (owner, cost, renewal date, notice period) in **Airtable** or the owner's tracker; remind the owner 30 days before renewals.
