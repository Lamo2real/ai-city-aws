# Google Ads campaigns

Connector: **Google Ads** (through Pipeboard; the owner signs in with their Google account).

1. Goal and conversion tracking first (Google Analytics 4 or Google Ads conversion tag); say so if tracking is missing.
2. Search: tightly themed ad groups, keywords with match types, negative keywords, responsive search ads with 10–15 headlines and 4 descriptions, sitelinks and callouts.
3. YouTube and Performance Max: assets from Design and Video & voice (images 1.91:1, 1:1, 4:5; videos 16:9 and 9:16), audience signals.
4. **Budget is the owner's decision**: ask with the plan. Create campaigns **paused**. Exception: when the brief comes from a workflow ("Spend the owner approved: …"), the owner already said yes to exactly that daily budget and those dates in AI City; publish it to run from the start date, never above that budget. Billing and funds are set by the owner in Google Ads → Billing.
5. Report what was created, the budget asked for, and the metrics to watch (CPC, CTR, conversion rate, CPA, ROAS).
