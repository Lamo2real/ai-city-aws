# Email marketing

- Consent first: only contacts who opted in; an unsubscribe link in every email; GDPR and the ePrivacy rules for the owner's countries.
- Core flows: welcome series, abandoned cart or browse, post-purchase, win-back. Then campaigns (launches, offers, newsletters).
- Each email: one goal, subject line plus preview text (two or three variants), mobile-first layout, one clear call to action.
- Build in **Klaviyo** or **HubSpot** as drafts; never schedule or send without the owner's yes.
- Report: segments, flows, sends planned, and the metrics to watch (open, click, conversion, unsubscribe rates).
