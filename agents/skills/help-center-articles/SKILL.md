# Help center articles

- Pick topics from the most frequent tickets and searches.
- One question per article; the title is the question; the answer in the first two lines; numbered steps with screenshots when it helps.
- Keep them current: a review date, and update when the product or policy changes.
- Publish in Intercom, Help Scout or the owner's help center tool when ready; add matching saved replies (macros).
