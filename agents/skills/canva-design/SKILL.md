# Canva design

Uses the **Canva** connector (the owner signs in once).

1. Search the owner's existing designs and brand kit first; reuse their colours, fonts and logos.
2. Pick the right format and size: Instagram post 1080×1350, story/reel 1080×1920, Facebook/LinkedIn post 1200×1200 or 1200×628, presentation 16:9, A4 resume or flyer.
3. Generate or autofill from a template; one clear focal point, readable type (no more than two fonts), contrast that passes on a phone, text under 20 % of an ad image.
4. Make variants when testing (two to three).
5. Export the right file (PNG for posts, MP4 for animated, PDF for print and resumes) and share the edit link.
6. Report: links, sizes, and what to check before publishing. Nothing is posted by you.
