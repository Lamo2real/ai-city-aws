# Privacy and GDPR

- Map personal data: what, why (lawful basis), where it is stored, who receives it (processors), how long it is kept (records of processing).
- Privacy notice in plain language for each site and app; cookie consent that blocks non-essential cookies until the visitor agrees.
- A data processing agreement (DPA) with every processor; check transfers outside the EU/EEA (SCCs, adequacy).
- Data-subject requests (access, deletion) answered within one month; a template and a log.
- Breach plan: assess, contain, notify the supervisory authority (IMY in Sweden) within 72 hours when required.
- Check current guidance on the authority's site; flag uncertain points for a lawyer.
