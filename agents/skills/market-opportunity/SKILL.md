# Market opportunity

1. Customer and problem: who exactly (B2B: role and company type; B2C: person and situation), the painful problem, how they solve it today.
2. Market size bottom-up: number of reachable customers × price × realistic share. Show the arithmetic and sources; avoid top-down "1% of a huge market".
3. Competitors: 5–10 alternatives (**Crunchbase**, **Exa**, **Product Hunt**, search), their prices, strengths, weaknesses and reviews.
4. Differentiation: why the research finding or our angle makes this better, cheaper or new; how long until others copy it.
5. Pricing: what customers pay for the alternatives; a price and model (subscription, one-off, usage, ads, services).
6. Risks: technical, legal (licences, data, regulation), market. Score the opportunity 1–5 on pain, size, competition, fit and speed to first revenue.
