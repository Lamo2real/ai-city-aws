# CRM hygiene

- Every contact belongs to a company; merge duplicates (same email or domain) after checking.
- Every open deal has: stage, amount, expected close date, next step with a date, owner. Flag deals with no activity in 14 days.
- Log calls and emails with a short summary (use **Gong**, **Fireflies** or **Granola** notes when on).
- Weekly pipeline report: new, moved, won, lost (with reasons), forecast for the month and quarter.
- Never delete records without the owner's yes; archive or mark lost instead.
