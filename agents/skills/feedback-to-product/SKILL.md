# Feedback to Product

- Tag feedback consistently (feature request, bug, confusion, praise) with the product area.
- Monthly: the top themes with counts, revenue or customers affected, two or three verbatim quotes each.
- Bugs go to Application and requests to Product as tickets (Jira or Linear) linking the conversations.
- Close the loop: when something ships, draft a reply to the customers who asked.
- Surveys (CSAT, NPS) through **Typeform** or **SurveyMonkey**, drafted for the owner's yes.
