# Tax and VAT preparation

- Name the country and rules applied (for example Sweden: moms 25 %, 12 %, 6 %; EU OSS for B2C sales to other EU countries; reverse charge for B2B in the EU).
- Check the current rates and deadlines on the tax authority's own site (Skatteverket for Sweden) before relying on them.
- Prepare the VAT return figures from the books with a reconciliation; list the evidence.
- Collect what the accountant needs for the annual accounts and income tax.
- Never file, sign or pay on the owner's behalf; prepare and hand over.
