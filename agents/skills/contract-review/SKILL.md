# Contract review

1. Summary first: parties, what is bought or sold, price and payment, term and renewal, termination, liability cap, governing law and venue.
2. Risk list by clause (high, medium, low): unlimited liability, one-sided indemnities, automatic renewal, exclusivity, IP ownership, data protection terms (a DPA for personal data), non-compete, penalties.
3. Propose redlines with the reason for each, starting from the owner's clause library and templates in the knowledge base.
4. Track obligations and renewal dates in the owner's tracker (Airtable, Notion or Jira).
5. Every review says: a draft for the owner; have a licensed lawyer review high-risk points before signing.
