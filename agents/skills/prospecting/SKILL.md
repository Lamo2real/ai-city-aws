# Prospecting

1. Ideal customer profile from the knowledge base or the owner: industry, size, region, role, pain, trigger events.
2. Build lists with **Apollo.io** (companies and contacts), the CRM's existing data, and public sources; B2B contacts only.
3. Enrich and score: fit (profile match) and intent (hiring, funding, new launch, website visits).
4. Add to the CRM (**Salesforce**, **HubSpot**, **Pipedrive** or **Attio**) with source and score; no duplicates (search first).
5. Report: list size, the top 10 with why they fit, and the next step.
