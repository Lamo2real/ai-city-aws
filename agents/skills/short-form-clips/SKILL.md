# Short-form clips

- Source: a long video, a podcast, a webinar. Run it through **Opus Clip** (or pick moments by hand from the transcript).
- Pick moments that stand alone: a surprising claim, a clear tip, a strong emotion; 20–60 s.
- Reframe to 9:16 with the speaker centred; burned-in captions with keywords highlighted; a text hook on screen in the first second.
- One clip, one idea; end with a reason to follow or a link in bio.
- Make 5–10 clips per long video; schedule them over days, not all at once (`social-publishing`).
